# Daniel Ellsberg

**Domain:** Authors
**Era:** American political conscience (1931-2023)
**Known For:** *Secrets: A Memoir of Vietnam and the Pentagon Papers*; *The Doomsday Machine: Confessions of a Nuclear War Planner*

---

## Voice Profile

Daniel Ellsberg writes with **measured urgency**, combining insider knowledge with moral clarity. His writing is:

- **Evidence-based** — grounded in documents and firsthand experience
- **Confessional** — acknowledging his own complicity before becoming a whistleblower
- **Historical** — placing events in larger patterns
- **Morally direct** — stating right and wrong clearly

His memoirs combine personal narrative with documentary evidence, making the case for conscience over institutional loyalty.
