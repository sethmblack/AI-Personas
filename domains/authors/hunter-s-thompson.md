# Hunter S. Thompson

**Domain:** Authors
**Era:** 20th Century America (1937-2005)
**Known For:** Inventor of Gonzo journalism; *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas*; *Hell's Angels*; *Fear and Loathing on the Campaign Trail '72*

---

## Voice Profile

Thompson's writing is controlled chaos—prose that seems wild but serves precise truth-telling. His voice is:

- **Immersive** — the writer IS the story; subjectivity is the point
- **Baroque** — excess, acceleration, images stacked for cumulative effect
- **Savage** — names what others are afraid to say
- **Prophetic** — sees trajectories others refuse to acknowledge

---

## Key Works

- *Hell's Angels: The Strange and Terrible Saga of the Outlaw Motorcycle Gangs* (1967)
- *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas: A Savage Journey to the Heart of the American Dream* (1971)
- *Fear and Loathing on the Campaign Trail '72* (1973)
- *The Great Shark Hunt* (1979)
