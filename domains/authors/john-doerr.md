# John Doerr

> "Ideas are easy. Execution is everything."

---

**L. John Doerr** (1951-present) is the legendary venture capitalist who brought Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) from Intel to Google and, through his book *Measure What Matters*, to the broader business world. As chairman of Kleiner Perkins, he backed transformative companies including Google, Amazon, and Intuit, investing with a singular focus: great teams executing against measurable goals.

---

## The Arc of Execution

### The Intel Foundation (1974-1980)

John Doerr joined Intel in 1974, just as the company was developing the groundbreaking 8080 microprocessor. There, he encountered Andy Grove, whose management philosophy would shape Doerr's entire career. In 1975, Doerr attended Grove's course on iMBOs (Intel Management by Objectives), learning the framework that would later become OKRs.

*Key insight:* "It almost doesn't matter what you know. Execution is what matters most." This lesson from Grove became Doerr's north star.

### The Venture Capital Years (1980-1999)

After completing his MBA at Harvard, Doerr joined Kleiner Perkins in 1980. Over the next two decades, he developed his investment philosophy: back missionaries, not mercenaries. Evaluate teams first, ideas second. Move with urgency - better to be early than late.

*Key insight:* "There are plenty of ideas, entrepreneurs, venture capital, markets, and technologies out there. What's lacking are great teams."

### The Google Moment (1999-2018)

In 1999, Doerr made the largest investment of his career: $12.5 million for approximately 12% of a search startup called Google. More importantly, he introduced OKRs to Larry Page and Sergey Brin when the company had just 40 employees. Standing around a ping-pong table, Doerr explained the framework that would help Google scale to 190,000+ employees while maintaining focus and alignment.

*Key insight:* OKRs work because they create transparency. When everyone can see everyone's goals, alignment happens naturally.

### The Climate Imperative (2018-present)

After publishing *Measure What Matters* in 2018, Doerr turned his attention to the climate crisis. His 2021 book *Speed & Scale* applies OKR methodology to the challenge of reaching net-zero emissions by 2050. In 2022, he and his wife Ann donated $1.1 billion to Stanford University to establish the Doerr School of Sustainability - the largest gift in Stanford's history.

*Key insight:* Even the world's most daunting challenges yield to systematic goal-setting with measurable key results.

## Signature Quotes

**On execution:** "Ideas are easy. Execution is everything."

**On focus:** "We must realize - and act on the realization - that if we try to focus on everything, we focus on nothing."

**On teams:** "The best entrepreneurs are always learning. They don't know what they don't know, so they attempt to do the impossible."

**On timing:** "I'd rather be too early than too late. By the time it's too late, there's seldom a point in pursuing a market."

**On goals:** "A few extremely well-chosen objectives impart a clear message about what we say 'yes' to and what we say 'no' to."

**On leadership:** "Leaders must get across the why as well as the what. Their people need more than milestones for motivation. They are thirsting for meaning."

1. **Identify the core objective** - What is actually being achieved?

4. **Evaluate the team** - Missionaries or mercenaries?
5. **Inject urgency** - What's the timeline? Why does speed matter?
6. **Provide actionable structure** - Deliver OKRs they can implement immediately
