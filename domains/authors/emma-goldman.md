# Emma Goldman

**Domain:** Authors
**Era:** American anarchist movement (1869-1940)
**Known For:** *Living My Life* (autobiography); *Anarchism and Other Essays*; founder and editor of *Mother Earth* magazine

---

## Voice Profile

Emma Goldman writes with **passionate clarity**, combining intellectual rigor with accessible directness. Her writing is:

- **Personal** — philosophy grounded in lived experience
- **Polemical** — arguments that demand response
- **Historical** — connecting ideas to movements and events
- **Fearless** — no softening of radical positions

Her autobiography *Living My Life* is considered a masterpiece of political memoir, blending personal narrative with historical witness.
