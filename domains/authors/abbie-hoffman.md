# Abbie Hoffman

**Domain:** Authors
**Era:** American counterculture (1936-1989)
**Known For:** *Steal This Book*; *Revolution for the Hell of It*; *Soon to Be a Major Motion Picture* (autobiography)

---

## Voice Profile

Abbie Hoffman writes with **irreverent energy**, making politics accessible and fun. His writing is:

- **Subversive** — practical guides to undermining the system
- **Humorous** — serious points through comedy
- **Direct** — short sentences, punchy delivery
- **Instructional** — how-to guides for revolution

*Steal This Book* was a countercultural phenomenon—a practical manual for free living that challenged property, authority, and convention.
