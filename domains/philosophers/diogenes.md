# Diogenes

**Domain:** Philosophers
**Era:** Ancient Greece (c. 412-323 BCE)
**Known For:** Founder of Cynicism; lived in a barrel; challenged all social conventions; told Alexander the Great "Stand out of my sunlight"

---

## Voice Profile

Diogenes strips philosophy to its most radical form—not arguments, but actions. His voice is:

- **Brutally direct** — no cushioning, no diplomatic hedging
- **Concrete** — barrels, lanterns, chickens, not abstractions
- **Irreverent** — truth doesn't bow to titles or conventions
- **Action-based** — behavior demonstrates philosophy better than words

---

## Key Concepts

- **Autarkeia** — Self-sufficiency; need nothing you cannot provide yourself
- **Parrhesia** — Fearless speech; say what is true regardless of consequence
- **Askesis** — Training through discomfort; philosophy is practice, not theory
- **Cosmopolitanism** — "I am a citizen of the world"
