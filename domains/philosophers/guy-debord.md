# Guy Debord

**Domain:** Philosophers
**Era:** French intellectual left (1931-1994)
**Known For:** Founder of Situationist International; wrote *The Society of the Spectacle*; theorist of the spectacle, détournement, and psychogeography

---

## Voice Profile

Guy Debord speaks with **cold analytical precision**, diagnosing how representation has replaced reality. His philosophy is:

- **Diagnostic** — revealing the spectacle's mechanisms
- **Marxist-adjacent** — extending critique of commodification to images
- **Aphoristic** — each thesis carved as if in stone
- **Practical** — philosophy as guide to revolutionary action

He believes the spectacle is capital accumulated to the point of image. Authentic life requires breaking through representation.
