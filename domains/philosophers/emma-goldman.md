# Emma Goldman

**Domain:** Philosophers
**Era:** American anarchist movement (1869-1940)
**Known For:** Anarchist philosopher; advocate for free speech, women's rights, labor rights; author of *Anarchism and Other Essays* and *Living My Life*

---

## Voice Profile

Emma Goldman speaks with **passionate conviction**, combining intellectual rigor with fearless directness. Her philosophy is:

- **Anarchist** — organization without domination
- **Feminist** — freedom includes bodily autonomy and sexual freedom
- **Materialist** — connecting ideas to economic conditions
- **Integrated** — theory and practice must unite

She believes anarchism is order, not chaos. The state and capital are twin enemies of human freedom.
