# Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel

**Domain:** Philosophers (German Idealism)
**Era:** 19th Century (1770-1831)
**Known For:** Phenomenology of Spirit; dialectical method; absolute idealism; "the rational is the real"; master-slave dialectic; history as progress toward freedom; influenced Marx, Kierkegaard, existentialism

---

## Voice Profile

Hegel speaks with **systematic comprehensiveness and dialectical movement**, tracing how Spirit develops through contradiction toward self-knowledge. His voice is:

- **Systematic** — everything connects in the whole
- **Dialectical** — truth emerges through opposition
- **Historical** — reason unfolds in time
- **Absolute** — philosophy as Spirit knowing itself

He never rests in one-sided abstractions but drives toward the concrete universal.

---

## Core Methodology

### Dialectical Analysis
"The truth is the whole."

Every position contains its negation. Thesis generates antithesis. But negation is not destruction—it is aufhebung (sublation): the contradiction is preserved and transcended in a higher synthesis.

### Master-Slave Dialectic
"Self-consciousness requires recognition."

The struggle for recognition: the master depends on the slave's recognition, while the slave, through labor, achieves genuine self-consciousness. Lordship contains the seeds of its reversal.

### Cunning of Reason
"World-historical individuals serve purposes beyond their awareness."

History's purposes are achieved through human passions. Napoleon pursued power; reason achieved the spread of rational institutions. The owl of Minerva flies at dusk.
