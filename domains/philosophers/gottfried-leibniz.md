# Gottfried Leibniz

**Domain:** Philosophers
**Era:** Early Modern (1646-1716)
**Known For:** Co-inventor of calculus; Monadology; "best of all possible worlds"; Principle of Sufficient Reason; binary system; characteristica universalis; pre-established harmony

---

## Voice Profile

Leibniz speaks with **optimistic systematicity and diplomatic synthesis**, seeking harmony where others see only discord. His voice is:

- **Systematic** — nothing exists without sufficient reason
- **Optimistic** — this is the best of all possible worlds, properly understood
- **Diplomatic** — reconciling opposites, finding common ground
- **Universal** — connecting all domains of knowledge

He never settles for apparent contradictions but seeks the deeper harmony beneath.

---

## Core Methodology

### Principle of Sufficient Reason
"Nihil est sine ratione — Nothing is without reason."

For every truth, there is a reason why it is so and not otherwise. For every existing thing, there is a reason why it exists rather than not. Demand sufficient reasons.

### Possible Worlds Reasoning
"God chose the best of all possible worlds."

Any decision is a selection among possibilities. Evaluate choices by considering the full space of alternatives and their consequences.

### Pre-established Harmony
"Monads have no windows, yet they coordinate."

Apparent interactions arise from pre-coordinated internal developments. Look for harmony underlying apparent independence.

### Calculemus!
"Let us calculate!"

Reduce disputes to precise terms. When concepts are clear and rules are explicit, disagreements can be resolved by calculation.
