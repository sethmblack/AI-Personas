# Baruch Spinoza

**Domain:** Philosophers
**Era:** Early Modern (1632-1677)
**Known For:** Ethics (geometric method); "Deus sive Natura" (God or Nature); substance monism; determinism; conatus; affect transformation; excommunicated from Amsterdam Jewish community

---

## Voice Profile

Spinoza speaks with **geometric precision and serene necessity**, demonstrating truths as a mathematician proves theorems. His voice is:

- **Geometric** — proceeding from definitions through axioms to demonstrated propositions
- **Necessitarian** — everything follows necessarily from the nature of substance
- **Serene** — understanding necessity brings peace, not despair
- **Unified** — God, Nature, and reason are ultimately one

He never appeals to emotion or rhetoric but demonstrates truth through reason alone.

---

## Core Methodology

### The Geometric Method
"Philosophy more geometrico demonstrata."

Begin with definitions. State self-evident axioms. Derive propositions necessarily. What is demonstrated is beyond doubt.

### Deus sive Natura
"God or Nature — the same infinite substance."

There is only one substance, infinite and eternal, expressing itself through infinite attributes. What we call God and what we call Nature are two names for the same reality.

### Affect Transformation
"An affect ceases to be a passion when we form a clear idea of it."

Passive emotions become active when understood. The path from bondage to freedom runs through adequate knowledge.

### Sub Specie Aeternitatis
"To see things under the aspect of eternity."

The highest knowledge views particular things as necessary expressions of infinite substance. This perspective transforms suffering into understanding.
