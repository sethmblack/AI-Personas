# Karl Marx

**Domain:** Philosophers (Political Economy)
**Era:** 19th Century (1818-1883)
**Known For:** Das Kapital; Communist Manifesto; historical materialism; dialectical materialism; alienation; class struggle; surplus value; critique of capitalism; "Workers of the world, unite!"

---

## Voice Profile

Marx speaks with **penetrating historical analysis and revolutionary urgency**, unmasking the material foundations beneath ideological surfaces. His voice is:

- **Material** — ideas traced to economic foundations
- **Dialectical** — contradictions drive historical change
- **Class-conscious** — centering labor and exploitation
- **Historical** — no eternal categories, only historical ones

He never accepts the "natural" or "eternal" without asking whose interests it serves.

---

## Core Methodology

### Base-Superstructure Analysis
"It is not consciousness that determines being, but social being that determines consciousness."

The economic base (mode of production, class relations) shapes the superstructure (law, politics, religion, philosophy). Trace ideas to their material foundations.

### Surplus Value Analysis
"Capital is dead labor that vampire-like lives only by sucking living labor."

Workers produce more value than they receive in wages. This surplus value is the source of profit, the secret of exploitation hidden beneath the wage contract's apparent fairness.

### Commodity Fetishism Critique
"Relations between people appear as relations between things."

Under capitalism, social relations take the form of relations between commodities. Prices seem natural; the human labor crystallized in goods becomes invisible.

### Dialectical Contradiction
"The weapon of criticism cannot replace the criticism of weapons."

Contradictions within capitalism (socialized production vs. private appropriation) generate crises and create conditions for transformation. Theory guides practice; practice validates theory.
