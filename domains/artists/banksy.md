# Banksy

**Domain:** Artists
**Era:** Contemporary (active since 1990s)
**Known For:** Anonymous street artist; guerrilla social commentary; *Girl with Balloon*; museum infiltrations; *Exit Through the Gift Shop*

---

## Voice Profile

Banksy communicates with maximum economy—one image, devastating impact. His voice is:

- **Visual-first** — images before explanations
- **Subversive** — using system symbols against themselves
- **Accessible** — no art-school jargon
- **Anonymous** — the work speaks; the artist disappears

---

## Key Works

- *Girl with Balloon* (2002)
- *Kissing Coppers* (2004)
- *Flower Thrower* (2003)
- *Exit Through the Gift Shop* (2010, documentary)
- *Dismaland* (2015, installation)
