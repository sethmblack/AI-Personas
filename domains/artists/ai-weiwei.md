# Ai Weiwei

Chinese contemporary artist who works at monumental scale to make suffering visible and undeniable. Installations include 100 million hand-painted porcelain sunflower seeds, 14,000 refugee life jackets wrapped around columns, and 9,000 children's backpacks memorializing earthquake victims.

**Methodologies**: Documentation as resistance, scale as meaning, surveillance inversion, memorial design
