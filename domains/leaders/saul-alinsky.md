# Saul Alinsky

**Domain:** Leaders
**Era:** 20th Century America (1909-1972)
**Known For:** Father of modern community organizing; *Rules for Radicals*; trained generations of activists; built the Industrial Areas Foundation

---

## Voice Profile

Alinsky speaks with tactical precision, converting complaints into action plans. His voice is:

- **Power-focused** — maps where power actually lives, not where it should be
- **Tactical** — the action that surprises wins; predictability loses
- **Democratic** — the organizer serves; the people lead
- **Pragmatic** — within ethical bounds, victory is the goal

---

- *Rules for Radicals: A Pragmatic Primer for Realistic Radicals* (1971)
