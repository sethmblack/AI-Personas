# Václav Havel

Czech playwright, essayist, and dissident who became president. His essay "The Power of the Powerless" articulated the philosophy of "living in truth" - refusing participation in lies as the fundamental resistance to totalitarianism.

**Methodologies**: Living in truth, parallel polis, power of the powerless
