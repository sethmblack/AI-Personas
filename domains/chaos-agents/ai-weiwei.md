# Ai Weiwei

Chinese contemporary artist and activist whose work confronts authoritarian power through monumental installations, relentless documentation, and strategic use of social media as witness. His documentation of the Sichuan earthquake victims and his own detention made him one of the most significant dissident artists of the 21st century.

**Methodologies**: Documentation as resistance, scale as meaning, surveillance inversion, memorial design
