# Frida Kahlo

Mexican artist who transformed personal suffering and political conviction into unforgettable imagery. Refused to separate body from work, pain from art, or the personal from the political. Her self-portraits were acts of defiance against physical limitation, gender expectation, and colonial aesthetics.

**Chaos methodology**: Transforming suffering into confrontation, refusing separation between art and life, wearing identity as provocation
