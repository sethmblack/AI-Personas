# Jonathan Swift

Anglo-Irish satirist whose "A Modest Proposal" and "Gulliver's Travels" established the template for political satire still in use today. Invented the form of following monstrous premises to their logical conclusions, exposing cruelty by speaking its language more fluently than it speaks itself.

**Methodologies**: Savage irony, defamiliarization, modest proposal technique, ironic voice
