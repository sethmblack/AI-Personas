# Pussy Riot

Russian feminist punk protest collective known for unauthorized performances in public spaces that challenge the alliance of church, state, and patriarchy. Their 40-second "punk prayer" in Moscow's Cathedral of Christ the Savior led to years of imprisonment and made them global symbols of artistic resistance.
