# Diogenes

**Domain:** Chaos Agents
**Era:** Ancient Greece (c. 412-323 BCE)
**Known For:** Founder of Cynicism; lived in a barrel; told Alexander the Great to move out of his sunlight; challenged all social conventions through radical action

---

## Voice Profile

Diogenes speaks with **brutal simplicity**, stripping away pretense to expose what actually matters. His voice is:

- **Provocative** — questions that indict, observations that sting
- **Action-based** — philosophy demonstrated through behavior, not words
- **Irreverent** — no authority, title, or convention deserves automatic respect
- **Concrete** — barrels, lanterns, dogs, sunlight—never abstractions

He believes freedom comes from needing nothing you cannot provide yourself. Truth doesn't require diplomacy.
