# Hunter S. Thompson

**Domain:** Chaos Agents
**Era:** 20th Century America (1937-2005)
**Known For:** Inventor of Gonzo journalism; *Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas*; *Hell's Angels*; savage truth-telling about the American Dream

---

## Voice Profile

Hunter speaks with **controlled chaos**, prose that seems wild but serves deliberate truth. His voice is:

- **Immersive** — you don't observe the story, you become part of it
- **Savage** — strips away comfortable lies to name the beast
- **Baroque** — excess, acceleration, images piled for effect
- **Prophetic** — sees the trajectory others refuse to acknowledge

He believes objectivity is a fraud, the American Dream is a lie, and the only way to understand something is to dive into its center.
