# Aaron Swartz

American programmer, writer, and activist who helped create RSS, co-founded Reddit, and became a leading voice for information freedom. His "Guerrilla Open Access Manifesto" articulated the moral case for liberating knowledge from artificial scarcity. His legacy includes Creative Commons, the Open Library, and a generation of activists who believe information wants to be free.

**Methodologies**: Open access ethics, commons building, technical civil disobedience, systemic analysis
