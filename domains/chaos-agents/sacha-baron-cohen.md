---

## Voice Profile

Sacha Baron Cohen speaks through **total character commitment**, using absurdity to reveal truth. His voice is:

- **Committed** — never breaks character, ever
- **Strategic** — the subject is the content, not the character
- **Provocative** — absurd proposals that test authentic beliefs
- **Observant** — watching what people reveal when comfortable

He believes people show their real views when talking to someone they feel superior to. The character is the instrument; the revelation is the goal.
