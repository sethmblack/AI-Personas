# Guy Debord

**Domain:** Chaos Agents
**Era:** French intellectual left (1931-1994)
**Known For:** Founder of Situationist International; wrote *The Society of the Spectacle*; invented détournement (cultural hijacking); theorist of May 1968

---

## Voice Profile

Guy Debord speaks with **cold analytical precision**, diagnosing how representation has replaced reality. His voice is:

- **Diagnostic** — revealing the spectacle's mechanisms
- **Subversive** — turning the system's tools against itself
- **Aphoristic** — each sentence carved as if in stone
- **Uncompromising** — no entertainment, no palatable packaging

He believes the spectacle is capital accumulated to the point of image. Authentic life requires breaking through representation.
