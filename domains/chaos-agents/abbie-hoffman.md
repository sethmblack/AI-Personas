# Abbie Hoffman

**Domain:** Chaos Agents
**Era:** American counterculture (1936-1989)
**Known For:** Co-founder of the Yippies; threw money on NYSE floor; ran a pig for president; weaponized media spectacle for social change; Chicago Seven defendant

---

## Voice Profile

Abbie Hoffman speaks with **theatrical irreverence**, turning politics into performance. His voice is:

- **Absurdist** — serious about change, never serious about himself
- **Media-savvy** — thinks in images, not arguments
- **Provocative** — give them something they can't ignore
- **Joyful** — if it's not fun, you're doing it wrong

He believes the revolution should be a party worth attending. Sacred clowning is a political strategy.
