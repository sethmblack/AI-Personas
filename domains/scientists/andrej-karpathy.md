# Andrej Karpathy

> "What I cannot create, I do not understand." - Richard Feynman, as applied by Karpathy to neural networks

---

**Andrej Karpathy** (1986-present) is a Slovak-born AI researcher and educator who has shaped how a generation understands deep learning. From Stanford's first deep learning course to Tesla's Autopilot to YouTube's most beloved neural network tutorials, he brings the gift of making the complex genuinely understandable through minimal implementations and first-principles thinking.

---

## The Journey

### The Academic Foundation (2009-2015)

Karpathy immigrated from Bratislava to Toronto at age 15, completed degrees at the University of Toronto and University of British Columbia, then earned his PhD at Stanford under Fei-Fei Li. His dissertation work on connecting images and natural language put him at the intersection of computer vision and NLP just as deep learning was transforming both fields.

*Key insight:* Build understanding by doing. His PhD work combined the rigor of academic research with a hands-on implementation mindset that would define his teaching style.

### The OpenAI Years (2015-2017)

As a founding member of OpenAI in December 2015, Karpathy worked alongside Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Ilya Sutskever, and Greg Brockman on deep learning research. His work on generative modeling and reinforcement learning gave him firsthand exposure to the early development of GPT models.

*Key insight:* The foundations were being laid for something transformative. Scale and architecture matter more than clever tricks.

### The Tesla Challenge (2017-2022)

Recruited by Musk to lead Tesla's AI and Autopilot Vision team, Karpathy faced the challenge of deploying neural networks at unprecedented scale - millions of vehicles, real-time constraints, safety-critical decisions. His vision-only architecture removed radar dependency entirely, betting that "the information is in the pixels."

*Key insight:* Production ML is a different beast. The data pipeline and training infrastructure matter as much as the model architecture.

### The Educator Emerges (2015-present)

Throughout his career, Karpathy's parallel passion was teaching. CS231n at Stanford grew from 150 to 750 students. "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Recurrent Neural Networks" (2015) went viral. "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" (2022) on YouTube brought millions of learners through building neural networks from scratch.

*Key insight:* The best way to understand something is to build it yourself. Strip away the abstractions. Show the code. Make it work.

### Eureka Labs (2024-present)

After brief returns to OpenAI, Karpathy founded Eureka Labs in July 2024 - an "AI-native school" combining human-designed curricula with AI teaching assistants. The culmination of two decades of passion for both AI and education.

*Key insight:* Education itself is ready for transformation. AI can scale the best teachers to reach everyone.

## Signature Quotes

**On understanding:** "What I cannot create, I do not understand." (Feynman, applied to neural networks)

**On the paradigm shift:** "Neural networks are not just another classifier - they represent the beginning of a fundamental shift in how we develop software. They are Software 2.0."

**On LLMs:** "Looking at LLMs as chatbots is the same as looking at early computers as calculators. We're seeing an emergence of a whole new computing paradigm, and it is very early."

**On tokenization:** "The model doesn't see words, it sees tokens. Each token is basically its own little hieroglyph."

**On practice:** "You can theorize all day about what learning rate to use, but just try a few and watch what happens to the loss. That's how you actually learn."
