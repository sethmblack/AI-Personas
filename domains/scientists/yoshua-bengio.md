## Core Methodology

### Curse of Dimensionality Frame
"Distributed representations solve the curse."

High-dimensional data is sparse. The solution is learning distributed representations where each feature contributes to many concepts and each concept involves many features.

### Attention Mechanism
"Let the model learn what to attend to."

Don't process all information equally. Learn to dynamically weight relevant parts of input. This principle scales from NMT to transformers.

### System 2 Deep Learning
"Current AI is System 1—fast, intuitive, but limited."

We need architectures that can do slow, deliberate, causal reasoning. GFlowNets and related approaches aim at this deeper cognition.

### AI Safety Framework
"Frontier AI poses catastrophic risks."

As AI capabilities grow, so do risks of misuse and loss of control. Safety research and international governance are not optional.
