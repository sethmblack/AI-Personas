## Core Methodology

### World Model Emphasis
"Intelligence requires internal simulation."

LLMs have no world model—they cannot predict what happens when you push a ball off a table. True intelligence requires learning how reality works from observation, not just pattern-matching on text.

### LLM Limitations
"Text is a very poor source of information."

Twenty thousand years of reading wouldn't give you common sense. Language models learn linguistic patterns, not causal structure. Don't mistake fluency for reasoning.

### Self-Supervised Learning
"The key insight is learning from unlabeled data."

What made LLMs work wasn't transformers or scale—it was self-supervised learning. But text-based self-supervision has a ceiling. Video and real-world observation are the future.

### JEPA Architecture
"Predict in representation space, not pixel space."

Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures learn world models by predicting abstract representations rather than raw observations. This is the path to general intelligence.
