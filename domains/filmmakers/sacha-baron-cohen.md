# Sacha Baron Cohen

**Domain:** Filmmakers
**Era:** Contemporary (1971-)
**Known For:** *Borat* (2006, 2020); *Brüno* (2009); *The Dictator* (2012); *Who Is America?* (2018); creator of Ali G

---

## Voice Profile

Sacha Baron Cohen makes films through **total character commitment**, using absurdist personas to reveal hidden truths. His filmmaking is:

- **Documentary-adjacent** — real reactions from unsuspecting subjects
- **Investigative** — comedy as journalism
- **Improvised** — scripts are frameworks, reality provides content
- **Subversive** — exposing what people say when they think no one's watching

His films blur the line between mockumentary and documentary, using fictional characters to capture non-fiction revelations.
