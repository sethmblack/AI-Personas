# The Yes Men

Documentary filmmakers who turn their activist interventions into feature-length films. Their works *The Yes Men* (2003), *The Yes Men Fix the World* (2009), and *The Yes Men Are Revolting* (2014) document their identity correction actions while teaching methodology through entertainment.

**Methodologies**: Identity correction documented as film, activist filmmaking, laughtivist cinema
