## HIGH Priority Skills

### 1. deadpan-escalation

**Description:** Transforms content using flat, emotionally neutral delivery while escalating to dark or absurd conclusions. The gap between delivery tone and content extremity creates comedy.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear process (start mundane → maintain flat affect → escalate content → land dark payoff)
- **Invocable:** User can request "make this deadpan" or "give this the Segura treatment"
- **Scoped:** One clear responsibility - delivery/content gap creation
- **Reusable:** Applies to any content that needs comedic transformation through tone/content contrast
- **Valuable:** Core Tom Segura technique that distinguishes his style from other comedians

**Example Input:** "Traveling with kids is challenging."

**Example Output:** "I took my kids on a plane recently. Just... a flight. And I'm watching other passengers look at me as we board like I'm bringing a lit bomb onto the aircraft. And honestly? They're right to be concerned."

**Skill Name:** `deadpan-escalation`

### 2. callback-architecture

**Description:** Structures longer content through deliberate tangent architecture where apparent diversions plant elements for later payoff. Creates satisfying comedic loops.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear structural steps (plant element A → tangent to B → reference C → return to A with payoff incorporating B and C)
- **Invocable:** Triggered by "structure this with callbacks" or "give this tangent architecture"
- **Scoped:** Specific to structural comedy writing
- **Reusable:** Applies to any story-based content, podcast format, or extended bits
- **Valuable:** Complex technique made systematic, essential for longer-form comedy

**Example Application:**
1. Start: "So I was at Target..."
2. Tangent: "...which reminds me of this guy I saw at the gym..."
3. Plant: "...he had this shirt that said..."
4. Return: "...anyway, back at Target, the cashier was wearing THE SAME SHIRT..."

**Skill Name:** `callback-architecture`

---

### 3. uncomfortable-truth-mining

**Description:** Identifies and articulates the observations everyone thinks but won't say, delivering them with matter-of-fact certainty that makes them undeniable.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear process (identify taboo observation → strip to honest core → deliver as reasonable fact → include self as target)
- **Invocable:** Triggered by "what's the uncomfortable truth here?" or "say what nobody will say"
- **Scoped:** Specific to truth-telling through comedy
- **Reusable:** Applies across any topic where polite consensus hides real feelings
- **Valuable:** Signature Segura value-add that creates his most memorable material

**Example Input:** Topic about parenting other people's children

**Example Output:** "Some people's kids are just... bad. I know you're not supposed to say that. But some kids are just awful. You meet them and you go, 'This kid sucks.' And it's true."

**Skill Name:** `uncomfortable-truth-mining`

## MEDIUM Priority Skills

### 4. bit-commitment

**Description:** Maintains full commitment to a comedic bit regardless of initial audience reaction, pushing through silence rather than abandoning material.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear principle (identify hesitation moment → maintain rather than retreat → push through to natural conclusion)
- **Invocable:** When performer feels tempted to bail on material
- **Scoped:** Specific to performance/delivery confidence
- **Reusable:** Applies to any comedy performance or content creation
- **Valuable:** Essential performance skill, but more about execution than writing/structure

**Use Case:** When a dark joke gets silence, resist urge to apologize or explain—maintain confidence and continue to payoff.

**Skill Name:** `bit-commitment`
