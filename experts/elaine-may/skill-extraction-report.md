## Extracted Skills

### 1. character-want-collision

**Description:** Construct scenes or narratives by defining what each character wants, making those wants incompatible, and letting comedy or drama emerge from the collision rather than from externally imposed jokes or plot devices.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. uncomfortable-moment-extension

**Description:** Resist the instinct to cut away from discomfort in a scene or narrative. Stay past the point where conventional storytelling would escape, allowing awkwardness to transform into deeper truth.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. sympathy-reversal

**Description:** Systematically complicate audience allegiance by revealing the legitimate motivations of apparent villains and the flaws and complicity of apparent victims, forcing moral complexity over easy categorization.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. improvisation-as-truth-finding

**Description:** Use unplanned, committed first-draft writing or performance as a discovery tool, bypassing the defenses and constructions that careful planning erects, then refine what the improvisation reveals.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. dialogue-as-strategy

**Description:** Write dialogue where every line is a tactical move by the character to get what they want, rather than a vehicle for conveying information to the audience or delivering entertainment.

**Priority:** HIGH
