## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Confrontational Reframe

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Reject the premise of a bad-faith or loaded question and impose your own framing that addresses the actual issue.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I respond to this unfair question?"
- "They're framing this wrong but I don't know how to push back"
- When facing questions that assume something problematic
- When the terms of debate disadvantage one side

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear steps: identify hidden premise, reject it, impose new frame |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by loaded questions or unfair framings |
| Scoped | Yes | Only handles reframing; does not build full argument |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any bad-faith premise across contexts |
| Valuable | Yes | Prevents being trapped by opponent's framing |

**Output:** Reframed response that exposes and rejects the problematic premise

### 2. Indignant Escalation Builder

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Build from neutral observation to justified outrage through calibrated emotional escalation that makes a point land harder.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I make this point hit harder?"
- "I want people to actually feel the injustice here"
- When logic alone isn't creating impact
- When building a persuasive argument about wrongdoing

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | 6-phase structure: setup, recognition, building, heating, peak, release |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when emotional impact is needed |
| Scoped | Yes | Only handles escalation; does not choose topic |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any observation that warrants outrage |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms flat points into emotionally resonant arguments |

**Output:** Escalated version of the original point with increasing intensity

### 3. Reverse Exotification

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Turn the lens of curiosity and scrutiny back on dominant culture practices to expose the double standard in how different cultures are examined.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I show them their perspective isn't universal?"
- "They treat our practices as weird but their practices are just as strange"
- When challenging ethnocentrism or cultural assumptions
- When demonstrating that "normal" is culturally constructed

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Steps: identify normalized practice, describe anthropologically, add outsider scrutiny |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by double standards in cultural treatment |
| Scoped | Yes | Only handles perspective flip; does not build full argument |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any dominant culture practice |
| Valuable | Yes | Powerful defamiliarization technique |

**Output:** Reframed description that makes the "normal" seem as strange as the "exotic"
