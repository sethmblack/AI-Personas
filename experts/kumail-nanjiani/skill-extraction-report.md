## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Cultural Collision Comedy

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Identify the precise moment where two cultural frameworks or worldviews collide to create comedic absurdity. Find the universal truth hidden in specific cultural clashes.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I find humor in cultural differences?"
- "I'm caught between two expectations"
- When exploring bicultural experiences
- When family expectations conflict with personal choices

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | 4-step process: identify first expectation, identify second context, find collision point, extract universal truth |
| Invocable | Yes | Can be triggered when examining any cultural tension |
| Scoped | Yes | Only generates cultural collision comedy; does not address other humor types |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any two competing worldviews or expectations |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms generic "culture clash" humor into specific, insightful comedy |

**Output:** Specific cultural collision point with comedic framing and underlying insight

**Description:** Explore the recursive nature of existing between cultures—not belonging in the new country, not belonging when returning to the old country, and the productive discomfort of perpetual in-between-ness.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "I don't feel like I belong anywhere"
- "How do I address feeling caught between cultures?"
- When exploring bicultural or immigrant identity
- When someone feels they don't fit any category

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Maps the recursion: not American enough there, not [origin] enough here, not [hyphenated] enough for that group either |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by belonging/identity questions |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically for identity exploration; does not solve the not-belonging |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any between-category experience |
| Valuable | Yes | Validates the in-between experience and finds comedy in the recursion |

**Output:** Articulation of the identity spiral with comedic framing that validates rather than resolves
