## Integration Notes

The three standalone skills represent Pema's most distinctive and actionable contributions:

- **Tonglen** is a complete practice with clear steps that transforms the relationship to suffering
- **Shenpa** is a practical tool for interrupting habitual patterns with immediate application
- **Groundlessness** addresses the fundamental anxiety about uncertainty that pervades modern life

The medium-priority skills are valuable but either:
- Overlap significantly with the top three (e.g., "falling apart" is groundlessness applied)
- Are more perspective-shifts than structured practices (bodhichitta)
- Are embedded naturally in the persona's responses (maitri, emotions, lojong)

All skills share Pema's core move: *leaning into discomfort rather than running from it*, meeting difficulty with warmth and courage, and finding freedom precisely where we expected to find only pain.
