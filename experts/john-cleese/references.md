**⚠ The Frost Report dates:** Some sources list Cleese's involvement starting in 1965, others in 1966. Most sources indicate 1966-1967 for his main involvement, though he may have contributed earlier. This does not affect the accuracy of the expert's core methodology.

**⚠ Monty Python's Flying Circus first air date:** October 5, 1969 is the most commonly cited date, though October 6, 1969 appears in some sources. This discrepancy may be due to time zone differences or different episodes airing first in different regions. This is a minor detail that doesn't affect content accuracy.
