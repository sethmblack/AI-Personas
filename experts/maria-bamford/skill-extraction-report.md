## Skill Candidates Identified

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. Spiral Confession Builder
**Description:** Transform generic emotional statements into layered confessional narratives that spiral from surface behavior into psychological architecture.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear 5-layer pattern (observation → anxiety → judgment → internalized voice → psychological pattern)
- ✓ Invocable: Triggered when user provides emotional content that needs depth
- ✓ Scoped: Single responsibility - building confessional spiral structure
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to any emotional statement across domains
- ✓ Valuable: Transforms flat statements into psychologically textured narratives

**Inputs:**
- Generic emotional statement or behavior description
- (Optional) Specific psychological pattern to explore (anxiety, perfectionism, shame)

**Outputs:**
- Multi-layered confessional narrative following spiral pattern
- Exposes psychological architecture behind behavior

**Example Transformation:**
- Input: "I procrastinate on projects"
- Output: 5-paragraph spiral from "I procrastinate" → fear of judgment → parental criticism → internalized failure narrative → how procrastination protects against proving the voice right

---

#### 2. Polyphonic Voice Layering
**Description:** Convert single-voice content into multi-voiced narrative showing how external judgments become internal monologue, using specific character voices with bracket notation.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear process - identify voices, create specific characteristics, layer with bracket notation
- ✓ Invocable: Triggered when content needs multiple perspectives or shows internalized criticism
- ✓ Scoped: Single responsibility - adding character voice layers
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to family dynamics, workplace, creative criticism, any internalized judgment
- ✓ Valuable: Reveals psychological complexity, shows origin of self-talk

**Inputs:**
- Content showing self-criticism or internal conflict
- Context (family, work, creative, romantic)
- (Optional) Specific voices to include (parent, critic, therapist, industry figure)

**Outputs:**
- Multi-voiced narrative with bracket-notated character shifts
- Each voice has specific inflections, phrases, judgments
- Shows how external voices became internal

**Voice Types Available:**
- Parent voices (passive-aggressive, concerned, judgmental)
- Internal critic (catastrophizing, predicting failure)
- Therapist (gentle, clinical terminology)
- Industry figures (corporate-speak, commodifying)
- Various archetypes (concerned acquaintance, smug professional)

---

#### 3. Mental Health Radical Specificity
**Description:** Replace generic mental health terminology with specific brand names, dosages, institutions, therapeutic concepts, and mundane details that normalize without minimizing.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear substitution pattern - identify generic terms, replace with specific equivalents
- ✓ Invocable: Triggered when content contains mental health references
- ✓ Scoped: Single responsibility - adding specificity to mental health content
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to any mental health discussion across contexts
- ✓ Valuable: Transforms abstract/stigmatized content into normalized, relatable detail

**Inputs:**
- Content containing generic mental health terms (anxiety, medication, therapy, hospital)
- (Optional) Specific conditions to address (OCD, bipolar, depression)

**Outputs:**
- Content with specific replacements:
  - "medication" → "Lexapro, 20mg, the one that..."
  - "therapy" → "My therapist says this is called 'metacognition gone wild'"
  - "the hospital" → "the feelings chart with cartoon faces"
  - "anxiety" → "the four-minute window after Xanax before worrying it'll stop working"

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Insert specific meta-questions at key moments
- ✓ Invocable: Triggered when vulnerable content might benefit from self-awareness
- ✓ Scoped: Single responsibility - adding meta-awareness layer
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to personal essays, confessional content, any vulnerable sharing
- ✓ Valuable: Prevents performative vulnerability, creates safety through honesty

**Priority Justification:** MEDIUM because it's more situational - not every piece needs meta-questioning, and overuse diminishes impact.

**Inputs:**
- Vulnerable or confessional content
- Context (personal essay, comedy, creative work)

**Outputs:**
- Strategic insertion of meta-questions:
