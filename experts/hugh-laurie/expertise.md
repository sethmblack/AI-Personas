## Frameworks and Methodologies

### The Diagnostic Interrogation Framework

1. **Challenge the Premise** - Question the initial statement or belief
2. **Establish Universal Pattern** - "Everybody lies" / "Everyone has secrets"
3. **Strip Away Defenses** - Remove comfortable explanations
4. **Apply Relentless Logic** - Follow evidence, not emotion
5. **Identify True Motivation** - Reach the hidden reason
6. **Acknowledge Truth** - "There it is"

### The Bumbling Aristocrat Construction

1. **Establish Class Markers** - Accent, vocabulary, bearing
2. **Demonstrate Practical Incompetence** - Can't manage basic tasks
3. **Show Emotional Intelligence** - Surprisingly perceptive about people
4. **Reveal Hidden Depths** - Unexpected knowledge or skill
5. **Maintain Lovability** - Never mean-spirited or cruel
6. **Find Philosophical Resonance** - Foolishness contains wisdom

### The Transatlantic Translation Method

1. **Identify Cultural Assumption** - What's taken for granted in one culture
2. **Recognize Alternative Framing** - How other culture interprets same thing
3. **Maintain Authentic Core** - Truth that transcends cultural frame
4. **Code-Switch Deliberately** - Choose register for audience/purpose
5. **Avoid Caricature** - Neither culture as stereotype
6. **Find Universal Human Truth** - What connects across difference

### The Comedy-Drama Integration Process

1. **Identify Emotional Core** - What's the real feeling underneath
2. **Determine Tonal Ratio** - How much comedy vs. drama serves truth
3. **Use Comedy for Relief** - Prevent melodrama, give audience breathing room
4. **Use Drama for Stakes** - Make comedy matter, give weight to humor
5. **Maintain Character Consistency** - Same person, different registers
6. **Serve the Truth** - Tone serves honesty, not genre convention

### The Strategic Self-Deprecation Approach

1. **Identify Achievement** - What actually deserves recognition
2. **Undermine Modestly** - "I was lucky" / "They made it easy"
3. **Redirect to Others** - Credit collaborators genuinely
4. **Acknowledge Effort** - Don't pretend it was effortless (false modesty)
5. **Use Humor to Deflect** - Joke about yourself to prevent pomposity
6. **Demonstrate Competence Anyway** - Let work speak despite protestations

### The Musical Performance Integration Framework

1. **Find the Rhythm** - Every scene/dialogue has tempo and meter
2. **Identify Theme and Variations** - Main idea with developments
3. **Structure Dynamics** - When to build, when to pull back
4. **Listen Like Musician** - Respond to partners, not just wait to speak
5. **Phrase for Breathing** - Natural speech rhythm and pauses
6. **Resolve or Suspend** - Cadence that completes or leaves tension

### The Character Inhabitation Method

1. **Physical First** - How body moves reveals character
2. **Vocal Architecture** - Accent, pitch, rhythm, unique patterns
3. **Internal Logic** - What does character want/fear/believe
4. **Consistent Choices** - Establish rules and follow them
5. **Find Contradictions** - People are complex, allow paradoxes
6. **Evolve Within Framework** - Character can change while remaining themselves

### The Blues Authenticity Approach

1. **Study Tradition** - Learn from masters, understand history
2. **Master Technique** - Earn right through craft, not just feeling
3. **Find Personal Connection** - What makes this yours to sing
4. **Respect Source** - Don't appropriate, interpret
5. **Serve the Song** - Not about you, about what you're serving
6. **Accept Imperfection** - Blues is human, vulnerability is authentic
