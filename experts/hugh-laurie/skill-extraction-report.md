## Extracted Skills

### 1. Bumbling Aristocrat Characterization
**File:** `skills/bumbling-aristocrat-characterization/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Create characters who appear incompetent through class privilege but reveal unexpected depths—either hidden intelligence or genuine obliviousness that becomes philosophically interesting. The Wooster archetype applied beyond period comedy.

**Core Capability:**
- Constructs characters through 6-phase framework (establish class markers → demonstrate practical incompetence → show emotional intelligence → reveal hidden depths → maintain lovability → find philosophical resonance)
- Balances foolishness with humanity
- Uses class privilege as comedy source while avoiding cruelty
- Makes incompetence endearing through good intentions
- Reveals wisdom hiding in apparent foolishness

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Creating characters with class privilege or unearned advantages
- Apparent incompetence masking other truths
- Social hierarchy comedy
- Upper-class twit archetype needed
- Period or contemporary aristocratic characters

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Character development for comedy writing
- Social satire through privileged incompetence
- Creating lovable fools with hidden depths
- Period drama/comedy characterization
- Exploring class dynamics through character

**Laurie Connection:**
Directly extracted from Laurie's mastery of the bumbling aristocrat across Bertie Wooster (definitive), Prince George (Blackadder), and Lieutenant George. Perfected the archetype: charming incompetence with unexpected emotional wisdom, class privilege without malice.

### 2. Diagnostic Interrogation
**File:** `skills/diagnostic-interrogation/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Apply House's relentless questioning technique to excavate truth: challenge assumptions, expose lies (especially self-deception), strip away comfortable explanations to reach core motivations. Socratic method weaponized for truth-telling.

**Core Capability:**
- 6-step diagnostic framework (challenge premise → establish universal pattern → strip defenses → apply relentless logic → identify true motivation → acknowledge truth)
- Distinguishes between what people say and what they mean
- Exposes self-deception through logical progression
- Maintains intellectual rigor while showing humanity
- Uses provocative questions to reach uncomfortable truths

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Truth excavation needed
- Assumptions require challenging
- Comfortable lies obscure reality
- Self-deception needs exposing
- Root cause analysis in human contexts
- Consulting or advisory scenarios

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Coaching and mentorship interrogation
- Root cause analysis in organizational contexts
- Stripping away excuses to reach real problems
- Interview techniques for getting past prepared answers
- Consulting frameworks for diagnosis
- Therapeutic or self-examination prompts

**Laurie Connection:**
Extracted from Laurie's portrayal of Dr. Gregory House across 177 episodes. House's diagnostic methodology became a philosophical approach: everyone lies, especially to themselves; symptoms point to causes; discomfort reveals truth. Laurie made medical diagnosis into existential interrogation.

### 4. Comedy-Drama Fusion
**File:** `skills/comedy-drama-fusion/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Integrate comic relief with dramatic depth to create richer, more truthful content that works the full emotional spectrum. Use comedy to make drama land harder; use drama to make comedy resonate deeper.

**Core Capability:**
- 6-step integration process (identify emotional core → determine tonal ratio → use comedy for relief → use drama for stakes → maintain character consistency → serve truth)
- Prevents melodrama through comic relief
- Gives weight to humor through dramatic stakes
- Maintains tonal coherence while mixing registers
- Serves emotional truth rather than genre convention

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Content needs both lightness and weight
- Tonal versatility required
- Pure comedy or drama feels insufficient
- Human truth requires full emotional range
- Preventing melodrama or frivolity
- Complex emotional content

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Writing that balances serious and light
- Presentations requiring tonal variety
- Content that can't be purely serious or purely funny
- Marketing messages with emotional range
- Storytelling with complex tonal requirements
- Leadership communication balancing gravitas and approachability

**Laurie Connection:**
Extracted from Laurie's seamless movement between Wooster (pure comedy) and House (drama with comic relief) using same fundamental craft. Proved comedy and drama share DNA: truth-telling, character consistency, emotional honesty. Each informs the other for richer performance.

### 5. Strategic Self-Deprecation
**File:** `skills/strategic-self-deprecation/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Use British modesty as strategic tool to build connection while demonstrating competence. Deflect praise, undermine authority, create likability through apparent humility—while paradoxically establishing credibility through confidence to mock yourself.

**Core Capability:**
- 6-phase approach (identify achievement → undermine modestly → redirect to others → acknowledge effort → use humor to deflect → demonstrate competence anyway)
- Distinguishes genuine humility from false modesty
- Uses self-deprecation to disarm rather than diminish
- Demonstrates confidence through willingness to mock self
- Navigates cultural differences in self-presentation
- Builds likability while maintaining authority

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Authority needs softening
- Likability matters alongside competence
- Deflecting tall poppy syndrome
- Disarming opposition or skepticism
- British communication with American audiences (or vice versa)
- Accepting recognition without arrogance

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Leadership communication balancing authority and approachability
- Accepting awards or recognition gracefully
- British professionals in American contexts
- Self-introduction that builds connection
- Deflecting excessive praise productively
- Creating likable expert personas

**Laurie Connection:**
Drawn from Laurie's masterful use of British self-deprecation throughout career: deflecting praise for House, undermining musical credibility while demonstrating it, expressing perpetual imposter syndrome while achieving massive success. Shows cultural strategy: modesty as strength, vulnerability as weapon.

## Skill Boundaries and Limitations

### When NOT to Use These Skills

**bumbling-aristocrat-characterization:**
- ✗ Characters without class privilege (archetype doesn't fit)
- ✗ Contexts where class comedy is inappropriate or offensive
- ✗ Modern meritocratic settings where privilege is genuinely harmful
- ✗ When competence rather than incompetence is the point
- ✓ Use when: Class dynamics are comedy source, lovable fools needed, privilege requires examination

**diagnostic-interrogation:**
- ✗ Vulnerable people in crisis needing support, not interrogation
- ✗ Situations requiring empathy before logic
- ✗ When challenging assumptions would be cruel
- ✗ Cultural contexts where direct questioning is rude
- ✓ Use when: Truth excavation welcome, intellectual rigor appropriate, uncovering root causes

**transatlantic-code-switching:**
- ✗ Forcing cultural markers where they don't serve content
- ✗ When content is culturally neutral and universal
- ✗ Creating caricatures of either British or American culture
- ✗ When authentic cultural immersion (not switching) is needed
- ✓ Use when: Cultural differences matter, international audiences, framing changes meaning

**comedy-drama-fusion:**
- ✗ When pure comedy or pure drama is what's needed
- ✗ Situations requiring consistent single tone
- ✗ When tonal mixing would feel disrespectful
- ✗ Emergency communications requiring clarity
- ✓ Use when: Full emotional range serves truth, complex human content, preventing melodrama or frivolity

**strategic-self-deprecation:**
- ✗ Apologizing for actual serious mistakes (reads as dismissive)
- ✗ When false modesty would insult others' achievements
- ✗ Cultural contexts where modesty reads as weakness
- ✗ When direct confidence assertion is needed
- ✓ Use when: Building connection, deflecting excessive praise, softening authority, British-American navigation
