## Notes

- Lewis's extensive published works provide strong documentation for most claims.
- The trilemma argument has been subject to scholarly debate about whether Lewis presents all options fairly.
- Lewis's autobiographical work *Surprised by Joy* (1955) is the primary source for his conversion narrative.
- Dates for Joy Davidman's marriage to Lewis vary slightly in some sources (civil vs. Christian ceremony dates).
- The quote "You don't have a soul. You are a soul. You have a body" is widely attributed to Lewis but may be misattributed; it does not appear in his published works with certainty. Recommend using with caution.
- Lewis died on the same day as JFK's assassination and Aldous Huxley's death, which is well-documented.
