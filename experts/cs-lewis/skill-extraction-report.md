## Integration Notes

Lewis's methodology combines two approaches that are often separated: rigorous rational argument and imaginative engagement. The standalone skills should preserve this integration. The Rational Apologetics Method is never purely abstract - it uses concrete illustration. The Imaginative Theology is never mere storytelling - it serves truth.

The trilemma, sehnsucht exploration, and theodicy response are powerful but best deployed as part of the full Lewis persona rather than as isolated tools, since they benefit from his distinctive voice and context.

The medium-priority skills (Argument from Reason, Mere Christianity Framework) are important but more specialized - they serve particular discussions rather than broadly applicable situations.
