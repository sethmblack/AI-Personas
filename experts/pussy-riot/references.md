## Trial and Legal Documents

### Trial Overview
- **Wikipedia - Pussy Riot 2012 Trial**: [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pussy_Riot_2012_trial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pussy_Riot_2012_trial) - Comprehensive timeline and documentation

### Key Dates
- **February 21, 2012**: Cathedral of Christ the Savior performance (~40 seconds)
- **March 3, 2012**: Arrest of Nadezhda Tolokonnikova and Maria Alyokhina
- **March 16, 2012**: Arrest of Yekaterina Samutsevich
- **Late July 2012**: Trial begins at Moscow Khamovniki District Court
- **August 8, 2012**: Closing statements delivered
- **August 17, 2012**: Conviction for "hooliganism motivated by religious hatred"; two-year sentences
- **October 10, 2012**: Samutsevich's sentence suspended on appeal
- **December 19, 2013**: Tolokonnikova and Alyokhina released under amnesty

### Charges and Verdict
- Charged with "hooliganism motivated by religious hatred" under Article 213 of Russian Criminal Code
- Judge Marina Syrova: defendants posed "a danger to society" for "grave" offenses including "the insult and humiliation of the Christian faith"
- Note: As of trial conclusion, court had not made full transcript publicly available

### Legal Analysis
- **NYU Law Review** - "Pussy Riot and the First Amendment: Consequences for the United States": [nyulawreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/NYULawReview-89-2-Koenig.pdf](https://www.nyulawreview.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/NYULawReview-89-2-Koenig.pdf)

### Related Legislation
- **June 30, 2013**: Putin signs bill imposing jail terms and fines for insulting religious feelings (widely seen as response to Punk Prayer)
- Pussy Riot's "Punk Prayer" video classified as extremist material by Moscow court (December 2012)

### Cathedral Performance (2012)
- **Date**: February 21, 2012
- **Location**: Cathedral of Christ the Savior, Moscow
- **Duration**: Approximately 40 seconds before security intervention
- **Song**: "Punk Prayer: Mother of God, Drive Putin Away"
- **Participants**: Five members (three later arrested)
- **Note**: Final video combined footage from Cathedral of Christ the Savior and Epiphany Cathedral at Yelokhovo (February 19)

## Post-Prison Organizations

### Zona Prava (Zone of Justice/Rights)
- **Founded**: March 2014 by Tolokonnikova and Alyokhina
- **Mission**: Prisoners' rights advocacy, legal representation for prison abuse victims
- **Location**: Complex of penal colonies in Republic of Mordovia, Russia
- **Activities**: Over 20 cases across Russia; 10+ cases in European Court of Human Rights
- **Status**: Designated "foreign agent" by Russian Ministry of Justice (September 29, 2021)
- Coverage: [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/russia-pussy-riot-prisoners-center/25295716.html), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/freed-pussy-riot-members-to-form-prisoners-rights-organization-5855018/)

### MediaZona
- **Founded**: 2014 by Tolokonnikova and Alyokhina
- **Editor-in-Chief**: Sergey Smirnov
- **Focus**: Russian judicial system, law enforcement, prison conditions
- **Partnership**: The Guardian
- **Status**: Blocked in Russia (March 6, 2022); designated "foreign agent" (September 2021)
- Wikipedia: [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediazona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediazona)
- Library of Congress: [loc.gov/item/lcwaN0032602](https://www.loc.gov/item/lcwaN0032602/)
- IJNET Coverage: [ijnet.org/en/story/news-site-mediazona-covers-conditions-russias-prison-system](https://ijnet.org/en/story/news-site-mediazona-covers-conditions-russias-prison-system)
