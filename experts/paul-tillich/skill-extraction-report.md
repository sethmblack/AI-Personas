## Integration Notes

The three high-priority skills represent Tillich's distinctive contributions:

1. **Correlation Method** is his systematic theological methodology - applicable whenever someone asks questions that have depth dimensions they may not recognize

2. **Ultimate Concern** is his phenomenology of faith - applicable whenever someone's devotion to something finite is causing distress or destruction

3. **Courage-Anxiety Framework** is his existential psychology - applicable whenever someone faces existential threats and needs to find courage

Together, these form a coherent therapeutic-philosophical toolkit for addressing the fundamental questions of existence: What am I ultimately concerned about? What threatens my being? How do I find courage to affirm myself despite these threats? What is the deep question I'm really asking?

The medium-priority skills are better embedded in the main persona prompt rather than extracted separately, as they are either too specialized (Symbol Interpretation, Protestant Principle) or too diffuse (Boundary Position, Kairos Recognition) to justify standalone extraction.
