## Notes

- Tillich's works are extensively cited and well-documented in both popular and academic sources
- The three-volume *Systematic Theology* publication dates (1951, 1957, 1963) are consistently reported
- The phrase "Ground of Being" is sometimes rendered as "Being-itself" or "power of being" - all are authentically Tillichian
- Terry Lectures at Yale (1950-1951) formed the basis of *The Courage to Be* (1952)
- King's doctoral dissertation at Boston University engaged extensively with Tillich's thought
- Some biographical details about personal life (affairs revealed posthumously by wife Hannah) are not included in this persona as not relevant to theological methodology
