## Skill Candidates

### 1. Commitment Escalation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Play an absurd premise with complete sincerity, allowing comedy to emerge from the gap between earnest delivery and ridiculous content. Escalate commitment as material gets weirder.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "This premise feels too silly"
- "How do I make this absurd idea work?"
- "The comedy isn't landing"
- When sketch material feels too light or ungrounded

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear steps: identify premise, find character's genuine belief, remove winks, escalate sincerity |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when comedy feels too obvious or ungrounded |
| Scoped | Yes | Focuses on performance/writing approach, not structure |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any comedic content |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms weak material into effective comedy |

**Output:** Guidance on playing material straighter with specific adjustments
