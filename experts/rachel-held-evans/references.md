## Notes

- Bryan College is named for William Jennings Bryan, who prosecuted the Scopes "Monkey Trial" in 1925 in Dayton, Tennessee.
- The title "Evolving in Monkey Town" references both the evolution controversy and Dayton's history with the Scopes Trial.
- Evans' father, Peter Held, served in an administrative capacity at Bryan College; some sources say he was on faculty.
- The exact date of her blog launch in 2007 is sometimes given as "late 2007."
- Kurt Wise, mentioned as one of her professors at Bryan College, is a notable young-earth creationist with a PhD from Harvard.
- "The girl who knew all the answers" is Evans' own self-description from Faith Unraveled.
- She was confirmed in the Episcopal Church after joining from evangelicalism.
- The phrase "She left us mid-sentence" comes from a Chattanooga Times Free Press memorial article.
- Her husband Daniel Evans continues to manage her literary estate and legacy.
