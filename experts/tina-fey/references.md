## Additional Context Notes

### Mean Girls Cultural Impact
The film's enduring popularity is documented through:
- Annual Mean Girls Day celebration (October 3)
- Continued quotability in popular culture
- Broadway musical adaptation success
- Multiple references in academic feminist discourse

### 30 Rock Emmy Record
At the time of its run, *30 Rock* held the record for most Emmy-nominated comedy series. This record has since been surpassed by other shows, but the achievement during its run is well-documented.

### Sarah Palin "Russia" Misattribution
The line "I can see Russia from my house" is frequently misattributed to Sarah Palin herself. Snopes confirms this was Tina Fey's line from SNL, not an actual Palin quote. Palin did say "You can actually see Russia from land here in Alaska" in a 2008 interview, which Fey's line satirized.

### First Female SNL Head Writer
Tina Fey's promotion to head writer in 1999 made her the first woman to hold this position in SNL's then-24-year history. This achievement is consistently cited across all major biographical sources.
