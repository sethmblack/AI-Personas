## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. feminist-comedy-craft

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear 6-step process defined in expertise.md (identify double standard → document lived experience → use self-deprecation → show absurdity through specifics → land on insight → never explain)
- **Invocable:** Natural triggers: "Make this feminist," "Point out the gender dynamics," "Smart comedy about women," "Expose the double standard"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - apply feminist lens to content through comedy without preaching
- **Reusable:** Applies to any content involving gender, workplace dynamics, cultural observations
- **Valuable:** Transforms serious feminist commentary into accessible, funny observations; makes the political personal

**Source Framework:** "The Feminist Comedy Framework" (expertise.md)

**Core Process:**
1. Identify the double standard (what men do vs. what women experience)
2. Document your lived experience of it (personal testimony)
3. Use self-deprecation to establish credibility (get there first)
4. Show the absurdity through specifics (details reveal truth)
5. Land on the insight (the "yes, exactly!" moment)
6. Never explain the feminism (let the audience connect the dots)

### 2. 30-rock-joke-density

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Systematic approach to layering multiple joke types in compressed space (dialogue + visual + character + callbacks + references + structural irony)
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Make this denser," "More jokes per minute," "Layer the comedy," "Add rewatchability," "Make it smarter"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - increase joke-per-minute rate through multi-layer construction
- **Reusable:** Works for any comedy writing, video scripts, sketches, sitcoms
- **Valuable:** Creates comedy that rewards repeat viewing; makes content work harder

**Source Framework:** "The 30 Rock Joke Density System" (expertise.md)

**Core Process:**
1. Identify 6 joke types: dialogue, visual, character, callbacks, references, structural
2. Layer 3+ types per scene/moment
3. Background gags operate while dialogue delivers different joke
4. Character quirks set up for later payoffs
5. Compress timing so jokes stack rapidly
6. Trust audience to catch what they catch (rewatchability)

### 4. character-as-cultural-mirror

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Method for creating characters that embody and critique cultural dynamics
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Create a character for this," "Embody this cultural issue," "Character-driven satire"
- **Scoped:** Focused on character creation as satirical tool
- **Reusable:** Works for sitcoms, sketches, film, any narrative comedy
- **Valuable:** Allows cultural critique through character rather than direct commentary

**Source Framework:** "The Character as Cultural Mirror" technique (expertise.md)

**Core Process:**
1. Identify cultural absurdity or dynamic
2. Create exaggerated but recognizable character who embodies it
3. Establish consistent quirks/behaviors
4. Let character reveal truth through their ridiculousness
5. Audience laughs without feeling attacked

**Examples:**
- Liz Lemon = every woman told to "lean in" while being called aggressive
- Jenna Maroney = women who've internalized objectification
- Jack Donaghy = conservative corporate America

**Note:** This overlaps with general character development. Consider whether it's distinct enough for standalone skill or better integrated into feminist-comedy-craft.

### 5. self-deprecating-authority

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Specific technique for establishing credibility through vulnerability
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Make this self-deprecating," "Establish authority," "Make this relatable"
- **Scoped:** Focused on single rhetorical technique
- **Reusable:** Works for comedy, persuasive writing, public speaking, personal essays
- **Valuable:** Disarms defensive audiences; makes serious points more palatable

**Source Framework:** "The Self-Deprecating Authority Move" (expertise.md)

**Core Process:**
1. Establish vulnerability first (undermine yourself)
2. Deliver the insight from that position (earned wisdom)
3. The self-deprecation gives permission to be right
4. Audience identifies rather than defends

**Note:** This might be better as a technique within feminist-comedy-craft rather than standalone skill. The self-deprecation is part of Step 3 in that framework.
