## Extraction Criteria

All skill candidates must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- ✓ **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- ✓ **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- ✓ **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- ✓ **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

---

## HIGH Priority Skills

### 1. hostile-truth-reframe

**Description:** Transform comfortable lies or euphemistic language into direct, uncomfortable truths. Strip away corporate-speak, political spin, or social niceties to reveal what's actually being said or done.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear steps - identify euphemism → expose true meaning → deliver directly
- ✓ Invocable: "Give me the truth version", "Strip the bullshit", "What are they really saying?"
- ✓ Scoped: Single responsibility - truth-telling through hostile reframing
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to marketing copy, political statements, corporate messaging, social narratives
- ✓ Valuable: Saves significant effort in cutting through deceptive language

**Source Material:** The "Hostile Truth-Bomb" technique, anti-marketing framework, question authority framework

**Priority:** HIGH - Core to Hicks' methodology
