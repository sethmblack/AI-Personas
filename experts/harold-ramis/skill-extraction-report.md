## HIGH Priority

### 1. philosophical-comedy-premise

**Source Pattern:** The Philosophical Comedy Framework
**Purpose:** Transform any premise or project into a story with embedded philosophical depth that entertains while exploring human truth
**Trigger:** "How do I make this story meaningful?" or "How do I add depth without being preachy?" or "What's the theme here?"
**Inputs:** Story premise, character descriptions, or project concept
**Outputs:** Identified philosophical question, absurd situation that embodies it, character matrix representing different responses to the truth
**Reasoning:** Clear 4-step process (identify truth, find absurd situation, populate characters, let collisions reveal philosophy). Highly actionable with repeatable methodology. Reusable across any narrative context. Saves significant development time by providing intellectual framework early.

---

### 2. ensemble-character-matrix

**Source Pattern:** The Ensemble Character Matrix
**Purpose:** Build a group of distinct characters with incompatible worldviews that generate organic comedy through friction
**Trigger:** "How do I build a strong ensemble?" or "My characters feel too similar" or "How do I create group dynamics?"
**Inputs:** Project context, number of characters needed, genre/tone
**Outputs:** Character matrix with distinct quadrants (Con Man, True Believer, Scientist, Everyman or appropriate variations), each with defined wants, beliefs, and comic functions
**Reasoning:** Clear matrix structure with defined archetypes and functions. Highly actionable - can be applied immediately to any group scenario. Demonstrated effectiveness across Ramis's filmography. Reusable for any ensemble-based project.

---

### 3. yes-and-development

**Source Pattern:** The "Yes, And" Development Method
**Purpose:** Develop ideas collaboratively by accepting and heightening premises rather than blocking them
**Trigger:** "How do we brainstorm effectively?" or "This idea seems too weird" or "How do I develop this concept further?"
**Inputs:** Initial premise or idea, context for development
**Outputs:** Fully developed concept with logical implications explored, unexpected truths discovered, and absurdity that contains meaning
**Reasoning:** Clear 5-step process (state, accept, imply, heighten, discover). Directly actionable improv methodology. Reusable across creative development, team brainstorming, and problem-solving contexts. Proven results in Ghostbusters development.

## MEDIUM Priority

### 4. authority-deflation-design

**Source Pattern:** The Authority Deflation Principle
**Purpose:** Construct satirical scenarios where authority figures are undermined through forces they cannot control while remaining human
**Trigger:** "How do I satirize power without being preachy?" or "How do I write a good antagonist?" or "How do I punch up effectively?"
**Inputs:** Authority figure or institution to satirize, context, desired tone
**Outputs:** Multi-dimensional authority character with genuine investment in their position, deflation mechanism that emerges from within the system, comedy that maintains humanity of the target
**Reasoning:** Clear conceptual framework with key points defined. Actionable for any satire or comedy targeting institutions. Slightly more context-dependent than other skills but still broadly applicable.

## LOW Priority

### 5. redemption-arc-structure

**Source Pattern:** The Redemption Comedy Framework
**Purpose:** Structure a character transformation arc that earns change through demonstrated growth
**Trigger:** "How does my character transform?" or "How do I make this change feel earned?"
**Inputs:** Stuck character, mechanism for repetition/reflection, desired end state
**Outputs:** 5-step arc from stuck state through failed attempts to genuine transformation
**Reasoning:** Clear 5-step process. However, more specialized to redemption narratives specifically. Overlaps with general story structure frameworks. Still valuable but narrower application than HIGH priority skills.
