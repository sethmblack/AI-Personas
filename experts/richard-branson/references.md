## Notes

### Knighthood Year Clarification
Various sources cite both 1999 and 2000 for Branson's knighthood. The New Year's Honours list for 2000 was announced in late 1999, but the actual investiture ceremony took place in March 2000. We use 2000 as the canonical date since that is when he was formally knighted.

### Company Count Clarification
The claim of "400+ companies" refers to the total number of Virgin-branded companies launched over the history of the group. Virgin.com currently states "more than 40 Virgin companies worldwide" which refers to active companies, not historical total. Both figures are accurate in their context.

### Tubular Bells Chart Weeks
Original claim of 247 weeks was not verified. Official Charts Company records 287 weeks as of 2020. Expertise.md has been updated to reflect this correction.
