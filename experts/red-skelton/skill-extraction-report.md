## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Physical Character Blueprint
**Priority:** HIGH
**Description:** Create a complete physical characterization through movement, posture, and gesture before adding words

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear steps - define walk, posture, facial expressions, signature movements
- **Invocable:** "Create physical blueprint for this character" or when character development is needed
- **Scoped:** Focused on physical embodiment only, not dialogue or backstory
- **Reusable:** Applies to any character creation (fiction, performance, communication personas)
- **Valuable:** Transforms abstract character concepts into concrete, performable physicality

**Key Elements:**
- Start with the walk
- Define posture and body language
- Identify signature gestures
- Determine facial expressions and reactions
- Select triggering prop (hat, costume element)

---

### 2. Pantomime Story Structure
**Priority:** HIGH
**Description:** Tell a complete story using only physical movement, gesture, and facial expression without words

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear sequence - establish setting, introduce conflict, build through physical beats, resolve
- **Invocable:** "Tell this story in pantomime" or when wordless communication is needed
- **Scoped:** Pure physical storytelling without dialogue
- **Reusable:** Applies to visual storytelling, presentations, demonstrations, video content
- **Valuable:** Creates universal communication that transcends language barriers

**Key Elements:**
- Establish location/setting through mime
- Show character state through body
- Build conflict with physical reactions
- Use props imaginatively (mimed or minimal)
- Land emotional beats with silence

---

### 3. Comedy-Pathos Balance
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Description:** Shift seamlessly from slapstick comedy to emotional depth within the same performance

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear technique - establish comedy pattern, create vulnerability moment, use silence/stillness, return to comedy
- **Invocable:** When content needs emotional depth beyond humor or when touching moment is appropriate
- **Scoped:** Specifically about balancing comedy and emotion, not about comedy itself
- **Reusable:** Applies to storytelling, speeches, content that benefits from emotional range
- **Valuable:** Adds depth and memorability to comedic content

**Key Elements:**
- Establish comedic rhythm first
- Find the human vulnerability in the character
- Use silence and stillness as transition
- Let emotion breathe without explaining
- Return to comedy naturally

---

### 4. Wholesome Humor Filter
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Description:** Convert potentially edgy or divisive humor into family-friendly comedy that celebrates rather than demeans

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear steps - identify the target, flip perspective to self or absurdity, find universal angle, celebrate don't mock
- **Invocable:** When humor risks being offensive or when family-friendly version is needed
- **Scoped:** Specifically about converting humor tone, not creating humor from scratch
- **Reusable:** Applies to any comedy writing, presentations, brand communication
- **Valuable:** Expands audience reach and creates enduring rather than shocking content

**Key Elements:**
- Identify what's being targeted
- Redirect to self-deprecation or situation absurdity
- Find universal experience everyone relates to
- Make target lovable, not mockable
- Test: would this work for all ages?

---

### 5. Character Consistency System
**Priority:** LOW
**Description:** Maintain distinctive character traits across multiple performances over time

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Document physical signatures, vocal patterns, catchphrases, create reference sheet
- **Invocable:** When character needs to be repeated/referenced over time
- **Scoped:** Maintenance of existing character, not creation
- **Reusable:** Applies to serialized content, brand personas, recurring characters
- **Valuable:** Builds audience recognition and trust
- **Lower priority:** More specific to ongoing character work; less universally applicable than other skills
