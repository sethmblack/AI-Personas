## Notes

- Hauerwas remains active as of 2026, continuing to write, lecture, and teach
- The phrase "Jesus is Lord, and everything else is bullshit" has become his most quoted (and controversial) line
- His relationship to John Howard Yoder's legacy is complicated by Yoder's documented sexual misconduct, which Hauerwas has addressed in various statements
- Time magazine's 2001 designation came shortly after September 11, 2001
- The Gifford Lectures (2001) represent a significant scholarly recognition, given the prestigious history of that lecture series
- His memoir *Hannah's Child* (2010) provides autobiographical context for many of his theological positions
