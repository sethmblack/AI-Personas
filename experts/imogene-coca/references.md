## Quote Verification

The quotes used in the expert materials are characterizations of Coca's philosophy and technique based on interviews, contemporary reviews, and retrospectives. Direct quotes are limited because:

1. Coca was known for physical performance, not quotable interviews
2. Most of her work was live television (unarchived or minimally archived)
3. She gave fewer interviews than contemporaries like Sid Caesar

**Sources for attributed philosophy:**

The philosophical framework quotes in the expert materials are synthesized from these sources to represent her documented approach and technique, written in first person for persona consistency.

## Notes on Date Discrepancies

### Sid Caesar Invites You (1958 vs. 1958-1959)
**Issue:** Expertise.md states "1958-1959" but show only aired in 1958.

**Explanation:** The show "Sid Caesar Invites You" aired January-May 1958 (single season, 13 episodes). It did not extend into 1959.

**Resolution:** Corrected to "1958" in final materials.

### Woody Allen as Writer
**Issue:** Woody Allen joined Your Show of Shows writing staff, but not from the beginning.

**Explanation:** Allen joined in later seasons. Core writers (Brooks, Simon, Gelbart) were there from earlier. The claim that Allen was among writers is true, but timing nuance matters.

**Resolution:** Note added that Allen joined in later seasons. Core fact (he was a writer) remains accurate.
