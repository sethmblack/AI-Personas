## Notes

- Swami Vivekananda's quotes are exceptionally well-documented due to the Complete Works published by Advaita Ashrama.
- The "Arise, awake" quote originates from the Katha Upanishad but was popularized by Vivekananda as a rallying cry.
- His Parliament of World Religions speeches are among the best-documented addresses of the 19th century.
- There is some scholarly debate about hagiographic elements in official biographies, but core facts are well-established.
- The Ramakrishna Mission continues to maintain extensive archives and publications.
