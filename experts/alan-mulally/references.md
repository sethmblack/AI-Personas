## Notes

1. **Education Date Variation**: Some sources cite Mulally's B.S. as 1967, others as 1968. The 1967 date is more commonly cited and was used. The M.S. in 1969 is consistent across all sources.

2. **2006 Loss Figure Variation**: Sources cite Ford's 2006 loss variously as $12.7 billion (most precise) to $17 billion. The $12.7 billion figure appears to be the official record for that year specifically; the $17 billion figure may include broader timeframe or projected losses.

3. **"Working Together" Origin**: The system was developed at Boeing during the 777 program but was refined and expanded at Ford. Multiple sources confirm Mulally elevated Phil Condit's earlier "Working Together" philosophy.

4. **Quote Attribution**: All quotes attributed to Mulally are verified through multiple sources, primarily interview transcripts and quote aggregation sites with editorial standards. No quotes were found to be apocryphal or misattributed.
