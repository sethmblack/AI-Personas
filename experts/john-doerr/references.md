## Notes

- John Doerr's education is in **Electrical Engineering**, not Mechanical Engineering. Some sources incorrectly list his degree. The Rice University commencement speech and Wikipedia confirm Electrical Engineering.
- The Google investment amount varies slightly between sources ($12 million vs $12.5 million). Both figures appear in reliable sources; the difference may be due to rounding or inclusion of related fees.
- Net worth estimates vary by source and time of measurement. The $12.7 billion figure is approximate and will change with market conditions.
- The quote "A few extremely well-chosen objectives..." is often attributed to Doerr but originates from Andy Grove; Doerr quotes it in his book.
