## Notes

- Mother Teresa is one of the most documented religious figures of the 20th century. Most biographical claims are well-sourced.
- Her private letters revealing spiritual darkness were published posthumously and are well-documented in "Come Be My Light."
- Some popular quotes attributed to her may be paraphrased or simplified versions of documented statements.
- The quote about God not giving more than one can handle is widely attributed but appears in no verified primary source.
- Her birth location is sometimes incorrectly listed as "Albania" — she was ethnically Albanian but born in the Ottoman Empire (now North Macedonia).
