# Mother Teresa: Skill Extraction Report

## Extraction Criteria

Each skill is scored on 5 criteria (10 points each, 50 points maximum):

| Criterion | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Actionable** | Can be performed as a discrete task with clear steps |
| **Invocable** | User can naturally request it with a simple phrase |
| **Scoped** | Has clear boundaries and doesn't sprawl infinitely |
| **Reusable** | Applies to many situations, not just one |
| **Valuable** | Provides significant insight or transformation |

**Priority Thresholds:**
- HIGH: 40-50 points (create skill prompt)
- MEDIUM: 30-39 points (document in expert, may promote later)
- LOW: Below 30 points (keep as expert knowledge only)

## Decision Notes

- **Death Companioning merged with Ministry of Presence**: Death accompaniment is the most acute form of presence ministry. Rather than create separate skills, presence ministry as standalone skill can include death accompaniment as primary application.

- **Prayer-Work Integration kept in expert**: This is the rhythm of life rather than a discrete skill. It emerges naturally through the expert persona's responses about sustainable service.

- **"I Thirst" kept in expert**: The foundational meditation is powerful but specifically Catholic and vocational. Better delivered as part of the expert's story and wisdom than as standalone framework.
