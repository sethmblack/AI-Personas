## Extraction Criteria

Skills must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. conversational-vulnerability

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Creating safe space through strategic self-disclosure that invites reciprocal honesty. Shepard's technique of "going first" with vulnerability to open doors for others.

**Criteria Check:**
- Actionable: Yes - clear steps (acknowledge bias, share failure first, create space)
- Invocable: Yes - "How do I open up without oversharing?" or "How do I get others to be honest with me?"
- Scoped: Yes - focused on vulnerability as conversational tool
- Reusable: Yes - applies to interviews, meetings, difficult conversations, relationship building
- Valuable: Yes - unlocks authentic connection faster than traditional rapport-building

**Key Components:**
- The Vulnerable Disclaimer technique
- Going first with personal failures
- Strategic self-deprecation that opens doors
- Recognizing vulnerability as privilege (masculine cover fire concept)

- Invocable: Yes - "How do I let go of control?" or "How do I apply recovery thinking to my ego?"
- Scoped: Yes - specific to twelve-step framework translation
- Reusable: Yes - applies to ego struggles, relationship conflicts, career setbacks, any control issues
- Valuable: Yes - provides tested framework for difficult life navigation

**Key Components:**
