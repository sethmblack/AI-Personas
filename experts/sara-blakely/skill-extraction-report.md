## HIGH Priority

### 1. failure-reframe

**Source Pattern:** The Failure Reframe Framework
**Purpose:** Transform the meaning of failure from outcome to inaction, freeing people from fear-based paralysis
**Trigger:** "I'm afraid to fail" or "I've failed" or "What if this doesn't work?" or "I'm scared to try"
**Inputs:** The fear, the attempted action, the perceived "failure," the goal
**Outputs:** Reframed perspective on failure, weekly failure practice, action prompt
**Reasoning:** Core Sara Blakely philosophy with clear 5-component framework. Used constantly in entrepreneurship coaching. Different from existing persistence-protocol (which addresses rejection after failure) - this addresses the fear BEFORE action. High impact, highly actionable.

**Overlap Check:**
- `persistence-protocol`: 30% overlap (both address failure, but persistence-protocol handles post-rejection while this handles pre-action fear). Distinct skill.

**Source Pattern:** Hidden Blessing Protocol
**Purpose:** Train the mind to immediately find opportunity within setback, converting obstacles into advantages
**Trigger:** "This went wrong" or "I hit a setback" or "My plan failed" or "This is bad news"
**Inputs:** The setback/obstacle, the original plan, the current situation
**Outputs:** Identified hidden blessings, reframed perspective, new opportunity paths
**Reasoning:** Explicit Sara Blakely technique with clear application. Different from obstacle-to-opportunity (which is Marcus Aurelius/Stoic) - this is specifically about immediate mindset shift and blessing-hunting. High frequency use case.

**Overlap Check:**
- `obstacle-to-opportunity`: 40% overlap (both convert setbacks to opportunities, but obstacle-to-opportunity is Stoic framework while this is Sara's specific "where is the hidden blessing?" question). Distinct enough to warrant separate skill.
