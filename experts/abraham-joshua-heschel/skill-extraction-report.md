## Extraction Criteria

## HIGH Priority Candidates (Recommend Implementation)

### 1. radical-amazement-practice

**Description:** Recover and cultivate the capacity for radical amazement—the foundational spiritual stance of wonder at existence itself. Counteract the eclipse of wonder caused by routine, cynicism, and reductionism.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Actionable | Clear process: identify what is taken for granted, strip familiarity, recover the question behind the answer, practice noticing |
| Invocable | "I feel spiritually empty" / "Life feels meaningless" / "Nothing seems special anymore" / "How do I recover wonder?" |
| Scoped | Single responsibility: recover wonder; does not diagnose clinical issues or solve practical problems |
| Reusable | Applies to spiritual emptiness, creative blocks, meaning crisis, cynicism, burnout, loss of motivation |
| Valuable | Addresses root cause of spiritual malaise; transforms orientation before addressing symptoms |

**Inputs:** Current state (emptiness, cynicism, boredom), specific domain if relevant
**Outputs:** Wonder recovery protocol, specific practices, reframed perception

**Heschel Foundation:**
- "Our goal should be to live life in radical amazement"
- "The beginning of awe is wonder, and the beginning of wisdom is awe"
- "As civilization advances, the sense of wonder declines"

---

### 2. prophetic-consciousness-activation

**Description:** Activate prophetic awareness—the capacity to sense moral urgency, connect contemplation to action, and experience responsibility for injustice. Move from passive awareness to engaged response.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Actionable | Clear process: identify the moral dimension, sense the divine pathos (what would God feel here?), connect prayer/contemplation to concrete action, embrace "few are guilty but all are responsible" |
| Invocable | "Is this just?" / "What should I do?" / "How do I translate awareness into action?" / "I see injustice but feel paralyzed" |
| Scoped | Single responsibility: connect spiritual awareness to moral action; not general ethics or activism strategy |
| Reusable | Applies to workplace ethics, social responsibility, organizational justice, personal moral dilemmas |
| Valuable | Provides theological grounding for action; addresses gap between knowing and doing |

**Inputs:** Situation with moral dimension, current relationship to action (paralysis, uncertainty, disconnection)
**Outputs:** Prophetic analysis, concrete action steps, integration of contemplation and engagement

**Heschel Foundation:**
- "Few are guilty, but all are responsible"
- "Indifference to evil is worse than evil itself"
- "I felt my legs were praying"
- "In the eyes of the prophet, the exploitation of the poor is as grave as idolatry"

---

### 3. sabbath-time-sanctification

**Description:** Apply Heschel's Sabbath principles to create sanctuary in time amid the tyranny of busyness and space-conquest. Build "cathedrals of hours" rather than merely optimizing schedules.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Actionable | Clear process: audit relationship to time, identify space-conquest tyranny, design time sanctification practices, implement boundaries |
| Invocable | "I'm burned out" / "There's no time" / "Work consumes everything" / "How do I rest?" / "I can't disconnect" |
| Scoped | Single responsibility: sanctify time; not productivity optimization or schedule management |
| Reusable | Applies to burnout, workaholism, work-life imbalance, inability to rest, technology addiction |
| Valuable | Addresses root cause of temporal crisis; provides theological alternative to productivity cult |

**Inputs:** Current time patterns, burnout symptoms, specific constraints
**Outputs:** Time sanctification design, boundary practices, Sabbath principles for modern context

**Heschel Foundation:**
- "The Sabbaths are our great cathedrals"
- "Judaism is a religion of time rather than space"
- "Six days a week we live under the tyranny of things; on the seventh day we try to become attuned to holiness in time"

### 4. depth-theology-questioning

**Description:** Recover the questions behind religious answers. When doctrine, ritual, or belief feels hollow, excavate the original experience that generated the formulation.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Actionable | Process: identify the doctrine/practice, ask "what question does this answer?", locate the original experience, reconnect formulation to source |
| Invocable | "Religion feels hollow" / "I go through the motions" / "These beliefs feel dead" |
| Scoped | Specific to religious/spiritual practice revitalization |
| Reusable | Applies to anyone struggling with inherited religion or feeling disconnected from tradition |
| Valuable | Revitalizes rather than abandons tradition |

**Recommendation:** Valuable but more specialized than the top three. Could be developed as a sub-skill of radical-amazement-practice.

### 5. divine-pathos-reframe

**Description:** Shift theological questions from "Does God exist?" to "What does God ask?" From deity-as-object to relationship-as-reality.

| Criterion | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Actionable | Process: reframe the question, identify where one is "addressed," sense the pathos (God's concern) in the situation |
| Invocable | "I don't know if I believe in God" / "Theological doubt paralyzes me" |
| Scoped | Specific to theological questioning |
| Reusable | Common modern situation; applies broadly |
| Valuable | Unsticks theological paralysis |

**Recommendation:** Valuable but overlaps significantly with radical-amazement-practice and prophetic-consciousness. Could be integrated rather than standalone.

## Integration Notes

These three skills form a coherent system:

1. **radical-amazement-practice** - Recover wonder; the foundational orientation
2. **prophetic-consciousness-activation** - Translate awareness into action
3. **sabbath-time-sanctification** - Create sanctuary in time for depth

The three interconnect:
- Radical amazement opens the capacity to sense the prophetic
- Prophetic consciousness requires contemplative depth (Sabbath)
- Sabbath rest enables renewed wonder and sustained action

This mirrors Heschel's own integration: mystical wonder, prophetic urgency, and Sabbath sanctity were never separate in his life or thought.

---

## Distinctive Value

These skills are distinctive to Heschel:

- **Radical amazement** - While other thinkers discuss wonder, Heschel's formulation is uniquely comprehensive (ontological, epistemological, ethical)
- **Prophetic consciousness** - Bridges mystical and activist traditions in a way few have achieved
- **Sabbath time sanctification** - The "palace in time" concept is Heschel's distinctive contribution to spirituality of time

No other expert in the library combines these three emphases.
