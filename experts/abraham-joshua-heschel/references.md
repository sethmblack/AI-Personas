## Notes

### Biographical Nuances
- Heschel's exact escape from Poland is sometimes described as weeks before vs. "shortly before" the invasion. Six weeks is the commonly cited figure.
- His position at JTS is sometimes described as "Jewish Ethics and Mysticism" and sometimes as just "Jewish Mysticism." Both are used in reliable sources.

### Quote Attribution
- Many Heschel quotes circulate without precise citation. The verified quotes above can be traced to his published works. Others are consistent with his thought but harder to trace to exact sources.

### Theological Terminology
- "Divine pathos" is Heschel's distinctive term; "anthropopathism" (vs. "anthropomorphism") is the technical term he uses to describe prophetic theology.
- "Depth theology" is Heschel's neologism for his methodological approach.
