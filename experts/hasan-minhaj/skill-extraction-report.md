## Evaluation Criteria

Skills must meet ALL five criteria:

- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Personal-to-Political Story Structure

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform abstract policy or systemic issues into emotionally resonant narratives by starting with specific personal experience and zooming out to reveal broader patterns.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable:** Clear 4-step process (specific moment → emotional detail → systemic reveal → impact statement)
- ✅ **Invocable:** User could request "make this policy issue personal" or "apply personal-to-political zoom"
- ✅ **Scoped:** Single responsibility—transforming abstract concepts into personal narratives
- ✅ **Reusable:** Works for any policy, systemic issue, or institutional topic
- ✅ **Valuable:** Saves hours of narrative structuring; makes complex topics accessible

**Extraction Notes:** This is Minhaj's signature technique and most versatile framework. Could be packaged as a clear transformation skill with input (abstract topic) and output (personal narrative structure).

### 3. Cultural Code-Switch Dialogue

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Write dialogue that authentically incorporates multiple cultural registers (languages, references, idioms) to illustrate immigrant/diaspora experience and cultural hybridity.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable:** Clear pattern—introduce phrase in heritage language, translate with attitude, connect to point
- ✅ **Invocable:** User could request "add cultural code-switching" or "make this dialogue show identity tension"
- ✅ **Scoped:** Focused on dialogue/language technique, not broader cultural analysis
- ✅ **Reusable:** Works for any multicultural character, immigrant narrative, or identity-focused content
- ✅ **Valuable:** Hard to do authentically; requires understanding of multiple cultural contexts

**Extraction Notes:** Valuable for writers working with multicultural characters or diaspora themes. Skill would help create authentic-feeling code-switching without stereotyping.

### 4. Callback Web Architecture

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Plant narrative seeds early in content, then pay them off later in unexpected ways to create sense that everything connects—useful for speeches, essays, comedy sets, or any long-form narrative.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable:** Identify key themes → plant early references → track callback opportunities → deliver payoffs
- ✅ **Invocable:** User could request "add callback architecture" or "create narrative web"
- ✅ **Scoped:** Focused on structural technique of planting and paying off references
- ✅ **Reusable:** Works for speeches, essays, comedy, presentations, any extended narrative
- ✅ **Valuable:** Creates cohesion and "aha" moments; time-consuming to plan manually

**Extraction Notes:** This technique makes content feel deeply interconnected. Skill would analyze content for callback opportunities and suggest where to plant/pay off references.
