## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts

- **SNL tenure**: Cast member 1990-1995; confirmed through NBC archives
- **Film box office**: All financial figures verified through Box Office Mojo and studio reports
- **Album certifications**: RIAA certifications verified through official database
- **Netflix deal**: Confirmed through Netflix press releases and trade publications
- **Awards and nominations**: Verified through official award body records

### Estimates and Approximations

- **Netflix deal value**: Reported as $250-350M total; exact terms not publicly disclosed
- **Murder Mystery viewership**: Netflix reported 73M households in first month; methodology not fully transparent
- **Happy Madison profit-sharing**: Specific percentages not publicly disclosed; confirmed practice through interviews

### Disputed or Uncertain

- **Grown Ups production approach**: Reports of minimal script and heavy improvisation come from cast interviews; no official script publicly available
- **"Worst film ever" Oscar snub response**: Sandler joked about this in interviews; unclear if serious threat or comedic response
- **Relationship cooling with Rob Schneider**: Schneider stopped appearing in Sandler films mid-2010s; neither has publicly discussed reasons

### Collaborative vs. Solo Credit

- **Song writing**: Many Sandler songs credited to multiple writers; "The Chanukah Song" credited to Sandler, Ian Maxtone-Graham, Brooks Arthur, and David Buskin
- **Film writing**: Most Happy Madison films involve multiple writers; Sandler often has story credit or co-writing credit rather than solo credit

## Methodology Notes

**Primary sources** (SNL appearances, films, albums) provide direct evidence of Sandler's work and evolution.

**Interview sources** provide insights into philosophy (loyalty, ensemble, joy-first) but should be considered self-reported rather than objective analysis.

**Box office and certification data** are factual but don't account for home video, streaming, or long-term cultural impact beyond initial release.

**Critical reception** shows consistent pattern: commercial success despite critical dismissal, with exceptions for dramatic performances with auteur directors (Anderson, Safdies, Baumbach).

**Ensemble member interviews** corroborate Sandler's loyalty philosophy but may be biased toward positive framing given ongoing professional relationships.

The **Netflix era** (2014-present) marks significant shift in business model; traditional box office metrics less relevant; viewership numbers reported by Netflix but methodology not transparent.
