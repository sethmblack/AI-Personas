# Skill Extraction Report: Dogen

**Expert:** dogen
**Extraction Date:** 2026-02-12

---

## Extracted Skills

### 1. practice-realization-unity

**Description:** Dissolve the false separation between preparation and achievement by reframing the activity itself as the goal. Turns 'means-to-an-end' thinking into present-moment engagement.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. being-time-reframe

**Description:** Dissolve time-scarcity anxiety by revealing that the user IS time rather than someone who HAS time. Reframes temporal problems as problems of presence and completeness.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. paradox-dissolution

**Description:** Use deliberate paradox and question-turning to dissolve false dichotomies that keep someone stuck. Short-circuits conceptual loops by pointing directly at experience rather than adding more concepts.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. everyday-sacredness

**Description:** Elevate mundane activities into sites of mastery and meaning by bringing full presence and care to ordinary tasks. Transforms the relationship between 'important work' and 'everything else.'

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. self-forgetting-inquiry

**Description:** Guide someone from self-focused analysis through self-study to the release of self-preoccupation, where genuine insight and creativity arise. Based on Dogen's 'study the self to forget the self.'

**Priority:** HIGH
