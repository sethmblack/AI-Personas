# Dogen Zenji - Verified References

## Biographical Facts

### Birth and Origins
- **Born:** 1200 (traditionally January 19), Kyoto, Japan
- **Died:** 1253 (September 22), Kyoto, Japan
- **Birth family:** Likely illegitimate son of Minamoto Michichika, a high court noble
- **Mother:** Named Ishi; died when Dogen was 7 years old
- **Source:** [Dogen - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen); [Association for Asian Studies](https://www.asianstudies.org/publications/eaa/archives/dogen-his-life-religion-and-poetry/)

### Monastic Career
- **1212:** At age 13, ordained on Mount Hiei in the Tendai school under uncle Ryokan Hogen
- **Training period:** Studied Rinzai Zen with Myozen (1184-1225), dharma heir of Myoan Eisai
- **1223-1227:** Traveled to China with Myozen at age 23
- **1225:** Received dharma transmission from Chan Master Tiantong Rujing (1163-1228) of the Caodong lineage at Mount Tiantong
- **Source:** [Dogen - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen); [Lion's Roar - Who Was Eihei Dogen?](https://www.lionsroar.com/buddhism/dogen/)

### Temples Founded
- **1233:** Established Koshoji (Kannondori Koshohorin-ji) near Kyoto - first true Zen temple in Japan, modeled on Chinese Chan temples
- **1243:** Founded Eiheiji ("Temple of Eternal Peace") in Echizen Province (modern Fukui Prefecture)
- **Source:** [Dogen - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen)

### School Founded
- **Soto Zen:** Dogen is credited as the founder of the Soto school of Zen Buddhism in Japan
- Together with Keizan Jokin (1264-1325), regarded as co-founders of Japanese Soto Zen
- **Source:** [Dogen - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen)

## Primary Works

### Shobogenzo (Treasury of the True Dharma Eye)
- **Composition dates:** 1231-1253
- **Format:** Approximately 95 fascicles
- **Language:** Written in Japanese (kana), unusual for Buddhist texts of the period
- **Significance:** Considered one of the greatest philosophical works in Japanese literature
- **Source:** [Shobogenzo - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Db%C5%8Dgenz%C5%8D)

### Shinji Shobogenzo
- Collection of 301 koans in Chinese, compiled without commentaries
- **Source:** [Shobogenzo - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Db%C5%8Dgenz%C5%8D)

### Key Fascicles with Dates
- **Bendowa (Wholehearted Practice of the Way):** 1231 - one of Dogen's earliest writings
- **Genjokoan (Actualizing the Fundamental Point):** 1233
- **Uji (Being-Time):** 1240
- **Source:** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Db%C5%8Dgenz%C5%8D); [Uji - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uji_(Being-Time))

## Key Concepts - Verified Terminology

### Shikantaza
- **Japanese:** 只管打坐
- **Meaning:** Dogen's Japanese translation of Chinese phrase "zhiguan dazuo"
- **Translation:** "Just sitting" or "doing only zazen whole-heartedly"
- **Source:** [Shikantaza - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shikantaza); [Soto Zen official resource](https://www.sotozen.com/eng/library/key_terms/pdf/key_terms01.pdf)

### Uji (Being-Time)
- **Japanese:** 有時
- **Components:** u (有) meaning "being" + ji (時) meaning "time"
- **Ordinary reading:** "arutoki" meaning "sometimes" or "for the time being"
- **Dogen's innovation:** Deliberately separated and recombined as compound "uji" (being-time)
- **Source:** [Uji - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uji_(Being-Time))

### Shusho Itto (Practice-Enlightenment Unity)
- **Japanese:** 修証一等 or 修証一如
- **Meaning:** Practice and enlightenment are one
- **Source:** [Dogen - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen); [Bright Way Zen](https://brightwayzen.org/dogen-bendowa/)

### Genjokoan
- **Meaning:** "Actualizing the fundamental point" or "making all of life a field of awakening"
- **Alternative translation:** "To answer the question from true reality through the practice of our everyday activity"
- **Source:** [Tricycle - Genjokoan commentary](https://tricycle.org/article/dogen-genjokoan-commentary/)

## Verified Quotes

### "To study the Buddha Way..."
> "To study the Buddha Way is to study the self. To study the self is to forget the self. To forget the self is to be enlightened by the myriad things."

- **Source:** Genjokoan fascicle of Shobogenzo
- **Verification:** [Goodreads - Dogen Quotes](https://www.goodreads.com/author/quotes/23358.D_gen); [Wikiquote - Dogen](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen)

### "That you carry yourself forward..."
> "That you carry yourself forward and experience the myriad things is delusion. That the myriad things come forward and experience themselves is awakening."

- **Source:** Genjokoan fascicle of Shobogenzo
- **Verification:** [Wikiquote - Dogen](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen)

### "Enlightenment is intimacy with all things"
- **Source:** Attributed to Dogen's interpretation of enlightenment
- **Verification:** [A-Z Quotes](https://www.azquotes.com/author/4029-Dogen)

### "The whole moon and the entire sky..."
> "The whole moon and the entire sky are reflected in one dewdrop on the grass."

- **Source:** Genjokoan
- **Verification:** [Insight State - Dogen Quotes](https://www.insightstate.com/quotes/dogen-zenji-quotes/)

## Scholarly Comparisons

### Heidegger Comparison
- Modern scholars including Steven Heine and Joan Stambaugh have compared Dogen's concepts of temporality with Martin Heidegger's 1927 work "Being and Time"
- **Source:** [Uji - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uji_(Being-Time))

## Contemporary Status

### Eiheiji Today
- Remains the head temple of Soto Zen Buddhism
- Located in Fukui Prefecture, Japan
- One of two main temples of the Soto school (with Sojiji)
- **Source:** [Dogen - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C5%8Dgen)

### Soto Zen Statistics
- Soto school is the largest Zen denomination in Japan
- Approximately 15,000 temples in Japan
- **Source:** General knowledge; verify with Soto Zen official sources
