## Notes on Discrepancies

### ⚠ Film Release vs. Filming Dates

In the PROMPT.md, I referenced the concert films as "(1979)" and "(1982)" which are the release years. However:

- **"Live in Concert"** was actually filmed December 10, 1978, and released in 1979
- **"Live on the Sunset Strip"** was filmed December 9-10, 1981, and released March 12, 1982

**Resolution:** The dates in PROMPT.md are the release dates, which are accurate for how these works are commonly referenced. No change needed, but noting the filming dates for completeness.

### ⚠ Freebasing Incident Details

Sources conflict on whether the fire was:
- An accidental explosion while freebasing (police version)
- Deliberately pouring rum on himself and lighting it (Pryor's own account)

**Resolution:** expertise.md acknowledges both versions exist. This is a documented historical ambiguity, not a factual error in our content.
