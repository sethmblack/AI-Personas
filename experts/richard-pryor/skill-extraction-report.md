## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Confessional Transform
**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:** This is Pryor's signature technique—transforming personal disasters and shameful experiences into universal truth through comedy. The methodology is clear and reusable across any context requiring vulnerability.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear steps (identify the shame, strip away euphemism, find the human truth, embody the moment)
- ✓ Invocable: User can request "make this confessional" or "apply Pryor's vulnerability technique"
- ✓ Scoped: One clear purpose—transform protected content into vulnerable truth-telling
- ✓ Reusable: Works for any content where honesty is being avoided
- ✓ Valuable: Saves hours of trying to figure out how to be authentically vulnerable

**Skill Description:** Transform sanitized or abstract content into confessional, vulnerable truth-telling by mining personal disaster for universal recognition. Takes "safe" content and makes it dangerously honest.

### 2. Multi-Character Scene Builder
**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:** Pryor's technique of creating entire scenes through character embodiment—not describing conflict but inhabiting all participants with distinct voices, perspectives, and internal logic. This is teachable and highly valuable for storytelling.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear process (identify perspectives, develop distinct voices, show collision of realities)
- ✓ Invocable: User can request "show this as a Pryor-style scene" or "embody multiple perspectives"
- ✓ Scoped: One clear purpose—transform description into embodied multi-voice scene
- ✓ Reusable: Works for any conflict, interaction, or situation with multiple viewpoints
- ✓ Valuable: Dramatically increases storytelling power vs. traditional narration

**Skill Description:** Transform narrative description into fully embodied multi-character scenes where each person's voice, logic, and perspective is inhabited completely. The comedy/truth emerges from collision of distinct realities.

### 3. Silence Punctuation Method
**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Rationale:** Strategic use of silence after uncomfortable truths to let audiences sit with discomfort. While valuable, this is more of a performance timing technique that may be harder to apply in written contexts.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear trigger conditions (after hard truth, before tonal shift)
- ✓ Invocable: User can request "add Pryor silences" or "let this breathe"
- ⚠ Scoped: Primarily works in performance/oral contexts; limited application to text
- ⚠ Reusable: Works in performance but less clear how to translate to other mediums
- ✓ Valuable: When applicable, creates powerful emotional impact

**Skill Description:** Use strategic silence/white space after uncomfortable truths to force audience recognition rather than rushing to the next point. Let the weight of the statement exist.

**Note:** May be more appropriate as a technique within other skills rather than standalone.

### 4. Code-Switching Analysis
**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Rationale:** Pryor's technique of moving fluidly between Black vernacular and "proper" English to demonstrate language as survival tool and resistance. This is a specific social commentary technique.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- ✓ Actionable: Show shift between registers, demonstrate what each code signals
- ✓ Invocable: User can request "show the code-switching" or "demonstrate language navigation"
- ✓ Scoped: Focused on linguistic identity performance
- ⚠ Reusable: Highly specific to racial/cultural identity contexts
- ✓ Valuable: When applicable, reveals hidden dynamics of assimilation and authenticity

**Skill Description:** Demonstrate how code-switching between linguistic registers reveals the performance of identity, assimilation pressures, and cultural navigation strategies.

**Note:** Valuable but domain-specific. May be better as a technique note within the expert rather than standalone skill.
