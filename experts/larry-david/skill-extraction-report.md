## HIGH Priority

### 1. social-autopsy

**Source Pattern:** The Social Contract Enforcement Framework
**Purpose:** Dissect any social interaction to expose hidden violations, unspoken obligations, and the absurdity of polite pretense
**Trigger:** "What's really going on here?" or "Why does this feel wrong?" or "Analyze this interaction"
**Inputs:** Description of a social interaction, conversation, or relationship dynamic
**Outputs:** Analysis identifying the hidden rules being violated, unspoken expectations, and why participants are uncomfortable or behaving oddly
**Reasoning:** Meets all 5 criteria. Actionable (clear steps: identify norm, find violation, expose dynamics). Invocable (direct trigger phrases). Scoped (social analysis only). Reusable (applies to any interaction). Valuable (provides insight that most analysis misses). Core Larry David methodology used constantly in both Seinfeld and Curb.

### 2. cringe-escalation-builder

**Source Pattern:** The Cringe Escalation Framework
**Purpose:** Construct a scenario where a minor social misstep cascades into catastrophic consequences through logical steps
**Trigger:** "How could this get worse?" or "Build an escalation" or "What's the worst case?"
**Inputs:** Initial situation or minor violation; optionally, target endpoint or stakes
**Outputs:** Step-by-step escalation showing how small problem compounds into disaster, with each step individually reasonable
**Reasoning:** Meets all 5 criteria. Actionable (5-step framework documented). Invocable (clear triggers). Scoped (narrative escalation only). Reusable (works for comedy, drama, risk analysis). Valuable (core technique used in every Curb episode). This is Larry David's signature structural method.

## MEDIUM Priority

### 3. pretty-good-reframe

**Source Pattern:** Signature Phrases / Comedy Philosophy
**Purpose:** Transform enthusiastic or positive content into Larry David's measured, honest assessment style
**Trigger:** "How would Larry react to this?" or "Give me an honest take" or "Curb the enthusiasm"
**Inputs:** Content expressing enthusiasm, good news, or positive assessment
**Outputs:** Reframed response with measured acknowledgment, hidden downsides exposed, "pretty, pretty good" ceiling applied
**Reasoning:** Meets all 5 criteria. Actionable (specific deflation techniques). Invocable (clear triggers). Scoped (tone transformation). Reusable (any positive content). Valuable (creates distinctive voice contrast). Medium priority because it's more stylistic than structural.

### 4. outline-method-scene

**Source Pattern:** The Outline Method (Curb Production Framework)
**Purpose:** Generate scene outlines in the Curb Your Enthusiasm style - situation and beats without dialogue
**Trigger:** "Create a Curb-style scene" or "Outline this interaction" or "Give me story beats"
**Inputs:** Situation premise, characters involved, desired outcome or collision
**Outputs:** Paragraph-form scene outline describing what happens without scripted dialogue, suitable for improvisation
**Reasoning:** Meets all 5 criteria. Actionable (specific format documented). Invocable (direct request). Scoped (scene outlines only). Reusable (any comedic scenario). Valuable (unique production method). Medium priority as it's more specialized to comedy writers.
