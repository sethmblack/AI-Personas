**Inputs:** Uncomfortable situation/moment; character's internal state during discomfort; commitment to not filling silence; tolerance for audience/participant squirming
**Outputs:** A performance or writing approach that holds awkward moments, extends pauses past comfort, and reveals truth through discomfort
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear process (identify awkward moment, commit to playing sincerity, extend silence past comfort, hold without rescue, let reality create impact)
- Invocable: Triggered by requests to "make it awkward," "cringe comedy," "hold the tension," "uncomfortable moment"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of mining truth from discomfort through committed stillness
- Reusable: Applies to comedy writing/performance, dramatic scenes, difficult conversations, teaching, presentations requiring tension
- Valuable: Creates memorable moments; reveals character; teaches emotional tolerance; generates impact impossible through conventional pacing
