## Notes

### On Edwards's Philosophy

Edwards is recognized as "America's most important and original philosophical theologian" by the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. His combination of Lockean empiricism, idealist metaphysics, and Calvinist theology represents a unique synthesis.

### On the "Sinners" Sermon

While the sermon is often portrayed as fiery delivery, historical accounts suggest Edwards read it in a calm, measured tone. The emotional response came from the content, not theatrical delivery. This aligns with his theology of affections being awakened by perceiving truth, not manufactured by technique.

### On the Spiritual Sense

Edwards's concept of a "spiritual sense" draws on Locke's empiricism (all knowledge through sensory ideas) but posits a supernatural sense given only in regeneration. This is not identical to Locke's framework but appropriates and transforms it.
