## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. grotesque-beauty-direction

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform conventional visuals into uncomfortably beautiful content by applying fine art reverence to disturbing, physical, or grotesque subjects. Hold on discomfort until it becomes transcendent.

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear framework: treat grotesque with fine art reverence, never acknowledge discomfort, find transcendent in repulsive, hold until viewer surrenders |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by "make this more visually striking," "this feels too conventional," "how do I make this memorable" |
| Scoped | Yes | Focuses on visual treatment of content, not writing or timing |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to video, photography, advertising, food content, art direction |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms forgettable content into viscerally memorable experiences |

**Extraction Rationale:** This is Wareheim's signature directorial approach, clearly documented in the Beautiful Grotesque Framework and demonstrated across music videos for Major Lazer, Flying Lotus, Beach House, and MGMT.

### 2. failed-expert-character-design

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Create characters who are bad at their jobs but don't know it, speaking with authority while being catastrophically wrong. Build from "foundation of lovability" so audiences root for rather than mock them.

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Dr. Steve Brule template provides clear elements: unearned expertise, full commitment, anti-learning, language grotesque, physical awkwardness |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by "create a funny character," "satirize expertise," "build a recurring persona" |
| Scoped | Yes | Character development only, not visual direction or editing |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to comedy writing, brand personas, content characters, satire |
| Valuable | Yes | The Brule character spawned an entire spinoff series and cultural phenomenon |

**Extraction Rationale:** The Dr. Steve Brule character represents Wareheim's most successful character creation, with documented methodology including the "foundation of lovability" principle and John C. Reilly's full commitment approach.
