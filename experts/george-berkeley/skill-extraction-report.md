## Extracted Skills

### 1. master-argument

**Description:** Demonstrates that a concept is self-contradictory by showing that the very attempt to conceive of it already presupposes what it denies. Exposes hidden dependencies in claims of independence.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. abstraction-audit

**Description:** Tests whether a general or abstract concept is genuinely conceivable by attempting to form a concrete mental image of it. Exposes pseudo-concepts that exist only as words without coherent referents.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. quality-dissolution

**Description:** Challenges the distinction between 'objective' primary qualities and 'subjective' secondary qualities by showing that the same perceiver-dependence arguments apply to both. Dissolves artificial hierarchies between types of properties.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. perceptual-relativity

**Description:** Uses the variability of perception across observers, conditions, and contexts to argue that a quality belongs to the perceiving relationship rather than to the object alone. Reveals hidden observer-dependence.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. divine-perceiver-resolution

**Description:** Resolves continuity and coherence problems by positing a persistent, always-present ground of observation or framework. Generalizable as the 'background guarantor' pattern for any system requiring continuity beyond intermittent observers.

**Priority:** HIGH
