# Tim Robinson - References

## Primary Sources

### Television Series

**Saturday Night Live (2012-2016)**
- **NBC Archives**. SNL Season 38. Cast member/featured player (2012-2013).
- Staff writer (2013-2016) after transition from performer.
- Co-creator with Mikey Day and Streeter Seidell of various sketches.
- Many rejected sketches later appeared in I Think You Should Leave.

**Detroiters (2017-2018)**
- **Comedy Central**. 2 seasons, 20 episodes total.
- Co-created with Sam Richardson.
- Dir. various; Exec. Prod. Tim Robinson, Sam Richardson, Jason Sudeikis.
- Premiered: January 2017; Final episode: November 2018.
- Source for: Commercial parody development, warmth-based comedy, Detroit specificity.

**I Think You Should Leave with Tim Robinson (2019-2023)**
- **Netflix**. 3 seasons.
- Season 1: 6 episodes, premiered April 23, 2019.
- Season 2: 6 episodes, premiered July 6, 2021.
- Season 3: 6 episodes, premiered May 30, 2023.
- Co-created with Zach Kanin.
- Source for: All sketch analysis, escalation methodology, signature moments.

**The Chair Company (2025)**
- **HBO/Max**. Season 1: 8 episodes.
- Premiered: October 12, 2025.
- Final episode Season 1: November 30, 2025.
- Co-created with Zach Kanin.
- Executive Producers: Adam McKay, Andrew DeYoung.
- Dir. pilot: Andrew DeYoung.
- Cast: Tim Robinson, Lake Bell, Sophia Lillis, Will Price, Joseph Tudisco.
- Source for: Genre expansion, serialized format, critical/commercial success.
- Renewed for Season 2 (November 2025).

### Film

**Friendship (2024)**
- Dir. Andrew DeYoung. A24.
- Premiered: Toronto International Film Festival, September 8, 2024 (Midnight Madness).
- US Limited Release: May 9, 2025.
- US Wide Release: May 23, 2025.
- Cast: Tim Robinson, Paul Rudd, Kate Mara, Jack Dylan Grazer.
- Rotten Tomatoes: 88% (202 reviews).
- Source for: Feature-length cringe methodology, extended escalation.

### Stand-Up and Live Performance

**The Second City Detroit Touring Company** (Early 2000s)
- Second City archives confirm Robinson's touring company membership.

**The Second City Detroit Mainstage** (Mid-2000s)

The **HBO era** provides more transparent ratings data, confirming commercial breakthrough with The Chair Company.
