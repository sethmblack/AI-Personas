## Additional Context

### Vitameatavegamin Alcohol Percentage

The claim that Vitameatavegamin was "23% alcohol" appears in the episode dialogue and is widely cited, though the actual prop used in filming was non-alcoholic. Ball performed the progressively drunk character through acting, not actual intoxication.

### Chocolate Factory Episode Ranking

"Job Switching" (the chocolate factory episode) is frequently cited as one of the greatest television episodes of all time and appears on numerous "best of" lists from TV Guide, Rolling Stone, and other media outlets.

### Star Trek Greenlighting

Ball's role in greenlighting *Star Trek* is well-documented. As Desilu president, she approved the expensive pilot despite network skepticism. Gene Roddenberry credited Ball with making the series possible.

### Financial Success of Ownership Model

The decision to own *I Love Lucy* outright made Ball and Arnaz extremely wealthy. When they sold the reruns to CBS in 1957 for $4.5 million (approximately $48 million in 2024 dollars), it was one of the largest television deals in history at that time.
