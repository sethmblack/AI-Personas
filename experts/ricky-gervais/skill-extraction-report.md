## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Cringe Comedy Architect

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform comfortable dialogue or scenes into uncomfortable, cringe-inducing moments by applying Gervais's silence-holding technique. Add pauses, awkward beats, and extended uncomfortable moments that don't get resolved with jokes or cutaways.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Clear 7-step process documented in expertise (setup → first failure → doubling down → compounding failures → unbearable peak → held silence → aftermath)
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "add awkwardness," "make this uncomfortable," "Gervais-style cringe," "hold the silence"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - creating and structuring cringe comedy moments through silence and escalation
- ✅ **Reusable**: Applies to any dialogue, scene writing, comedy scripts, even presentation moments
- ✅ **Valuable**: Transforms comfortable, forgettable content into memorable, distinctive comedy that mirrors The Office innovation

**Input:** Comfortable dialogue or scene
**Output:** Scene restructured with cringe escalation pattern and uncomfortable silences that breathe

### 2. Mockumentary Framework Builder

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Restructure content to include documentary-style elements: talking head interviews, camera awareness, fourth-wall breaks, and the gap between characters' self-perception (interviews) and reality (observed behavior).

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Clear framework - add talking heads, insert Jim-looks to camera, structure interview/reality gaps
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "add documentary style," "mockumentary format," "break fourth wall," "Office-style structure"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - applying documentary format conventions to fictional content
- ✅ **Reusable**: Works for scripts, presentations, social media content, educational material
- ✅ **Valuable**: The Office proved this format revolutionizes comedy; makes characters' delusions transparent through documentary observation

**Input:** Linear narrative or dialogue
**Output:** Restructured with documentary elements - interviews, camera awareness, observational gaps

### 3. David Brent Character Psychology Generator

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Create characters using David Brent's psychological pattern: deep insecurity driving desperate need for approval, zero self-awareness about how they're perceived, every attempt to be liked making them more unlikeable.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Five-element psychological framework (deep insecurity → performance mode → zero self-awareness → self-justification loop → tragic humanity)
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "create delusional character," "Brent-style character," "self-sabotaging protagonist"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - character creation with specific psychological pattern
- ✅ **Reusable**: Applies to fiction writing, screenplay, comedy sketches, even understanding real-world behaviors
- ✅ **Valuable**: David Brent is one of comedy's most iconic characters; this psychology generates compelling, cringe-worthy protagonists

**Input:** Character concept or role
**Output:** Fully developed character with Brent psychological pattern and behavioral traits
