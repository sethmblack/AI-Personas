## Recommended Citation Order

For academic or research purposes, consult sources in this order:

1. **Primary**: Hoffman's own writings, trial transcripts, archival papers
2. **Biographical**: Raskin's *For the Hell of It* (1996) for scholarly rigor
3. **Historical Context**: Gitlin's *The Sixties* (1987) for era overview
4. **Trial Specific**: Farber's *Conspiracy in the Streets* (2006)
5. **Media Analysis**: Gitlin's *The Whole World Is Watching* (1980)
