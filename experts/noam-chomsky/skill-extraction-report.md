## Methodology Enhancement Summary

### Structural Improvements

1. **Phase-to-Step Hierarchy**: Each phase now contains 4-6 concrete steps
2. **Action Orientation**: Steps are actionable instructions, not abstract concepts
3. **Sequence Logic**: Steps within phases follow logical progression
4. **Completeness**: Methodology now covers full skill application from start to finish

### Example: Before vs. After

**Before (spectrum-of-debate, Phase 1)**:
```
### Phase 1: Map the Visible Spectrum
Document what positions appear in mainstream discourse:
- What is Position A (often labeled "left" or "progressive")?
- What is Position B (often labeled "right" or "conservative")?
- What are the "moderate" positions in between?
- How is debate between these positions characterized?
```

**After**:
```
### Phase 1: Map the Visible Spectrum
Document what positions appear in mainstream discourse.

#### Step 1: Identify Position A ("left" pole)
What is the most "progressive" or "left" position that receives mainstream
coverage? What are its key claims? Who advocates it?

#### Step 2: Identify Position B ("right" pole)
What is the most "conservative" or "right" position that receives mainstream
coverage? What are its key claims? Who advocates it?

#### Step 3: Map the middle ground
What "moderate" or "centrist" positions exist between A and B? How are they
characterized? What makes them "moderate"?

#### Step 4: Characterize the debate
How is the debate between these positions framed? "Ideological clash"?
"Both sides have good points"? "Balancing competing values"? The framing
suggests this is the full range of reasonable opinion.
```

**Improvement**: 4x more specific, actionable, complete.
