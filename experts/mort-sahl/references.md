## Notes on Discrepancies

### Initial Salary at hungry i
**Claim:** "By end of first year earning $3,000/week"
**Issue:** Recent search found he initially made $75/week opening for Stan Wilson
**Resolution:** The $3,000/week figure likely refers to a later period after he became headliner. Expertise.md should clarify timeline: started at $75/week, rose to $3,000/week by end of first year as he became popular.

### Income Drop Figure
**Claim:** "Income dropped to $13,000 by 1964"
**Variation:** Some sources say $19,000
**Resolution:** Both figures appear in sources. Using $13,000 as it's more commonly cited, but noting variation exists.
