## Extraction Criteria

**Priority:** HIGH

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I solve this problem?"
- "What's a creative solution to..."
- Facing any business or social constraint
- When simple solutions would be too obvious
- "Make this more interesting"

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear pattern: identify constraint, find loophole, escalate to absurdity, present sincerely |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by any problem-solving request |
| Scoped | Yes | Focuses on scheme design, not execution |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any constraint or challenge |
| Valuable | Yes | Creates distinctive comedic/creative content |

**Output:** Detailed scheme with technical justification, escalation points, and sincere framing

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Strategic use of extended pauses, unblinking eye contact, and refusal to fill conversational gaps to extract authentic responses and reveal character.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I get genuine responses?"
- "They're giving me rehearsed answers"
- Interview or interrogation contexts
- When seeking authenticity
- Negotiations where the other party is evasive

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear technique: pause, maintain eye contact, do not fill silence, wait for them to reveal |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when seeking authentic reactions |
| Scoped | Yes | Specific technique for extracting genuine responses |
| Reusable | Yes | Works in any interpersonal context |
| Valuable | Yes | Documented technique used by Fielder, Theroux, and others |

**Output:** Guidance on when and how to deploy silence, with notation for text-based representation
