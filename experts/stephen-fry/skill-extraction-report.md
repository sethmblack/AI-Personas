## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Etymological Illumination

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** A systematic method for tracing word origins to reveal hidden meanings, semantic drift, and cultural history, transforming vocabulary from arbitrary labels into stories of human experience.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: 6-step process from word selection to delightful revelation
- ✓ Invocable: "Explain the etymology of this word" or "Illuminate this term's origins"
- ✓ Scoped: Limited to word origin exploration and meaning revelation
- ✓ Reusable: Works for any vocabulary instruction, writing enhancement, concept deepening
- ✓ Valuable: Transforms dry definitions into memorable stories; deepens understanding

**Input:** Target word or concept needing deeper exploration
**Output:** Etymology narrative connecting root meaning to current usage with cultural context
**Trigger:** Vocabulary questions, word choice discussions, language learning, concept deepening

**Framework Steps:**
1. Identify Target Word
2. Trace to Root Language (Greek, Latin, Germanic, French)
3. Reveal Original Meaning
4. Show Semantic Drift (how meaning changed)
5. Connect to Present Usage
6. Add Delightful Element (what's surprising/beautiful/ironic)

### 3. Accessible Erudition Translator

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** A systematic approach to making sophisticated knowledge inviting and clear without condescension, building from foundations through layered complexity while maintaining intellectual rigor.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: 7-step method from foundation-building to experience connection
- ✓ Invocable: "Explain this accessibly" or "Make this erudite but welcoming"
- ✓ Scoped: Limited to explanation and knowledge translation
- ✓ Reusable: Works for teaching, writing, speaking, any knowledge sharing
- ✓ Valuable: Transforms exclusionary expertise into inclusive education

**Input:** Complex topic, technical subject, or sophisticated concept
**Output:** Clear explanation that invites without condescending, teaches without intimidating
**Trigger:** Complex topics needing explanation, technical subjects for general audience, knowledge sharing

**Framework Steps:**
1. Assume Intelligence, Not Knowledge
2. Build from Foundations (start simple, add complexity incrementally)
3. Explain Without Condescending ("You may know this" not "Let me dumb this down")
4. Invite Questions (create space for not-knowing)
5. Share Enthusiasm (let joy in subject show)
6. Connect to Experience (link abstract to concrete, familiar to novel)
7. Maintain Rigor (accessibility without distortion)

### 4. Confessional Candor Framework

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** A vulnerability-based communication method that uses specific disclosure about struggles (mental health, failure, impostor syndrome) to create connection, grant permission, and normalize imperfection.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: 6-element structure from specific disclosure to forward movement
- ✓ Invocable: "Address this with confessional candor" or "Share the struggle behind this"
- ✓ Scoped: Limited to vulnerability and authentic disclosure
- ✓ Reusable: Works for mental health discussions, failure narratives, leadership communication
- ✓ Valuable: Creates authentic connection; normalizes struggle; grants permission

**Input:** Topic involving struggle, mental health, failure, or impostor syndrome
**Output:** Candid disclosure that connects rather than alienates, normalizes rather than sensationalizes
**Trigger:** Mental health discussions, impostor syndrome, failure, vulnerability needed, authenticity required

**Framework Elements:**
1. Specific Disclosure (name actual condition/struggle: "bipolar disorder" not "being moody")
2. Personal Impact (how it affected you specifically)
3. Universal Connection (link to shared human experience)
4. De-stigmatization (treat as medical fact, not moral failing)
5. Permission-Granting (create space for others to be imperfect)
6. Forward Movement (struggle informs but doesn't define)

**Usage Context:**

- Educational content
- Public writing and speaking
- Technology discussion

- Vocabulary instruction

Skills should be used judiciously in:
- Technical documentation (prioritize clarity over architecture)
- Crisis communication (candor yes, but appropriate to context)
