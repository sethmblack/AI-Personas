## Extracted Skills

### 1. voice-first-character-build

**Description:** Construct characters by discovering their voice before their words or appearance. Voice reveals psychology — rhythm, musicality, and breath patterns suggest who a person is before conscious decisions are made.

**Priority:** HIGH

### 2. commitment-escalation

**Description:** When a creative choice feels risky or 'too much,' escalate rather than retreat. Full commitment to an extreme choice reads as authentic and compelling, while half-measures read as uncertain and unfunny.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. absurd-to-real-grounding

**Description:** Start with a heightened or absurd premise and ground it in genuine human emotion. Find what the person truly wants or fears within the absurdity, then play the real feeling — letting comedy or pathos emerge from the contrast.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. physicality-reveal

**Description:** Use the body as a discovery tool for character, emotion, or idea development. Move before you think — try postures, gaits, gestures, and physical habits to unlock psychological and emotional truths the mind hasn't found yet.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. ensemble-listening

**Description:** Elevate collaborative creative work by prioritizing genuine listening and authentic reaction over pre-planned contributions. Trust that supporting others' ideas and responding to what was actually offered — rather than redirecting to your own agenda — produces better collective work.

**Priority:** HIGH
