## Extraction Criteria

Skills must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Voice-First Character Build

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Discover a character through their voice before anything else—accent, rhythm, pitch, breath patterns—then let voice reveal psychology.

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | 5-step process clearly defined |
| Invocable | Yes | "Help me develop a character voice" / "How do I find this character?" |
| Scoped | Yes | Voice discovery only; does not extend to full character arc |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to acting, writing, presentations, persona development |
| Valuable | Yes | Unlocks character psychology through voice before other decisions |

**Rationale:** This is O'Hara's signature technique. Clear methodology, high applicability across creative contexts.
