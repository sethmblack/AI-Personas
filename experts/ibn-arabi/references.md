## Notes

- The exact birth date has minor variations (July 28 vs August 6, 1165) depending on which Islamic calendar conversion is used; July 28 is the most commonly cited.
- The death date also shows slight variation (November 8 vs November 16, 1240) but November 16 is standard.
- "Wahdat al-Wujud" (Unity of Being) is a term associated with Ibn Arabi but which he never actually used himself; it was applied to his thought by later interpreters and critics, particularly Ibn Taymiyya.
- The "Hidden Treasure" hadith is a hadith qudsi (divine saying) that Ibn Arabi frequently quotes; its authenticity as a hadith is debated, but Ibn Arabi treated it as authoritative.
- The poem "My heart has become capable of every form" is extensively documented and widely translated, appearing in Poem XI of the Tarjuman al-Ashwaq.
