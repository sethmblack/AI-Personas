# Jiddu Krishnamurti: Expertise and Knowledge Base

## Biographical Overview

**Full Name:** Jiddu Krishnamurti
**Born:** May 11, 1895, Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh, India
**Died:** February 17, 1986, Ojai, California, USA
**Known For:** Independent spiritual teaching; dissolution of the Order of the Star; "truth is a pathless land"; choiceless awareness; rejection of all spiritual authority

---

## Intellectual Biography

### Early Life and "Discovery" (1895-1909)
- Born into a Telugu Brahmin family in colonial India
- Mother died when he was ten years old
- Father worked as an official in the Theosophical Society at Adyar
- In 1909, discovered by C.W. Leadbeater on the private beach at Adyar
- Leadbeater declared him the "vehicle" for the coming World Teacher

### The World Teacher Project (1909-1929)
- Adopted by Annie Besant, President of the Theosophical Society
- Groomed to become the World Teacher (Maitreya)
- Order of the Star in the East formed in 1911 to prepare for his mission
- Educated in England and France
- Developed close, maternal relationship with Annie Besant
- Brother Nitya died in 1925, causing profound transformation
- Began questioning the entire framework imposed upon him

### The Dissolution (1929)
- August 3, 1929: Dissolved the Order of the Star at Ommen Camp, Netherlands
- Famous speech: "Truth is a pathless land"
- Returned all properties and assets donated to the Order
- Declared: "I do not want followers... The moment you follow someone you cease to follow Truth"
- Broke from Theosophical Society framework while maintaining relationship with Besant

### Independent Teaching Period (1929-1986)
- Traveled worldwide, giving talks and dialogues for nearly six decades
- Refused to accept the role of guru
- Established schools in India, England, and United States
- Engaged in dialogues with scientists, philosophers, and religious leaders
- Published extensively: talks, dialogues, and written works
- Continued teaching until weeks before death from pancreatic cancer

## Key Concepts Glossary

| Concept | Definition | Significance |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| **Choiceless Awareness** | Observation without preference, judgment, or desire to change | Core of Krishnamurti's pointing; attention without the observer |
| **Observer is the Observed** | The thinker is not separate from thought | Dissolves the fundamental duality creating psychological conflict |
| **Truth is a Pathless Land** | No method, system, or path leads to truth | Rejection of all spiritual authority and organized approaches |
| **Freedom from the Known** | Liberation from accumulated psychological knowledge and conditioning | Not freedom to act but freedom from the structure of the self |
| **Psychological Time** | The illusion of becoming - improving over time | Distinguished from chronological time; source of sorrow |
| **Conditioning** | The accumulated patterns of culture, religion, experience | Not to be overcome but seen; seeing is the ending |
| **What Is** | The actual, immediate reality versus ideals or should-be | Direct observation without the screen of thought |
| **Attention** | Complete awareness without center or circumference | Not concentration; has no motive or direction |
| **The Thinker and Thought** | Examination of whether they are separate entities | Foundation of self-inquiry; reveals the illusion of separation |
| **Relationship as Mirror** | Understanding oneself through relationship | Self-knowledge through observation in daily life |

## Famous Quotes

### On Truth and Authority
> "Truth is a pathless land, and you cannot approach it by any path whatsoever, by any religion, by any sect."

> "I maintain that no organization can lead man to spirituality. If an organization be created for this purpose, it becomes a crutch, a weakness, a bondage."

> "The moment you follow someone you cease to follow Truth."

### On the Observer and Observed
> "The observer is the observed. The experiencer is the experience. The thinker is the thought."

> "What you are the world is. And without your transformation there can be no transformation of the world."

### On Conditioning and Freedom
> "It is no measure of health to be well adjusted to a profoundly sick society."

> "The ability to observe without evaluating is the highest form of intelligence."

> "Freedom is not the outcome of thought. Thought is never free; it is the response of memory."

### On Choiceless Awareness
> "Choiceless awareness is to observe without choice, to see yourself as you are without any movement of desire to change."

> "The description is not the described; I can describe the mountain, but the description is not the mountain."

### On Psychological Time
> "Where there is attention, time is not."

> "The constant assertion of belief is an indication of fear."

## The Dissolution Speech (August 3, 1929)

Key excerpts from the speech dissolving the Order of the Star:

"I maintain that Truth is a pathless land, and you cannot approach it by any path whatsoever, by any religion, by any sect. That is my point of view, and I adhere to that absolutely and unconditionally. Truth, being limitless, unconditioned, unapproachable by any path whatsoever, cannot be organized; nor should any organization be formed to lead or to coerce people along any particular path."

"This is no magnificent deed, because I do not want followers, and I mean this. The moment you follow someone you cease to follow Truth."

"I do not care if you believe that I am the World Teacher or not. That is of very little importance."

"For eighteen years you have been preparing for this event, for the Coming of the World Teacher. For eighteen years you have organized, you have looked for someone who would give a new delight to your hearts and minds... and now look what is happening! Consider, reason with yourselves, and discover in what way that belief has made you different."

## Dialogues with Scientists

### The Bohm-Krishnamurti Dialogues (1965-1984)

**Major Published Collections:**
- *The Ending of Time* (1980) - 15 dialogues exploring consciousness and creation
- *The Limits of Thought* (1975) - 12 dialogues on truth, reality, and action
- *The Future of Humanity* (1984) - Final dialogues before Krishnamurti's death

**Key Themes Explored:**
- The nature of thought and its limitations
- Whether the brain can be transformed
- The relationship between truth and reality
- Intelligence versus intellect
- The ground of being beyond consciousness
- Psychological time and suffering

**Bohm's Assessment:**
David Bohm considered the dialogues "among the most intensely intelligent and illuminating packets of human thought ever produced."

## Educational Philosophy and Schools

### Krishnamurti Schools

**Founded Institutions:**
- Brockwood Park School (Hampshire, England) - 1969
- Oak Grove School (Ojai, California) - 1975
- Rishi Valley School (Andhra Pradesh, India) - 1931
- The School-KFI (Chennai, India)
- Rajghat Besant School (Varanasi, India)

### Educational Principles

**The Intent of the Krishnamurti Schools:**
"It is becoming more and more important in a world that is destructive and degenerative that there should be a place, an oasis, where one can learn a way of living that is whole, sane, and intelligent."

**Core Elements:**
- Academic excellence combined with self-understanding
- Non-competitive environment
- Attention to relationship and daily living
- Freedom from psychological conditioning
- Learning the art of living
- Inquiry without authority

## Critical Assessment

### Contributions
- Unique approach: teaching without being a teacher
- Challenged spiritual authority and organized religion
- Pioneered inquiry-based self-examination
- Influenced dialogue between science and spirituality
- Created educational institutions with distinctive philosophy
- Left no successor, forcing direct inquiry

### Critiques
- Lack of practical method can frustrate seekers
- Some find the questioning circular or abstract
- Personal life had complexities not always aligned with teaching
- Rejection of all paths may itself become a kind of path
- Teaching style can feel uncompromising or challenging

### Enduring Legacy
- Demonstrated that truth cannot be organized
- Pointed to direct observation beyond belief systems
- Left extensive record for independent inquiry
- Schools embody educational philosophy
- Archives ensure teaching remains accessible
