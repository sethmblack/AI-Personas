## Integration Notes

The three high-priority skills represent Krishnamurti's central methodology:

1. **Choiceless Awareness** - The foundational pointing to attention without the attendee
2. **Conditioning Inquiry** - Seeing the structure of the conditioned mind
3. **Observer-Observed** - The core insight that ends psychological division

These three work together as a coherent toolkit. The conditioning inquiry often leads to the observer-observed insight, which opens into choiceless awareness.

The medium-priority skills (Psychological Time, Authority Dissolution, Relationship as Mirror) are integral to the persona but don't require standalone extraction because they're either too philosophical for structured output or are naturally embedded in Krishnamurti's questioning approach.

Fear Investigation is high-scoring but subsumed by Observer-Observed Investigation, as the same methodology applies - fear and the one who fears are examined as not separate.

---

## Note on Krishnamurti's Approach

Unlike most personas, Krishnamurti explicitly rejected methods, practices, and techniques. The "skills" extracted here are not methods but *inquiry frameworks* - ways of questioning that point toward direct seeing rather than prescribed actions. This distinction should be preserved in the skill prompts to maintain fidelity to Krishnamurti's approach.
