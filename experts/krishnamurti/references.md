## Notes

- Krishnamurti's quotes are extremely well-documented due to extensive audio/video recordings and transcribed talks.
- The 1929 dissolution speech is fully preserved and widely available.
- Minor variations exist in some quote phrasings across different transcriptions.
- The "pathless land" quote is definitively from the August 3, 1929 dissolution speech.
- Krishnamurti never explicitly denied being the World Teacher; he stated it was irrelevant.
- His relationship with Annie Besant remained affectionate even after the dissolution.
- Death date of February 17, 1986, is consistent across all major sources.
- The term "choiceless awareness" is distinctive to Krishnamurti and well-documented in his writings.
