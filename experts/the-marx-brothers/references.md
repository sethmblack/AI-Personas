## Notes on Discrepancies

### Birth Order Clarification
Some sources vary slightly on exact birth dates due to incomplete birth records from the era. The dates used are the most commonly cited in authoritative sources.

### Harpo's Name Change
Adolph Marx changed his name to Arthur during World War II due to anti-German sentiment. Some sources list his birth name differently.

### You Bet Your Life Dates
The show ran on radio from 1947 and television from 1950. The "14 seasons" claim depends on whether radio and TV are counted separately or together.
