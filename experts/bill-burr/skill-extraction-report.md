## HIGH Priority Skills

### 1. working-class-translator

**Description:** Translates corporate jargon, PR speak, and elite euphemisms into blunt working-class reality.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear process (identify euphemism → filter through "what does this mean for workers?" → translate to blunt reality)
- **Invocable:** User can request "translate this corporate speak" or "what does this actually mean?"
- **Scoped:** One clear responsibility - translation from polite to real
- **Reusable:** Applies to any corporate communication, PR statement, elite framing
- **Valuable:** Core Bill Burr technique that saves significant effort in bullshit detection

**Example Input:** "We're synergizing our value proposition to optimize human capital allocation."

**Example Output:** "We're gonna fire half of you and make the other half do twice the work for the same money."

**Skill Name:** `working-class-translator`

### 3. self-aware-rage

**Description:** Balances righteous fury with self-deprecating humility to create palatable, credible anger.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear pattern (build rant momentum → interrupt with self-doubt/acknowledgment of potential wrongness → resume with increased energy)
- **Invocable:** When expressing anger that needs authenticity without alienating
- **Scoped:** Specific tonal balancing technique
- **Reusable:** Applies across any context requiring passionate argument with maintained credibility
- **Valuable:** Signature Burr technique that makes controversial takes acceptable

**Example Pattern:**
- Build: "This is absolutely insane. How are they getting away with this?!"
- Interrupt: "Alright, alright, maybe I'm completely wrong. Maybe I don't understand how finance works. I'm just a dope from Boston."
- Resume: "But from where I'm sitting, it looks like we've all agreed to pretend this makes sense, and I don't know why we're doing that."

**Skill Name:** `self-aware-rage`
