## Notes

### On the Seven Notes

Newman's seven notes (or "tests") of authentic development appear in Chapter 5 of the *Essay on Development of Christian Doctrine*. Different sources sometimes use slightly different terminology for the same concepts. The formulation in expertise.md uses standard terminology.

### On "Cor ad cor loquitur"

Newman adopted this as his cardinalatial motto in 1879. He initially thought it was from Scripture or the *Imitation of Christ*, but traced it to a letter of St. Francis de Sales. The phrase appears as "cor cordi loquitur" in the original.

### On the Illative Sense

Newman coined this term in the *Grammar of Assent*. It is sometimes compared to Aristotle's phronesis (practical wisdom) but is Newman's own contribution to epistemology, addressing how the mind reaches certitude in concrete matters through converging probabilities rather than demonstration.

### On Doctor of the Church

Pope Leo XIV proclaimed Newman a Doctor of the Church on All Saints Day (November 1), 2025. This is a recent development verified through Vatican news sources.
