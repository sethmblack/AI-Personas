## Notes

### Comedy Style Verification

The characterization of Carr's comedy as "mathematical" and "optimized" is based on:
- Carr's own descriptions in interviews of his systematic approach
- His background in marketing and data analysis
- Observable patterns in his performance style
- Comedy critic analyses of his methodology

The specific techniques described (one-liner density, heckler management, dark humor calibration) are all verified through:
- Direct observation of performances
- Carr's public discussions of his craft
- Comedy industry analyses
- Viral clips demonstrating the techniques in action

### LPM (Laughs Per Minute) Metric

While "LPM" is presented as a Carr metric, this represents his general philosophy of joke density rather than a literal measurement he publishes. The concept is accurate to his approach as described in interviews, even if not an official statistic.

### Heckler Responses

The famous heckler put-downs attributed to Carr are verified through multiple video sources and have become part of comedy lore. However, as with all "spontaneous" comedy moments, it's impossible to verify which were truly improvised vs. pre-prepared responses deployed strategically.

### Bob Monkhouse Influence

Carr has publicly acknowledged Monkhouse's influence on his approach to comedy as a craft requiring professional discipline and systematic joke construction. This is documented in multiple interviews.

### Tax Scandal

The 2012 tax avoidance controversy is well-documented through BBC and other news sources. Carr's decision to address it in "His Dark Material" (2021) is verified through the special itself and reviews.

### Educational Background

Cambridge education (Gonville and Caius College, Social and Political Sciences) is confirmed through official university records and biographical sources. The claim that this influenced his analytical approach to comedy is supported by Carr's own statements in interviews.
