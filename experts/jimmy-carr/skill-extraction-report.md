### 1. one-liner-construction

**Source Pattern:** The Carr One-Liner Formula / Comedy as Mathematics / The Efficiency Principle
**Purpose:** Engineer efficient one-liner jokes using mathematical comedy structure with optimal setup-punchline ratios.
**Trigger:** "Help me write a joke" or "Make this funny" or "How do I punch this up?"
**Inputs:** Topic or premise, target audience, tone constraints (clean vs. dark), desired impact
**Outputs:** Multiple one-liner variations, setup-punchline analysis, rule-of-three structures, tag options, timing notes
**Reasoning:** Carr's joke construction methodology is highly systematic and actionable. The setup-punchline formula, rule of three, and misdirection techniques are reproducible. Applies to any situation requiring concise humor: presentations, social media, speeches, marketing copy.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Clear steps: 6-8 word setup, identify false expectation, pivot 90 degrees, land clean
- Invocable: YES - Natural triggers around making content funnier or writing jokes
- Scoped: YES - Single responsibility: one-liner joke construction
- Reusable: YES - Presentations, speeches, social media, marketing, conversation
- Valuable: YES - Transforms weak jokes into sharp ones, teaches comedy structure

### 2. heckler-destruction

**Source Pattern:** The Heckler Destruction Algorithm / Heckler Destruction: Advanced Techniques / The Graduated Response Model
**Purpose:** Deploy systematic response to interruptions, hecklers, and chaos in live performance or presentation settings.
**Trigger:** "Someone interrupted me" or "How do I handle disruption?" or "They're stealing my spotlight"
**Inputs:** Interruption type, heckler intent, audience dynamics, professional context, desired force level
**Outputs:** Graduated response options (levels 1-4), pre-prepared arsenals for common types, recovery scripts, timing recommendations
**Reasoning:** Carr's heckler management system is one of his most distinctive methodologies. The five-step algorithm (acknowledge, assess, calibrate, destroy, move on) is immediately actionable. The graduated response model provides clear options. Valuable beyond comedy: handling meeting disruptions, Q&A management, hostile questions.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Five-step algorithm with clear decision points
- Invocable: YES - Disruption and interruption scenarios
- Scoped: YES - Managing interruptions and maintaining control
- Reusable: YES - Comedy, presentations, meetings, Q&A sessions, teaching
- Valuable: YES - Converts chaos into control, provides confidence under pressure

**Score: 48/50** | **Priority: HIGH**

### 3. dark-humor-calibration

**Source Pattern:** Dark Humor Methodology / The Permission Structure / The Permission Principle
**Purpose:** Assess risk/reward of controversial material and build permission structures to successfully deploy dark or edgy content.
**Trigger:** "Is this too dark?" or "Can I say this?" or "How far can I go?"
**Inputs:** Material content, audience composition, context (corporate vs. comedy club), risk tolerance, trust level with audience
**Outputs:** Risk assessment, permission-building strategy, escalation ladder, release mechanisms, ownership framework
**Reasoning:** Carr's permission structure for dark humor is sophisticated and transferable. The five-step process (signal early, build trust, escalate gradually, own completely, provide release) is actionable beyond comedy. Applies to any situation requiring navigation of controversial or sensitive topics: difficult conversations, policy changes, challenging feedback.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Five-step permission structure with clear progression
- Invocable: YES - Questions about controversial or edgy content
- Scoped: YES - Managing controversial material and audience permission
- Reusable: YES - Comedy, presentations, difficult conversations, sensitive topics
- Valuable: YES - Enables effective handling of taboo subjects without backlash

**Score: 47/50** | **Priority: HIGH**

### 4. joke-optimization

**Source Pattern:** Material Development Process / The Optimization Principle / Testing Cycle
**Purpose:** Debug joke structure, timing, and efficiency using LPM (Laughs Per Minute) analysis and systematic testing.
**Trigger:** "This joke isn't landing" or "How do I make this funnier?" or "Why isn't this working?"
**Inputs:** Current joke version, performance data (where it succeeded/failed), audience type, delivery notes
**Outputs:** Structural diagnosis, word-by-word editing recommendations, timing adjustments, angle variations, test-or-kill decision
**Reasoning:** Carr's optimization methodology is pure engineering: test, measure, refine, lock or kill. The systematic approach (test 3 times, track results, edit ruthlessly) is reproducible. Valuable for anyone using humor regularly: presenters, content creators, marketers.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Clear testing cycle with specific criteria
- Invocable: YES - Joke failure or improvement requests
- Scoped: YES - Joke refinement and optimization
- Reusable: YES - Stand-up, presentations, content creation, marketing
- Valuable: YES - Saves time by killing bad jokes early, perfects good ones

**Score: 46/50** | **Priority: HIGH**
