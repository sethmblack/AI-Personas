# Robert Anton Wilson - Reference Document

## Biographical Facts

### Birth and Early Life
- **Full Name**: Robert Edward Wilson (later adopted "Anton" as middle name)
- **Born**: January 18, 1932, Methodist Hospital, Brooklyn, New York
- **Died**: January 11, 2007, Capitola, California (one week before his 75th birthday)
- **Heritage**: Irish Catholic
- **Parents**: John Joseph Wilson (longshoreman) and Elizabeth "Milli" Wilson
- **Early Residences**: Flatbush, Brooklyn; moved to Gerritsen Beach around age 4-5; South Slope section of Brooklyn at age 13

### Health
- Contracted polio as a child; treated with the Kenny Method (developed by Elizabeth Kenny), which the AMA repudiated at the time
- Effects of polio remained throughout life, causing minor muscle spasms and occasional cane use
- In 2000, experienced major bout of post-polio syndrome that continued until death
- A fall in mid-2006 left him bedridden for his final seven months under home hospice care

### Education
- Catholic grammar schools in Brooklyn
- Brooklyn Technical High School (selective admission)
- Brooklyn Polytechnic Institute (1952-1957): studied electrical engineering and mathematics intermittently; did not complete degree
- New York University (1957-1958): English education undergraduate program; did not complete degree
- Paideia University: Completed doctoral thesis titled "The Evolution of Neuro-Sociological Circuits: A Contribution to the Sociobiology of Consciousness" (1979), which became the basis for *Prometheus Rising*

### Family
- **Wife**: Arlen Riley Wilson (married 1958; she died May 22, 1999, following a series of strokes)
- **Children**: Four, including Christina Wilson Pearson and Patricia Luna Wilson
- **Daughter Luna's Death**: On October 2, 1976, Luna (age 15) was murdered during a robbery at the clothing store where she worked on Telegraph Avenue in Berkeley. She was struck repeatedly with a credit card machine. Her brain was cryopreserved by the Bay Area Cryonics Society (later American Cryonics Society) - she was their first preservation case.

## Playboy Years (1965-1971)

### Position and Role
- **Title**: Associate Editor at Playboy magazine, Chicago, Illinois
- **Duration**: 1965 (or 1966, sources vary slightly) to 1971
- **Key Responsibility**: Co-edited the Playboy Forum (letters section responding to Hugh Hefner's "Playboy Philosophy" editorial column) with Robert Shea

### Prior Editorial Work
- Co-editorship of School for Living's *Balanced Living* magazine in Brookville, Ohio (starting 1962)
- Associate editor at Ralph Ginzburg's quarterly magazine *fact:* in New York
- 1965 article on Timothy Leary in *The Realist* reportedly facilitated his hiring at Playboy

### Significance
- The Playboy Forum received many letters containing conspiracy theories; these inspired *The Illuminatus! Trilogy*
- Met co-author Robert Shea at Playboy
- Wilson's own words on leaving: "Playboy paid me a higher salary than any other magazine at which I had worked and never expected me to become a conformist or sell my soul in return. I enjoyed my years in the Bunny Empire. I only resigned when I reached 40 and felt I could not live with myself if I didn't make an effort to write full-time at last."

## The Illuminatus! Trilogy (1975)

### Publication Details
- **Authors**: Robert Shea and Robert Anton Wilson
- **Written**: 1969-1971
- **First Published**: September 1975 by Dell Publishing
- **Original Format**: Three separate paperback volumes
  1. *The Eye in the Pyramid*
  2. *The Golden Apple*
  3. *Leviathan*
- **Cover Art**: Carlos Victor Ochagavia
- **Omnibus Edition**: 1984 (most common format since)

### Editorial History
- The authors conceived it as a single continuous volume; the three-part division was a commercial decision by Dell
- Publishers required cutting 500 pages to reduce printing costs on what was considered a risky venture
- Wilson stated most ideas from the cut material made it into his later works
- Took several years to find a publisher due to the unusual nature of the work

### Content and Themes
- Described as "a fairy tale for paranoids"
- Satirical examination of conspiracy theories, occult symbolism, secret societies, and 1960s counterculture
- Quotes the *Principia Discordia* extensively
- Characters Malaclypse the Younger and other Discordian figures appear
- Mixes true information with fiction ("guerrilla ontology")

### Recognition
- **1986**: Prometheus Hall of Fame Award (honoring classic Libertarian fiction)
- Became a cult classic but did not achieve large mainstream sales
- Stage adaptation premiered November 23, 1976, in Liverpool, England (directed by Ken Campbell)

## Discordianism

### Wilson's Role
- Recognized within Discordianism as an Episkopos, Pope, and Saint
- Helped publicize Discordianism through writings and interviews
- Wrote introduction to the 1979 Loompanics Unlimited edition of the *Principia Discordia*

### Principia Discordia
- **Primary Authors**: Greg Hill (Malaclypse the Younger) and Kerry Wendell Thornley (Lord Omar Khayyam Ravenhurst)
- **Origins**: Grew from late-night bowling alley discussions between Hill and Thornley in late 1950s California
- **First Edition**: 1963, printed using Jim Garrison's Xerox printer
- **Second Edition**: 1965, titled "Principia Discordia or How The West Was Lost," limited to five copies
- **1979 Loompanics Edition**: Added Wilson's introduction and afterword by Malaclypse the Younger

### Malaclypse the Younger (Greg Hill)
- **Born**: May 21, 1941
- **Died**: July 20, 2000
- Worked for Western Union in Whittier, California
- Began developing Discordianism with Thornley around 1958-1959
- During early circulation of the *Principia Discordia*, rumors claimed the author was Richard Nixon, Timothy Leary, or Robert Anton Wilson

### Kerry Thornley
- Co-founder of Discordianism with Greg Hill
- Had served in the military with Lee Harvey Oswald; wrote a novel about Oswald before the JFK assassination
- Collaborated with Wilson on Operation Mindfuck
- Also wrote *Zenarchy*, combining Zen philosophy with anarchism

### Core Discordian Concepts
- Worship of Eris, Greek goddess of Chaos and Discord
- Described as "a Marx Brothers version of Zen"
- Purpose: to "pull the rug from under people's belief systems with paradoxical thinking aimed at highlighting how much of what we perceive to be 'reality' is actually a projection from our own minds"

## Key Concepts

### Reality Tunnels
- **Origin**: Term coined by Timothy Leary; expanded extensively by Wilson in *Prometheus Rising* (1983)
- **Definition**: The theory that every individual interprets the world differently through a subconscious set of mental filters formed from beliefs and experiences
- **Wilson's term**: "Neurological relativism" - each individual's unique neurological system enables them to perceive reality in their own way
- **Key insight**: Does not necessarily deny objective truth exists, but emphasizes that our access to it is mediated through senses, experience, conditioning, prior beliefs, and other non-objective factors
- **Practical application**: Wilson believed one could break down old reality tunnels and create new ones through techniques including neuro-linguistic programming, meditation, controlled use of hallucinogens, hypnosis, biofeedback, and "forcibly acting out other reality tunnels"

### Maybe Logic
- **Core principle**: Generalized agnosticism - not just about God, but about everything
- **Wilson's stated goal**: "To try to get people into a state of generalized agnosticism, not agnosticism about God alone but agnosticism about everything"
- **Documentary**: *Maybe Logic: The Lives and Ideas of Robert Anton Wilson* (2003), featuring 25+ years of Wilson footage, released on DVD May 30, 2006
- **Maybe Logic Academy**: Educational institution inspired by Wilson's approach

### Guerrilla Ontology
- **Definition** (Wilson's own): "An approach adopted in my novels that mixes the elements of each book so that the reader must decide on each page, 'How much of this is real and how much is a put on?'"
- **Purpose**: To shake up certainty; force readers to see things through different reality grids rather than a single grid
- **Broader meaning**: A form of philosophical warfare attempting to undermine authority by subverting symbols, signs, and semiotic systems
- **Techniques**: Culture jamming, reality-hacking, subvertising, meme-magic
- **Wilson quote**: "The Western World has been brainwashed by Aristotle for the last 2,500 years. The unconscious, not quite articulate, belief of most Occidentals is that there is one map which adequately represents reality. By sheer good luck, every Occidental thinks he or she has the map that fits. Guerrilla ontology, to me, involves shaking up that certainty."

### Chapel Perilous
- **Origin**: Term from Arthurian legend (Sir Thomas Malory's *Le Morte d'Arthur*); first used as occult term by Wilson in *Cosmic Trigger*
- **Wilson's definitions**:
  - "A stage in the magickal quest in which your maps turned out to be totally inadequate for the territory and you're completely lost."
  - "In researching occult conspiracies, one eventually faces a crossroad of mythic proportions (called Chapel Perilous in the trade). You come out the other side either stone paranoid or an agnostic; there is no third way."
  - "Chapel Perilous, like the mysterious entity called 'I,' cannot be located in the space-time continuum; it is weightless, odorless, tasteless and undetectable by ordinary instruments. Indeed, like the Ego, it is even possible to deny that it is there. And yet, even more like the Ego, once you are inside it, there doesn't seem to be any way to ever get out again, until you suddenly discover that it has been brought into existence by thought and does not exist outside thought."
- **Psychological state**: Uncertainty about whether events were affected by supernatural forces or are products of imagination

### E-Prime
- **Origin**: Addition to Alfred Korzybski's General Semantics
- **Premise**: Most usages of the verb "to be" lead to inaccurate communication because they imply something absolute or objective when the speaker is merely detailing their opinion
- **Wilson's quote on "is"**: "'Is,' 'is,' 'is'—the idiocy of the word haunts me. If it were abolished, human thought might begin to make sense. I don't know what anything 'is'; I only know how it seems to me at this moment." (from *Nature's God*)

## Cosmic Trigger Trilogy

### Overview
- Three-volume autobiographical and philosophical work
- Documents Wilson's process of "self-induced brain change" and vignettes from his earlier life

### Cosmic Trigger I: The Final Secret of the Illuminati (1977)
- Written after returning from the Liverpool *Illuminatus!* stage production
- Explores his experiences during and after writing *Illuminatus!*
- Documents his journey through Chapel Perilous
- Main discovery: "Reality" (though a noun, commonly conceptualized as a definite, unchanging "thing") is mutable and subjective
- Written partly as response to processing his daughter Luna's murder in October 1976

### Key Themes
- Brain-change experimentation
- Occult and mystical experiences
- The nature of reality and perception
- Personal transformation and loss

## Operation Mindfuck

### Origin
- Developed by Kerry Thornley and Robert Anton Wilson in 1968
- Named by Wilson and Robert Shea in *The Illuminatus! Trilogy*
- Connected to Thornley's growing paranoia related to the JFK assassination investigation

### Purpose and Methods
- Important practice in Discordian religion
- Goal: "Sow the culture with paranoia" by publicly attributing "all national calamities, assassinations, or conspiracies" to the Bavarian Illuminati
- Broader purpose: Undermine faith in government, authority, and consensus reality through pranks and confusion
- Described as "a Marx Brothers version of Zen" designed to mess with mainstream views of reality

### Tactics
- Civil disobedience
- Culture jamming
- Performance art
- Publishing fake letters (including conspiracy theories in Playboy Forum)
- Subtle challenges to reality that create uncertainty and paranoia

### Specific Target
- The Garrison Inquiry into the JFK assassination
- Thornley had met Lee Harvey Oswald during military service
- Thornley had written a novel about Oswald before the assassination
- When Thornley learned members of Garrison's investigation team believed in the "Illuminati," he encouraged Wilson and Shea to "feed the paranoia"

### Legacy
- Stuart Rathbone described it as "a deliberate campaign of revolutionary disinformation"
- Themes have had lasting impact on anarchism, pseudo-sciences, and mainstream culture

## Death and Final Blog Post

### Final Months
- June 22, 2006: Paul Krassner reported on The Huffington Post that Wilson was under hospice care at home
- Fall of 2006: Acute financial hardship - lacked sufficient funds to cover rent beyond one month
- October 2, 2006: Douglas Rushkoff reported Wilson's financial trouble; picked up by Slashdot, Boing Boing, and the Church of the SubGenius
- Friends established the "Friends of Robert Anton Wilson" relief fund
- October 5, 2006: Wilson posted response: "Dear Friends, my God, what can I say. I am dumbfounded, flabbergasted, and totally stunned by the charity and compassion that has poured in here the last three days."
- October 10, 2006: Fundraising succeeded beyond expectations, raising enough to support him for at least six months

### Final Blog Post
- **Title**: "Do Not Go Gently Into That Good Night" (referencing Dylan Thomas poem)
- **Date**: January 6, 2007 (five days before his death)
- **Famous line**: "Please pardon my levity, I don't see how to take death seriously. It seems absurd."

### Death and Memorial
- **Died**: January 11, 2007, at his home in Capitola, California
- **Cremation**: January 18, 2007 (his 75th birthday)
- **Memorial Service**: February 18, 2007
- **Ashes scattered**: Off the Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk in Santa Cruz, California (same location as his wife Arlen's ashes)

## The 8-Circuit Model of Consciousness

### Origins
- Originally developed by Timothy Leary in the late 1960s-early 1970s (with Brian Barritt)
- First appeared as seven circuits in Leary's *Neurologic* (1973)
- Eight-circuit model fully formulated in Leary's *Exo-Psychology* and Wilson's *Cosmic Trigger* (1977)
- Wilson befriended Leary in the early 1970s and contributed significantly to developing the model

### Wilson's Contributions
- Expanded the model in *Prometheus Rising* (1983)
- Reversed the order of two circuits (neurogenetic as sixth, metaprogramming as seventh)
- Added practical exercises at the end of each chapter
- Further developed in *Quantum Psychology* (1990)

### The Circuits
- First four circuits: "Larval circuits" for surviving and functioning in terrestrial human society
- Last four circuits: "Post-terrestrial," concerning altered states of consciousness, enlightenment, mystical experiences, psychedelic states, and psychic abilities
