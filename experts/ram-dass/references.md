## Notes

- Ram Dass' birth year is consistently given as 1931, making him 88 at death in December 2019.
- Some sources give the stroke date as February 1997; others simply say "1997." February 1997 is the most commonly cited.
- "Fierce Grace" as a term for the stroke is well-documented in the 2001 documentary of the same name and in his subsequent writings.
- The story of Maharaj-ji taking a large dose of LSD with no effect is recounted by Ram Dass but cannot be independently verified. It is included as part of his account/teaching, not as historical fact.
- Neem Karoli Baba is also spelled "Neem Karoli Baba" or "Neem Karoli Maharaj" - all refer to the same person.
- George Harrison's song "Be Here Now" from *Living in the Material World* (1973) was written in 1971, directly inspired by the book.
