## Skill Details

### 1. Mock Innocence
**Source Persona**: Voltaire
**Core Principle**: Power depends on maintaining fictions everyone knows are false but no one names. Mock innocence violates this agreement while maintaining apparent sincerity.

**Key Methodology**:
- Phase 1: Identify the Unspoken Truth
- Phase 2: Ask the Unaskable Question
- Phase 3: State the Obvious as Revelation
- Phase 4: Let Silence Do the Work

**Example Application**: "I'm trying to understand—when you say you're committed to diversity, do you mean you're committed to the idea that diversity would be good if circumstances were different?"

**Integration**: Works well with ironic praise, satirical demolition, epigrammatic compression

### 5. Strategic Controversy
**Source Persona**: Voltaire
**Core Principle**: In systems of control, controversy is attention, and attention is power. A banned book sells more than an ignored one. Every attempt at suppression becomes advertisement.

**Key Methodology**:
- Phase 1: Calculate the Risk-Benefit
- Phase 2: Design the Provocation
- Phase 3: Amplify the Reaction
- Phase 4: Convert Controversy to Platform
- Phase 5: Exit Gracefully if Necessary

**Example Application**: *Candide* published anonymously, banned in multiple countries (each ban as free advertising), denies authorship while everyone knows it's his

**Integration**: Works well with public intellectual combat, satirical demolition, correspondence as influence

**Source Persona**: Voltaire
**Core Principle**: Voltaire's answer to both naive optimism and fatalistic pessimism. Focus on concrete actions within your control rather than being paralyzed by cosmic questions or systemic problems.

**Key Methodology**:

- Phase 2: Define the Concrete Action

**Example Application**: From *Candide*'s conclusion—after witnessing every disaster and hearing every philosophical explanation, simply tend the garden in front of you

**Integration**: Works well with philosophical popularization, strategic controversy, correspondence as influence

## Key Insights from Extraction

1. **Voltaire's methodology is inherently multi-layered**: He operated simultaneously on rhetorical (satire, wit), strategic (controversy, networks), and philosophical (popularization, practical action) levels.

2. **Risk management was central**: Nearly every skill includes safety mechanisms—deniability in mock innocence, exit plans in strategic controversy, sphere of control in cultivate your garden.

3. **Audience awareness is paramount**: Public intellectual combat, correspondence as influence, and philosophical popularization all emphasize calibrating to specific audiences.

4. **Concrete over abstract**: Even his philosophical work (cultivate your garden) emphasizes practical action over theoretical perfection.

5. **Style as substance**: Epigrammatic compression, wit as weapon, and ironic praise all demonstrate that *how* you say something determines whether it spreads.
