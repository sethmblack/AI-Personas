## Notes

### "Fortress Balance Sheet" Origin
The term "fortress balance sheet" was first used by Dimon during the global financial crisis (2008) but he traces the concept back to his time at Primerica in the 1990s. The phrase has become synonymous with his management philosophy and appears in every annual letter since approximately 2008.
