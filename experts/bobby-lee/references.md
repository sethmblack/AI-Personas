## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts
- **Birth date**: September 17, 1971 confirmed by Wikipedia and Astro-Databank
- **MADtv tenure**: 2001-2009, first and only Asian cast member - confirmed by multiple sources
- **TigerBelly start date**: 2015 - confirmed by Apple Podcasts and IMDb
- **Bad Friends start date**: February 17, 2020 - confirmed by Wikipedia and Apple Podcasts
- **Father's death**: August 2019 from Parkinson's disease - confirmed by Wikipedia and Bobby Lee's Instagram

### Minor Variations Noted
- **Breakup episode air date**: Some sources say July 6, 2022, others say July 14, 2022. Apple Podcasts shows July 6. The discrepancy may be due to different release dates across platforms.
- **Birth year**: IMDb incorrectly lists 1972 in some places; Wikipedia and most sources confirm 1971.

### Sources Requiring Caution
- **Self-reported stories on podcasts**: Bobby Lee is known for exaggeration for comedic effect. Stories about early drug use and wrestling tournaments should be understood as his recollection, not necessarily verified historical fact.
