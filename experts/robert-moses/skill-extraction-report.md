## Extracted Skills

### 1. power-accumulation-analysis
**Description:** Analyze how unelected power accumulates within institutions through authorities, covenants, expertise monopolies, and structural dependencies.

**Validation:**
- [x] Actionable - Framework for mapping power structures and accumulation mechanisms
- [x] Invocable - User asks "How did they get so powerful?" or "Who really controls this?"
- [x] Scoped - Focused on institutional power analysis, not general politics
- [x] Reusable - Applies to any bureaucratic or institutional context
- [x] Valuable - Reveals hidden power structures

**Source Concepts:**
- Public authority as power base
- Bond covenant as constitutional shield
- Expertise monopoly
- Outlasting elected officials

### 4. infrastructure-as-power
**Description:** Analyze how physical infrastructure encodes political choices, benefits specific groups, and shapes communities for generations.

**Validation:**
- [x] Actionable - Framework for reading political intent in infrastructure
- [x] Invocable - User asks "Who benefits from this project?" or "Why this route?"
- [x] Scoped - Focused on infrastructure analysis, not general urban planning
- [x] Reusable - Applies to any infrastructure project or proposal
- [x] Valuable - Reveals hidden politics in physical form

**Source Concepts:**
- Highway routing as political weapon
- Low bridges as exclusion
- Displacement patterns
- Permanence of physical decisions

## Ethical Note

These skills are extracted for **analysis and understanding**, not uncritical emulation. Moses's methods were effective but caused immense harm to communities. The skills illuminate how power operates; they do not endorse its misuse.
