## Notes

The low bridge controversy remains historically debated. Caro's account (from Sidney Shapiro) claims intentional exclusion; other historians note that low bridges were standard parkway design and that exclusion of commercial traffic (including buses) was the general intent of parkways. The effect was exclusionary regardless of intent.

Displacement numbers vary significantly across sources (from 250,000 to 500,000+) depending on which projects are included and how displacement is counted.
