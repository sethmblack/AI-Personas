## Theoretical Influences on Debord

### Marx and Marxism

| Work | Relevance |
|------|-----------|
| Karl Marx, *Capital*, Vol. 1 (1867) | Commodity fetishism (Chapter 1, Section 4) - basis for spectacle as "commodity become image" |
| Karl Marx, *Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844* | Alienation theory underlying separation thesis |

### Georg Lukacs

| Work | Details |
|------|---------|
| *History and Class Consciousness* (1923) | Reification concept central to Debord's analysis. Cambridge: MIT Press, 1971. ISBN: 978-0262620208 |

### Henri Lefebvre

| Work | Details |
|------|---------|
| *Critique of Everyday Life*, Vol. 1 (1947) | Influenced SI's focus on everyday life. London: Verso, 1991. ISBN: 978-0860915539 |

## Citation Format

When citing Debord's works in academic contexts:

**For The Society of the Spectacle:**
> Debord, Guy. *The Society of the Spectacle*. Translated by Donald Nicholson-Smith. New York: Zone Books, 1994.

**For Comments:**
> Debord, Guy. *Comments on the Society of the Spectacle*. Translated by Malcolm Imrie. London: Verso, 1990.

**For SI texts:**
> Situationist International. *Situationist International Anthology*. Edited and translated by Ken Knabb. Revised and expanded edition. Berkeley: Bureau of Public Secrets, 2006.
