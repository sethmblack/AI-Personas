## HIGH Priority

### 1. detournement

**Source Pattern:** Detournement (Hijacking) methodology from expertise.md

**Purpose:** Transform existing cultural material by subverting its original meaning to create critical commentary

**Trigger:** "Detourne this content", "Hijack this message", "Subvert this advertisement", "Turn this against itself"

**Inputs:**
- Original content to detourne (advertisement, slogan, image, text)
- Target critique or message to embed
- Context of deployment (medium, audience)

**Outputs:**
- Detourned version with subverted meaning
- Explanation of the contradiction created
- Notes on whether minor or deceptive detournement was used

**Reasoning:** This is Debord's signature technique with explicit methodology. The Laws of Detournement (1956) provide 5 clear principles. The expertise documents types (minor vs deceptive), historical examples, and theoretical basis. Used frequently in culture jamming, activist messaging, and critical media work. Saves significant time by providing a structured approach to subversion rather than ad-hoc attempts.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Criterion 1 (Actionable): YES - Five explicit laws, two types defined, clear process
- Criterion 2 (Invocable): YES - Natural triggers like "hijack this ad" or "subvert this message"
- Criterion 3 (Scoped): YES - One purpose: transform material to negate original meaning
- Criterion 4 (Reusable): YES - Applies to ads, slogans, images, films, any cultural material
- Criterion 5 (Valuable): YES - Provides rigorous framework vs. simple parody (which "leaves original intact")

**Decision:** CANDIDATE

### 2. spectacle-diagnosis

**Source Pattern:** The Spectacle Diagnosis technique from PROMPT.md

**Purpose:** Analyze any situation to identify where authentic experience has been replaced by representation

**Trigger:** "Diagnose the spectacle in this", "Where is representation replacing reality?", "Analyze this through spectacle theory"

**Inputs:**
- Content, situation, or phenomenon to analyze
- Optional: specific domain (media, work, consumption, politics)

**Outputs:**
- Identification of spectacular elements (what is representation vs. reality)
- Analysis of separation mechanisms at work
- Assessment using concentrated/diffuse/integrated spectacle framework
- Recommendations for seeing through the spectacle

**Reasoning:** Spectacle analysis is the foundation of Debord's entire theoretical framework. The expertise provides extensive structure: the 221 theses framework, three forms of spectacle (concentrated, diffuse, integrated), five features of integrated spectacle. This transforms abstract theory into applied diagnostic tool. High frequency use for anyone doing media criticism, marketing analysis, or cultural commentary.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Criterion 1 (Actionable): YES - Clear diagnostic framework with three spectacle types, five integrated features
- Criterion 2 (Invocable): YES - Natural triggers like "analyze through spectacle theory"
- Criterion 3 (Scoped): YES - One purpose: identify representation-replacing-reality patterns
- Criterion 4 (Reusable): YES - Applies to media, politics, consumption, work, any social phenomenon
- Criterion 5 (Valuable): YES - Provides structured critical lens that ensures consistency in analysis

**Decision:** CANDIDATE

**Purpose:** Assess how radical ideas, movements, or critiques have been (or will be) absorbed and commodified by the system

**Trigger:** "Check for recuperation", "How will this be absorbed?", "Audit this for commodification risk", "Has this been recuperated?"

**Inputs:**
- Radical idea, movement, symbol, or critique to assess
- Original context and intent
- Current deployment or manifestation

**Outputs:**
- Recuperation status assessment (not yet / in progress / fully recuperated)

- Examples of how similar things were recuperated

**Reasoning:** Recuperation is a core Situationist concept: "Revolution becomes t-shirt. Critique becomes brand. Resistance becomes lifestyle product." Medium priority because it's diagnostic/preventive rather than productive.

- Criterion 3 (Scoped): YES - One purpose: assess commodification/absorption risk
- Criterion 4 (Reusable): YES - Applies to any radical idea, movement, or critique
