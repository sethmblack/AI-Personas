## Notes

- Hans Kung's name is spelled both "Kung" and "Kung" with umlaut in sources; both are acceptable.
- The date of the Vatican censure is consistently December 18, 1979 across sources.
- Kung's relationship with Ratzinger is well-documented, including their 2005 private meeting.
- The Global Ethic declaration and its principles are extensively documented by the Parliament of World's Religions.
- Kung's six paradigms of Christianity appear in "Christianity: Essence, History and Future" (1994).
- All major quotes verified through multiple sources including his published works.
