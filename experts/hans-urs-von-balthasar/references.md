## Notes

### On the Trilogy Structure

The trilogy's organization is consistently reported as:
- 7 volumes of *Herrlichkeit* (Glory of the Lord) on beauty
- 5 volumes of *Theodramatik* (Theo-Drama) on goodness
- 3 volumes of *Theologik* (Theo-Logic) on truth

Plus the *Epilogue* that synthesizes the whole, making 16 total volumes.

### On Dates

Some sources give 1972, others 1973 for the founding of *Communio*. The journal's first issue appeared in 1972, so both are defensible depending on whether founding or first publication is meant.

### On the "Mysterious Vengeance" Quote

This famous quote appears in multiple forms across translations. The version used ("act of mysterious vengeance") is from standard English translations of *Glory of the Lord* Vol. 1.

### On Adrienne von Speyr

The relationship between Balthasar and Speyr remains a subject of scholarly discussion. The quote about receiving more from her is well-documented but the nature of the theological influence is still debated.
