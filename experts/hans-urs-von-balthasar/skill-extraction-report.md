#### 2. theodramatic-interpretation

**Source Pattern:** Theo-Drama / Dramatic Theory
**Purpose:** Analyze situations as dramatic performances with given roles, unfolding action, and demanded responses - moving from spectator to participant
**Trigger:** "What should I do?", "What's my role here?", "This is happening to me", "I feel like a spectator", situations requiring action-meaning integration
**Inputs:** Description of situation unfolding; actors involved; current sense of role (or role confusion); what action is demanded
**Outputs:** Analysis of: 1) The drama (what is unfolding), 2) The dramatis personae (who is involved and their roles), 3) Your given role (mission that constitutes identity in this context), 4) The demanded response (what the drama calls forth from you)
**Reasoning:** Central to Balthasar's theodramatic theory - we are already on stage, already in the drama. Meets all criteria: actionable (specific dramatic categories), invocable (common existential questions), scoped (role and action interpretation), reusable (applies to any situation requiring decision), valuable (transforms passive observation into engaged participation). Estimated 2+ uses/week.

### MEDIUM Priority

#### 3. transcendentals-integration

**Source Pattern:** Unity of Beauty, Goodness, and Truth
**Purpose:** Demonstrate the convertibility and mutual dependence of beauty, goodness, and truth - restoring wholeness when one has been separated from the others
**Trigger:** "Is this true/good/beautiful?", fragmented analysis, ethics without attraction, truth without beauty, "just the facts" reductionism
**Inputs:** Description of phenomenon being analyzed; which transcendental(s) are being emphasized; which are being neglected or dismissed
**Outputs:** Analysis of: 1) How all three transcendentals are present (or should be), 2) What is lost by separation, 3) How to restore integration, 4) The "mysterious vengeance" - consequences of dismissing beauty
**Reasoning:** Distinctive Balthasarian contribution - beginning with beauty because without it truth lacks cogency and goodness lacks motivation. Actionable (integration steps), invocable (common in technical contexts that dismiss beauty), scoped (transcendentals specifically), reusable (applies wherever fragmented analysis occurs). Estimated 1-2 uses/week, especially valuable in technical and organizational contexts.
