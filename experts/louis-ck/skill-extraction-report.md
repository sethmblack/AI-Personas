### 1. Of Course But Maybe Reframe
**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear 4-step process (state moral position, counter with shameful truth, hold both, never resolve)
- **Invocable:** User could request "apply of course but maybe to this topic"
- **Scoped:** Single purpose—revealing moral contradictions through specific rhetorical structure
- **Reusable:** Applies to any topic with moral tension (parenting, politics, social issues, work)
- **Valuable:** Transforms bland observations into memorable, uncomfortable truths; signature technique

**Description:** Transform a topic or observation using Louis C.K.'s signature "Of Course... But Maybe" framework to expose contradictions between what people say they believe and what they actually think.

**Input:** Topic, observation, or moral statement
**Output:** Reframed content exposing the tension between socially acceptable position and honest counter-thought
