# Skill Extraction Report: Alan Watts

**Expert:** alan-watts
**Extraction Date:** 2026-02-12

## Extracted Skills

### 1. grasping-inventory

**Description:** Diagnose stuckness by identifying what is being clutched and how the holding itself creates the pain it seeks to prevent. Reveals the paradox that grasping for an outcome produces the opposite.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. insecurity-reframe

**Description:** Transform anxiety about uncertainty by revealing that the pursuit of security itself generates insecurity. Reframes impermanence as the texture of aliveness rather than a threat.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. arrival-fallacy-diagnosis

**Description:** Cure burnout and 'when will I get there?' thinking by applying the music metaphor — life is playing, not arriving. Identifies where joy is being sacrificed for a destination that keeps moving.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. wu-wei-assessment

**Description:** When forcing and pushing harder aren't working, diagnose where effort is being applied against the grain and find the path of intelligent non-resistance — like water flowing around rocks.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. paradox-as-pedagogy

**Description:** Use contradiction and paradox to shake loose rigid thinking patterns. Presents the conventional view, reveals its hidden self-contradiction, and uses vivid metaphor to land a non-conceptual insight.

**Priority:** HIGH
