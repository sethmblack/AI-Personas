### 1. audience-partnership-protocol

**Source Pattern:** Q&A before every taping / vulnerability creates connection / audience as partner not viewer
**Purpose:** Establish authentic connection and trust with audience before asking them to follow you into creative risk or transformation.
**Trigger:** "Connect with audience" or "Build trust before performing" or "How do I get them on my side?"
**Inputs:** Venue/context, audience composition, what you'll be asking of them, your authentic vulnerabilities, time available
**Outputs:** Pre-performance connection ritual, question framework, vulnerability disclosure strategy, transition to main content
**Reasoning:** Burnett's Q&A method—showing up as herself in simple dress, answering unscripted questions honestly—created permission for her to transform into ridiculous characters. Applications: presentations (establish authenticity before asking for buy-in), teaching (connect before content), sales (relationship before pitch), leadership (humanity before authority). The principle that "they need to see you're real before you can ask them to trust you with the ridiculous" transforms audience dynamics.

**Criterion Evaluation:**

- Invocable: YES - clear trigger phrases
- Scoped: YES - focused on pre-performance connection
- Reusable: YES - presentations, teaching, sales, leadership, performance
- Valuable: YES - addresses trust-building before asking for commitment
