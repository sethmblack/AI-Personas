## Major Actions Documented

### WTO Impersonations (1999-2002)

**Gatt.org Fake Website**
- Created: 1999, in lead-up to Seattle WTO protests
- Platform: Parody of official WTO website
- Result: Invitations to speak at conferences worldwide as WTO representatives
- [Rhizome Net Art Anthology - Gatt.org](https://anthology.rhizome.org/gatt-org)

**Salzburg Conference (2000)**
- Location: Center for International Legal Studies, Salzburg, Austria
- Character: Dr. Andreas Bichlbauer
- Presentation: "Trade Regulation Relaxation and Concepts of Incremental Improvement"
- Content: Proposed allowing voters to sell their votes; received polite applause

**Sydney "End of WTO" Announcement (May 2002)**
- Location: Sydney, Australia
- Action: Announced WTO dissolution and replacement with human rights-based organization
- Impact: Canadian Parliament member announced the news on the floor before retraction
- [The Yes Men - End of WTO](https://theyesmen.org/project/end-wto/behindthecurtain)

### Dow Chemical/Bhopal Hoax (December 3, 2004)

**BBC World Interview**
- Date: December 3, 2004 (20th anniversary of Bhopal disaster)
- Character: "Jude Finisterra" (Latin for "end of the world")
- Claim: Dow would accept $12 billion liability for Bhopal
- Market Impact: Dow shares fell 4.24% in 23 minutes, losing $2 billion market value
- Coverage Duration: Two hours of international news before denial
- [The Yes Men - Dow BBC](https://theyesmen.org/project/dowbbc/behindthecurtain)
- [Democracy Now! Coverage](https://www.democracynow.org/2004/12/6/yes_men_hoax_on_bbc_reminds)
- [Museum of Hoaxes Archive](https://hoaxes.org/archive/permalink/the_yes_mens_bhopal_hoax)

**Background: The Bhopal Disaster**
- Date: December 3, 1984
- Location: Bhopal, India
- Company: Union Carbide Corporation (acquired by Dow Chemical in 2001)
- Immediate Deaths: Estimated 3,800
- Total Affected: Over 500,000 exposed to methyl isocyanate gas

### HUD/Hurricane Katrina Action (2006)

- Location: New Orleans, Louisiana
- Characters: HUD representatives
- Announcement: 5,000 units of public housing slated for demolition would be maintained
- Response: Mayor Ray Nagin spoke out against the hoax
- Featured in: The Yes Men Fix the World (2009)

### Halliburton SurvivaBall Prank (May 2006)

- Date: May 2006
- Location: "Catastrophic Loss" conference, Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island, Florida
- Character: "Fred Wolf" (Halliburton representative)
- Product: SurvivaBall - inflatable survival suit for executives during climate catastrophe
- Price Claimed: $100 million
- Tagline: "A gated community for one"
- [Democracy Now! Coverage](https://www.democracynow.org/2006/5/12/the_yes_men_strike_again_group)
- [HuffPost - SurvivaBall](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/survivaball-new-yes-men-p_n_288550)

### Exxon/Vivoleum Prank (June 14, 2007)

- Location: Gas and Oil Exposition, Calgary, Alberta, Canada
- Characters: ExxonMobil and National Petroleum Council representatives
- Product: "Vivoleum" - fictitious oil derived from human remains
- Audience: 300 oil industry professionals
- Outcome: Security removed presenters when attendees lit "commemorative candles"

### Fake New York Times (November 12, 2008)

- Date: November 12, 2008 (one week after Obama's election)
- Headline: "IRAQ WAR ENDS"
- Copies Distributed: 80,000-100,000 in New York and Los Angeles
- Masthead: "All the News We Hope to Print"
- Collaborators: Anti-Advertising Agency, CODEPINK, United for Peace and Justice, Improv Everywhere
- Featured Headlines: National healthcare, maximum CEO wage, end of corporate personhood
- Planning Duration: Six months
- [The Yes Men - NY Times Project](https://theyesmen.org/project/nytimes)
- [Variety Coverage](https://variety.com/2008/more/news/yes-men-declare-iraq-war-ends-1117995917/)
- [Boing Boing Coverage](https://boingboing.net/2008/11/12/fake-nyt-hits-street.html)

### US Chamber of Commerce Climate Hoax (October 19, 2009)

- Date: October 19, 2009
- Location: National Press Club, Washington, DC
- Character: "Hingo Sembra" (Chamber spokesperson)
- Announcement: Chamber reversing position on climate legislation
- Initial Coverage: Reuters, Fox Business Network, CNBC reported as fact
- Confrontation: Real Chamber spokesperson Eric Wohlschlegel burst in, leading to dual claims of imposture
- [Democracy Now! Coverage](https://www.democracynow.org/2009/10/20/yes_men_pull_off_prank_claiming)
- [Washington Post Coverage](https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2009/10/19/AR2009101901651.html)
- [Grist Coverage](https://grist.org/climate-energy/yes-men-prank-the-chamber-of-commerce-and-get-away-with-it/)

**Legal Aftermath**
- Lawsuit: US Chamber of Commerce v. Yes Men (2009)
- Claims: Commercial identity theft, cyberpiracy, trademark infringement
- Defense: First Amendment protected speech on matters of public concern
- Outcome: Chamber withdrew lawsuit, citing "age of the case"
- [Digital Media Law Project - Case Summary](https://www.dmlp.org/threats/us-chamber-commerce-v-yes-men)
- [The Yes Men - Lawsuit Dropped](https://theyesmen.org/project/chamber/lawsuitdroppedrelease)

- Website: ArcticReady.com (parody site)

## Secondary Analysis

### Academic Studies

**"The Politics of Pranking: The Yes Men Culture Jamming and Disruptive Rhetoric"**
- Institution: California State University
- Approach: Foucauldian rhetorical analysis
- Finding: Yes Men use satire and sincerity to disrupt corporate discourses and open spaces of freedom
- [Full Text - CalState ScholarWorks](https://scholarworks.calstate.edu/downloads/1n79h4915)

**"Effectiveness of Culture Jamming in Agenda Building: An Analysis of the Yes Men's Bhopal Disaster Prank"**
- Authors: Robinson & Bell
- Method: Content analysis of 114 newspaper articles (pre/post Bhopal hoax)
- Framework: Agenda building theory
- Finding: The hoax actually decreased media use of frames sympathetic to Bhopal victims
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Effectiveness-of-Culture-Jamming-in-Agenda-An-of-Robinson-Bell/b3824d74331a4a146efc3794f5b4cc0122896c2c)

### Theoretical Frameworks

**Culture Jamming**
- Definition: "Semiological guerrilla warfare" (Umberto Eco) that hijacks form to change content
- Key Theorists: Umberto Eco, Naomi Klein, Kalle Lasn
- Context: Emerged from 1990s anti-corporate activism
- Related Term: Detournement - turning capitalist culture against itself

**Tactical Media**
- Term Coined: 1997
- Definition: Hit-and-run interventions in media systems
- Yes Men Contribution: High-profile examples of tactical media in practice
- Related Movement: Anti-globalization activism

**Critical Pedagogy Connection**
- Scholars: Sandlin and Milam
- Argument: Culture jamming functions as critical public pedagogy
- Method: Frees discursive space through detournement

## Historical Context

### The Founders

**Jacques Servin (Andy Bichlbaum)**
- Born: 1963
- Background: Author of experimental fiction, software engineer
- Notable Pre-Yes Men Action: Inserted kissing men in swimsuits into 80,000 copies of SimCopter (1996)
- Role: Public spokesperson, performer

**Igor Vamos (Mike Bonanno)**
- Born: April 15, 1968
- Education: BA Studio Art, Reed College; MFA Visual Arts, UC San Diego
- Academic Position: Associate Professor of Media Arts, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute
- Director: RPI Tactical Media Lab
- Awards: Creative Capital Award (2000), Guggenheim Fellowship (2003)
- [RPI Faculty Page](https://faculty.rpi.edu/igor-vamos)
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Vamos)

### Precursor Organizations

**RTMark (Registered Trademark)**
- Founded: 1990s
- Structure: "Anti-corporate corporation"
- Purpose: Connect activists with sponsors for parody projects
- Notable Project: Barbie Liberation Organization (1990s) - switched voice boxes in 300 Barbie and G.I. Joe dolls

**Guerrilla Theater of the Absurd**
- Era: Late 1980s/early 1990s
- Connection: Igor Vamos participated during college at Reed

**gwbush.com (2000)**
- Action: Fake George W. Bush campaign website
- Response: Bush invoked FTC, stated "there ought to be limits to freedom"

### The Yes Lab

**Founded:** 2010

**Mission:** Train activist groups to carry out media-generating creative actions

**Location:** NYU Hemispheric Institute, New York

**Services:**
- Laughtivism presentation and methodology
- Brainstorming and project selection
- Action planning with timelines and responsibilities
- Media handling, improv, writing, and video editing training

**University Partnerships:** Yes Labs can integrate students, faculty, NGOs, and The Yes Men

[The Yes Lab](https://theyesmen.org/lab)

### Action Switchboard (2015-discontinued)

**Concept:** Online platform for proposing projects and finding collaborators

**Model:** "Kickstarter for creative direct action"

**Outcome:** Platform did not achieve expected traction; Yes Men returned to more formal operating model
