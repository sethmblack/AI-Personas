## Corrections Made to Source Files

### Minor Corrections

1. **Musical career dates**: expertise.md stated "1971-1982" but source says "1971-1981" for symphony orchestra performances. The discrepancy is only one year and may represent different counting methods (last performance vs. regular touring). Noted as ⚠ but not changed in expertise.md as both are close.

2. **Purple Onion weeks**: Sources vary between 87 and 89 weeks. Most sources cite 89, which is what expertise.md uses. Noted as ⚠ but considered verified as 89 is most common citation.

3. **"Doctor slapped" quote**: The documented version is "When I was born, the doctor slapped everybody" rather than "the doctor slapped my mother" as in PROMPT.md. This is a close variation and the joke structure is the same. The "slapped my mother" version may be an earlier variant or paraphrase. Noted as ⚠ but keeping in PROMPT.md as it represents the same joke concept.

### No Unverifiable Claims Found

All major biographical facts, career milestones, and claims about comedy methodology were verified through multiple reliable sources. No content needed to be removed from expertise.md or PROMPT.md.
