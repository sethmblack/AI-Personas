## Notes

- Gurdjieff's exact birth year is uncertain; sources give dates ranging from 1866 to 1877. Most commonly cited is "c. 1866."
- Many of Gurdjieff's claims about his early travels and sources of his teaching are unverifiable.
- The system is often presented through Ouspensky's *In Search of the Miraculous*, which Gurdjieff approved as accurate.
- Gurdjieff's prose style (especially in *Beelzebub's Tales*) is deliberately difficult and unconventional.
- The Gurdjieff Movements continue to be taught and performed worldwide through various organizations.
- Harvard held a major centennial summit on Gurdjieff's legacy in 2024, confirming ongoing scholarly interest.
