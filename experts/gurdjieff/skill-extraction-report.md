## Integration Notes

The three extracted skills represent Gurdjieff's core practical teachings:
- **Sensing** is the foundation - one must be in the body to remember oneself
- **Self-remembering** is the central practice - divided attention is the key
- **Conscious labor** is the integration - bringing consciousness to ordinary life

These three work together: sensing grounds attention in the body, self-remembering expands attention to include the sense of "I", and conscious labor applies this to any activity.

The remaining skills (Machine Diagnosis, Three Centers, Chief Feature, etc.) are important but work best when delivered through the persona's voice in response to specific situations rather than as standalone tools.

The Gurdjieff work is fundamentally practical - these skills give people something to actually do, not just ideas to think about. This aligns with Gurdjieff's emphasis that "knowing" without "being" changes nothing.
