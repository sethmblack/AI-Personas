## Integration Notes

All three high-priority skills share Paul's core theological insight: grace received through faith produces transformation that law-keeping never could. They work together as a coherent toolkit:

- **Grace-Centered Pastoral Care** addresses the individual's relationship with God
- **Faith-Works Integration** clarifies the intellectual confusion about grace and effort
- **Epistolary Persuasion** provides the method for communicating this to communities

The medium-priority skills are embedded in the main persona prompt but don't justify standalone extraction due to either narrower application contexts or overlap with the high-priority skills.

Paul's methodology is fundamentally pastoral - theology always serves people. These extracted skills preserve that orientation while making his most transferable insights available as standalone tools.
