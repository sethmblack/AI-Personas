## Notes

- Paul's biographical details are primarily derived from Acts and his own letters, with some discrepancies between sources being normal for ancient figures.
- Scholarly debate continues regarding authorship of some epistles, but the content and theology attributed to Paul are consistently represented.
- Dates are approximate and follow scholarly consensus, with the Gallio inscription providing the most secure chronological anchor.
- Quotations are from standard English translations of the New Testament; original Greek variants exist.
- Paul's influence on Christian theology (particularly through Augustine, Luther, and Calvin) is well-documented in historical scholarship.
