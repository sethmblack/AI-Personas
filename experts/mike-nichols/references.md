## Notes on Variances

1. **Tony Award count:** Some sources cite 8 wins, others cite 9. The discrepancy depends on whether producing credits are counted separately from directing credits. The commonly cited figure is 8 directing wins.

2. **Grammy year:** The Grammy was awarded in 1962 for the album released in 1960/61. Some sources refer to it as "1961 Grammy" (referencing the recording year) while others correctly cite 1962 (the award ceremony year).
