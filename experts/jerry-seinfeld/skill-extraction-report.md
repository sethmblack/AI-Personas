## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. observational-comedy-breakdown

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear 5-step process defined in expertise.md (identify universal experience → zoom to specific → question logic → reveal absurdity → land on truth)
- **Invocable:** Natural triggers: "Find the comedy in this," "Make this observational," "What's funny about this?"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - transform any situation into observational comedy using Seinfeld's framework
- **Reusable:** Applies to any content, situation, or topic needing comedic angle
- **Valuable:** Saves significant analysis time by providing structured methodology for finding humor

**Source Framework:** "The Observational Comedy Framework" (expertise.md lines 27-36)

**Core Process:**
1. Identify universal experience
2. Isolate the specific detail
3. Question the logic
4. Reveal the absurdity
5. Land on the truth (insight as punchline)

---

### 2. joke-construction-polish

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** 6-step refinement process defined in expertise.md (find premise → write versions → test aloud → cut ruthlessly → refine rhythm → polish)
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Polish this joke," "Refine this comedy," "Make this tighter," "Cut the extra words"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - take rough comedy material and refine to perfection
- **Reusable:** Works for any comedic content, writing, or communication needing compression
- **Valuable:** Transforms weak material into strong material through systematic refinement

**Source Framework:** "The Joke Construction Process" (expertise.md lines 38-48)

**Core Process:**
1. Find the premise (observation or setup)
2. Write multiple versions (never settle on first draft)
3. Test aloud (comedy is oral, not written)
4. Cut ruthlessly (every extra word weakens)
5. Refine the rhythm (timing is in syllables)
6. Polish to perfection (know when it's done)

---

### 3. everyday-absurdity-mining

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear methodology for identifying comedic potential in mundane situations
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Find the comedy here," "What's absurd about this?," "Mine this for humor"
- **Scoped:** Focused on finding and extracting humor from everyday situations
- **Reusable:** Applies to any topic, situation, or observation
- **Valuable:** Trains attention on overlooked details that contain universal truth

**Source Material:** Combines "Making the Invisible Visible" + "Observing the Gap Between Intention and Action" techniques

**Core Process:**
1. Identify what everyone does but never discusses
2. Examine the stated reason vs. actual reason
3. Find the gap between intention and action
4. Zoom to the specific behavioral detail
5. Frame it as genuine puzzlement

**Note:** This overlaps significantly with observational-comedy-breakdown. May be redundant. Consider combining or making this a sub-skill.

---

### 4. compression-edit

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Specific techniques for reducing word count while increasing impact
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Compress this," "Tighten this up," "Cut the fat," "Make this punchier"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - reduce syllables, words, and unnecessary language
- **Reusable:** Works for comedy, writing, presentations, any communication
- **Valuable:** Significantly improves impact through economy

**Source Material:** "Compression and Precision" section (expertise.md)

**Core Techniques:**
- Shave letters off words, reduce syllables
- Use parallelism for rhythm
- Cut every extra word
- Strong verbs, minimal adjectives
- Economy of language creates impact

**Note:** This might be better as part of joke-construction-polish rather than standalone skill. The compression is part of the polish process.
