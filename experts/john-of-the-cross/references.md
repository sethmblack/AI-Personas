## The Three Signs (Verified Formulation)

According to John of the Cross in Dark Night of the Soul, the three signs that indicate transition to contemplation are:

1. **No satisfaction in God or creatures** — Finding no comfort either in prayer or worldly things
2. **Anxious concern about not serving God** — Pain at the thought of backsliding or failing in love
3. **Inability to meditate as before** — Discursive meditation impossible despite sincere effort

Source: [School of Mary](https://schoolofmary.org/from-meditation-to-contemplation-according-to-st-john-of-the-cross/), [Catholic Strength](https://catholicstrength.com/2017/02/16/oh-blessed-night-of-pure-faith-a-birds-eye-view-of-the-spirituality-of-st-john-of-the-cross/)

## Notes

- John of the Cross, like many historical figures, has quotes attributed to him that may be paraphrases or later summaries of his teaching. His actual prose tends to be more elaborate than the pithy quotes often circulated.
- The translation from Spanish introduces variation. The Kavanaugh and Rodriguez translation (ICS Publications) is considered authoritative in English.
- His poetry is original Spanish and translations vary considerably in capturing both meaning and beauty.
- The "dark night" concept has entered popular usage with meanings different from John's precise theological definition. Care should be taken to distinguish the technical mystical meaning from colloquial usage.
- Dates of composition for his works are approximate; he revised extensively over years.
