# John of the Cross: Skill Extraction Report

## Extraction Criteria

Each skill is scored on 5 criteria (10 points each, 50 points maximum):

| Criterion | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Actionable** | Can be performed as a discrete task with clear steps |
| **Invocable** | User can naturally request it with a simple phrase |
| **Scoped** | Has clear boundaries and doesn't sprawl infinitely |
| **Reusable** | Applies to many situations, not just one |
| **Valuable** | Provides significant insight or transformation |

**Priority Thresholds:**
- HIGH: 40-50 points (create skill prompt)
- MEDIUM: 30-39 points (document in expert, may promote later)
- LOW: Below 30 points (keep as expert knowledge only)

## Decision Notes

- **Spiritual State Diagnosis merged into Dark Night Navigation**: The diagnosis of spiritual states is most often invoked in the context of desolation. Rather than create a separate assessment skill, this becomes part of the dark night diagnostic process.

- **Mortification of Appetites merged into Spiritual Detachment**: These address the same core issue (attachment) from slightly different angles. The detachment skill incorporates the mortification methodology.

- **Mystical Poetry Interpretation kept in expert**: John's hermeneutic is valuable but cannot be reduced to a procedure. The symbolic interpretation emerges from the full persona's engagement rather than from following steps.

- **Theological Virtue Cultivation kept in expert**: Faith, hope, and charity are the instruments of John's entire system, not a discrete practice. They infuse everything rather than standing alone.

## Unique Methodological Contributions

What John of the Cross offers that few other experts provide:

1. **Precision about spiritual states**: Unlike vague spiritual encouragement, John provides diagnostic criteria. The three signs are a clinical tool for discernment.

2. **Reframing darkness as divine work**: Where others might offer comfort or distraction, John transforms understanding of suffering itself.

3. **The nada doctrine**: A complete inversion of spiritual acquisition—receiving everything by desiring nothing.

4. **The paradox of presence through absence**: God is closest when seeming most distant. This is not mere consolation but theological precision about divine encounter.

These methodologies are distinctly Sanjuanist and represent genuine intellectual property of his spiritual system.
