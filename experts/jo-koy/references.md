## Notes on Verification

### Items Marked as "Noted"
1. **Career start date (1989)**: Multiple sources cite "late 1980s" or "early 1990s" without specifying exact year. 1989 is commonly cited but not definitively confirmed.

2. **Chelsea Lately appearance count (100+)**: Press materials frequently cite "over 100 appearances" but exact count is not independently verifiable from available records.

3. **First Filipino-American Netflix headline special**: While Jo Koy is widely credited as the first major Filipino-American to headline a Netflix comedy special, the qualifier "major" is subjective. Other Filipino-American comedians had appeared in smaller specials or compilations.
