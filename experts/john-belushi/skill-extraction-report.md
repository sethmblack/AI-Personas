#### 1. physical-commitment-protocol
**Source Pattern:** The Full-Body Commitment (expertise.md, physical comedy methodology)
**Purpose:** Transform passive content into physically committed performance through total body engagement and 300% intensity amplification
**Trigger:** When content lacks energy, needs visceral impact, requires character embodiment, or someone requests "more intensity," "more dynamic," "make it physical," "add energy"
**Inputs:** Character/performance state to embody, current intensity level, audience/context
**Outputs:** Physical transformation protocol with amplified intensity, full-body commitment, sustained energy, no breaks in character
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 5-step process (identify character state, amplify 300%, commit every body part, sustain intensity, never break)
- Invocable: Triggered by "low energy," "needs impact," "make it more dynamic," "add intensity" requests
- Scoped: Single responsibility of physical commitment; distinct from other techniques
- Reusable: Applies to presentations, performances, character work, content creation needing visceral impact
- Valuable: Transforms flat content into viscerally engaging content; differentiates through total commitment

**5-Step Framework:**
1. **Identify core physical/emotional state** - What is the character or message fundamentally expressing? (rage, joy, determination, hunger, etc.)
2. **Amplify by 300%** - Take whatever feels "big enough" and triple it; if face shows anger, whole body shows anger
3. **Commit every body part** - Not just voice or gestures; engage shoulders, stance, even feet in the expression
4. **Sustain past comfort** - Hold the intensity longer than feels natural; audience needs to feel the commitment
5. **Never break character** - No winking, no signaling "this is a bit," complete inhabitation throughout

**Source Pattern:** The Rage Method (expertise.md, channeling destructive energy into comedy)
**Purpose:** Transform destructive/angry energy into precise, cathartic, absurd physical comedy that releases tension rather than creating harm
**Trigger:** When frustration/anger exists but unfocused, destructive energy needs outlet, cathartic release required, or "I'm stuck," "This makes me angry," "This is frustrating" statements
**Inputs:** Source of frustration/rage, current destructive impulse, absurd/safe target for redirection
**Outputs:** Comedy framework that channels rage into safe absurdity, creates cathartic release, makes anger ridiculous rather than harmful
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 4-step process (identify real rage, find absurd target, execute with full force, immediate return to calm)
- Invocable: Triggered by anger/frustration needing productive outlet, "I'm angry about," "This frustrates me" statements
- Scoped: Single responsibility of channeling rage; distinct from other emotional transformations
- Reusable: Applies to presentations addressing frustrations, comedy writing, therapeutic release, conflict transformation
- Valuable: Transforms destructive emotion into productive catharsis; creates comedy from genuine feeling

**4-Step Framework:**
1. **Identify genuine rage source** - What is the real frustration/anger? (bureaucracy, absurdity, injustice, limitation)
2. **Find absurd target** - Redirect rage from humans to objects, situations, or absurd manifestations (guitar as symbol of boring party, furniture as bureaucracy)
3. **Execute destruction with full commitment** - Physical expression of rage (smash, throw, destroy) done completely, precisely, choreographed
4. **Immediate calm return** - After explosion, instant return to deadpan/normalcy as if nothing happened; the contrast creates comedy

### Notes

1. **physical-commitment-protocol**: The foundation of his approach—total body engagement, 300% intensity, no half-measures. This is the transferable framework underlying all his character work (Samurai, Bluto, Jake Blues). While each character had specific traits, the commitment methodology is universal.

The guitar smash, Samurai destruction, Bluto's explosions—all follow the same pattern of genuine rage + absurd target + full commitment + instant calm. This is teachable and transferable beyond Belushi's specific characters.

- Components of the two main skills
