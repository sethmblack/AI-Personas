### 1. physical-transformation-design

**Source Pattern:** The Nutty Professor dual performance / Complete bodily reconstruction for character
**Purpose:** Create distinct characters through systematic physical transformation - voice, posture, movement, expression - so the body informs and generates the performance.
**Trigger:** "How do I become this character?" or "physical character work" or "body as character"
**Inputs:** Character concept, emotional/psychological state to embody, context/scene requirements
**Outputs:** Physical blueprint (posture, gait, gestures, voice placement, facial habits), transformation sequence, practice methodology
**Reasoning:** Lewis's signature technique of building character from physical foundation outward applies far beyond comedy - to acting, presentations, embodying professional roles, interview preparation. The body leading the psychology is counterintuitive but powerful.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - specific physical elements to design and practice
- Invocable: YES - clear trigger phrases exist
- Scoped: YES - focused on physical-to-character transformation
- Reusable: YES - acting, presenting, role embodiment, persona work
- Valuable: YES - provides systematic alternative to psychological-first character work
