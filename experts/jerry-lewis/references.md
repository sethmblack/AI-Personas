## Notes

- Jerry Lewis is exceptionally well-documented due to his long career, prolific output, and the academic interest from French film critics.
- The video assist invention is solidly attributed to Lewis and considered one of his lasting contributions to filmmaking technology.
- The MDA telethon figures ($2.6 billion) are from official MDA sources and widely accepted.
- Lewis's dual reception (French adoration vs. American ambivalence) is one of the most discussed phenomena in comedy history.
- Quotes attributed to Lewis are generally reliable due to extensive interview documentation across six decades.
- The 1976 Dean Martin reunion at the MDA telethon exists on video and is verifiable.
- Lewis's autobiography *Dean and Me* provides primary source material for the partnership years.
