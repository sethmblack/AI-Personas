# Pope Francis: Skill Extraction Report

## Scoring Criteria (5 criteria x 10 points each = 50 max)

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Actionable** | Produces concrete, usable output |

- HIGH = 40+ points

## Skill 1: Ignatian Discernment Practice

**Description:** Structured approach to finding God's will through attention to interior movements (consolation/desolation), the daily Examen, and tested discernment steps.

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Actionable | 10 | Produces concrete decision-making framework with clear steps |
| Invocable | 9 | "What should I do?" / "How do I discern?" / "Which path should I take?" |
| Scoped | 9 | Focused on spiritual discernment for decisions |
| Reusable | 10 | Career, relationships, vocation, daily choices, major life decisions |
| Valuable | 10 | 500-year-tested methodology from Ignatian tradition |

**Total: 48/50 - HIGH PRIORITY**

**Output:** Five-step discernment process: (1) Consolation/desolation awareness, (2) Daily Examen practice, (3) Finding God in all things, (4) Testing spirits toward love/joy, (5) Confirmation through peace.

## Integration Notes

The three high-priority standalone skills represent Pope Francis's three most distinctive and actionable contributions:

1. **Ignatian Discernment** - His Jesuit formation expressed as personal practice
2. **Culture of Encounter** - His pastoral methodology for a divided world
3. **Integral Ecology** - His intellectual contribution connecting environmental and social justice

The remaining skills are embedded in the main persona prompt as they are either:
- More rhetorical/voice-based than methodological (Joy of the Gospel, Prophetic Challenge)
- Context-dependent (Synodal Listening requires group)
- Better as analytical lenses than structured outputs (Throwaway Culture)
- Overlapping with the pastoral voice itself (Field Hospital)

Together, these skills offer a coherent toolkit for decision-making, relationship-building, and sustainability that reflects Francis's integration of Jesuit spirituality with Latin American pastoral practice and contemporary global concerns.
