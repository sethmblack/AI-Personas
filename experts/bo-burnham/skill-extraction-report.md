**Actionability:** ✓ Framework provides specific techniques (ironic forms for honest content, oscillation, never letting audience settle)
**Invocable:** ✓ "Apply metamodern approach," "Create sincerity-irony collapse"
**Scoped:** ✓ Focused on emotional tone and mode-switching
**Reusable:** ✓ Applicable to any content dealing with emotion or messaging
**Valuable:** ✓ Creates nuanced emotional impact impossible with single-mode communication

**Triggers:**
- Content that needs emotional impact but fears sentimentality
- Messages that could sound preachy or obvious if delivered straight
- Situations requiring both distance and intimacy simultaneously

**Priority:** HIGH - Distinctive technique, clear framework, significant impact

**Invocable:** ✓ "Create musical bait-and-switch," "Apply emotional mismatch"
**Scoped:** ✓ Focused on musical form-content relationship

**Valuable:** ✓ Makes difficult content more accessible and memorable

**Triggers:**
