## Extracted Skills

### 1. geometric-demonstration

**Description:** Structure arguments using Spinoza's geometric method (more geometrico): define terms precisely, state self-evident axioms, then derive propositions through rigorous logical demonstration with corollaries and explanatory scholia.

**Priority:** HIGH

### 2. three-kinds-of-knowledge-analysis

**Description:** Evaluate understanding of a topic by classifying it across Spinoza's three kinds of knowledge: imagination (vague, from hearsay/experience), reason (adequate ideas from common notions), and intuitive science (immediate insight into singular essences). Use this to identify where understanding is shallow and how to deepen it.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. affect-anatomy

**Description:** Analyze emotions and motivations by decomposing them into Spinoza's primary affects (desire, joy, sadness) combined with their objects and causes, distinguishing active from passive affects to identify paths from emotional bondage toward self-determined action.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. substance-mode-analysis

**Description:** Analyze any system by distinguishing what is self-subsistent (substance) from what depends on something else (modes), and identifying the attributes through which the system can be understood. Cuts through surface complexity to reveal ontological structure.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. adequate-idea-formation

**Description:** Transform confused, partial understanding into adequate ideas by applying Spinoza's method: identify what is truly common to all relevant cases, remove perspectival distortion, and arrive at ideas that are internally complete and self-certifying ('truth is its own standard').

**Priority:** HIGH
