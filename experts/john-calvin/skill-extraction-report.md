## Skill Analysis

### Skill 1: systematic-biblical-exposition

**What it does:**
Takes a theological question or topic and develops sound doctrine through:
1. Careful exegesis of relevant biblical texts (grammatical-historical method)
2. Systematic organization of biblical teaching
3. Practical application for Christian life

**Why it's distinctive:**
Calvin's method integrates three phases that others often separate:
- Exegesis (commentaries) - what the text says
- Dogmatics (Institutes) - how it fits the whole system
- Preaching (sermons) - how it applies to life

**Teachable elements:**
- Reject allegory; seek the author's original intent
- Use the analogy of faith (Scripture interprets Scripture)
- Move from text to doctrine to application
- Maintain "clear brevity" - the chief virtue of interpretation
- Acknowledge where Scripture is silent

---

### Skill 2: reformed-church-governance

**What it does:**
Provides a framework for organizing church leadership and governance according to biblical principles, emphasizing:
- Plurality of qualified elders
- Distinction of offices by function
- Representative decision-making
- Proper relationship between church and civil authority

**Why it's distinctive:**
Calvin's polity influenced Presbyterian, Reformed, and many evangelical churches. It balances authority with accountability through collective leadership.

**Teachable elements:**
- Four offices: pastor, teacher, elder, deacon
- Elders govern in council (consistory/session)
- No single individual rules; plurality is essential
- Church discipline as pastoral care
- Separation of church and state roles

---

### Skill 3: regulative-worship-design

**What it does:**
Designs worship services by asking: "What has God commanded?" rather than "What has God forbidden?" Only elements with biblical warrant are included.

**Why it's distinctive:**
The regulative principle produces a specific approach to worship that:
- Centers on the preached Word
- Emphasizes simplicity over ornamentation
- Prioritizes congregational participation
- Removes elements that distract from God's glory

**Teachable elements:**
- Worship must have biblical warrant
- Simplicity reflects New Covenant maturity
- The Word (read and preached) is central
- Sacraments administered according to Christ's institution
- Congregational singing (especially psalms)
- Prayer as corporate activity
- No images or elaborate ceremonies
