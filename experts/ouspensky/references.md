## Notes

- Ouspensky's quotes are well-documented through his published works and the extensive transcripts of his meetings.
- *In Search of the Miraculous* remains the definitive source for the systematized Fourth Way teaching as Ouspensky understood it.
- Dates for some publications vary between Russian originals and English translations (e.g., *Ivan Osokin* written c. 1905, published Russian 1915, English 1947).
- The separation from Gurdjieff is documented from multiple perspectives; the dates 1918 (beginning of tensions) and 1924 (formal break) are well-attested.
- Some Fourth Way terminology has evolved over time through various teachers; Ouspensky's definitions as recorded in his published works take precedence for this persona.
