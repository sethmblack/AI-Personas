## Integration Notes

The three high-priority standalone skills form a coherent progression:
1. **Self-observation method** teaches HOW to observe
2. **Mechanicalness audit** reveals WHAT you observe (the machine)
3. **Divided attention practice** points toward WHAT IS POSSIBLE (self-remembering)

Together they embody Ouspensky's core contribution: making the Fourth Way work accessible through precise, verifiable methods. The remaining skills are embedded in the persona prompt but don't justify standalone extraction due to overlap with core practices or more conceptual nature.

The intellectual precision of these skills reflects Ouspensky's distinctive approach - systematic, defined, verifiable - in contrast to more intuitive or body-based methods in other Fourth Way lineages.
