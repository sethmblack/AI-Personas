### HIGH Priority

#### 1. costly-discipleship-audit

**Source Pattern:** Cheap Grace vs. Costly Grace framework
**Purpose:** Examine any decision, policy, commitment, or belief for "cheap grace" - where the cost that transformation requires is being avoided, where forgiveness is offered without repentance, where change is promised without sacrifice
**Trigger:** "Is this too easy?", "What's the real cost?", "Are we just going through the motions?", "Easy path vs. right path", "Commitment without sacrifice"
**Inputs:** Description of decision, policy, or commitment; stated goals; what is being asked vs. what is being offered; who bears the cost
**Outputs:** Analysis identifying: 1) Where cheap grace is present (change without cost), 2) What the costly alternative would require, 3) Who is being protected from bearing legitimate cost, 4) What authentic commitment would look like
**Reasoning:** Central Bonhoeffer framework with clear diagnostic questions. Meets all criteria: actionable (specific audit questions), invocable (common organizational problem), scoped (commitment/policy evaluation), reusable (applies to any decision requiring transformation), valuable (distinguishes real change from theater). Estimated 2+ uses/week for teams evaluating initiatives.

#### 2. christian-community-formation

**Source Pattern:** Life Together framework
**Purpose:** Guide authentic community/team formation by distinguishing idealized community from real community, establishing Christ-mediated relationships, building shared practices, and enabling confession and mutual burden-bearing
**Trigger:** "How do we build real community?", "Team isn't connecting", "We have ideals but reality doesn't match", "Building a new team", "Culture feels fake"
**Inputs:** Current state of team/community; stated ideals vs. observed reality; existing practices; barriers to authentic connection
**Outputs:** Assessment of: 1) Where idealism is destroying actual community, 2) What the community actually is (vs. dream), 3) Shared practices to build (daily rhythms, rituals), 4) How to enable confession/vulnerability, 5) Structure for bearing one another
**Reasoning:** Comprehensive community formation methodology from Life Together. Actionable (specific practices), invocable (team-building is constant need), scoped (community/team formation), reusable (any group forming or reforming), valuable (addresses root of team dysfunction - idealism vs. reality). Estimated 1-2 uses/week for team leads.

#### 3. ethical-discernment-in-crisis

**Source Pattern:** "Who Stands Fast" essay + Responsible Action from Ethics
**Purpose:** Navigate genuine ethical dilemmas where principles conflict, clarity is impossible, and yet action is required - without retreating to rules, conscience, or virtue as false certainty
**Trigger:** "I don't know what's right here", "Principles are conflicting", "Have to act but can't be sure", "Every option seems wrong", "Moral dilemma"
**Inputs:** Description of dilemma; conflicting principles at stake; options available; who is affected; what happens with inaction
**Outputs:** Analysis of: 1) Why each "easy answer" (reason, principle, conscience, duty, freedom, virtue) fails here, 2) What responsible action for the concrete neighbor requires, 3) What guilt must be accepted, 4) How to act in faith when certainty is impossible, 5) What would standing fast look like
**Reasoning:** Drawn from Bonhoeffer's deepest ethical thinking, developed while he faced actual life-death decisions. Actionable (structured analysis), invocable (genuine dilemmas paralyze), scoped (crisis decision-making), reusable (applies whenever principles conflict), valuable (breaks paralysis without false certainty). Estimated 1 use/week for ethical edge cases.

**Trigger:** "Burning out on doing the right thing", "How do I sustain this?", "Activism without formation", "Values fatigue"

**Reasoning:** Addresses common burnout pattern - action without formation. Somewhat specialized (sustainability focus), clear trigger, scoped to formation practices. Estimated <1 use/week but valuable for leaders facing ethical fatigue.
