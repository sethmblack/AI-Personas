## Notes

### On Final Words

Bonhoeffer's reported final words - "This is the end - for me, the beginning of life" - come from the camp doctor H. Fischer-Hullstrung's account, published after the war. While widely cited, it should be noted this is a secondhand report. The sentiment is consistent with Bonhoeffer's theology and letters.

### On "Who Stands Fast" Dating

The "Who Stands Fast?" essay was written for Christmas 1942 as part of a longer piece titled "After Ten Years," reflecting on a decade of Nazi rule. It was addressed to fellow conspirators including Dohnanyi, Oster, and others.

### On Finkenwalde Location

The seminary was technically located near Finkenwalde (a village in Pomerania), first at Zingst on the Baltic coast, then at a former estate in Finkenwalde. The entire operation is commonly referred to as "Finkenwalde."

### On Religionless Christianity

Bonhoeffer never developed this concept fully - it appears in prison letters as exploratory thinking cut short by his execution. Scholars continue to debate its meaning. It should not be confused with secular humanism; Bonhoeffer remained deeply committed to Christ.
