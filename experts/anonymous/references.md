# Anonymous - Verified References

This document compiles verified sources on the Anonymous hacktivist collective, including documentaries, documented operations, academic research, and journalistic coverage.

## Documentaries

### We Are Legion: The Story of the Hacktivists (2012)
- **Director:** Brian Knappenberger
- **Runtime:** 93 minutes
- **Premiere:** 2012 Slamdance Film Festival
- **Synopsis:** The first comprehensive documentary on Anonymous, tracing the collective's origins from 4chan to its evolution into a global hacktivist movement. Features interviews with Anonymous members, academics, and journalists. Covers Project Chanology, Operation Payback, and support for Arab Spring movements.
- **Featured Subjects:** Julian Assange, Aaron Barr, various Anonymous members
- **Critical Reception:** 73% on Rotten Tomatoes, 66% on Metacritic
- **Availability:** Netflix, Prime Video
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - We Are Legion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Are_Legion)

### The Hacker Wars (2014)
- **Director:** Vivien Lesnik Weisman
- **Runtime:** 91 minutes
- **Release:** October 17, 2014
- **Synopsis:** Profiles three prominent hacktivists connected to Anonymous: Andrew "Weev" Auernheimer, Barrett Brown, and Jeremy Hammond. Examines government prosecution of hacktivists and debates over internet freedom.
- **Featured Interviews:** Glenn Greenwald (Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist), Thomas Drake (former NSA Senior Executive)
- **Themes:** Information freedom, surveillance state, prosecution of digital activists
- **Availability:** Netflix, Apple TV
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - The Hacker Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hacker_Wars)

## Major Operations

### Project Chanology (2008)
- **Target:** Church of Scientology
- **Trigger:** Church's copyright claim against leaked Tom Cruise interview video (January 2008)
- **Launch Date:** January 21, 2008 ("Message to Scientology" video)
- **Initial Tactics:**
  - DDoS attacks on Scientology websites (January 18-25, 2008)
  - Prank calls to Scientology centers
  - Black faxes
- **Evolution:** Shifted to legal protests in February 2008
- **Key Date:** February 10, 2008 - First global street protests with Guy Fawkes masks
- **Significance:** First major Anonymous operation to gain worldwide attention; established the collective as political activists beyond internet trolling; popularized the Guy Fawkes mask as symbol of Anonymous
- **Sources:**
  - [Wikipedia - Project Chanology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Chanology)
  - [Vice - The Video That Made Anonymous](https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-video-that-made-anonymous/)

### Operation Payback / Operation Avenge Assange (2010)
- **Context:** WikiLeaks under pressure after publishing U.S. diplomatic cables
- **Trigger:** PayPal, Visa, Mastercard, Amazon, and others cut services to WikiLeaks
- **Timeline:**
  - December 7-9, 2010: DDoS attacks launched
  - December 8: MasterCard and Visa websites taken down
  - December 9: PayPal attacked; PayPal released frozen WikiLeaks funds
- **Methods:** Low Orbit Ion Cannon (LOIC) - voluntary botnet tool enabling mass participation
- **Participation:** ~2,200 participants in Anonymous chat rooms during peak attacks
- **Legal Consequences:**
  - December 9, 2010: 16-year-old arrested in The Hague
  - January 27, 2011: FBI executed 40+ search warrants
  - 13 members later pleaded guilty
- **John Perry Barlow (EFF Co-founder) Quote:** "The shot heard round the world - this is Lexington"
- **Sources:**
  - [Wikipedia - Operation Payback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Payback)
  - [NPR - Operation Payback Targets WikiLeaks' Foes](https://www.npr.org/2010/12/09/131937254/-operation-payback-targets-wikileaks-foes)
  - [Computer Weekly - Thirteen Plead Guilty](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/2240210833/Thirteen-plead-guilty-to-Anonymous-DDoS-attack-on-Paypal)

### HBGary Federal Hack (2011)
- **Target:** HBGary Federal (security firm) and CEO Aaron Barr
- **Date:** February 5-6, 2011
- **Trigger:** Aaron Barr claimed to have identified Anonymous leaders and planned to sell information to FBI
- **Methods:**
  - SQL injection on HBGary website
  - Social engineering (phishing)
  - Exploited weak/reused passwords
- **Results:**
  - 70,000+ company emails published
  - Barr's Twitter account hijacked
  - Revealed HBGary's proposals to target WikiLeaks for Bank of America
  - Exposed development of "Magenta" Windows rootkit
- **Consequences:**
  - Aaron Barr resigned as CEO
  - HBGary Federal closed; HBGary Inc. acquired by ManTech (2012)
  - Congressional investigation requested
- **Legacy:** Core hackers later formed LulzSec (Tflow, Topiary, Sabu, Kayla)
- **Sources:**
  - [Krebs on Security - HBGary Federal Hacked](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2011/02/hbgary-federal-hacked-by-anonymous/)
  - [Wikipedia - HBGary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HBGary)
  - [NPR - Anonymous Hack Brings Security Firm to Its Knees](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2011/02/16/133814783/how-anonymous-exacted-revenge-on-firm-that-threatened-to-out-them)

### Operation Tunisia (2011)
- **Context:** Tunisian Revolution / Arab Spring
- **Launch Date:** January 2, 2011
- **Targets:** Tunisian government websites (president, prime minister, ministries, stock exchange)
- **Methods:**
  - DDoS attacks on government sites
  - "Care packets" with cybersecurity advice (translated into Arabic/French)
  - Greasemonkey script to evade government phishing
  - Website defacement (PM's website replaced with Anonymous message)
- **Key Participants:** Tflow, Sabu
- **Outcome:** President Ben Ali fled Tunisia on January 14, 2011 (10 days after operation began)
- **Sources:**
  - [Al Jazeera - Anonymous and the Arab Uprisings](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2011/5/19/anonymous-and-the-arab-uprisings)
  - [Wikipedia - Operation Tunisia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Tunisia)

### Operation Egypt (2011)
- **Launch Date:** January 25-26, 2011
- **Context:** Egyptian Revolution
- **Tactics:**
  - DDoS attacks on Egyptian government websites
  - Collaboration with Telecomix to bypass censorship
  - Provided communication tools during internet blackout
- **Target:** Egyptian Ministry of Information website (taken down)
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - Anonymous (hacker group)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anonymous_(hacker_group))

### Stratfor Hack (2011)
- **Target:** Strategic Forecasting Inc. (Stratfor) - private intelligence firm
- **Date:** December 2011
- **Key Participant:** Jeremy Hammond ("sup_g")
- **Data Compromised:**
  - 60,000 credit card numbers
  - 5 million emails
  - 200 GB of data
  - $700,000 in fraudulent charges
- **Publication:** WikiLeaks released emails as "Global Intelligence Files"
- **Revelations:**
  - Dow Chemical hired Stratfor to spy on Yes Men
  - Coca-Cola hired Stratfor to monitor activists
- **FBI Involvement:** Sabu (Hector Monsegur), secretly an FBI informant, provided FBI server for data exfiltration
- **Legal Outcome:** Jeremy Hammond sentenced to 10 years (November 15, 2013); released November 2020
- **Sources:**
  - [Wikipedia - Jeremy Hammond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Hammond)
  - [Rolling Stone - The Rise and Fall of Jeremy Hammond](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/the-rise-and-fall-of-jeremy-hammond-enemy-of-the-state-183599/)
  - [FBI Press Release](https://archives.fbi.gov/archives/newyork/press-releases/2013/jeremy-hammond-sentenced-to-10-years-in-prison-for-hacking-into-the-stratfor-website-and-other-company-federal-state-and-local-government-websites)

### OpISIS (2015)
- **Context:** Response to Charlie Hebdo attack (January 2015) and Paris attacks (November 2015)
- **Targets:** ISIS social media accounts, recruitment websites
- **Methods:**
  - Identifying and reporting ISIS Twitter/Facebook accounts
  - Website takedowns
  - Flooding ISIS accounts with counter-imagery (including goats)
  - Database compilation of ISIS-linked accounts
- **Claimed Results:**
  - ~20,000 Twitter accounts reported (Anonymous claim)
  - 60,000-70,000 accounts claimed by CtrlSec (affiliated group)
- **Controversy:** Twitter disputed accuracy of claims; some legitimate research sites accidentally targeted
- **#OpParis:** Follow-up operation after November 2015 Paris attacks
- **Sources:**
  - [Foreign Policy - Anonymous vs. the Islamic State](https://foreignpolicy.com/2015/11/13/anonymous-hackers-islamic-state-isis-chan-online-war/)
  - [Sophos - Anonymous Takes Down Terrorist Social Media](https://news.sophos.com/en-us/2015/02/10/anonymous-takes-down-dozens-of-terrorist-social-media-accounts-in-opisis/)
  - [CBS News - 2015 Was a Huge Year for Anonymous](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/anonymous-hackers-isis-donald-trump-2015/)

### 2011 PlayStation Network Involvement (Disputed)
- **Context:** Sony sued George "Geohot" Hotz for PS3 jailbreaking
- **Anonymous Action:** April 4, 2011 - DDoS attack on PlayStation Network
- **Major Breach:** April 17-19, 2011 - 77 million accounts compromised (24-day outage)
- **Anonymous Involvement:** Disputed
  - Sony claimed Anonymous was responsible (file found with "Anonymous" and "We Are Legion")
  - Anonymous denied involvement in data breach
  - LulzSec later claimed responsibility for intrusion
- **Distinction:** Anonymous conducted DDoS protests; massive data theft attributed to others
- **Sources:**
  - [Wikipedia - 2011 PlayStation Network Outage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_PlayStation_Network_outage)
  - [NPR - Was Anonymous Behind PlayStation Hack?](https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2011/05/06/136061056/was-anonymous-behind-playstation-hack)

## Academic Analysis

### Books

**Hacker, Hoaxer, Whistleblower, Spy: The Many Faces of Anonymous (2014)**
- **Author:** Gabriella Coleman (McGill University, Wolfe Chair in Scientific and Technological Literacy)
- **Publisher:** Verso Books
- **Description:** Six years of anthropological research; considered the definitive academic study of Anonymous
- **Endorsements:**
  - Julian Assange: "Easily the best book on Anonymous"
  - Chronicle of Higher Education: Coleman is "the leading interpreter of digital insurgency"
  - Mustafa Al-Bassam (Tflow): "This is the ultimate piece on Anonymous... a masterpiece"
- **Source:** [Verso Books](https://www.versobooks.com/products/21-hacker-hoaxer-whistleblower-spy)

**We Are Anonymous: Inside the Hacker World of LulzSec, Anonymous, and the Global Cyber Insurgency (2012)**
- **Author:** Parmy Olson (Forbes London Bureau Chief)
- **Publisher:** Little, Brown and Company
- **Description:** Exclusive access including interviews with all six core LulzSec members
- **Focus:** Jake Davis (Topiary), Hector Monsegur (Sabu), and the LulzSec splinter group
- **Praise:** Jon Stewart called it "An incredible story"
- **Source:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/We-Are-Inside-LulzSec-Insurgency/dp/0316213527)

### Theoretical Frameworks

**Leaderless Resistance**
- **Concept:** Strategy where independent cells operate without central coordination
- **Characteristics:**
  - Resistant to infiltration and informants
  - No center to destroy
  - Asymmetric warfare principles
- **Application to Anonymous:** The collective exemplifies digital leaderless resistance
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - Leaderless Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaderless_resistance)

**Research on Decentralized Movements**
- Studies on Extinction Rebellion, Occupy Wall Street, and Anonymous examine how leaderless structures function
- Key finding: "Leaderless" does not mean "leadershipless" - leadership is distributed rather than absent
- Challenge: Consensus-building difficulties without central authority
- **Source:** [SAGE Journals - Extinction Rebellion Study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/17427150211005578)

## Related Entities

### LulzSec
- **Formation:** May 2011 (from Anonymous members who conducted HBGary hack)
- **Core Members:** Sabu (Hector Monsegur), Topiary (Jake Davis), Tflow, Kayla, and others
- **Duration:** 50 days ("50 days of lulz")
- **Notable Actions:** Sony Pictures, PBS, CIA website, Arizona Department of Public Safety
- **Dissolution:** Key members arrested after Sabu became FBI informant

### WikiLeaks
- **Relationship:** Mutual support; Anonymous defended WikiLeaks during 2010 pressure campaign
- **Collaboration:** Published Stratfor emails as "Global Intelligence Files"
- **Julian Assange:** Featured in "We Are Legion" documentary

### Telecomix
- **Collaboration:** Worked with Anonymous during Arab Spring to bypass censorship
- **Focus:** Communication tools and circumvention technology
