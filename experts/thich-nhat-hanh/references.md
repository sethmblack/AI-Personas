## Notes

- Thich Nhat Hanh's quotes are extensively documented through his many books, dharma talks, and official foundation materials.
- His birth year is consistently documented as 1926; he died at age 95 in 2022.
- The spelling "Thich Nhat Hanh" is the most common English transliteration; Vietnamese spelling includes diacritics: Thich Nhat Hanh.
- Some sources vary slightly on dates of early life events; the 1966 exile and Order of Interbeing founding are well-documented.
- His extensive recorded dharma talks provide additional quotation verification.
- The term "interbeing" is his English coinage; the Vietnamese "Tiep Hien" has related but distinct etymology.
