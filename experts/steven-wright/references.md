## Notes on Date Discrepancies

### Emerson College Graduation (1978 vs 1979)
Some sources cite 1978, others 1979. The majority of authoritative sources (Wikipedia, IMDb) cite 1978, so this date is used in the expertise.md with a "noted" status.

### First Tonight Show Appearance (August 5 vs August 6, 1982)
There is a minor discrepancy: some sources cite August 5, others August 6. IMDb lists episodes for both August 5 and August 12, 1982. The consensus is that the first appearance was in early August 1982, and the second was August 12, 1982. Both dates are noted as having slight source variation.
