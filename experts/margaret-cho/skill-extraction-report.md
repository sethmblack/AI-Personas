### 1. confessional-to-political-pipeline

**Source Pattern:** The Confessional-to-Political Pipeline framework
**Purpose:** Transform raw personal disclosure into structural political critique, moving from individual suffering to systemic analysis
**Trigger:** "How do I connect my personal story to larger issues?" or "How do I make my experience politically meaningful?"
**Inputs:** Personal experience or trauma to analyze; target audience; desired political insight
**Outputs:** Structured narrative arc moving from personal disclosure to systemic critique with call to defiance
**Reasoning:** This is Cho's core methodology - the engine that powers all her comedy. Five clear, repeatable steps. Used in every major special. Transforms any personal material into politically resonant content. Saves significant time in developing material that connects personal to political. High reusability across all personal narrative work.

### 2. mother-voice-character-construction

**Source Pattern:** The Mother Voice Method framework
**Purpose:** Create a loving character who delivers uncomfortable truths through their cultural perspective, allowing critique without direct judgment
**Trigger:** "How do I create a character based on someone I love?" or "How do I explore family/cultural dynamics through character?"
**Inputs:** Relationship to model (parent, elder, authority); their distinctive speech patterns; cultural context; truth to reveal
**Outputs:** Character voice with specific accent/syntax, thematic purpose, and technique for returning to personal voice with insight
**Reasoning:** Cho's most iconic technique - three decades of refinement. Clear 5-step methodology with technical and thematic elements. Applicable to any character-based comedy or personal narrative. Provides framework for loving critique that avoids mockery. High-value skill for performers, memoirists, and personal essayists.

## MEDIUM Priority

### 3. identity-intersection-analysis

**Source Pattern:** Intersection Mapping framework
**Purpose:** Map how multiple marginalized identities compound and interact, revealing what single-issue analysis misses
**Trigger:** "How do I address identity complexity?" or "How do multiple parts of identity interact?"
**Inputs:** List of relevant identities in situation; context of discrimination or erasure; single-issue framing to critique
**Outputs:** Analysis of compound vulnerabilities, potential solidarities, who gets erased, and perspective from the intersection
**Reasoning:** Core to Cho's approach as someone who exists at multiple intersections (Korean-American, bisexual, woman, survivor). Clear 5-step process. Highly relevant for diverse audiences and contemporary identity discussions. Reusable across any identity-based analysis.

---

### 4. beautiful-ugly-dialectic

**Source Pattern:** The Beautiful/Ugly Dialectic framework
**Purpose:** Hold celebration and self-deprecation simultaneously, refusing audience comfort with either pure confidence or pure vulnerability
**Trigger:** "How do I talk about my flaws without self-hatred?" or "How do I claim power while acknowledging mess?"
**Inputs:** Positive claim to assert; undermining reality to acknowledge; desired comedic tension
**Outputs:** Dialectical statement that holds both extremes, creates comedy through tension, and lands on survival rather than resolution
**Reasoning:** Distinctive Cho technique for body positivity and self-acceptance work. Clear 5-step process. Prevents the two failure modes of personal disclosure (pure bragging, pure self-loathing). Applicable to any vulnerable content creation.
