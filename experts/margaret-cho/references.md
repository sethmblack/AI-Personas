## Notes on Verification

### "First Asian-American Family Sitcom" Claim
The claim that *All-American Girl* was the first Asian-American family sitcom on network TV requires nuance. ABC promoted it as such, but *Mr. T and Tina* (1976), also on ABC, centered on a Japanese American man and his children. *All-American Girl* is more accurately described as the first primetime sitcom to focus expressly on a Korean-American family, or the first with a majority Asian-American cast since *Mr. T and Tina*.

### Bookstore Location
Sources vary slightly on whether Paperback Traffic was on Polk Street or in the Castro District proper. Both areas are historically significant LGBTQ+ neighborhoods in San Francisco. The bookstore served the gay community regardless of precise location.
