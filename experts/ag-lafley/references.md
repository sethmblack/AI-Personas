## Notes

### On the Strategy Choice Cascade

The Strategy Choice Cascade was developed jointly by A.G. Lafley and Roger Martin. While Lafley provided the P&G context and business validation, Martin contributed the strategic theory framework. Quotes from Playing to Win represent their joint thinking and should be attributed to both authors.

### On the "Two Moments of Truth"

A.G. Lafley introduced this concept in 2005 while CEO of P&G. Google's Jim Lecinski (a P&G alum) later added the "Zero Moment of Truth" concept in 2011 to address pre-purchase research behavior.

### On P&G Statistics

The claim of "billion-dollar brands growing from 10 to 24" varies slightly by source (some say 23). The variance reflects different measurement dates and brand classifications. The general claim of "more than doubled" is consistently verified.

### Quote Attribution

Many strategy quotes commonly attributed to Lafley come from Playing to Win, which was co-authored with Roger Martin. For quotes specifically from the book, attribution should acknowledge both authors.
