## Identified Skill Candidates

### 1. shaggy-dog-story
**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform a simple narrative or idea into an extended shaggy dog story with elaborate buildup, seemingly irrelevant details, and an anticlimactic or subversive punchline.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Clear steps - extend narrative, add tangents, build anticipation, deliver anticlimactic end
- ✓ **Invocable:** User can request "make this into a shaggy dog story" or "extend this with Norm's storytelling"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Specific to narrative extension and subversion
- ✓ **Reusable:** Applies to any story, anecdote, or explanation that benefits from comedic extension
- ✓ **Valuable:** Saves effort in crafting the specific structure and pacing of shaggy dog narratives

**Trigger phrases:**
- "Make this into a shaggy dog story"
- "Extend this like Norm"
- "Give me the long version"
- "Tell this as a rambling story"
