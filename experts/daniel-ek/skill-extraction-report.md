### 1. freemium-model-design

**Source Pattern:** Freemium Model Design framework
**Purpose:** Design freemium pricing strategies that use free tiers as aggressive growth engines while building sustainable paths to monetization
**Trigger:** "How should we price this?", "Should we offer a free tier?", "Freemium vs. subscription?", "How do we compete with free alternatives?", "Design pricing model"
**Inputs:** Product/service description, target market, competitive landscape, current pricing (if any), conversion goals
**Outputs:** Freemium model design with: free tier value proposition, conversion triggers, monetization path, projected economics, key metrics to track
**Reasoning:** Clear 4-component framework (free tier, conversion funnel, ad revenue, network effects). Highly actionable with specific metrics (conversion rate, LTV). Used in any pricing decision for digital products. High value - pricing strategy is a critical business decision. Distinct from existing platform skills which focus on ecosystem, not monetization mechanics.

**5-Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Clear components, metrics, and design steps
- Invocable: YES - "Design a freemium model for X"
- Scoped: YES - Focused on pricing strategy, not general business model
- Reusable: YES - Applies to SaaS, apps, content, services
- Valuable: YES - Pricing is critical; saves weeks of strategy work

### 2. two-sided-marketplace-design

**Source Pattern:** Two-Sided Marketplace Design framework
**Purpose:** Design and balance two-sided marketplaces that serve both supply (creators) and demand (consumers) with appropriate tools and value capture
**Trigger:** "Building a marketplace", "How do we serve both sides?", "Creator tools vs. consumer features", "Marketplace economics", "Balance supply and demand"
**Inputs:** Marketplace concept, supply side description, demand side description, current tools for each side, value capture mechanism
**Outputs:** Marketplace design with: supply-side value proposition and tools, demand-side value proposition and tools, matching mechanism, balance assessment, value capture strategy
**Reasoning:** Clear 4-component model (consumer side, creator side, matching layer, value capture). Highly actionable with balance principles. Used whenever building platforms with multiple constituencies. High value - marketplace design failures are common and costly. Distinct from existing platform skills which focus on ecosystem/developer platforms, not marketplace balance.

**5-Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Clear components and balance principles
- Invocable: YES - "Design the marketplace balance for X"
- Scoped: YES - Focused on two-sided marketplace mechanics
- Reusable: YES - Applies to any marketplace business
- Valuable: YES - Marketplace balance is critical success factor

### 3. better-than-piracy-framework

**Source Pattern:** The Better-Than-Piracy Framework
**Purpose:** Analyze entrenched competitors (including free/illegal alternatives) and design strategies to out-compete through superior experience rather than enforcement
**Trigger:** "How do we compete with free?", "They're giving it away", "Can't beat the incumbent", "Users are using workarounds", "Shadow IT problem", "Piracy issue"
**Inputs:** Product/service description, entrenched competitor/alternative description, why users use the inferior solution, current competitive approach
**Outputs:** Competitive strategy with: user motivation analysis, experience gap identification, speed/convenience opportunities, implementation priorities
**Reasoning:** Clear 4-step methodology (understand usage, identify superiority, obsess over gap, make legal option more convenient). Highly actionable with specific focus on 200ms-type experience metrics. Used when facing entrenched free/cheap alternatives. High value - reframes competitive strategy from litigation/enforcement to product excellence. Unique perspective not in existing skills.

**5-Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Clear 4-step process with specific focus areas
- Invocable: YES - "How do I compete with [free alternative]?"
- Scoped: YES - Focused on competing with entrenched/free alternatives
- Reusable: YES - Applies to shadow IT, open source, piracy, workarounds
- Valuable: YES - Reframes competitive thinking; high strategic impact
