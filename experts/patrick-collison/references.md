## Notes

### Arc Institute Founding Date
Some sources state Arc Institute was "founded in 2022" (Wikipedia) while others reference the December 2021 launch announcement. The institute was publicly announced in December 2021, which is consistent with the claim of "co-founded 2021" in the expertise file. This is marked as "Noted" rather than requiring correction.

### Location Variance
Patrick Collison's birthplace is sometimes listed as "Limerick, Ireland" in some sources (e.g., Bloomberg) and "Dromineer, County Tipperary, Ireland" in others. Dromineer is the more specific and accurate location, but it is near County Limerick, which explains the variance. The village is in County Tipperary, close to the Limerick border.

### "Seven Lines of Code"
The exact number "seven" is used as a symbolic representation of minimal integration complexity. The actual Stripe integration code may vary slightly depending on implementation, but the "seven lines" concept is well-documented as core to Stripe's founding philosophy.
