## Key Concepts

### The Trust Battery

Tobi's metaphor for understanding interpersonal trust in organizations.

**How it works:**
- Trust between colleagues is like a phone battery charge
- New relationships start at approximately 50% charge
- Every interaction either charges or depletes the battery slightly
- Positive interactions: meeting commitments, shipping on time, reliable behavior
- Negative interactions: broken promises, chronic lateness, missed deadlines
- Low batteries demand constant attention (like a phone battery low alert)
- High batteries enable autonomy (you don't think about a fully charged phone)

**Why it matters:**
- Allows discussion of trust without involving egos
- Makes feedback concrete: "Your battery dropped 0.1% because you were late"
- Enables honest assessment: "Your trust battery is at 30% - let's diagnose why"
- Separates trust issues from personal attacks

### Team, Not Family

Shopify explicitly rejects the "family" metaphor for corporate culture.

**Key distinctions:**
- Families: You're born into them, can't choose them, can't be "un-familied"
- Teams: You earn your spot, can be cut, compete at the highest level
- Family thinking makes firing poor performers "incredibly hard"
- Team thinking enables "we literally only want the best people in the world"

**From Tobi's internal memo:**
- "Shopify, like any other for-profit company, is not a family. The very idea is preposterous."
- Cautioned against terms like "Shopifam" that give wrong impressions
- Especially harmful for junior employees who haven't worked elsewhere

### Anti-Meeting Culture

Meetings are treated as bugs in the organizational system.

**The January 2023 Calendar Purge:**
- Cancelled all recurring meetings with more than two people
- Approximately 12,000 calendar events deleted across the company
- Wednesdays made entirely meeting-free
- Large meetings (50+ people) restricted to six-hour window on Thursdays
- Called a "chaos monkey" disruption to test organizational resilience

**Underlying philosophy:**
- "No one joins Shopify to sit in meetings. They come to build."
- Builders and crafters need uninterrupted time
- If a meeting matters, people will reschedule it
- If it doesn't matter, deleting it saves everyone's time

### Digital by Default

Shopify's remote work philosophy announced May 21, 2020.

**Key announcement:**
- "As of today, Shopify is a digital by default company."
- "Office centricity is over."
- Most employees would work remotely permanently
- Offices would be limited to 20-25% capacity
- Offices become "on-ramps to the digital workplace" not the workplace itself

**Culture perspective:**
- "If you want to have a great culture, the trick is to evolve it forward with your environment."
- "We cannot go back to the way things were. This isn't a choice; this is the future."
- "We choose to jump in the driver's seat, instead of being passengers to the changes ahead."

### Arm the Rebels

Shopify's positioning against Amazon and centralized marketplaces.

**Origin of the phrase:**
When asked if Shopify was trying to be "the next Amazon," Tobi responded:
- "Amazon is trying to build an empire and Shopify is trying to arm the rebels."
- "Shopify is literally meant to be an alternative to Amazon."
- "Shopify isn't here to beat anyone; we're here to arm the rebels."

**What it means operationally:**
- Enable independent entrepreneurs to compete with giants
- Reduce the friction of starting and running an online business
- Make the tools of e-commerce accessible to everyone
- Level the playing field between small merchants and mega-platforms

### Reversibility Framework

How Tobi categorizes decisions for appropriate deliberation.

**Core principle:**
- Fully reversible decisions: Make as quickly as possible
- Irreversible decisions: Worth really, really understanding
- "You can never un-VC fund yourself"
- Most decisions are more reversible than people treat them

### The Red Queen Race

From Alice in Wonderland - "you have to keep running just to stay still."

**Applied to Shopify:**
- In a company growing 20-40% year over year, everyone must improve by at least that percentage just to re-qualify
- Includes Tobi himself
- This is "exactly the kind of environment that our top performers tell us they want"
- Learning together, surrounded by people on their own growth journey
- Working on meaningful and hard problems

### Chaos Monkey Philosophy

Borrowed from systems engineering - deliberately testing resilience.

**Shopify applications:**
- Calendar purge (2023) tested whether coordination would break without meetings
- Results: Organization discovered which meetings actually mattered
- Staff reported it "felt liberating" once rationale was explained
- Reveals whether systems are robust or merely comfortable

### Blue-Collar Innovation

Tobi's view that innovation is incremental, not dramatic.

**Key quote:**
- "People take this Hollywood view, which is like people in lab coats saying 'eureka' suddenly, and then innovation has happened."
- "Innovation is actually much more blue collar, it's much more vocational."
- "It's the frequent incremental improvement of the things that we care about."
