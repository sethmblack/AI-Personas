#### 1. awkward-sincerity-engine
**Source Pattern:** The Awkward Sincerity Framework (expertise.md)
**Purpose:** Generate comedy and authentic engagement through genuine commitment to vulnerable or embarrassing premises without ironic distance
**Trigger:** When creating characters who must seem unaware of their own awkwardness; exploring social discomfort; finding humor in vulnerability; making content that requires emotional authenticity over polish
**Inputs:** Character premise or embarrassing position, target audience, context for delivery, desired level of discomfort
**Outputs:** Character voice/behavior guidelines, dialogue that maintains sincerity, scene structure that lets awkwardness breathe, guidance on avoiding self-aware "winking"
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Has clear 4-step process (create embarrassing passion, express sincerely to someone, let moment breathe, never wink)
- Invocable: Triggered by requests to "create an awkward character," "find the sincerity," "make it uncomfortable but sweet"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of generating sincere vulnerability; distinct from ironic comedy or mean-spirited mockery
- Reusable: Applies to character creation, dialogue writing, brand voice, content that needs authenticity, presentations that need relatability
- Valuable: Enables comedy and connection that ironic distance cannot achieve; creates recognition rather than superiority in audience

#### 2. lo-fi-aesthetic-framework
**Source Pattern:** The Lo-Fi Aesthetic Philosophy (expertise.md)
**Purpose:** Design content that communicates authenticity and intimacy through deliberate roughness in production values
**Trigger:** When polish would undermine the message; creating nostalgic or found-footage feeling; establishing intimacy through imperfection; content needs to feel "real" rather than "produced"
**Inputs:** Content to be produced, target aesthetic era (80s VHS, early YouTube, public access), available production resources, authenticity goals
**Outputs:** Production guidelines (camera work, lighting, audio, editing), visual style rules, checklist for what to include/avoid, guidance on calibrating roughness level
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Has clear technical elements table (handheld camera, poor lighting, off-center framing, audio issues, dated graphics, jump cuts) with specific purposes
- Invocable: Triggered by requests to "make it lo-fi," "public access style," "VHS aesthetic," "make it feel real"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of aesthetic roughness for authenticity; distinct from actual incompetence or unintentional poor quality
- Reusable: Applies to video production, brand content, social media, presentations, any visual medium where polish might create distance
- Valuable: Changes viewer relationship to content (lean in vs. sit back); creates complicity and intimacy; differentiates from slick competition

### MEDIUM Priority

#### 3. mumblecore-pacing-protocol
**Source Pattern:** The Mumblecore Influence (expertise.md)
**Purpose:** Structure dialogue and scenes with realistic hesitation, interruption, trailing thoughts, and extended awkward pauses
**Trigger:** When natural conversation matters more than efficient dialogue; when awkward pauses serve the comedy or authenticity; when characters should feel like real people talking rather than delivering lines
**Inputs:** Scene context, characters involved, emotional undercurrents, desired pacing (from slightly hesitant to painfully extended)
**Outputs:** Dialogue with verbal fillers and self-corrections, pacing guidance for pauses, direction on when to let moments breathe, examples of trailing sentences and qualified statements
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Has clear dialogue characteristics list (verbal fillers, false starts, trailing off, self-corrections, over-explanation, qualification) with examples
- Invocable: Triggered by requests for "naturalistic dialogue," "let it breathe," "mumblecore pacing," "make the dialogue feel real"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of naturalistic dialogue rhythm; distinct from broader character work or plot structure
- Reusable: Applies to screenwriting, podcast scripts, brand voice, chatbot dialogue, any written conversation that should feel unscripted
- Valuable: Transforms "written" dialogue into recognizable human speech; creates intimacy through imperfection; makes characters feel lived-in
