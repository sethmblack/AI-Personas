## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts
- **Birth date and place:** September 3, 1984, San Diego, California. Confirmed through multiple interviews and official sources.
- **USC education:** Attended USC School of Cinematic Arts, where he met Beck Bennett and Dave McCary. Confirmed in multiple interviews.
- **SNL tenure:** Hired 2013, departed 2022 (Season 47). Nine seasons confirmed through NBC records.
- **Good Neighbor formation:** 2007 at USC. Confirmed by all members in various interviews.
- **Brigsby Bear premiere:** Sundance Film Festival 2017. Festival records confirm.
- **Bruce Chandling character:** Created with Streeter Seidell. Confirmed in interviews.

### Production Information
- **Brigsby Bear:**
  - Director: Dave McCary
  - Writers: Kyle Mooney, Kevin Costello
  - Distributor: Sony Pictures Classics
  - Premiere: Sundance 2017
  - Theatrical release: July 2017

- **Good Neighbor:**
  - Members: Kyle Mooney, Beck Bennett, Nick Rutherford, Dave McCary
  - Platform: YouTube
  - Active years: 2007-2013
  - Channel maintained some activity after SNL hiring

### SNL Specific
- **Hiring year:** 2013 (Season 39)
- **Status change:** Promoted to repertory player 2015 (Season 41)
- **Departure:** 2022 (after Season 47), along with multiple other long-tenured cast members
- **Frequent collaborators:** Beck Bennett (performer), Dave McCary (director)

## Academic and Cultural Context

### Mumblecore Movement
- **Bujalski, Andrew.** *Funny Ha Ha* (2002). Often cited as first mumblecore film.
- **Swanberg, Joe.** Various films (2005-2012). Key mumblecore director.
- **Duplass Brothers.** *The Puffy Chair* (2005), *Baghead* (2008). Mumblecore filmmakers who crossed to mainstream.

Mooney has not explicitly cited mumblecore as an influence, but critics and comedy scholars have noted the stylistic similarities, particularly in dialogue patterns and lo-fi aesthetic.

### YouTube Comedy Evolution
- **Good Neighbor** operated during YouTube's transitional period (2007-2013)
- Pre-Partner Program era through early monetization
- Their success anticipated the platform's later dominance in comedy
- Part of a cohort including Derrick Comedy (Donald Glover), Lonely Island (pre-SNL)

### Public Access Television Reference Points
- **Wayne's World** (SNL, film) - Earlier parody of public access that Mooney builds on
- Actual public access archives (various local cable networks) - Source material for aesthetic
- VHS trading culture of 1980s-90s - Informs "found footage" feeling

## Citation Notes

When citing Kyle Mooney's work:

1. **For SNL sketches:** Include season, episode, and air date when possible
2. **For Good Neighbor videos:** Include original upload date (often visible on YouTube)
3. **For interviews:** Note publication and date; Mooney's statements have evolved over his career
4. **For *Brigsby Bear*:** Distinguish between Sundance premiere (2017) and theatrical release (July 2017)

Primary sources are preferred for factual claims. Interview statements about creative philosophy should be attributed specifically rather than treated as universal truths.
