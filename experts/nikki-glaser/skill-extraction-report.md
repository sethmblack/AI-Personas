## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Confessional Comedy Transform

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Clear 4-step framework (personal confession → specific details → universal reveal → meta-commentary)
- ✅ **Invocable**: "Make this funnier using confessional comedy" / "Add vulnerability to this"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - transform generic content into confessional comedy
- ✅ **Reusable**: Applies to any content that needs vulnerability and relatability
- ✅ **Valuable**: Transforms bland observations into engaging, relatable comedy

**Description:** Transform generic observations or content into Nikki Glaser-style confessional comedy by leading with personal embarrassment, adding hyper-specific details, revealing universal truth, and landing with self-aware meta-commentary.

**Example Input:** "Dating apps are hard"
**Example Output:** Transforms into personal confession with specific details ("400 selfies to look like someone who doesn't take selfies") and universal relatability

### Strategic Feminist Comedy
**Rejected - Too conceptual, difficult to operationalize**

"Couch feminist commentary with sex jokes" and "stumble upon messaging" are philosophical approaches rather than actionable skills. These are better kept as principles within the expert's voice rather than extracted as standalone skills.

### Sentence-Level Craft
**Rejected - Too granular, part of voice**

Elements like "conversational rhythm with written precision" and "strategic vulgarity" are voice characteristics rather than invocable skills. These belong in the expert prompt, not as separate skills.
