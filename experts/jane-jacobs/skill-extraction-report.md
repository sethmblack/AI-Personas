# Skill Extraction Report: Jane Jacobs

**Expert:** Jane Jacobs
**Domain:** Urban Planning, Community & Emergent Systems
**Extraction Date:** 2026-02-10

---

## Extracted Skills

### 1. street-level-observation
**Description:** Diagnose urban problems by walking neighborhoods and watching what actually happens, rather than relying on plans or statistics.

**Validation:**
- [x] Actionable - Clear methodology: walk at different times, watch who's there, note what's open
- [x] Invocable - User asks "What's wrong with this neighborhood?" or "Why doesn't this area work?"
- [x] Scoped - Focused on observation methodology, not full urban analysis
- [x] Reusable - Applies to any urban or community context
- [x] Valuable - Grounds analysis in reality rather than theory

**Source Concepts:**
- The sidewalk ballet
- Walk the streets at different hours
- Empirical observation vs. theoretical planning
- Trust what you see over what planners say

---

### 2. mixed-use-analysis
**Description:** Evaluate how the separation or combination of uses (residential, commercial, industrial, civic) affects street life and neighborhood vitality.

**Validation:**
- [x] Actionable - Framework: identify primary uses, map hours of activity, find dead zones
- [x] Invocable - User asks "Should we zone this for..." or "Why is this area dead?"
- [x] Scoped - Focused specifically on use mixing, not all four generators
- [x] Reusable - Applies to any zoning or development question
- [x] Valuable - Counters default single-use planning

**Source Concepts:**
- Mixed primary uses
- All-hours activity
- Single-use zoning kills streets
- Diversity of uses creates diversity of people

---

### 3. eyes-on-the-street
**Description:** Analyze natural surveillance and street life as the primary source of urban safety, and design for conditions that create it.

**Validation:**
- [x] Actionable - Checklist: ground-floor activity, windows, reasons to be present, hours of use
- [x] Invocable - User asks "This area feels unsafe" or "How do we make it safer?"
- [x] Scoped - Focused on safety through design, not policing
- [x] Reusable - Applies to any street, neighborhood, or development
- [x] Valuable - Proven by empirical research

**Source Concepts:**
- Natural surveillance
- Shopkeepers as watchers
- Street-facing apartments
- Continuous presence deters crime

---

### 4. emergent-order-recognition
**Description:** See the hidden systems and self-organizing patterns in apparent urban disorder, rather than imposing order from above.

**Validation:**
- [x] Actionable - Framework: identify what's working despite seeming chaotic, trace the hidden logic
- [x] Invocable - User asks "This seems chaotic" or "How does this work?"
- [x] Scoped - Focused on recognizing emergent systems, not all complex systems
- [x] Reusable - Applies to urban, organizational, and social contexts
- [x] Valuable - Counters the instinct to impose top-down order

**Source Concepts:**
- Marvelous order in seeming disorder
- Bottom-up vs. top-down
- What looks like chaos contains hidden logic
- Trust the emergent over the planned

---

## Skills NOT Extracted

### "community-organizing"
**Reason:** While Jacobs was an effective organizer, her specific methods were situational (fighting Moses, stopping expressways). The transferable insight is better captured in the observation and analysis skills.

### "economic-development-analysis"
**Reason:** Her economic ideas (import replacement, cities first) are important but would require a separate deep dive. The urban planning skills are more distinctive and actionable.

### "four-generators-audit"
**Reason:** The four generators are better understood as a framework within mixed-use-analysis and street-level-observation than as a separate skill. Would be too similar to existing skills.
