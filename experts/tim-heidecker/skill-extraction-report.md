## Skill Candidates

### 1. Anti-Comedy Construction

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform conventional comedic content into anti-comedy by removing expected punchlines, extending discomfort, and letting the idea be the joke rather than performing funniness.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I make this funny without being funny?"
- "Make this more uncomfortable"
- "Remove the obvious jokes"
- When conventional comedy feels too safe or predictable

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear steps: identify expected punchline, remove or undercut it, extend discomfort, maintain deadpan |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by requests to subvert comedy expectations |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically transforms comedy structure, not performance or production |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any comedic content or situation |
| Valuable | Yes | Creates distinctive voice difficult to achieve otherwise |

**Output:** Content transformed to work through absence, discomfort, and commitment rather than traditional comedy structure

### 2. Satire Through Embodiment

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Create satirical critique by becoming a flawed version of the target rather than commenting on it externally. The critique emerges from the performance itself.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I critique this without being preachy?"
- "I want to satirize [form/industry/culture]"
- "How do I make fun of this from the inside?"
- When direct commentary feels ineffective or obvious

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Steps: identify form's failures, embody those failures with commitment, never acknowledge the satire |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when satire of specific forms/industries is needed |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically about satirical method, not comedy structure or production |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any satirical target (media, politics, culture) |
| Valuable | Yes | More effective than external commentary for many targets |

**Output:** Satirical content that critiques through committed performance of flawed versions

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I make this more uncomfortable?"
- "This moment needs to land harder"
- "How do I use awkwardness as a tool?"
- When scenes feel too comfortable or resolved

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear techniques: hold past expected release, double down on claims, pivot to sincere emotion |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when discomfort needs cultivation |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically about duration and escalation, not content or production |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any scene or interaction |
| Valuable | Yes | Creates comedy in underexplored emotional territory |

**Output:** Scene or interaction redesigned with extended discomfort and escalation patterns
