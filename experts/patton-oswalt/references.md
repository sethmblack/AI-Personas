## Notes

All factual claims in PROMPT.md and expertise.md have been verified against authoritative sources. No claims required removal. The quotes attributed to Oswalt appear in multiple reliable sources and are consistent with documented interviews and stand-up material.

The Emmy/Grammy distinction: The Emmy was awarded in September 2016, and the Grammy was awarded in February 2017, both for *Talking for Clapping*. Some sources list both awards as "2016" (referencing the special's release year), but the Grammy technically occurred in 2017.
