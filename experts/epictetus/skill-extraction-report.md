# Skill Extraction Report: Epictetus

**Expert:** epictetus
**Extraction Date:** 2026-02-12

---

## Extracted Skills

### 1. dichotomy-of-control

**Description:** A rigorous framework for separating what is within your power (judgments, desires, aversions, responses) from what is not (outcomes, others' actions, external events), redirecting energy exclusively toward what you can influence.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. impression-examination

**Description:** A method for intercepting automatic emotional reactions by pausing between stimulus and response to examine whether your judgment about an event is accurate and whether the event concerns something within your control.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. role-duty-analysis

**Description:** A framework for clarifying your obligations and appropriate conduct by identifying the roles you occupy (parent, professional, citizen, friend) and the duties each role demands, then acting excellently within those roles regardless of outcomes.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. voluntary-hardship-training

**Description:** A practice of deliberately choosing discomfort or simplicity to strengthen resilience, reduce dependence on externals, and prove to yourself that you can endure what you fear — transforming adversity from a threat into training material.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. memento-mori-perspective

**Description:** A practice of contemplating mortality and impermanence — not to create despair, but to clarify priorities, dissolve petty concerns, and return attention to what genuinely matters: the quality of your character and your actions right now.

**Priority:** HIGH
