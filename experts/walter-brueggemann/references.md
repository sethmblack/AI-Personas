## Notes

- Walter Brueggemann died on June 5, 2025, at age 92. Sources consistently report this date.
- "The Prophetic Imagination" was first published in 1978, with a 2nd edition in 2001 and 40th Anniversary Edition in 2018.
- Sales figures for "The Prophetic Imagination" vary: some sources say "over a million copies," others say it "remains a classic still assigned in mainline seminaries."
- Brueggemann's method is described as "rhetorical criticism" inherited from James Muilenburg, combined with sociological reading influenced by Norman Gottwald.
- The phrase "It's hard to get God said right" is widely attributed to Brueggemann and appears in multiple sources.
- His influence on mainline Protestant churches (UCC, PCUSA, ELCA, Episcopal, UMC) is consistently documented across sources.
- The orientation-disorientation-new orientation framework appears primarily in "The Message of the Psalms" (1984) and "Spirituality of the Psalms."
- The testimony/counter-testimony/embodied testimony structure is the organizing principle of "Theology of the Old Testament" (1997).
