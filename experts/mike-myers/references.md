# Mike Myers - References

## Primary Sources

### Film Works (as Writer/Actor/Producer)

**Wayne's World:**
- *Wayne's World* (1992). Dir. Penelope Spheeris. Paramount Pictures. Box office: $183.1M worldwide.
- *Wayne's World 2* (1993). Dir. Stephen Surjik. Paramount Pictures. Box office: $48.2M worldwide.

**Austin Powers Trilogy:**
- *Austin Powers: International Man of Mystery* (1997). Dir. Jay Roach. New Line Cinema. Box office: $67.7M.
- *Austin Powers: The Spy Who Shagged Me* (1999). Dir. Jay Roach. New Line Cinema. Box office: $312M.
- *Austin Powers in Goldmember* (2002). Dir. Jay Roach. New Line Cinema. Box office: $296.6M.

**Shrek Franchise (Voice):**
- *Shrek* (2001). Dir. Andrew Adamson & Vicky Jenson. DreamWorks Animation. Box office: $487.9M. Academy Award: Best Animated Feature.
- *Shrek 2* (2004). Dir. Andrew Adamson, Kelly Asbury, Conrad Vernon. DreamWorks Animation. Box office: $935.2M.
- *Shrek the Third* (2007). Dir. Chris Miller & Raman Hui. DreamWorks Animation. Box office: $799M.
- *Shrek Forever After* (2010). Dir. Mike Mitchell. DreamWorks Animation. Box office: $752.6M.

**Other Films:**
- *So I Married an Axe Murderer* (1993). Dir. Thomas Schlamme. TriStar Pictures.
- *54* (1998). Dir. Mark Christopher. Miramax.
- *The Love Guru* (2008). Dir. Marco Schnabel. Paramount Pictures.
- *Bohemian Rhapsody* (2018). Dir. Bryan Singer. 20th Century Fox. (Uncredited appearance as Ray Foster, EMI executive)

### Television (Saturday Night Live)

**SNL Cast Member:** 1989-1995 (6 seasons)

**Major Recurring Characters Created:**
- Wayne Campbell (Wayne's World) - First appearance: February 18, 1989
- Dieter (Sprockets) - German art show host
- Linda Richman (Coffee Talk) - Jewish New York talk show host
- Simon - British child in bathtub

**Notable Hosting Appearances:**
- Hosted SNL after leaving cast: October 26, 1996; October 21, 2000; October 4, 2014

### Stand-Up and Stage

**Second City Toronto:** 1986-1988
- Where Wayne Campbell character was created and developed
- Training ground for character-driven sketch comedy

- **Voytilla, Stuart and Scott Petri.** *Writing the Comedy Film: Make 'Em Laugh*. Michael Wiese Productions, 2003. ISBN: 978-0941188920.
  - Screenwriting analysis; examines Wayne's World's meta-humor structure

## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts

- **Birth**: Michael John Myers, May 25, 1963, Scarborough, Ontario, Canada
- **SNL tenure**: 1989-1995 (6 seasons) - confirmed by NBC archives
- **Box office figures**: Verified through Box Office Mojo and The Numbers databases
- **Academy Award**: Shrek won Best Animated Feature, 2002 (74th Academy Awards)
- **Oxford English Dictionary entries**: "Not!" (1992), "Shagadelic" (1999) - confirmed in OED

### Well-Documented but Not Independently Verified

- **Second City Toronto dates**: 1986-1988 widely reported but exact dates vary by source
- **Shrek re-recording cost**: $4-5 million estimate appears in multiple sources but no official DreamWorks confirmation
- **Chris Farley 80-90% recording completion**: Reported in documentaries but exact percentage varies

### Disputed or Uncertain

- **Exact inspiration for characters**: Myers has given varying accounts of character origins in different interviews over decades
- **Whether "Not!" originated with Myers or was already in youth slang**: Some sources suggest Myers popularized existing slang rather than inventing it
- **Contract disputes on various films**: Numerous reports of Myers being "difficult" but specifics often come from anonymous sources

### Sources Requiring Caution

- **Tabloid reports about on-set behavior**: Multiple films (Austin Powers 3, The Love Guru, Shrek) had reports of difficult behavior; often from unnamed sources
- **The Love Guru failure narratives**: Film was commercial and critical failure; post-hoc analyses should be treated as interpretation
- **"Feud" stories with Dana Carvey**: Tabloid reports of Wayne's World tension; both have denied in later interviews
