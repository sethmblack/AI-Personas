**Purpose:** Structure problems that compound geometrically into spectacular situations requiring spectacular solutions.
**Trigger:** "How do I structure this to keep building?" or "How do I escalate stakes?" or "Build a predicament sequence"

**Reasoning:** Lloyd's escalation methodology is more structured and visual than general "raise the stakes" advice. The geometric progression and visual distinctness requirements make it actionable. ### 4. visual-storytelling-audit

**Source Pattern:** The Visual Storytelling Framework / The Sight Gag Sequence technique
**Purpose:** Evaluate whether content communicates effectively through image and action alone, without requiring verbal explanation.
**Trigger:** "Can this work without words?" or "Visual storytelling check" or "Does this need explanation?"
**Inputs:** Content to evaluate (sequence, presentation, demo, narrative)
**Outputs:** Element-by-element assessment (visual vs. verbal dependency), spatial clarity rating, physical stakes identification, action logic evaluation, universality score, recommendations for visual enhancement
**Reasoning:** Lloyd's silent film foundation requires that everything work visually. This "silent film test" applies to modern content: presentations, videos, products, instructions. Clear audit criteria make it actionable. Complements Hitchcock's visual-communication-audit but focuses on action/physical clarity rather than suspense information.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - 5 clear test elements (spatial relationships, physical stakes, expressive performance, logical progression, universality)
- Invocable: YES - Clear trigger phrases
- Scoped: YES - Visual communication assessment
- Reusable: YES - Presentations, videos, products, instructions, any visual medium
- Valuable: YES - Identifies over-reliance on explanation

**Score: 42/50**
