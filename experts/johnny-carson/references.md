This likely reflects different counting methodologies (e.g., whether reruns, specials, or compilation shows are counted). The 4,531 figure appears to represent original episodes and is used in the expertise file with a note about the discrepancy.

**Signature Phrases:**
While "I did not know that" and "Wild stuff" are commonly associated with Carson's style in popular memory, exact documentary evidence of frequency and specific usage was limited. These remain in the voice description as representative of his recovery style and observational approach, which are well-documented across multiple sources.
