## Skill Candidates Identified

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. carson-save-technique
**Rationale:** The Carson Recovery Framework is highly actionable with clear steps. This is Carson's signature technique for transforming failure into comedy.

**Procedure:**
1. Acknowledge the failure immediately
2. Comment on the failure (e.g., "I did not know that")
3. Transform it - the bombing becomes the bit
4. Move forward without dwelling or explaining

**Trigger conditions:**
- Material falls flat
- Joke doesn't land
- Presentation fails
- Awkward moment needs recovery

**Value:** Transforms failure into unexpected success, applicable to any communication context where recovery is needed.

---

#### 2. carson-monologue-structure
**Rationale:** Clear, repeatable framework for building comedy sequences with momentum.

**Procedure:**
1. Opening: Topical reference everyone knows
2. Body: Series of jokes building in intensity
3. Callback: Reference earlier material for continuity
4. Button: Strong finish that signals "moving on"

**Trigger conditions:**
- Need to structure comedy material
- Creating presentation opener
- Building talk or speech with humor
- Organizing topical commentary

**Value:** Provides architecture for comedy that builds momentum rather than random joke collection.

---

#### 3. carson-interview-architecture
**Rationale:** Four-phase framework for interviews that balance entertainment and genuine conversation.

**Procedure:**
1. Phase 1: Let the guest tell their story
2. Phase 2: Find the comedy angle (usually with a throwaway line)
3. Phase 3: Set up their best moment
4. Phase 4: Get out of the way

**Trigger conditions:**
- Conducting interviews
- Podcast conversations
- Panel discussions
- Guest appearances

**Value:** Creates conditions for guests to shine while maintaining entertainment value. Applicable to any interview context.

---

### MEDIUM PRIORITY

#### 4. carnac-inversion-technique
**Rationale:** The answer-before-question format is a specific structural technique for creating absurdist humor.

**Procedure:**
1. Present the answer first
2. Build anticipation
3. Reveal the question
4. Exploit disconnect for comedy

**Trigger conditions:**
- Need for absurdist humor
- Game show format
- Comedy sketch structure
- Subverting expectations

**Value:** Demonstrates structural inversion as comedy technique, but narrower application than other skills.

---

### LOW PRIORITY

#### 5. timing-and-pause-technique
**Rationale:** While Carson's timing is legendary, teaching "timing" is extremely difficult without live feedback and performance context. This is more craft than extractable procedure.

**Analysis:** Timing is THE core of Carson's technique, but it's not procedurally teachable through a skill prompt. It requires:
- Real-time audience feedback
- Years of practice
- Intuitive feel for rhythm
- Context-dependent judgment

**Decision:** NOT extracting as standalone skill. Timing advice should remain embedded in voice definition and transformation examples.

---

#### 6. reaction-shot-technique
**Rationale:** Facial expressions and camera awareness are performance techniques, not text-based procedures.

**Analysis:** The "stare" and reaction shots are visual performance techniques that don't translate to text-based AI assistance. While important to Carson's style, they can't be invoked as a procedural skill.

**Decision:** NOT extracting. Keep as reference in expertise.md for understanding Carson's approach.
