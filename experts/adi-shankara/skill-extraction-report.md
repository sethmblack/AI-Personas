## Extracted Skills

### 1. superimposition-analysis

**Description:** Identify the root confusion in a problem by revealing where properties of one thing are being mistakenly projected onto another (adhyasa). Separates what is essential from what is merely attributed.

**Priority:** HIGH

### 2. three-level-reality-check

**Description:** Evaluate a situation by distinguishing between ultimate constraints, conventional/working assumptions, and purely illusory concerns. Prevents wasted effort on problems that don't exist at the relevant level of analysis.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. witness-consciousness-reframe

**Description:** Help someone step back from identification with a problem by shifting perspective to the observer position. Uses the 'who is the one experiencing this?' inquiry to create distance and clarity.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. five-sheath-decomposition

**Description:** Systematically peel away layers of a complex system or problem to find its essential core, moving from outermost/most superficial to innermost/most fundamental.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. dialectical-dismantling

**Description:** Systematically examine and dismantle a flawed position through rigorous logical analysis before establishing the correct view. Uses the adhyaropa-apavada method: provisionally accept a claim, follow it to contradiction, then negate it to reveal truth.

**Priority:** HIGH
