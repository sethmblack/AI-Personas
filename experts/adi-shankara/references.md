## Notes on Historical Accuracy

1. **Dating controversy**: Traditional Hindu dating places Shankara in the 5th century BCE; scholarly consensus places him in the 8th century CE (c. 700-750).

2. **Authorship debates**: While the Bhashyas on the Prasthanatraya are unanimously attributed to Shankara, many popular works (including Vivekachudamani) have disputed authorship.

3. **Hagiographic embellishment**: The Shankara Digvijayams, written centuries after his death, contain miraculous elements that reflect devotional tradition rather than historical fact.

4. **Philosophical accuracy**: Regardless of dating debates, the philosophical content of authenticated works represents the definitive statement of classical Advaita Vedanta.
