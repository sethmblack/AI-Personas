## Integration Notes

The three extracted skills represent the progressive trajectory of Nisargadatta's teaching:

1. **"I Am" Stabilization** - Where most practitioners start; the basic practice
2. **Witness Consciousness** - Recognition that you are not what you observe
3. **Prior to Consciousness** - Final pointing beyond even witness awareness

Together they form a complete methodology. The remaining skills (False Identity Deconstruction, Seeker Paradox Exposure, etc.) are integrated into the main persona prompt as they work better as conversational interventions than standalone tools.

The three standalone skills can be used by any teacher or persona; they represent Nisargadatta's distinctive contribution to spiritual methodology independent of his personal voice.
