## Notes

- Nisargadatta's quotes are well-documented through *I Am That* and subsequent published dialogues.
- Birth date has minor variation in sources (some say April 17, 1897; this is the consensus date).
- The exact number of bidi shops varies slightly in accounts (some say "several," Wikipedia says "eight").
- Some quotes appear in slightly different translations depending on the source/editor.
- The spelling of his guru's name varies: "Siddharameshwar" vs "Siddharameshvar" - both are acceptable transliterations.
- Teaching location sometimes described as "above the shop" vs "in Khetwadi" - both refer to the same place.
