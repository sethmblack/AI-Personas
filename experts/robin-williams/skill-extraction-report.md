### 2. Free Association Ideation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** A brainstorming method that follows associative connections (sound, meaning, emotional resonance) rather than logical progression, creating unexpected insights through lateral thinking.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Can be structured as: (1) Start with seed concept, (2) Follow first association, (3) Let each idea trigger next without judgment, (4) Continue for set time/iterations, (5) Review connections for insights
- ✓ **Invocable:** Triggered by "brainstorm," "think creatively about," "find unexpected connections," or "what if we approached this differently?"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Single responsibility - generating ideas through associative thinking
- ✓ **Reusable:** Product naming, problem-solving, creative writing, innovation workshops, marketing campaigns
- ✓ **Valuable:** Breaks conventional thought patterns; generates novel combinations that linear thinking misses

**Estimated Usage:** High - applicable to all creative and strategic work

### 3. Emotional Pivot Technique

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** A writing/communication method that deliberately shifts between comedy and sincerity to create emotional depth and maintain audience engagement.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Can be structured: (1) Identify serious point to make, (2) Lead with humor/lightness, (3) Pivot to sincerity at key moment, (4) Optional: return to levity to prevent heaviness
- ✓ **Invocable:** Triggered by "make this more engaging," "balance humor and seriousness," or "this feels too heavy/too light"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Single responsibility - managing tonal shifts for emotional impact
- ✓ **Reusable:** Public speaking, writing (articles, scripts, books), difficult conversations, presentations
- ✓ **Valuable:** Makes serious topics accessible; prevents tonal monotony; builds trust through vulnerability

**Estimated Usage:** Medium - useful but more specialized than the other two
