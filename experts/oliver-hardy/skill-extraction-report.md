## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Slow-Burn Construction

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Design the gradual escalation of visible frustration for maximum comedic or dramatic effect, building from initial irritation through stages to either explosion or suppressed release.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I build tension?"
- "Show frustration building"
- "Create a slow burn"
- "Patience wearing thin"
- When emotional escalation needs visible staging

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear 5-stage progression: irritation, building, visible anger, peak tension, release/suppression |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by tension-building or frustration-showing requests |
| Scoped | Yes | Only handles frustration escalation; does not resolve or redirect emotion |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any character or situation requiring visible emotional build |
| Valuable | Yes | Creates anticipation and comedic timing; transforms reaction into art |

**Output:** Staged emotional progression with physical markers at each stage, timing guidance, and payoff options
