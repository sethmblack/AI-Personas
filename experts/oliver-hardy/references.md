## Notes

- The catchphrase is commonly misquoted as "another fine mess" but the actual line is "another nice mess"
- Hardy's "D'oh!" exclamation is sometimes cited as the origin of Homer Simpson's catchphrase, but Dan Castellaneta has cited James Finlayson's elongated "D'ooooh!" as the direct inspiration, with Hardy as secondary influence
- Stan Laurel's quote about not performing without Hardy is widely circulated but appears in various forms; the sentiment is consistently verified across sources
- Hardy's nickname "Babe" (reportedly from Babe Ruth) is well-documented in biographies
- Weight estimates vary in sources from 250-350 pounds; 300 pounds represents the commonly cited figure at his peak film years
