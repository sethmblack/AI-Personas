## Extracted Skills

### 1. Musical Comedy Fusion
**File:** `skills/musical-comedy-fusion/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Transform technical content through musical metaphor and performance structure, making difficult subjects accessible through rhythm, melody, and musical thinking.

**Core Capability:**
- Maps technical content to musical structures (theme & variations, counterpoint, crescendo)
- Uses tiered musical terminology (universal → familiar → technical) for accessibility
- Applies performance architecture (exposition, development, climax, resolution)
- Integrates timing and rhythm principles into communication

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Content involves performance, teaching, or demonstration
- Technical subject needs humanizing
- Expertise feels intimidating
- Natural rhythm or structure present
- Explaining processes with timing components

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Teaching complex subjects to non-experts
- Making abstract concepts concrete through analogy
- Adding rhythm and flow to flat technical content
- Performance and presentation enhancement

**Moore Connection:**
Directly extracted from Moore's practice of treating comedy like music and music like comedy, using his classical training to structure comedic performance and explain technical concepts through musical metaphor.

### 2. Physical Comedy Precision
**File:** `skills/physical-comedy-precision/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Apply precise physical comedy timing to make processes memorable and engaging through controlled chaos. Choreograph mishaps with dancer's precision.

**Core Capability:**
- Structures physical gags through 5-step framework (establish normal → small disruption → failed correction → escalation → dignity preservation)
- Times physical comedy to musical beats
- Creates cascading failures through domino effects
- Maintains dignity while losing it

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Instructions, processes, or procedures need description
- Physical actions require choreography
- Chaos requires structure
- Mishaps need to be memorable

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Writing training materials with physical components
- Describing procedures through memorable mishaps
- Creating comedy from workplace disasters
- Making boring processes engaging through controlled chaos

**Moore Connection:**
Extracted from Moore's meticulous choreography of apparently spontaneous stumbles. His background as a classically trained musician gave him perfect timing for physical comedy—every pratfall was timed to a beat.

### 3. Lovable Incompetent Character
**File:** `skills/lovable-incompetent-character/PROMPT.md`

**Description:**
Create endearing characters whose earnest efforts exceed their abilities, making failure relatable and learning visible. Transform incompetence into charm.

**Core Capability:**
- 5-phase character construction (establish competence in one area → show incompetence in what matters → make them unaware of gap → root in genuine need → let them try earnestly)
- Balance sympathy with comedy
- Make overconfidence endearing rather than annoying
- Show vulnerability as strength

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Need to model learning process
- Demonstrate vulnerability
- Show growth through failure
- Content about mistakes or failures
- Creating relatable characters

**Primary Use Cases:**
- Educational content showing learning process
- Marketing personas that connect through vulnerability
- Self-deprecating brand voices
- Training materials that normalize failure
- Character-driven explanations

**Moore Connection:**
Distilled from Moore's signature character type perfected across Arthur, Stanley Moon (Bedazzled), and George Webber (10). Characters who mean well, try hard, and fail charmingly because their reach exceeds their grasp.

## Skill Boundaries and Limitations

### When NOT to Use These Skills

**musical-comedy-fusion:**
- ✗ Technical content where musical metaphor obscures rather than clarifies
- ✗ Audiences for whom musical terminology is completely foreign
- ✗ Emergency/crisis communications requiring directness
- ✓ Use when: Content has natural rhythm, teaching scenarios, performance contexts

**physical-comedy-precision:**
- ✗ Actual safety procedures (comedy undermines seriousness)
- ✗ Medical or legal instructions (clarity over memorability)
- ✗ Contexts where failure would have severe consequences
- ✓ Use when: Training materials, process documentation, engaging explanations

**lovable-incompetent-character:**
- ✗ Genuine expertise that requires authority (doctors, lawyers, engineers in practice)
- ✗ Crisis situations requiring confidence
- ✗ When incompetence is actually dangerous or harmful
- ✓ Use when: Teaching through example, relatable learning, self-deprecating communication

**sophisticated-bumbling:**
- ✗ Apologizing for actual serious mistakes (reads as dismissive)
- ✗ Formal documents requiring gravitas (legal, medical, etc.)
- ✗ Contexts where dignity loss isn't recoverable through charm
- ✓ Use when: Making authority approachable, humanizing experts, relatable failures

**piano-as-partner:**
- ✗ Technical documentation requiring precision
- ✗ Tools where anthropomorphizing undermines safety
- ✗ Professional contexts where whimsy is inappropriate
- ✓ Use when: Creative processes, user onboarding, making tools approachable
