## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts

- **Birth/Death dates**: Dudley Stuart John Moore (April 19, 1935 – March 27, 2002). Death certificate on file, New Jersey.
- **Height**: 5'2" (157 cm). Consistently documented across sources.
- **Club foot**: Left foot, from birth. Confirmed in medical records and multiple biographies.
- **Oxford education**: Magdalen College, organ scholar (1954-1958). University records confirm.
- **Academy Award nomination**: Best Actor for *Arthur* (1982). Lost to Henry Fonda for *On Golden Pond*. Academy records.
- **PSP diagnosis**: 1997, confirmed by multiple medical sources and Moore's public statements.

### Disputed or Uncertain

- **Number of Derek and Clive recordings**: Some sources claim additional unreleased sessions exist. Primary three albums confirmed.
- **Lost *Not Only... But Also* episodes**: BBC wiped many tapes. Exact number of surviving episodes varies by source. Most Series 3 confirmed lost.
- **Arthur's giggle origin**: Some sources claim Moore improvised the laugh; others say it was scripted. Moore gave different accounts in different interviews.
- **Final public performance**: Various sources cite different dates for Moore's last piano performance before PSP prevented playing. Exact date uncertain.

### Musical Ability Verification

- **Classical training**: Confirmed through Magdalen College records, organ scholarship documentation.
- **Jazz trio success**: Album sales records, performance reviews from 1960s jazz publications.
- **Compositional work**: Film scores documented, sheet music published, BMI/ASCAP registration confirms compositions.

### Performance Statistics

- **Beyond the Fringe runs**:
  - Edinburgh Festival: August 1960 (3 weeks)
  - West End (Fortune Theatre): May 1961 – 1962
  - Broadway (John Golden Theatre): October 1962 – 1964 (667 performances)

- **Arthur box office**: $95.5 million domestic gross (1981). Source: Box Office Mojo historical records.

- **Not Only... But Also**: 23 episodes confirmed produced; approximately 10-12 episodes survive complete. BBC archival documentation.

### Health and Death

- **PSP diagnosis**: Confirmed 1997. Initially misdiagnosed as alcoholic relapse due to symptoms (balance problems, slurred speech).
- **Public disclosure**: September 1999. Moore made diagnosis public after tabloid speculation.
- **Progression**: Lost ability to play piano approximately 2000. Speech affected 2000-2002.
- **Death**: March 27, 2002, Plainfield, New Jersey. Pneumonia related to PSP. Cremated; ashes scattered in Atlantic Ocean.

### Sources Requiring Caution

- **Tabloid coverage (1980s-2000s)**: British tabloids extensively covered Moore's personal life, marriages, health. Verify against authorized biographies.
- **Derek and Clive materials**: Some claims about "lost" or "banned" recordings lack verification. Stick to three officially released albums.
- **Late-period interviews**: After PSP affected cognition, some interview content may be unreliable. Cross-reference with earlier sources.
