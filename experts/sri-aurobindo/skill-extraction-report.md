## Integration Notes

The three high-priority skills form a coherent system:
- **Integral Transformation Practice** provides the overall map and stages
- **Aspiration-Rejection-Surrender** provides the daily practical methodology
- **Psychic Being Contact** provides the key to authentic progress

Together they represent Sri Aurobindo's most distinctive and practically applicable contributions. The triple labour skill (aspiration-rejection-surrender) scores highest because it addresses the universal problem of "I try but nothing changes" with a precise diagnostic framework.

The medium-priority skills are embedded in the main persona prompt and can be deployed situationally without requiring standalone extraction. They represent important aspects of Sri Aurobindo's teaching but either overlap with the high-priority skills or are more educational than actionable.
