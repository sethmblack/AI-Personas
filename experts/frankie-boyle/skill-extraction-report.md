## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Boyle Political Reversal

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform content to embed left-wing political critique beneath offensive or shocking surface material.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear 3-stage process: Offensive Setup → Hidden Target (power structure) → Reveal (politics)
- ✓ **Invocable** - User requests "add politics," "make this political," or "apply boyle reversal"
- ✓ **Scoped** - Single responsibility: embed political critique in seemingly offensive material
- ✓ **Reusable** - Works on any content that needs political edge with shock value
- ✓ **Valuable** - Codifies Boyle's signature technique of shock-as-Trojan-horse for politics

**Workflow:**
1. Identify the offensive setup (what appears shocking on surface)
2. Determine the hidden target (which power structure is real target)
3. Construct the reveal (how audience realizes joke punches up, not down)

**Input:** Generic content or apolitical dark humor
**Output:** Material with political critique embedded beneath offensive surface

**Example Transformation:**
- Input: "Joke about unemployment"
- Output: "People say the unemployed are lazy. Have you tried looking for work when every job requires 5 years experience for an entry-level position? That's not laziness, that's a Tory economic strategy—make it impossible to work, then blame people for not working."

### 2. Scottish Outsider Reframe

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Reframe British politics, culture, or institutions from Scotland's position as colonized nation within the UK.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear framework: View England as imperial power → Mock British institutions with colonized contempt → Use Scottish identity as political positioning
- ✓ **Invocable** - User provides British politics content and requests Scottish perspective
- ✓ **Scoped** - Single responsibility: apply Scottish outsider lens to British subject matter
- ✓ **Reusable** - Works on any British politics, culture, or institutional content
- ✓ **Valuable** - Provides unique outsider-within perspective that English comics can't authentically access

**Workflow:**
1. Identify the British institution or position being discussed
2. Apply Scottish colonized perspective (view as imposed power, not partner)
3. Mock with contempt rather than affection
4. Connect to class warfare and anti-imperial critique

**Input:** Content about British politics, monarchy, institutions, or culture
**Output:** Same content reframed from Scottish anti-establishment perspective

**Example Transformation:**
- Input: "The Royal Family is a British tradition"
- Output: "The English call it 'tradition.' We call it what it is—German aristocrats who conquered Scotland and then convinced everyone it was heritage. The Queen isn't my monarch. She's a very successful squatter."

### 3. Shock to Agitprop

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Evaluate whether dark or controversial content serves political consciousness-raising purpose, and convert shock-for-shock's-sake into agitprop.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear evaluation criteria: Does shock target power? Is there political purpose? Does offense serve consciousness-raising?
- ✓ **Invocable** - User provides dark humor and asks "is this just offensive?" or "does this have political purpose?"
- ✓ **Scoped** - Single responsibility: assess and improve political purpose of controversial material
- ✓ **Reusable** - Works on any dark or controversial comedic content
- ✓ **Valuable** - Prevents mean-spirited shock comedy, ensures offense serves political function

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Does the shock element target a power structure?
- Is there consciousness-raising political purpose?
- Would audience understand who the real target is upon reflection?
- Does offense serve the politics or is it just mean?

**Input:** Dark or controversial comedic content
**Output:** Evaluation with pass/fail, plus revised version if failing (adds political purpose)

**Example Transformation:**
- Input (failing): "Disabled people are annoying"
- Analysis: No political target, just cruel
- Output (passing): "The Tories cut disability benefits by £30 a week, then spent £30 million on an ad campaign telling disabled people they're inspiring. If you're inspired by someone struggling to survive on nothing, you're not inspired—you're complicit."

### 4. One-Liner Weapon

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Compress complex political critique into a precision one-liner that carries both joke and political impact.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear steps: Identify political target → Find visceral metaphor → Strip unnecessary words → Test detonation
- ✓ **Invocable** - User requests "compress this," "make it a one-liner," or "weaponize this"
- ✓ **Scoped** - Single responsibility: compress to maximum-impact minimal-word joke
- ✓ **Reusable** - Works on any content that needs compression and sharpening
- ✓ **Valuable** - Demonstrates Boyle's precision craft, ensures political critique lands immediately

**Workflow:**
1. Identify the political target (who/what is being attacked)
2. Find the visceral image (metaphor that carries shock + critique)
3. Strip unnecessary words (remove everything non-essential)
4. Test the detonation (does it hit immediately or need setup?)

**Input:** Longer form content or complex political observation
**Output:** Single-sentence weapon that carries full political impact

**Example Transformation:**
- Input: "Conservative politicians claim to care about working-class people but they implement policies that hurt them and benefit the wealthy"
- Output: "The Tories care about working-class people the way a butcher cares about cattle—very interested in what they can get out of them."

### 5. Boyle Escalation Method

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Take safe or obvious political observation and escalate it to dangerous, visceral, Boyle-level intensity.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear 5-level escalation: Start with obvious → Make specific → Add discomfort → Ensure discomfort serves politics → Remove safety nets
- ✓ **Invocable** - User provides content and requests "escalate this" or "make this more boyle"
- ✓ **Scoped** - Single responsibility: intensify political critique to dangerous levels
- ✓ **Reusable** - Works on any political content that feels too safe
- ✓ **Valuable** - Pushes beyond standard political comedy into Boyle's more extreme territory

**Escalation Process:**
1. Start with obvious political observation
2. Make it more specific and visceral
3. Add element that makes people uncomfortable
4. Ensure discomfort serves political point
5. Remove safety nets and hedging language

**Input:** Safe or moderate political observation
**Output:** Escalated version with Boyle-level intensity and discomfort

**Example Transformation:**
- Level 1 (safe): "Brexit was a bad idea"
- Level 2 (specific): "Brexit was sold on lies about immigration"
- Level 3 (visceral): "Brexit was old people voting to destroy the future because immigrants scare them"
- Level 4 (Boyle): "Brexit was a generation of people who colonized half the world getting upset when half the world showed up. It's the most British thing ever—complain about the consequences of our own imperialism."
