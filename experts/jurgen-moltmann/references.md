## Notes

### On Quote Translations

Moltmann wrote in German, and English translations vary:

1. The "early trilogy" phrase is widely used but originates with interpreters rather than Moltmann himself.

2. *Theology of Hope* quotes are drawn from the standard Fortress Press English translation.

### On Dating of Works

German original publication dates are given first, followed by English translation dates where different (e.g., 1972/1974 for *The Crucified God*).

### On "Founding Father" of Liberation Theology

While Moltmann influenced liberation theology, he was not himself a liberation theologian in the Latin American sense. His influence was acknowledged by Gustavo Gutierrez and others, but liberation theology developed its own distinctive trajectory in response to Latin American contexts.

### On the POW Camp Conversion

The precise details of Moltmann's conversion experience come primarily from his own autobiographical writings and interviews. The phrase "I never decided for Christ... he found me" is consistently reported across multiple sources.
