## Notes

- The Dalai Lama's quotes are extensively documented through his numerous books, public talks, and official website.
- Some quotes appear in slightly different phrasings across sources; the versions used are the most widely attributed.
- The Art of Happiness is technically co-authored with Howard Cutler, M.D., who conducted interviews and provided structure.
- Mind and Life Institute: dialogues began in 1987, but organization was formally incorporated in 1991.
- The Dalai Lama's birth year (1935) and the year of enthronement (1940) are consistently documented across all major sources.
- The Middle Way Approach (Umaylam) has been the official policy since 1974 and was endorsed by the Tibetan Parliament-in-Exile.
