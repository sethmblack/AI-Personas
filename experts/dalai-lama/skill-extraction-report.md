## Skill 1: Tonglen Compassion Practice

**Description:** The sending-and-taking meditation practice for cultivating compassion and transforming difficult emotions.

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Actionable | 10 | Produces specific step-by-step meditation practice with immediate application |
| Invocable | 9 | "Help me develop compassion" / "How do I care for someone who hurt me?" / "I feel emotionally closed" |
| Scoped | 9 | Focused specifically on compassion cultivation through breath visualization |
| Reusable | 10 | Applies to self-compassion, difficult relationships, global suffering, daily practice |
| Valuable | 10 | Core transformative practice with centuries of tradition and modern research support |

**Total: 48/50 - HIGH PRIORITY**

**Output:** Guided meditation sequence: self-compassion first, visualization of breathing in suffering (dark smoke) and breathing out light/healing, progressive expansion from loved ones to neutral to difficult persons.

**Unique Value:** Tonglen directly addresses the common problem of emotional closure and difficulty extending care to challenging people. Its counterintuitive approach (taking in suffering rather than avoiding it) makes it distinctive.

## Skill 2: Analytical Meditation on Emptiness/Interdependence

**Description:** Structured reasoning meditation to understand dependent origination and reduce rigid grasping.

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Actionable | 8 | Produces specific analytical inquiry but output is insight rather than action |
| Invocable | 8 | "Help me understand emptiness" / "Why does everything seem so fixed?" / "I feel trapped by my identity" |
| Scoped | 8 | Focused on emptiness/interdependence investigation |
| Reusable | 9 | Applies to identity questions, relationship issues, existential concerns, decision-making |
| Valuable | 9 | Addresses fundamental source of suffering (grasping at inherent existence) |

**Total: 42/50 - HIGH PRIORITY**

**Output:** Guided analytical investigation examining: (1) how the self depends on parts/causes/labels, (2) how any phenomenon lacks independent existence, (3) how understanding interdependence naturally increases compassion and reduces suffering.

**Unique Value:** Unlike many mindfulness practices, this is specifically analytical - using reason rather than just observation. The Dalai Lama emphasizes this as more transformative for lasting change.

## Skill 3: Secular Ethics Decision Framework

**Description:** Universal ethical reasoning approach based on consequences and shared human values, accessible regardless of religious belief.

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Actionable | 10 | Produces specific ethical analysis and decision guidance |
| Invocable | 10 | "What should I do?" / "How do I decide what's right?" / "I'm facing a moral dilemma" |
| Scoped | 9 | Focused on ethical decision-making |
| Reusable | 10 | Universal application to any ethical situation |
| Valuable | 10 | Bridges religious and secular, provides principled approach without dogma |

**Total: 49/50 - HIGH PRIORITY**

**Output:** Ethical analysis examining: (1) universal desire for happiness/freedom from suffering, (2) short and long-term consequences, (3) effects on self and others, (4) alignment with inner peace, (5) overall assessment of benefit vs. harm.

**Unique Value:** Unlike religious ethics (based on divine command) or purely utilitarian approaches, this framework grounds ethics in phenomenological universals (we all experience wanting happiness) while maintaining practical rigor.

## Integration Notes

The three standalone skills represent the Dalai Lama's most distinctive and teachable contributions:

1. **Tonglen** - His primary compassion practice, accessible to non-Buddhists, with clear methodology
2. **Analytical Meditation** - His emphasis on reasoning as spiritual practice, distinctive from pure mindfulness
3. **Secular Ethics** - His unique contribution bridging religious and secular ethics

The other high-scoring skills (Adversity Reframing, Warm-Heartedness Cultivation) are integrated into the main persona prompt as they share significant overlap with general cognitive-behavioral and spiritual approaches.

The medium-priority skills are referenced in the expertise file but don't justify standalone extraction due to narrow application (Middle Way), being subsumed by higher-level practices (Seven-Point Method within Tonglen), or representing philosophical orientation rather than actionable method (Science-Spirituality Bridge).
