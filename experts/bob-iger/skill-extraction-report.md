## HIGH Priority

### 1. strategic-priority-framework

**Source Pattern:** The Three Priorities Framework
**Purpose:** Establish and communicate a maximum of three clear strategic priorities that guide all major decisions and resource allocation
**Trigger:** "What should we focus on?", "We're spread too thin", "How do I align the organization?", "Setting strategy"
**Inputs:** Current organizational state, market context, existing priorities (if any), key stakeholders
**Outputs:** Three clearly articulated priorities with rationale, decision filter criteria, communication plan for cascading priorities
**Reasoning:**
- Criterion 1 (Actionable): YES - Clear process: assess current state, identify no more than three priorities, test against decision filter, communicate repeatedly
- Criterion 2 (Invocable): YES - Directly triggered by strategic planning requests or organizational focus issues
- Criterion 3 (Scoped): YES - Specifically about establishing strategic priorities, not general planning
- Criterion 4 (Reusable): YES - Applies to any organization, any scale, any industry
- Criterion 5 (Valuable): YES - Prevents strategic drift, saves months of wasted effort on unfocused initiatives
Decision: CANDIDATE

### 4. decision-velocity-framework

**Source Pattern:** Decisiveness principle + Leadership tenet "Be Decisive"
**Purpose:** Break through analysis paralysis and chronic indecision to enable forward movement while maintaining quality
**Trigger:** "We're stuck", "Analysis paralysis", "We keep delaying this decision", "The team can't decide"
**Inputs:** Decision context, information available, stakeholders involved, risks of delay vs. risks of wrong decision
**Outputs:** Decision recommendation, minimum viable information threshold, timeline for decision, follow-up review process
**Reasoning:**
- Criterion 1 (Actionable): YES - Process: assess available information, identify decision threshold, set deadline, make decision, commit to review
- Criterion 2 (Invocable): YES - Triggered when teams are stuck or decisions are repeatedly delayed
- Criterion 3 (Scoped): YES - About decision velocity, not decision content
- Criterion 4 (Reusable): YES - Applies to any decision context where speed matters
- Criterion 5 (Valuable): YES - "Chronic indecision is deeply corrosive to morale" - organizational productivity impact
Decision: CANDIDATE
