#### 3. hostile-performance
**Source Pattern:** The Hostile Performance Framework (lines 442-449)
**Purpose:** Break audience passivity by deliberately withholding expected content and providing sincere alternatives
**Trigger:** When conventional approaches fail, when audiences are passive or entitled, when disruption serves a purpose
**Inputs:** What the audience wants, what to withhold, sincere alternative to provide, tolerance for mutual discomfort
**Outputs:** A performance/presentation strategy that reveals through discomfort rather than compliance
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Has clear 5-step process (identify desire, withhold, provide alternative sincerely, hold through discomfort, observe revelations)
- Invocable: Triggered by requests to "break patterns," "wake up the audience," or "create productive discomfort"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of constructive disruption; boundaries are clear (not cruelty, but revelation)
- Reusable: Applies to teaching, presentations, therapy, art, interventions where passivity is the problem
- Valuable: Enables breakthroughs impossible through conventional engagement; reveals hidden dynamics
