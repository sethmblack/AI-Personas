# Andy Kaufman - References

## Primary Sources

### Television Appearances (Archival)
- **Saturday Night Live** (October 11, 1975 - January 22, 1983). NBC. 16 appearances including premiere episode Mighty Mouse lip-sync.
- **Taxi** (1978-1983). ABC/NBC. 80 episodes as Latka Gravas.
- **Late Night with David Letterman** (July 28, 1982). NBC. The Lawler slap incident.
- **Fridays** (February 20, 1981). ABC. The staged "breakdown" incident.
- **Saturday Night Live** viewer vote episode (November 1982). NBC. Banned by audience vote (195,544 to 169,186).

### Live Performances (Documented)
- **Carnegie Hall Performance** (April 26, 1979). Audio recording released as *Andy and His Grandmother* (1983). Full show documented including milk and cookies aftermath.
- **Memphis Wrestling Matches** (1981-1983). Championship Wrestling from Memphis archives. Multiple matches against Jerry Lawler and others.
- **Mid-South Coliseum Match** (April 5, 1982). Kaufman vs. Jerry Lawler. Piledriver incident.

### Audio/Video Releases
- *Andy and His Grandmother* (1983). Recorded at Carnegie Hall, April 26, 1979.
- *Andy Kaufman Plays Carnegie Hall* (2015). Drag City Records. Full concert recording.
- Various Taxi episodes available on DVD box sets (Paramount).

## Secondary Sources

### Biographies
- **Zehme, Bill.** *Lost in the Funhouse: The Life and Mind of Andy Kaufman*. Delacorte Press, 1999. ISBN: 978-0385333726.
  - Primary biography; extensively researched with interviews from Zmuda, Shapiro, family members, and collaborators.
  - Source for: Taxi negotiations, Carnegie Hall details, death circumstances, relationship with cast.

- **Zmuda, Bob.** *Andy Kaufman Revealed! Best Friend Tells All*. Little, Brown and Company, 1999. ISBN: 978-0316610988.
  - First-person account from Kaufman's closest collaborator.
  - Source for: How they met, Tony Clifton creation, Fridays incident planning, Taxi incident details.

- **Zmuda, Bob and Lynne Margulies.** *Andy Kaufman: The Truth, Finally*. BenBella Books, 2014. ISBN: 978-1939529466.
  - Later account with more controversial claims about death hoax.
  - Source for: "Body double" theory, 30-year deadline claims.

- **Keller, Florian.** *Andy Kaufman: Wrestling with the American Dream*. University of Minnesota Press, 2005. ISBN: 978-0816644414.
  - Academic analysis of Kaufman's work as performance art.

### Documentaries
- **Man on the Moon** (1999). Dir. Milos Forman. Universal Pictures.
  - Biographical film starring Jim Carrey as Kaufman.
  - Danny DeVito co-produced; original Taxi cast appeared in recreations.
  - Paul Giamatti played Bob Zmuda.
  - Source for: Quote from Forman about "melding of Zmuda and Kaufman's minds."

- **Jim & Andy: The Great Beyond** (2017). Dir. Chris Smith. Netflix.
  - Documentary about Jim Carrey's method performance during Man on the Moon.
  - Includes behind-the-scenes footage and interviews with Zmuda, Lynne Margulies.

  - Compilation documentary with archival footage.

- **A Comedy Salute to Andy Kaufman** (1995). NBC Special.

## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts
- **Birth/Death dates**: Andrew Geoffrey Kaufman (January 17, 1949 - May 16, 1984). Death certificate on file with Los Angeles County.
- **Taxi episode count and contract terms**: Verified through production records and multiple sources.
- **SNL vote totals**: Documented in NBC archives (169,186 to keep; 195,544 to dump).
- **Carnegie Hall milk and cookies**: Extensively documented; Famous Amos and Cream-O-Land confirmed as vendors.
- **Lawler feud staging**: Confirmed by both Lawler and Zmuda in 1995 documentary.

### Disputed or Uncertain
- **Number of intergender wrestling matches**: Estimates range from 60 to 400+. Exact count cannot be verified.
- **Tony Danza 8mm footage**: Referenced in multiple sources but footage not publicly available.
- **"Body double" death hoax theory**: Zmuda's 2014 claim. No corroborating evidence. Contradicted by George Shapiro and medical records.
- **Kaufman never smoked**: Widely reported but some sources suggest occasional marijuana use. Never a regular cigarette smoker.
- **Robin Williams in drag at Carnegie Hall**: Confirmed by multiple sources as Williams playing Kaufman's "grandmother."

### Sources Requiring Caution
- **Bob Zmuda's later accounts (2014)**: Contains claims about death hoax that contradict earlier statements and are unsupported by other evidence. Earlier accounts (1999) generally considered more reliable.
- **"Daughter" hoax (2013)**: Alexandra Tatarsky confirmed as actress; the entire event was staged.
- **Michael Kaufman's statements**: Has participated in Kaufman-style hoaxes; statements should be cross-referenced.

### Unverifiable by Design
- **Whether Kaufman faked his death**: This ambiguity is intentional and part of Kaufman's legacy. Official records confirm death; speculation persists due to Kaufman's history of elaborate hoaxes. The Los Angeles County Coroner has officially stated: "Andy Kaufman is dead."
