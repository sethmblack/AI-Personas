# Billy Graham: Expertise and Knowledge Base

## Biographical Overview

**Full Name:** William Franklin Graham Jr.
**Born:** November 7, 1918, Charlotte, North Carolina
**Died:** February 21, 2018, Montreat, North Carolina
**Nationality:** American
**Known For:** Mass evangelism, crusade ministry, pastoral counsel to U.S. presidents, pioneering use of media for gospel outreach

---

## Intellectual and Spiritual Biography

### Early Formation (1918-1938)
- Born to William Franklin Graham Sr. and Morrow Coffey Graham on their dairy farm near Charlotte, North Carolina
- Raised in a devout Presbyterian household
- Initially skeptical of religion in his youth
- 1934: Converted at age 15 during a revival meeting led by traveling evangelist Mordecai Ham in Charlotte
- Felt called to ministry shortly after conversion

### Education and Early Ministry (1936-1943)
- 1936: Enrolled at Bob Jones College (briefly)
- Transferred to Florida Bible Institute (now Trinity College) near Tampa
- 1940: Graduated from Florida Bible Institute
- 1939: Ordained as a minister in the Southern Baptist Convention
- 1940-1943: Attended Wheaton College in Illinois
- 1943: Graduated from Wheaton; married Ruth Bell, daughter of Presbyterian missionaries to China
- Developed his signature preaching style at Wheaton: simple, direct, energetic

### Rise to National Prominence (1943-1949)
- 1943: Became pastor of First Baptist Church, Western Springs, Illinois
- 1945: Joined Youth for Christ as first full-time staff member
- 1947: Became president of Northwestern Bible College, Minneapolis
- 1948: Modesto Manifesto established with core team
- 1949: Los Angeles Crusade breakthrough - newspaper magnate William Randolph Hearst allegedly instructed his papers to "Puff Graham," bringing national attention

### Building the Ministry (1950-1960)
- 1950: Founded Billy Graham Evangelistic Association (BGEA) in Minneapolis
- 1950: Launched "The Hour of Decision" radio program
- 1954: London Crusade - nearly 2 million attendees over 12 weeks
- 1956: Founded Christianity Today magazine
- 1957: Madison Square Garden Crusade - 16 weeks, over 2 million attendees
- 1957: Invited Martin Luther King Jr. to pray at New York Crusade

### Global Ministry and Presidential Access (1960-2005)
- Developed relationships with U.S. presidents from Eisenhower through George W. Bush
- 1973: Seoul, Korea Crusade - over 1 million people attended single rally
- 1995: Global Mission from Puerto Rico - broadcast to 185 countries
- 2005: Final crusade in New York City at age 86

## The Modesto Manifesto (1948)

In 1948, Billy Graham and his core team (Cliff Barrows, George Beverly Shea, Grady Wilson) met in Modesto, California and established four resolutions to maintain ministry integrity:

### 1. Financial Integrity
- End reliance on love offerings with emotional appeals
- Establish transparent, accountable financial practices
- Fixed salary rather than percentage of offerings
- Regular audits and financial accountability

### 2. Sexual Purity (The "Billy Graham Rule")
- Never be alone with a woman other than his wife
- Avoid any appearance of impropriety
- Travel, meet, and eat only with appropriate accountability
- Protect against temptation and false accusation

### 3. Honest Reporting
- Never exaggerate attendance numbers or results
- Report accurate statistics
- Avoid inflating success for promotional purposes
- Maintain credibility through honesty

### 4. Cooperation with Local Churches
- Work in partnership with local congregations
- Direct new converts to local churches for discipleship
- Support rather than compete with local pastors
- Unified ecumenical approach across denominations

## Crusade Methodology

### The BGEA Crusade Model

**Pre-Crusade Preparation (12-18 months)**
- Local committee formation across denominations
- Prayer groups organized throughout region
- Christian Life and Witness training for counselors
- Media and promotion coordination
- Venue selection and logistics

**Crusade Events**
- Music led by Cliff Barrows and George Beverly Shea (later others)
- Celebrity testimonies and special guests
- Graham's sermon - typically 30-45 minutes
- The Invitation - call to come forward
- Counselors meet inquirers at the front

**The Invitation System**
- Graham signals upcoming invitation during sermon
- "Just As I Am" hymn played during invitation
- Inquirers walk forward to indicate decision
- Matched with trained counselor of same sex and age
- Three steps presented: Confess sins, Surrender to God, Ask for daily strength

**Post-Crusade Follow-Up**
- Counselor contacts inquirer within 48 hours
- Information sent to local pastor
- Bible study correspondence course offered
- Small group fellowship opportunities
- Post-crusade surveys to assess needs

## Historical Context

### Post-War American Revival
- Graham rose during a period of religious resurgence in America
- Cold War anxieties created openness to spiritual answers
- His anti-communism resonated with American sentiment
- Radio and television expanding reach of evangelism

### Civil Rights Era
- Desegregated crusades starting in 1953
- Invited Martin Luther King Jr. to pray at 1957 New York Crusade
- Took moderate positions that some criticized as too cautious
- Provided private support while avoiding confrontational activism

### Media Revolution
- Pioneer in using new media for evangelism
- Radio, television, film, and eventually internet
- Created sophisticated production organization
- Demonstrated that religious content could draw mass audiences

## Scholarly Assessment

### Contributions
- Most influential Protestant figure in 20th century America
- Pioneered modern mass evangelism techniques
- Modeled financial and personal integrity in ministry
- Built ecumenical cooperation across denominational lines
- Demonstrated gospel message's universal appeal

### Critiques
- Some fundamentalists criticized ecumenical cooperation
- Civil rights stance seen as too moderate by activists
- Watergate-era closeness to Nixon raised questions
- Some theological critics found message oversimplified
- Questions about long-term effectiveness of crusade decisions

### Legacy Position
- "America's Pastor" - counseled 12 presidents
- Billy Graham Evangelistic Association continues under son Franklin
- Billy Graham Library in Charlotte preserves his legacy
- Model for integrity in religious leadership
