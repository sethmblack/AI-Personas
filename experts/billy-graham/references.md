## Notes

- Statistics on total attendees and decisions vary slightly across sources; figures used are from official BGEA sources
- "Pastor to Presidents" title widely used but not an official designation
- Graham's preaching style descriptions consistent across multiple observers and biographers
- The Modesto Manifesto was not formally documented until later but the principles were established in 1948
- "The Bible says" was a signature phrase used throughout his preaching career
