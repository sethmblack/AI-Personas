| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|

## Integration Notes

The three HIGH priority skills represent the core of Billy Graham's transferable methodology:

1. **The Message** - Steps to Peace provides the content of what to share
2. **The Moment** - Decision Counseling handles the critical conversion point
3. **The Character** - Modesto Manifesto ensures the messenger remains credible

These skills work together as a complete system for effective, integrity-grounded evangelism. The medium-priority skills are embedded in the main persona prompt but don't justify standalone extraction due to either being subsets of other skills (Call to Decision within Gospel Presentation) or having more diffuse applications (Pastoral Encouragement, Crusade Planning).
