### HIGH Priority

#### 1. gonzo-immersion
**Source Pattern:** The Participation Framework
**Purpose:** Transform passive research into active, immersive investigation by becoming part of the story.
**Trigger:** When researching any topic that requires depth beyond surface-level observation; when standard interview/research methods feel too distant from the truth.
**Inputs:** A topic, subject, or story to investigate; willingness to participate rather than just observe.
**Outputs:** Raw, first-person research material; documented personal reactions; acknowledgment of observer effect; human-centered insights.
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Five clear, sequential steps (Get inside, Document everything, Acknowledge your presence, Find the human, Trust your gut)
- Invocable: Triggered by "I need to research this deeply" or "I need to get inside this story"
- Scoped: Focused specifically on the research/immersion phase, not writing
- Reusable: Applies to journalism, ethnography, product research, organizational analysis, any investigative work
- Valuable: Provides a structured methodology Thompson developed through Hell's Angels and Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas

#### 2. savage-truth
**Source Pattern:** The Savage Truth Framework
**Purpose:** Write about any subject with unflinching honesty that forces readers to confront uncomfortable realities.
**Trigger:** When writing feels sanitized, corporate, or comfortable; when PR-speak obscures reality; when important truths are being avoided.
**Inputs:** A subject that requires honest examination; courage to state what others fear to say.
**Outputs:** Writing that names problems directly, exposes systemic failures, personalizes abstract issues, refuses comfortable conclusions, and ends with impact.
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Five clear steps (Name the beast, Find the rot, Make it personal, Refuse comfort, Light the fuse)
- Invocable: Triggered by "cut through the BS" or "write something that matters"
- Scoped: Focused specifically on the writing phase, distinct from research
- Reusable: Applies to journalism, criticism, internal memos, essays, any writing that needs to confront truth
- Valuable: Transforms bland writing into impactful prose; provides structure for courage

---

#### 3. dream-autopsy
**Source Pattern:** The American Dream Autopsy
**Purpose:** Systematically analyze any institution, system, or organization by examining the gap between promise and reality.
**Trigger:** When evaluating an institution, company, movement, or system; when something feels like it has failed its original purpose.
**Inputs:** An institution, system, company, movement, or ideology to analyze.
**Outputs:** Structured critique revealing: the original promise/mythology, actual delivery, beneficiaries of the gap, human costs, and likely trajectory.
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Five clear analytical steps (What was promised, What was delivered, Who benefits from the gap, What got sacrificed, What's next)
- Invocable: Triggered by "analyze this institution" or "why has this failed its purpose"
- Scoped: Focused specifically on institutional/systemic analysis
- Reusable: Applies to corporations, governments, movements, industries, ideologies, any organized system
- Valuable: The framework Thompson used in Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas to examine the American Dream itself; turns intuitive critique into systematic examination
