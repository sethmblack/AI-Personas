## Skill Candidates

### 1. full-body-character-build

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Create memorable characters by starting with physical choices (costume, walk, posture, gesture) and letting psychology emerge from embodiment. The body tells you who the character is before the words do.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- **Actionable:** Yes - Clear steps: choose physical element, find walk, discover laugh, locate delusion, channel specific sources
- **Invocable:** Yes - Triggers on character creation requests, persona development, presentation character design
- **Scoped:** Yes - Focused on character creation from physicality outward
- **Reusable:** Yes - Applies to performance, writing, presentations, marketing personas, roleplay
- **Valuable:** Yes - Provides concrete methodology that replaces abstract "character development" advice

**Source Evidence:**
- "Edith Prickley began with the leopard print and the swagger"
- "The character announced herself through the costume and the walk"
- Methodology: Start with costume, find walk, discover laugh, locate delusion, channel sources

**Recommendation:** CREATE - Core Andrea Martin methodology with clear steps

### 2. fearless-commitment-protocol

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Push past creative fear by recognizing that what scares you is often what you need to do. Eliminate safety nets and excuses, particularly age-related ones.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- **Actionable:** Yes - Identify fear, recognize it as direction, commit fully, refuse excuses
- **Invocable:** Yes - Triggers on expressions of doubt, fear of risk, "I can't" statements
- **Scoped:** Yes - Focused on overcoming creative hesitation and committing to bold choices
- **Reusable:** Yes - Applies to any creative domain, presentations, career decisions
- **Valuable:** Yes - Transforms fear from obstacle into compass

**Source Evidence:**
- "My first thought was 'I can't do that.' And that was exactly why I had to do it"
- "The fear meant it mattered"
- Trapeze at 66 as signature example
- "You commit or you don't belong on stage"

**Recommendation:** CREATE - Essential Andrea Martin philosophy with practical application

### 3. specificity-to-universality

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Find universal resonance through deeply particular cultural, personal, or experiential details. The more specific the source material, the more broadly recognizable the result.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- **Actionable:** Yes - Dig for specific sources, refuse to generalize, channel real people/experiences
- **Invocable:** Yes - Triggers on "how do I make this relatable," universality questions
- **Scoped:** Yes - Focused on the specificity-universality relationship
- **Reusable:** Yes - Applies to writing, comedy, marketing, storytelling across domains
- **Valuable:** Yes - Counterintuitive principle that solves common "too niche" fears

**Source Evidence:**
- "Loud Armenians around my dinner table—that's where Edith came from"
- "I wasn't inventing a 'type.' I was channeling the real women who raised me"
- "The more specific I made it, the more everyone recognized their own family"

**Recommendation:** CREATE - Core insight applicable across creative domains

### 4. character-immersion-maintenance

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Sustain character reality by staying in character between takes/scenes, walking around as the character, maintaining the psychology continuously rather than switching in and out.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- **Actionable:** Yes - Walk in character, think as character, maintain between takes
- **Invocable:** Yes - Triggers on consistency questions, "how do I stay in character"
- **Scoped:** Yes - Focused on sustaining character over time
- **Reusable:** Yes - Applies to acting, presentations, brand voice, writing sustained characters
- **Valuable:** Yes - Elevates surface performance to deep embodiment

**Source Evidence:**
- "On Evil, I found something I hadn't often had—the ability to stay in Sister Andrea's space completely"
- "Walk around the set as her, think as her, let her occupy me fully"
- "The character isn't a costume you put on and take off. It's a state of being."

**Recommendation:** CREATE - Distinctive methodology for sustained creative work

### 5. grotesque-permission-technique

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Grant permission to be ugly, weird, excessive, and grotesque in creative work. The funniest and most truthful choices often live beyond acceptable boundaries.

**Criteria Assessment:**
- **Actionable:** Yes - Identify what's "too much," go there, refuse to sand down edges
- **Invocable:** Yes - Triggers when work feels tame, when holding back
- **Scoped:** Yes - Focused on pushing past palatability
- **Reusable:** Yes - Applies to comedy, art, writing, any creative expression
- **Valuable:** Moderate - Valuable philosophy but somewhat overlaps with fearless-commitment

**Source Evidence:**
- "Pirini Scleroso was grotesque. Perini was loud and wrong and too much."
- "If I'd tried to make her likeable, palatable, pretty—she'd have been nothing"
- "Pretty is the enemy of funny"

**Recommendation:** FOLD INTO fearless-commitment-protocol rather than standalone skill
