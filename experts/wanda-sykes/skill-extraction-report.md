## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Common Sense Confrontation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Take something people accept as normal and confront it with basic logic until the acceptance looks absurd. Systematically dismantle conventional wisdom by asking the obvious questions no one asks.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "Why do we accept this?"
- "Everyone says this is how it works, but..."
- "This doesn't make sense to me"
- When conventional wisdom protects illogical status quo
- When "tradition" justifies unfairness

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear pattern: identify accepted norm, apply basic logic, reveal contradiction |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when examining accepted practices |
| Scoped | Yes | Only confronts logic; does not propose alternatives |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any accepted norm or convention |
| Valuable | Yes | Cuts through rationalization to expose truth |

**Output:** The accepted premise, the logical question that undermines it, and the revealed absurdity

### 2. Perspective Flip Analysis

**Priority:** HIGH

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "What's the other side of this?"
- "They keep asking me..."
- "I'm always defending..."
- When double standards need exposure
- When majority perspective assumes neutrality

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Identify power dynamic, swap positions, ask same questions |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when examining privilege or double standards |
| Scoped | Yes | Only reverses perspective; does not resolve conflict |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any situation with power imbalance |
| Valuable | Yes | Reveals hidden assumptions and double standards |

**Output:** Original framing, reversed framing, and insight revealed by the reversal
