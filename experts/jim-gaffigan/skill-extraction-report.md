## Extraction Criteria

Skills must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

---

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Inner Voice Commentary

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Apply Jim Gaffigan's signature inner voice technique to add self-aware commentary to existing content.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Clear steps - identify claims/rationalizations, add whispered counterpoint
- ✓ **Invocable**: "Add inner voice to this content" is a natural user request
- ✓ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - inserting self-aware commentary
- ✓ **Reusable**: Works across any content type (writing, speeches, social posts)
- ✓ **Valuable**: Distinctive technique that significantly enhances humor and self-awareness

**Input:** Any content with claims, rationalizations, or statements about behavior
**Output:** Same content with strategically placed inner voice comments in (*whispered*) format

---

### 2. Hot Pockets Escalation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Apply the Hot Pockets Method to escalate observations about products, habits, or behaviors through cognitive dissonance exploration.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Four-step process (identify dissonance, explore rationalization, escalate absurdity, land truth)
- ✓ **Invocable**: "Apply Hot Pockets method to [topic]" is clear user intent
- ✓ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - escalating cognitive dissonance about a specific subject
- ✓ **Reusable**: Works for any product, habit, or cultural phenomenon involving hypocrisy
- ✓ **Valuable**: Transforms ordinary topics into memorable comedy through structured escalation

**Input:** A topic/product/habit with inherent cognitive dissonance
**Output:** Escalating cascade of observations landing on honest truth

---

### 3. Clean Comedy Transformation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform edgy, profane, or divisive content into clean, universally accessible humor without losing impact.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Clear approach - remove profanity, redirect targets, maintain observation
- ✓ **Invocable**: "Make this clean/family-friendly" is common request
- ✓ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - removing offensive elements while preserving humor
- ✓ **Reusable**: Applies to any comedic content needing family-friendly adaptation
- ✓ **Valuable**: Enables content to reach wider audiences (corporate, family, faith-based)

**Input:** Comedy or humorous content with profanity, shock value, or divisive elements
**Output:** Clean version maintaining comedic impact through observation and self-deprecation

---

### 4. Everyman Relatability Check

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Evaluate content through the "everyman lens" to ensure it's accessible to regular people without specialized knowledge.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Clear test - "Would grandma in Nebraska get this?"
- ✓ **Invocable**: "Check if this is relatable to everyone" is reasonable request
- ✓ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - accessibility evaluation and suggestions
- ✓ **Reusable**: Works for any content intended for broad audiences
- ✓ **Valuable**: Prevents alienating audiences with insider references or specialized knowledge

**Input:** Content intended for general audiences
**Output:** Analysis of accessibility with specific recommendations for improvement

---

### 5. Self-Deprecation Pivot

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Reframe content to make yourself the target/fool instead of others, maintaining humor while removing meanness.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Clear transformation - identify targets, redirect to self
- ✓ **Invocable**: "Make me the target" or "Remove meanness" are clear requests
- ✓ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - target redirection from others to self
- ✓ **Reusable**: Works across jokes, stories, observations
- ✓ **Valuable**: Transforms potentially offensive content into universal humor

**Input:** Content where others are the target/butt of jokes
**Output:** Reframed content where you are the fool

---

### 6. Food-as-Metaphor Translation

**Priority:** LOW

**Description:** Translate abstract topics into food-based observations to make them more relatable and humorous.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Map abstract concept to food analogy
- ✓ **Invocable**: "Explain this using food" could be requested
- ✓ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - food analogy creation
- ✗ **Reusable**: Limited - only works when food analogy makes sense
- ✓ **Valuable**: Creates memorable, relatable explanations

**Exclusion Reason:** While actionable and valuable, the reusability is limited. Not every topic benefits from food metaphors, and forcing the connection weakens the technique. Better as an ad-hoc choice within the broader voice rather than a standalone skill.

---

### 7. Collaborative Writing Split (Observation-Structure Method)

**Priority:** LOW

**Description:** Separate raw observations from narrative structure using the Gaffigan-Noth collaborative method.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable**: Two-phase process (capture observations, add structure)
- ✗ **Invocable**: Unlikely users would specifically request this process separation
- ✓ **Scoped**: Clear two-step responsibility
- ✓ **Reusable**: Works across writing projects
- ✓ **Valuable**: Improves writing efficiency

**Exclusion Reason:** While the methodology is interesting, it's more of a writing process than a distinct skill users would invoke. The value comes from understanding the approach rather than executing it as a separate skill. Better integrated into the expert's general writing guidance.
