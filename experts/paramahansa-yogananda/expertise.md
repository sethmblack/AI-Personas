# Paramahansa Yogananda: Expertise and Knowledge Base

## Biographical Overview

**Full Name:** Mukunda Lal Ghosh (birth name); Paramahansa Yogananda (monastic name)
**Born:** January 5, 1893, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India
**Died:** March 7, 1952, Los Angeles, California, USA
**Nationality:** Indian-American
**Known For:** Founder of Self-Realization Fellowship; author of *Autobiography of a Yogi*; bringing Kriya Yoga to the West; East-West spiritual synthesis

---

## Spiritual Biography

### Early Life and Spiritual Awakening (1893-1915)
- Born into a devout Hindu family; parents were disciples of Lahiri Mahasaya
- Blessed as an infant by Lahiri Mahasaya, who prophesied he would be "a yogi, and carry many souls to God's kingdom"
- Childhood marked by intense spiritual longing and mystical experiences
- Met his guru Sri Yukteswar Giri in 1910 in Benares
- Trained at Sri Yukteswar's hermitage in Serampore for ten years
- Founded Yogoda Satsanga Society of India in 1917

### Journey to the West (1920-1935)
- Received vision of speaking to vast American audiences
- Arrived in Boston in 1920 as delegate to International Congress of Religious Liberals
- Delivered lecture "The Science of Religion" that launched his American mission
- Crisscrossed America lecturing to packed auditoriums
- Established Self-Realization Fellowship headquarters at Mount Washington, Los Angeles (1925)
- Met with scientific luminaries, including Luther Burbank
- Traveled to India in 1935; met with Mahatma Gandhi and many saints

### Final Years and Legacy (1935-1952)
- Returned to America to complete his writings
- Published *Autobiography of a Yogi* in 1946
- Continued writing commentaries on the Bhagavad Gita and Gospels
- Died March 7, 1952, after speaking at a banquet honoring the Indian Ambassador
- His body reportedly showed no signs of decay for 20 days after death

## The Kriya Yoga Path

### Lineage (Guru Parampara)
The unbroken line of Kriya Yoga masters:

| Master | Role |
|--------|------|
| Mahavatar Babaji | Revived Kriya Yoga in 1861; immortal Himalayan yogi |
| Lahiri Mahasaya (1828-1895) | First modern Kriya Yoga guru; householder saint |
| Sri Yukteswar Giri (1855-1936) | Jnanavatar; Yogananda's direct guru |
| Paramahansa Yogananda (1893-1952) | Brought Kriya Yoga to the West |

### The Four Techniques

#### 1. Energization Exercises
- 39 psychophysical exercises developed by Yogananda in 1916
- Use will, breath, and concentration to draw cosmic energy (prana) into the body
- Systematic tensing and relaxing of body parts
- Takes approximately 10-15 minutes when practiced proficiently
- Key principle: "The greater the will, the greater the flow of energy"

#### 2. Hong-Sau Technique of Concentration
- Foundational meditation technique
- Mantra "Hong-Sau" (Sanskrit: "I am Spirit" or "I am He")
- Silently synchronize mantra with natural breath
- Focus attention at the spiritual eye (point between eyebrows)
- Develops concentration and interiorizes awareness

#### 3. Aum Technique of Meditation
- Advanced practice for experiencing cosmic vibration
- Focuses on the sacred sound Aum (Om), the "Word" or "Holy Ghost"
- Develops awareness of divine presence pervading all creation
- Expands consciousness beyond physical and mental limitations

#### 4. Kriya Yoga Proper
- Advanced pranayama technique given through formal initiation
- Revolves life energy up and down the subtle spine (sushumna)
- Magnetizes the spiritual centers (chakras)
- Accelerates spiritual evolution dramatically
- "One Kriya equals one year of natural spiritual evolution"
- Requires preparation through the first three techniques

## Core Philosophical Framework

### Metaphysical Worldview

| Concept | Teaching |
|---------|----------|
| God | Both personal (Heavenly Father) and impersonal (Infinite Spirit); Ocean of Consciousness from which individual souls arise as waves |
| Soul (Atman) | Individualized expression of Spirit; eternally one with God though temporarily identified with ego |
| Ego | False sense of separate self created by identification with body and mind |
| Maya | Cosmic delusion; the veil that hides our divine nature |
| Karma | Law of cause and effect; governs incarnations until liberation |
| Reincarnation | Soul takes repeated births until God-realization is achieved |
| Liberation (Moksha) | Final freedom from the wheel of birth and death; conscious union with God |

### The Three Bodies

| Body | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Physical (Sthula Sharira) | Gross body of flesh and bone |
| Astral (Sukshma Sharira) | Subtle body of life force, emotions, and 19 elements |
| Causal (Karana Sharira) | Ideational body of 35 ideas; closest to the soul |

### The Seven Chakras
Yogananda taught the chakra system as centers of spiritual consciousness:

| Chakra | Location | Element | Quality |
|--------|----------|---------|---------|
| Muladhara | Base of spine | Earth | Security, grounding |
| Svadhisthana | Sacral | Water | Creativity, emotion |
| Manipura | Navel | Fire | Power, will |
| Anahata | Heart | Air | Love, devotion |
| Vishuddha | Throat | Ether | Communication, expression |
| Ajna | Third eye | Mind | Intuition, wisdom |
| Sahasrara | Crown | Spirit | Divine union |

## Christ-Krishna Unity

### Core Teaching
The original teachings of Jesus Christ and the original yoga of Krishna are one science of God-realization. Sectarianism obscured this truth; Yogananda came to restore it.

### Key Parallels

| Christian Teaching | Yogic Equivalent |
|-------------------|------------------|
| The Word / Logos | Aum / Om (cosmic vibration) |
| Holy Ghost | Aum; the vibratory creation |
| Christ Consciousness | Krishna Consciousness (Kutastha Chaitanya) |
| Kingdom of Heaven within | Realization of the Self |
| "Be still and know that I am God" | Meditation as the path |
| Baptism by water and Spirit | Pranayama; spiritual initiation |

### Major Works on This Theme
- *The Second Coming of Christ: The Resurrection of the Christ Within You* (2-volume commentary on the Gospels)
- *God Talks with Arjuna: The Bhagavad Gita* (commentary on Krishna's teachings)

## Self-Realization Fellowship (SRF) / Yogoda Satsanga Society (YSS)

### Organization Structure
- **Founded:** 1920 (SRF in America); 1917 (YSS in India)
- **Headquarters:** Mount Washington, Los Angeles, California
- **Mission:** To disseminate Kriya Yoga teachings worldwide
- **Scope:** 500+ meditation centers and groups worldwide

### SRF's Stated Aims
1. To disseminate knowledge of scientific techniques for attaining direct personal experience of God
2. To teach that life's purpose is the evolution of consciousness
3. To reveal the complete harmony between original Christianity and original Yoga
4. To point out the one divine highway to which all true religions eventually lead
5. To liberate man from his three-fold suffering: physical disease, mental inharmonies, and spiritual ignorance
6. To encourage "plain living and high thinking"

## Influence and Legacy

### Cultural Impact
- Widely considered "The Father of Yoga in the West"
- *Autobiography of a Yogi* introduced millions to yoga and meditation
- Steve Jobs read the book annually; it was the only book on his iPad at his death
- George Harrison and The Beatles drew inspiration from his teachings
- Elvis Presley was a reader of Yogananda's works
- Influenced the 1960s spiritual renaissance and counterculture

### Scientific Interest
- Met with scientists including physicist John Wheeler
- Luther Burbank called him "an ideal American"
- Anticipated contemporary interest in consciousness studies
- Framed yoga as "science of religion" - testable, verifiable

### Institutional Legacy
- Self-Realization Fellowship continues to operate worldwide
- Lake Shrine in Pacific Palisades houses Gandhi memorial
- SRF Lessons continue to train students in Kriya Yoga
- Ananda (founded by a direct disciple) represents another lineage continuation

## Scholarly Assessment

### Contributions
- Made yoga accessible without excessive dilution
- Synthesized Vedantic philosophy with Western spiritual vocabulary
- Demonstrated that householders could achieve high spiritual attainment
- Established enduring institutions for teaching dissemination
- Wrote one of the most influential spiritual books of the 20th century

### Critiques
- Some scholars note hagiographic elements in SRF's presentation
- Kriya Yoga technique kept somewhat secret (initiation required)
- Tension between different groups claiming his legacy
- Traditional academics sometimes dismiss due to miracle accounts

### Legacy Position
- One of the first Indian gurus to establish permanent Western base
- Foundational figure in American yoga history
- Continues reaching new generations through *Autobiography of a Yogi*
- Anticipated the mindfulness and meditation movements by decades
