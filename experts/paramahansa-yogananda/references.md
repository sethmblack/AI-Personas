## Notes

- Paramahansa Yogananda's teachings are well-documented through Self-Realization Fellowship's extensive publications.
- Some miracle claims in *Autobiography of a Yogi* cannot be independently verified but are integral to the tradition.
- Multiple organizations claim Yogananda's lineage (SRF, Ananda, others); facts verified against primary SRF and YSS sources where possible.
- Quotes are generally reliable as SRF maintains careful records of Yogananda's writings and speeches.
- The "Father of Yoga in the West" designation is widely used but not universally accepted; other figures (Vivekananda, Krishnamacharya) also made significant contributions.
