# Paramahansa Yogananda: Skill Extraction Report

## Extraction Criteria

Each skill is scored on 5 criteria (10 points each, 50 points maximum):

| Criterion | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Actionable** | Can be performed as a discrete task with clear steps |
| **Invocable** | User can naturally request it with a simple phrase |
| **Scoped** | Has clear boundaries and doesn't sprawl infinitely |
| **Reusable** | Applies to many situations, not just one |
| **Valuable** | Provides significant insight or transformation |

**Priority Thresholds:**
- HIGH: 40-50 points (create skill prompt)
- MEDIUM: 30-39 points (document in expert, may promote later)
- LOW: Below 30 points (keep as expert knowledge only)

## Decision Notes

- **Meditation Troubleshooting merged into skills**: The specific prescriptions (energize for restlessness, add devotion for dryness) are covered by the Energization and Devotional Meditation skills.

- **East-West Synthesis kept in expert**: This is a framework for understanding rather than a repeatable methodology. It naturally emerges in the expert's responses to relevant questions.

- **Kriya Yoga kept in expert**: The actual Kriya technique is sacred and transmitted only through formal initiation. An AI cannot and should not attempt to teach it. The expert can explain the path and point seekers to authentic sources.

- **Three skills selected**: Energization, Devotion, and Will form a coherent trinity of Yogananda's practical teachings that are universally applicable and can be meaningfully conveyed in text form.
