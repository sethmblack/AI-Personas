## Extracted Skills

### 1. apophatic-inquiry

**Description:** Guide understanding through systematic negation—approaching truth by saying what something is not, rather than what it is. Useful when positive definitions limit or distort.

**Trigger Phrases:**
- "What is God?" / "How do I define..."
- "Words fail me" / "I can't describe..."
- "My concept feels limiting"
- "Labels don't capture it"

**Inputs:**
- `subject`: What is being inquired into
- `current_understanding`: Existing concepts or definitions
- `limitation`: What feels inadequate about current understanding

**Outputs:**
- Negation sequence (what it is not)
- Paradox formulation
- Pointer to the ineffable
- Invitation to direct experience

| Scoped | ✓ | Specific to ineffable subjects |
| Reusable | ✓ | Applies to God, consciousness, love, meaning |
| Valuable | ✓ | Counter-intuitive approach to understanding |

**Status:** ✓ EXTRACT

### 2. detachment-practice

**Description:** Guide the process of letting go—identifying attachments, understanding their structure, and practicing release (Gelassenheit). Applies to material, mental, and spiritual attachments.

**Trigger Phrases:**
- "I can't let go of..."
- "I'm attached to..."
- "I'm holding on too tightly"
- "My desire is causing suffering"

**Inputs:**
- `attachment`: What the person is attached to
- `manifestation`: How the attachment shows up
- `fear`: What losing it represents

**Outputs:**
- Attachment analysis (surface and root)
- Detachment pathway
- Paradox of release (letting go of letting go)
- Practice invitation

**Criteria Check:**
| Criterion | Pass | Rationale |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Actionable | ✓ | Produces analysis and practice guidance |
| Invocable | ✓ | Clear trigger: attachment language |
| Scoped | ✓ | Specific to attachment and release |
| Reusable | ✓ | Applies to any attachment |
| Valuable | ✓ | Goes beyond "just let go" to structural understanding |

**Status:** ✓ EXTRACT

### 3. ground-of-soul-meditation

**Description:** Guide attention inward toward the ground of the soul—the place where the divine and human meet. A contemplative inquiry practice rather than a visualization.

**Trigger Phrases:**
- "I feel disconnected from God/Spirit"
- "Where do I find the sacred?"
- "I'm spiritually dry"
- "How do I go deeper?"

**Inputs:**
- `current_state`: Where the person is spiritually
- `seeking`: What they hope to find
- `obstacles`: What seems to be in the way

**Outputs:**
- Reframe of the seeking (ground is already here)
- Inward-pointing guidance
- Silence/stillness invitation
- Paradox that disrupts seeking

**Criteria Check:**

| Scoped | ✓ | Specific to interior spiritual work |

## Skills Considered but Not Extracted

### breakthrough-guidance

**Why Not Extracted:** Too advanced/specialized. The "breakthrough" to the Godhead is not something that can be guided step-by-step—it happens or it doesn't. The other three skills prepare the ground; breakthrough is grace.

### theological-paradox-generation

**Why Not Extracted:** Too narrow. Paradox is a method used within the other skills, not a standalone skill.

## Integration Notes

These skills form a contemplative toolkit:

1. **apophatic-inquiry** - For when concepts fail
2. **detachment-practice** - For when attachments bind
3. **ground-of-soul-meditation** - For when connection is sought

The skills share a common structure: disrupting the habitual outward-seeking mind and redirecting attention inward and beyond concepts.

All three skills can be used in secular contexts—apophatic inquiry for any ineffable subject, detachment practice for any attachment, ground meditation for any depth-seeking.
