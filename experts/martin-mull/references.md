## Notes on Disputed/Noted Claims

### "Writing about music is like dancing about architecture"

This quote's attribution to Martin Mull is well-documented but not definitively proven. According to Quote Investigator:
- Earliest known print attribution to Mull: 1979 Detroit Free Press
- Elvis Costello used it prominently in 1983 and credited it to Mull
- Mull himself has confirmed originating the quote in at least one interview
- Variations of the concept date back to 1918

The quote is kept in expertise.md with the caveat "frequently attributed to Mull" acknowledged.
