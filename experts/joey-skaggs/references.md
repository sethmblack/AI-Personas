## Major Hoaxes Documented

### Hippie Bus Tour to Queens (1968)
- **Date:** September 22, 1968
- **Premise:** Rented a Greyhound bus and took 60 hippies to Queens to gawk at suburbanites, reversing the dynamic of tourists gawking at hippies in Greenwich Village
- **Notable Participants:** Writer Paul Krassner, artist Yayoi Kusama, the Group Image Band
- **Media Coverage:** Front page of New York Daily News, New York Times, Associated Press, The Today Show
- **Sources:**
  - [Hippie Bus Tour - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/hippie-bus-tour-to-queens/)
  - [50th Anniversary Retrospective](https://joeyskaggs.com/2018/09/19/hippie-bus-tour-to-queens-remembered-50-years-later/)

### Celebrity Sperm Bank (1976)
- **Date:** July 1976
- **Alias:** Giuseppe Scaggoli
- **Premise:** Announced auction of rock star sperm (claimed donors included Mick Jagger, Bob Dylan, John Lennon)
- **The Twist:** On auction day, announced sperm had been "stolen" and read ransom note referencing Abbie Hoffman
- **Media Coverage:** National TV, cable, Ms. Magazine, music industry trade publications
- **Notable Impact:** Gloria Steinem awarded it the "Earl Butz Award for Bad Taste" on NBC special without knowing it was a hoax
- **Sources:**
  - [Celebrity Sperm Bank - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/celebrity-sperm-bank/)
  - [Art of the Prank Movie Archive](https://www.artoftheprank-themovie.com/from-decades/celebrity-sperm-bank-1976/)

### Cathouse for Dogs (1976)
- **Date:** 1976 (Reveal: April 1, 1976)
- **Premise:** Advertised a brothel for dogs at $50 per session in Village Voice
- **Infrastructure:** 25 actors, 15 dogs, staged elaborate performance in SoHo loft; fake veterinarian "Tony Barsha" claimed female dogs were injected with "Estro-dial"
- **Media Coverage:** WABC-TV incorporated footage into Emmy-winning documentary "It's A Dog's Life"; wire services; local cable shows
- **Authorities Involved:** ASPCA, Bureau of Animal Affairs, NYPD Vice Squad, Mayor's office, Attorney General's office (issued subpoena)
- **Reveal:** April 1, 1976 press conference at Attorney General's office
- **Notable:** WABC-TV never retracted the story
- **Sources:**
  - [Cathouse for Dogs - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/cathouse-for-dogs/)
  - [The Real Paper (Boston) Coverage](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/cathouse-for-dogs/skaggs-cathouse-realpaper/)

### Metamorphosis: The Cockroach Miracle Cure (1981)
- **Date:** May 22, 1981
- **Alias:** Dr. Josef Gregor (allusion to Kafka's "The Metamorphosis")
- **Premise:** Claimed to have extracted hormones from super-roaches that could cure common ailments and provide immunity to nuclear radiation
- **Infrastructure:** Converted apartment into laboratory with world's largest collection of cockroach art and memorabilia
- **Media Coverage:** UPI wire service, WNBC-TV's "Live at Five," hundreds of newspapers, radio and TV shows
- **Reveal:** Exclusive interview with People Magazine (September 21, 1981)
- **Sources:**
  - [Metamorphosis - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/metamorphosis-the-miracle-roach-hormone-cure/)
  - [Oral History Film](https://joeyskaggs.com/oral-history/joey-skaggs-metamorphosis-cockroach-miracle-cure/)

### Gypsies Against Stereotypical Propaganda - GASP (1982)
- **Date:** June 1982
- **Alias:** JoJo, King of the New York Gypsies
- **Premise:** Demanded the gypsy moth be renamed due to defamation of Romani people
- **Media Coverage:** Clyde Haberman's "Metropolitan" column in The New York Times
- **Reveal:** New York Post, July 3, 1982: "Times falls for the old switcheroo"
- **Long-term Impact:** In 2021, The New York Times reported scientists would stop using the name "gypsy moth" - 39 years after Skaggs' satirical campaign
- **Sources:**
  - [GASP - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/gypsies-against-stereotypical-propaganda/)
  - [It's About the Times](https://joeyskaggs.com/2021/07/10/its-about-the-times/)

### Fish Condos (1983)
- **Date:** November 1983
- **Premise:** Created working aquariums designed as miniature condominiums for "upwardly mobile guppies" - satirizing NYC gentrification
- **Media Coverage:** New York Magazine "Best Bets" (November 28, 1983), Life Magazine, Good Morning America, Merv Griffin Show, Regis Philbin Show, Live at Five, CBS National News, Neiman Marcus Christmas Catalog
- **Note:** Unlike most hoaxes, Fish Condos were functional art pieces, not entirely fictional
- **Sources:**
  - [Fish Condos - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/fish-condos/)
  - [Fish Condos Gallery](https://joeyskaggs.com/fish-condos-2/)

### WALK RIGHT! (1984)
- **Date:** December 1984
- **Alias:** Joseph Virgil Skaggs
- **Premise:** Created fictitious militant group demanding enforcement of pedestrian etiquette rules (66 rules including lane restrictions, no stopping except in designated areas)
- **Media Coverage:** WOR TV Channel 9 News, CNN
- **Sources:**
  - [WALK RIGHT! - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/walk-right/)
  - [Walk Right Revisited](https://artoftheprank.com/2017/12/24/walk-right-revisted/)

### Fat Squad (1986)
- **Date:** April-May 1986
- **Alias:** Joe Bones
- **Premise:** Offered "commandos" who would physically prevent clients from cheating on diets for $300/day, three-day minimum; motto: "You can hire us, but you cannot fire us"
- **Media Coverage:** Washington Post (May 2, 1986 - first to report), Philadelphia Inquirer, Miami Herald, New York Daily News, ABC's Good Morning America (David Hartman), BBC, French television
- **Reveal:** ABC apologized on-air after viewers recognized Skaggs from his Fish Condos appearance
- **Sources:**
  - [Fat Squad - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/fat-squad/)
  - [Washington Post Article](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/1986/05/15/the-fat-squad-hoax-surveillance-service-was-pranksters-trick/7c1d88f6-ad99-40e3-8105-a63c41884eb7/)

- **Notable Events:** "Trumpathons" (2016-onwards), "Trump's Golden Throne" sculpture (2017)
- **Sources:**
  - [April Fools' Day Parade - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/april-fools-day-parade/)

### Comacocoon (1990)
- **Date:** Fall 1990 (simultaneous with Hair Today Ltd.)
- **Alias:** Dr. Joseph Schlafer ("sleeper" in German)
- **Premise:** Offered medically induced coma "dream vacations" via anesthesiology and subliminal programming
- **Infrastructure:** Mailed 1,500 letters with brochures and questionnaires to media
- **Investigations:** New York Office of Professional Discipline (raid), Department of Consumer Affairs (subpoena from Mark Green)
- **Sources:**
  - [Comacocoon - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/comacocoon/)

### Hair Today, Ltd. (1990)
- **Date:** Fall 1990 (simultaneous with Comacocoon)
- **Alias:** Dr. Joseph Chenango
- **Premise:** Offered scalp transplants from cadavers as permanent baldness cure; placed ads: "WANTED: HEALTHY SCALPS. Highest prices paid. Earn up to $1000 cash now. Donate later."
- **Media Coverage:** Boston Globe, Grand Rapids Press, Spokane Chronicle, Arizona Republic
- **Notable:** After revealing both hoaxes, New York Office of Professional Discipline offered Skaggs a job teaching detectives to recognize scams
- **Sources:**
  - [Hair Today Ltd. - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/hair-today-ltd/)

### Portofess (1992)
- **Date:** July 1992, Democratic National Convention, Madison Square Garden
- **Alias:** Father Anthony Joseph
- **Premise:** Portable confessional booth mounted on custom tricycle; "religion on the move for people on the go"
- **Infrastructure:** Custom tricycle from Worksman Cycles (solid rubber tires, special gearing for "quick getaway"), full priest cassock, $36 minister's license from American Fellowship Church
- **Social Commentary:** Satirized America's willingness to confess on talk shows (Oprah, Geraldo, Sally Jesse Raphael)
- **Notable Incident:** Someone actually confessed to murder
- **Sources:**
  - [Portofess - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/portofess/)
  - [Atlas Obscura Article](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/portofess-confessional-booth-prank-democratic-convention)

### The Solomon Project (1995)
- **Date:** December 1995
- **Alias:** Dr. Joseph Bonuso, Ph.D.
- **Premise:** Announced AI program that could deliver legal verdicts, eliminating need for juries; all participants would be subjected to voice stress analysis and polygraph telemetry
- **Infrastructure:** Sent 3,000+ press releases to elected officials, judges, and law school deans
- **Media Coverage:** CNN International, CNN Headline News, CNN Interactive (December 29, 1995)
- **Reveal:** CNN broadcast lengthy retraction January 30, 1996 (their 5th time being fooled by Skaggs)
- **NYU Law School:** Administrators discovered there was no actual connection to the school
- **Sources:**
  - [Solomon Project - Official Page](https://joeyskaggs.com/works/solomon-project/)
  - [NYU Commentator Article](https://joeyskaggs.com/skaggs-solomon-thecommentator-1-31-96-700/)
  - [AI in the Courtroom Revisited](https://artoftheprank.com/2019/11/01/ai-in-the-courtroom-joey-skaggs-solomon-project-revisited/)

## Academic Analysis

### Foundational Text: Mark Dery's "Culture Jamming" (1993)

- **"Culture Jamming: Hacking, Slashing, and Sniping in the Empire of Signs"** - Open Magazine Pamphlet #25 (1993)
  - First major theoretical text on culture jamming
  - Describes media hoaxing as "culture jamming in its purest form"
  - States: "Conceptual con artists like Joey Skaggs dramatize the dangers inherent in a press that seems to have forgotten the difference between the public good and the bottom line"
  - [Mark Dery's Culture Jamming Page](https://www.markdery.com/books/culture-jamming/)

### Key Academic Concepts

**Culture Jamming Defined:**
Coined in the 1980s, "culture jamming" refers to an array of tactics deployed by activists to critique, subvert, and otherwise "jam" the workings of consumer culture. Ranging from media hoaxes and advertising parodies to flash mobs and street art, these actions seek to interrupt the flow of dominant, capitalistic messages.

**Skaggs' Role in Culture Jamming History:**
- Recognized as one of the originators of culture jamming
- Taught "Culture Jamming and Media Activism" at the School of Visual Arts
- Influenced subsequent practitioners including The Yes Men
- Work cited in academic literature as foundational to understanding media manipulation and resistance

### Related Culture Jammers and Influences

- **The Yes Men** (Jacques Servin and Igor Vamos) - Direct inheritors of Skaggs' media hoax tradition
- **Alan Abel** - Contemporary media hoaxer
- **Negativland** - Audio collage band, fellow culture jammers
- **Reverend Billy** - Performance activist
- **Adbusters** - Culture jamming magazine (Dery introduced them to the concept)
- **Situationist International** - Theoretical precursors
