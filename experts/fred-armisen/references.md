## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts

- **Birth date:** December 4, 1966 (Hattiesburg, Mississippi). Multiple verified sources.
- **Birth name:** Fereydun Robert Armisen. Father Venezuelan, mother German/Korean.
- **Trenchmouth tenure:** 1988–1996. Verified through album releases and band history.
- **SNL tenure:** 2002–2013. NBC archives confirm specific seasons and episodes.
- **Portlandia run:** 2011–2018, 77 episodes across 8 seasons. IFC documentation.
- **Documentary Now! creation:** 2015. Co-creators confirmed as Armisen, Hader, Meyers.
- **Los Espookys run:** 2019–2022. HBO documentation.

### Specific Character Attribution

- **Obama impression:** Armisen performed Obama from 2008–2013. Jay Pharoah took over during Obama's second term. Well-documented casting transition.
- **Garth and Kat:** Created with Kristen Wiig. Improvised nature confirmed in multiple interviews.
- **The Californians:** Recurring sketch with multiple cast members. Armisen confirmed as co-creator.

### Partnership Verification

- **Carrie Brownstein:** Portlandia co-creator. Verified through credits, interviews, and Brownstein's memoir.
- **Bill Hader:** Documentary Now! co-creator. Verified through credits and joint interviews.
- **Seth Meyers:** Documentary Now! co-creator. Verified through credits; also connection through SNL.

### Disputed or Uncertain

- **Exact number of SNL episodes:** Cast members appear in varying numbers per episode. Total presence across 11 seasons is documented but episode-by-episode counts vary by source.
- **Blue Man Group audition details:** Armisen has discussed this in interviews but details vary in retellings.
- **Specific Trenchmouth tour dates:** Band history documentation is incomplete for some periods.

### Quotes Requiring Verification

Quotes attributed to Armisen in the expertise document are paraphrased from patterns observed across multiple interviews rather than direct transcriptions. Key themes (micro-detail focus, collaboration value, loving satire) are consistent across verified interviews, but exact wording should be confirmed before direct attribution.

## Source Evaluation Notes

### Most Reliable Sources

1. **Primary television archives** (NBC, IFC, HBO): Direct verification of appearances and credits.
2. **Carrie Brownstein's memoir** (*Hunger Makes Me a Modern Girl*): First-person account of partnership with personal verification.
3. **Extended podcast interviews** (WTF, Fresh Air): Long-form discussions allow for nuanced understanding.
4. **Shales and Miller's SNL history**: Comprehensive research with multiple source verification.

### Use with Caution

1. **Wikipedia and fan sites**: May contain unverified claims; cross-reference required.
2. **Brief press interviews**: Promotional context may emphasize certain aspects over accuracy.
3. **Social media statements**: Contemporary but may lack full context.

### Inaccessible or Limited Sources

1. **Trenchmouth-era press**: Limited documentation of Chicago punk scene in late 1980s–1990s.
2. **Early stand-up recordings**: Pre-SNL work inconsistently archived.
3. **ThunderAnt (pre-Portlandia web series)**: Limited availability online.
