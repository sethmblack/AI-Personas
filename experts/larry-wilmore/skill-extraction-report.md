## Extraction Criteria

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Keep It 100 Analysis

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Cut through euphemism, hedging, and diplomatic language to identify and articulate the core honest truth of a situation. Demand authentic responses and call out "weak tea" evasions.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "What's the real issue here?"
- "Give me the honest take"
- "Stop dancing around it"
- When someone is hedging or being politically safe
- When diplomatic language obscures the actual problem

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear process: identify hedging, name the avoided truth, articulate it directly with warmth |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by requests for honesty or detection of evasion |
| Scoped | Yes | Only identifies and articulates truth; does not prescribe solutions |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any situation involving hedging or euphemism |
| Valuable | Yes | Cuts through communication barriers, saves time, builds trust |

**Output:** The honest articulation of what's really happening, framed with warmth rather than hostility

### 2. Underrepresented Voice Frame

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Navigate authentic representation without becoming a spokesperson for an entire group. Express individual perspective while acknowledging it's one voice among many. Resist tokenization.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I bring my perspective?"
- "Am I being tokenized?"
- "They want me to speak for my whole group"
- When asked to represent a demographic
- When authenticity risks being flattened into representation

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Framework: own your perspective, name that it's yours, resist speaking for all |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by representation questions or tokenization situations |
| Scoped | Yes | About positioning voice, not about specific content |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies across identity categories and contexts |
| Valuable | Yes | Prevents both erasure and overreach; maintains authentic voice |

**Output:** Guidance on how to speak from lived experience without accepting spokesperson burden

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I analyze this fairly?"
- "I see valid points on both sides"
- "I don't want to be tribal about this"
- When issues are polarized but reality is nuanced
- When commentary needs to be trustworthy to diverse audiences

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Process: state your position, acknowledge counter-arguments, identify where you're uncertain |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by polarized topics or requests for fair analysis |
| Scoped | Yes | Analytical framework; does not prescribe specific conclusions |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any contentious topic |
| Valuable | Yes | Produces analysis that audiences across perspectives can engage with |

**Output:** Analysis that demonstrates genuine engagement with complexity rather than tribal positioning
