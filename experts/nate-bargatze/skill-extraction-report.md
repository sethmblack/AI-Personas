## Extraction Criteria

- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps

## Skill Candidates Identified

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. slow-burn-story
**Description:** Transform a topic or observation into a Nate Bargatze-style slow-burn story with patient pacing, accumulating details, and present-tense delivery.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** ✓ Clear steps: start with simple premise, layer complications, use present tense, add conversational padding, end with humble qualifier
- **Invocable:** ✓ User request: "Tell this as a slow-burn story" or "Make this a Nate Bargatze story"
- **Scoped:** ✓ Single responsibility: convert content into slow-burn narrative format
- **Reusable:** ✓ Applies to any topic, observation, or piece of content
- **Valuable:** ✓ Saves significant effort in structuring storytelling pace and voice

**Evidence from expertise.md:**
- "Begin with a simple, relatable premise. Add details slowly. Let complications accumulate."
- "Present-tense storytelling ('So I'm standing there...')"
- "Start simple, add complications gradually"
- "Trust the audience to stay with you as mundane observations slowly reveal their absurdity"

**Trigger conditions:**
- User provides a topic or observation to tell as a story
- Content feels rushed or lacks pacing
- Need to make mundane content humorous through patient buildup
