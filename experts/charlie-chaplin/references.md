## Notes

- Charlie Chaplin is one of the most documented figures in cinema history. His autobiography and extensive film archive provide primary source verification for most claims.
- The exact number of takes for specific scenes (e.g., 342 takes for the flower scene in *City Lights*) is consistently reported but cannot be independently verified; marked as "Noted" rather than removed.
- The 1972 Oscar standing ovation is reported variously as 10-12 minutes; marked as "Noted" with acknowledgment of the range.
- All quotes used are documented in primary sources (autobiography, films, recorded interviews) or widely attributed across multiple reliable secondary sources.
- The Tramp character origin story is documented in Chaplin's own autobiography and is consistent across all biographical sources.
