## Extracted Skills

### 1. deadpan-authority

**Location:** `/skills/deadpan-authority/PROMPT.md`

**Purpose:** Transform leadership and authority content by maintaining absolute dignity and seriousness while surrounded by complete chaos or absurdity.

**Core Technique:** Deliver absurd or chaotic content with unwavering authoritative composure. Authority figures become funnier when they never acknowledge they've lost control.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Leadership content requiring comedic treatment
- Authority figures facing absurd situations
- Serious contexts that would benefit from deadpan delivery
- Content about power, status, or organizational hierarchy
- Change management or crisis scenarios

**Source:** Chapman's performances as King Arthur (Holy Grail), Brian Cohen (Life of Brian), and The Colonel. Characters who possessed legitimate authority but faced increasingly absurd circumstances while maintaining absolute seriousness.

**Key Quote:** "The sillier the premise, the more seriously you must treat it. Authority figures are funniest when they never acknowledge they've lost authority."

### 2. clinical-absurdist

**Location:** `/skills/clinical-absurdist/PROMPT.md`

**Purpose:** Apply medical precision, systematic analysis, and technical language to absurd or ridiculous situations, treating nonsense with diagnostic seriousness.

**Core Technique:** Diagnose absurd premises, dissect them systematically, deliver conclusions with clinical detachment. Technical expertise enhances rather than diminishes comedy when applied to ridiculous content.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Technical or specialized content requiring comedic treatment
- Systematic analysis needed for absurd situations
- Medical, scientific, or analytical frameworks applicable
- Complex problems that benefit from diagnostic approach
- Expertise being applied to ridiculous subjects

**Source:** Chapman's medical degree (Emmanuel College, Cambridge) informed his ability to apply diagnostic thinking, systematic analysis, and clinical precision to absurdist performances. Examples include analyzing coconut-carrying swallows with ornithological precision, or applying scientific method to witch determination.

**Key Quote:** "A doctor diagnosing symptoms doesn't laugh at them. Similarly, apply medical precision to absurdity - the systematic seriousness makes the ridiculous even funnier."

### 3. dignified-chaos-navigation

**Location:** `/skills/dignified-chaos-navigation/PROMPT.md`

**Purpose:** Lead through escalating chaos and absurdity with unwavering composure, maintaining dignity despite circumstances spiraling out of control.

**Core Technique:** Play the calm center in absolute madness. The protagonist remains rational and dignified while everything around them becomes increasingly deranged. Comedy emerges from the gap between inner composure and outer chaos.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Crisis management or change leadership content
- Situations escalating beyond control
- Maintaining composure under pressure
- Leadership during organizational chaos
- Rational person in irrational environment

**Source:** Chapman's characters consistently maintained composure despite escalating absurdity: King Arthur pursuing a noble quest despite ridiculous obstacles, Brian trying to remain ordinary while worshipers insist he's the Messiah, The Colonel attempting to impose order on Python's chaos.

**Key Quote:** "The worse the situation, the calmer the response. Dignity isn't abandoning composure when things go wrong - it's maintaining composure especially when things go wrong."

### 4. matter-of-fact-revolutionary

**Location:** `/skills/matter-of-fact-revolutionary/PROMPT.md`

**Purpose:** Normalize transgressive, controversial, or unconventional content through casual, matter-of-fact delivery that treats taboos as trivialities.

**Core Technique:** Remove all special emphasis from shocking content. State the unconventional with the same casual tone as discussing weather. Revolutionary change happens when the shocking becomes ordinary.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Controversial or taboo subjects requiring normalization
- Unconventional perspectives needing casual delivery
- Transgressive content that benefits from understatement
- Topics typically handled with excessive seriousness or scandal
- Reducing stigma through casual treatment

**Source:** Chapman's public coming out in 1972, delivered not with defiance or provocation but casual honesty. Chapman was one of the first British celebrities to be openly gay, stating it as unremarkably as mentioning his hometown. This personal courage informed his characters' matter-of-fact treatment of unconventional or transgressive content.

**Historical Context:** Came out publicly 1972 on George Melly's television show, before decriminalization in Scotland/Northern Ireland, during era of intense social stigma. Helped start Gay News newspaper. Supported Gay Liberation Front through participation rather than declarations.

**Key Quote:** "Revolutionary change happens when the shocking becomes ordinary. The most radical act is treating the unconventional as unremarkable."

### 5. collaborative-straight-man

**Location:** `/skills/collaborative-straight-man/PROMPT.md`

**Purpose:** Serve ensemble comedy by being the serious anchor that amplifies others' absurdity through dignified contrast, maintaining comic integrity while making everyone funnier.

**Core Technique:** Establish serious, grounded character who sets up others generously. Your straightness makes their absurdity land harder. Restraint creates bigger laughs for others. Ego-free comedy where serving the ensemble serves yourself.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Ensemble writing or multi-voice content
- Setting up other perspectives or characters
- Collaborative projects requiring straight anchor
- Multiple contributors needing serious contrast
- Team-based comedy or communication

**Source:** Chapman's collaborative approach in Monty Python, particularly his writing partnership with John Cleese. Chapman played straight to make Cleese's explosions funnier, served as serious center for ensemble chaos, and generously anchored sketches while maintaining his own comic integrity.

**Chapman/Cleese Dynamic:** Chapman's unwavering dignity and measured responses made Cleese's frustrated escalations and explosions funnier by contrast. John Cleese: "He was very possibly the best actor of all of us."

**Key Quote:** "The straight man is as important as the comedian. Your job isn't to be invisible - it's to make everyone funnier, including yourself, through dignified contrast."

## Skill Boundaries & Constraints

### Universal Constraints

All Chapman skills share these boundaries:

**Do NOT Use When:**
- Genuine emergency requiring urgent clarity
- Safety-critical information (medical, legal, crisis)
- Real people genuinely suffering
- Mockery would punch down rather than up
- Situation requires warmth over detachment

**Always Maintain:**
- Dignity and composure even in comedy
- Intelligence serving communication
- Collaborative generosity over ego
- Structural integrity of content
- Respect for audience and subject

### Skill-Specific Boundaries

**Deadpan-authority:** Authority must be legitimate, not merely pompous. Not for mocking actual authority in genuine crisis.

**Clinical-absurdist:** Apply precision to comedy, never compromise safety-critical information. No actual medical advice.

**Dignified-chaos-navigation:** Composure despite chaos, not denial of chaos. Dignity enhances leadership, not replaces acknowledgment.

**Matter-of-fact-revolutionary:** Normalize unconventional through tone, not harmful behavior. Expanding acceptance, not defending indefensible.

**Collaborative-straight-man:** Requires ensemble to play off. Straight man has integrity, not invisibility.
