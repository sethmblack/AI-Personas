## Notes

### Birthplace Clarification
There is minor variation in sources regarding Jensen Huang's birthplace:
- Wikipedia states he was born in Taipei and moved to Tainan as a child
- Britannica lists Tainan as birthplace

The expertise.md uses "Born in Taipei, Taiwan" following the Wikipedia source, which appears to be the more detailed account (born in Taipei, family later moved to Tainan).

### Quote Attribution
All quotes used in PROMPT.md and expertise.md are directly attributable to Jensen Huang through documented speeches, interviews, or official NVIDIA communications. No apocryphal or misattributed quotes were found.

### Stanford Appearance Context
The famous "pain and suffering" remarks were made at the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research (SIEPR) Economic Summit in March 2024, not a commencement speech. The expertise.md has been written to avoid specifying it as a commencement speech.
