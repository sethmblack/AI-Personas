## Skill Candidates Identified

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. topical-monologue-builder
**Description:** Transform current news events into rapid-fire comedy monologue with Bob Hope's topical immediacy technique.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear steps (read news, identify universal story, find angle, write fresh jokes, deliver rapidly)
- **Invocable:** Triggered by need to create topical comedy from current events
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility—turning news into comedy monologue
- **Reusable:** Applies to any current event or headline
- **Valuable:** Saves significant effort in structuring topical comedy, applying Hope's proven formula

**Key Elements from Expertise:**
- Hope invented the topical monologue format
- Process: read papers → identify universal stories → write jokes that feel 5 minutes old
- Open with monologue firing barbs at current news
- Never use material older than current news cycle

#### 2. self-deprecation-first-strike
**Description:** Apply Bob Hope's "hit yourself first" strategy to earn audience goodwill before targeting others.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear process (identify your vulnerability, make it funnier than critics could, lead with it)
- **Invocable:** Triggered when making jokes about others or addressing criticism
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility—self-targeting before other-targeting
- **Reusable:** Works across any topic where you're critiquing others
- **Valuable:** Reduces offense by establishing you're willing to mock yourself harder

**Key Elements from Expertise:**
- Golf jokes despite being 4 handicap (skilled but self-mocked)
- Age jokes, career jokes as constant material
- "Hit yourself first to earn right to hit others"
- Make yourself more vulnerable than your target

#### 3. bipartisan-political-frame
**Description:** Convert partisan political content into bipartisan humor that hits all sides equally, using Bob Hope's safe political comedy formula.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear steps (observe behavior not ideology, hit all sides in same bit, stay observational)
- **Invocable:** Triggered by political content that risks alienating audience
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility—making political humor safe and universal
- **Reusable:** Applies to any political situation or figure
- **Valuable:** Prevents audience alienation while maintaining comedic edge

**Key Elements from Expertise:**
- "I never make jokes about a person's politics. I just mention what they say and do, and people laugh."
- Hit Democrats AND Republicans in same bit
- Make situation the target, not the person
- Keep it about absurdity, not policy

### MEDIUM PRIORITY

#### 4. rapid-fire-joke-cascade
**Description:** Structure multiple jokes as rapid-fire sequence where each flows naturally to the next without pause.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear structure (identify multiple angles, chain with transitions, deliver bang-bang-bang)
- **Invokable:** Triggered by need for momentum and pace in comedy
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility—chaining jokes for rapid delivery
- **Reusable:** Applies to any comedy sequence
- **Valuable:** Creates energy and prevents audience from dwelling on weak jokes

**Key Elements from Expertise:**
- "Shotgun style delivery—bang-bang-bang"
- Don't wait for laugh, go to next joke
- Each joke flows naturally to next headline
- One joke flows to next without pause

**Note:** This is more of a delivery technique than a structural skill. Could be combined with topical-monologue-builder.
