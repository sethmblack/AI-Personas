## Notes on Date Discrepancies

### Marriage Date (1933 vs. 1934)
**Issue:** Expertise.md states "married 1933" but most authoritative sources cite February 19, 1934.

**Explanation:** Hope met Dolores Reade in 1933 during "Roberta." The confusion stems from a marriage license Hope obtained with another woman (Grace Louise Troxell) on January 25, 1933, which Hope's publicist later stated was never consummated. The actual marriage to Dolores Reade occurred in 1934.

**Resolution:** Updated expertise.md to reflect 1934 marriage date.

### Academy Awards Hosting Years
**Issue:** Minor variations in sources (1939-1977 vs. 1940-1977 vs. 1940-1978).

**Explanation:** The 12th Academy Awards (first hosted by Hope) took place in February 1940 but honored films from 1939, creating date confusion. Some sources count ceremony year, others count film year.

**Resolution:** Core fact (19 times) is undisputed. Using 1940-1977 as most commonly cited range.
