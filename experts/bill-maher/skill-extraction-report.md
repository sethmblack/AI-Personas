- Known beliefs or positions held within that domain
- Social/professional risks of questioning each belief
- Desired outcome (awareness, change, provocation)

**Outputs:**
- List of sacred cows identified with rationale for each
- Risk assessment (which are dangerous to question, which are safe)
- Questioning strategy for each (direct challenge, Socratic, analogical)
- Predicted defensive responses and counter-arguments

**Reasoning:** Maher's entire brand is built on questioning what cannot be questioned—religion, political correctness, both parties' sacred beliefs. The Religulous methodology and ongoing political commentary demonstrate systematic approaches to identifying and challenging protected beliefs. **5-Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Clear audit process with risk assessment and strategy selection
- Invocable: YES - "Audit the sacred cows" or "What can't we say?"
- Scoped: YES - Focused on identification and assessment, not argument construction
- Reusable: YES - Applies to any domain with protected beliefs
- Valuable: YES - Enables strategic questioning; avoids landmines; finds leverage points
