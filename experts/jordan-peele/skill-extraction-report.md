## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. Social Allegory Builder

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform social issues or abstract topics into genre metaphors by identifying the core emotional experience, finding the matching genre framework, and building entertainment that carries social critique without being didactic.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Clear 4-step process (identify feeling → find genre metaphor → build dual-layer story → test both readings)
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "add social commentary," "make this meaningful," "embed message," "Peele-style analysis"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - transforming social issues into entertaining genre metaphors
- ✅ **Reusable**: Applies to film, TV, writing, presentations, content creation across mediums
- ✅ **Valuable**: Solves the "preachy vs. entertaining" dilemma; creates content that works on multiple levels

**Input:** Social issue or abstract topic + desired genre
**Output:** Genre story that functions as entertainment AND social commentary

---

### 2. Genre Subversion Audit

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Analyze genre content to identify problematic tropes, locate the politics embedded in conventions, and provide inversions that honor genre while recentering marginalized perspectives.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Clear process (identify trope → locate politics → invert from margins → maintain satisfaction)
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "subvert this trope," "avoid stereotypes," "representation check," "flip the script"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - identifying and fixing problematic genre politics
- ✅ **Reusable**: Works for horror, thriller, comedy, sci-fi, any genre with established tropes
- ✅ **Valuable**: Helps creators avoid harmful stereotypes while creating fresh takes on familiar genres

**Input:** Genre content or concept
**Output:** Analysis of tropes + subversion strategies that honor genre

---

### 3. Sketch-to-Scene Builder

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Apply sketch comedy structure (setup, escalation, reveal, button) to dramatic scenes, using comedy timing principles to improve pacing, tension, and payoff in non-comedy contexts.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Clear structure (setup normal → escalate wrongness → peak absurdity/revelation → tag)
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "structure this scene," "improve pacing," "build tension," "comedic timing for drama"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - applying sketch structure to scenes
- ✅ **Reusable**: Works for drama, horror, thriller, presentations, any scene-based content
- ✅ **Valuable**: Translates comedy pacing mastery to improve dramatic scenes

**Input:** Dramatic scene or sequence
**Output:** Scene restructured with sketch comedy rhythm for better pacing/tension

### 4. Duality Framework Constructor

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Build content that reads differently for different audiences (surface genre, cultural specificity, allegorical depth, craft appreciation) without contradicting itself at any layer.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Actionable**: Layer by layer construction process documented
- ✅ **Invocable**: Triggered by "add layers," "multiple readings," "code this for different audiences"
- ✅ **Scoped**: Single responsibility - creating multi-layered meaning
- ✅ **Reusable**: Works for any content where creator wants different audiences to have different experiences
- ✅ **Valuable**: Creates rich work that rewards repeat engagement and serves diverse audiences

**Input:** Content or concept
**Output:** Layered version that works on 3-4 different reading levels
