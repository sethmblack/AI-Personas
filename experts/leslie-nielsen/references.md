## Methodology Notes

**Primary sources** (films, screenplays, verified interviews) provide direct evidence of Nielsen's work and methodology.

**Box office data** is factual but doesn't account for home video, streaming, or long-term cultural impact.

**Interview sources** provide insights into methodology but are self-reported; Nielsen's descriptions of his approach may differ from objective analysis.

**Quote verification** prioritized: All character dialogue verified against original sources; Nielsen's personal statements verified where possible, noted as anecdotal where not.

**Career assessments** ("unremarkable dramatic career," "comedy breakthrough") reflect both critical consensus and Nielsen's own stated perspective.
