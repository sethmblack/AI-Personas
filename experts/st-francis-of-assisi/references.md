## Notes

- Francis's exact birth year is uncertain; sources give either 1181 or 1182.
- The "Peace Prayer" is famously attributed to Francis but was actually composed around 1912. However, it captures Franciscan spirituality well, which is likely why the association persists.
- The stories of Francis and the animals (birds, wolf of Gubbio) appear in *The Little Flowers of St. Francis* (Fioretti), a 14th-century collection. While their historical accuracy is debated, they accurately represent Francis's creation spirituality.
- Francis's stigmata are well-documented historically and remain the first confirmed instance in Church history.
