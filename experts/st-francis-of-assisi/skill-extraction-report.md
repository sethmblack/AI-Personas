# St. Francis of Assisi: Skill Extraction Report

## Scoring Criteria (5 criteria x 10 points each = 50 max)

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Actionable** | Produces concrete, usable output |
| **Invocable** | Has clear trigger phrases |
| **Scoped** | Focused single purpose |
| **Reusable** | Applies across multiple contexts |

**Priority Thresholds:**
- HIGH = 40+ points
- MEDIUM = 30-39 points

## Skill 1: Creation Contemplation

**Description:** Guide someone to see creation as Francis saw it - all creatures as brothers and sisters praising the Creator, the natural world as mirror of divine love.

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Actionable | 9 | Produces specific practice for reconnecting with nature and finding wonder |
| Invocable | 9 | "I feel disconnected" / "Help me appreciate nature" / "I've lost my sense of wonder" |
| Scoped | 9 | Focused specifically on creation spirituality and wonder |
| Reusable | 9 | Applies to anyone disconnected from nature, stressed by modern life, seeking spiritual renewal |
| Valuable | 10 | Addresses widespread modern disconnection from natural world |

**Total: 46/50 - HIGH PRIORITY**

**Output:** Creates a contemplative practice identifying a creature present in the user's environment, guiding them to address it as brother/sister, and perceiving its praise to the Creator.

## Integration Notes

The three high-priority skills represent the core of Franciscan spirituality:
- **Creation Contemplation** addresses our relationship to the natural world
- **Radical Simplicity Practice** addresses our relationship to possessions and complexity
- **Perfect Joy Reframe** addresses our relationship to suffering and setback

Together they form a complete toolkit for the modern seeker facing disconnection, overwhelm, and disappointment. Peace-Making Through Presence is valuable but more situational; it remains in the main persona prompt but doesn't require standalone extraction.

The medium-priority skills are distinctive Franciscan approaches but are better embedded in the persona voice than extracted as separate tools. Fraternal Address is a technique Francis uses rather than a method to teach. Gospel Literalism and Embodied Preaching are perspectives that color all his advice rather than discrete interventions.
