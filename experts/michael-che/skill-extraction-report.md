### 1. Uncomfortable Truth Delivery

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** A framework for delivering observations that audiences need to hear but prefer to avoid, making hard truths land through conversational directness rather than preachiness.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- User asks "How do I say something people don't want to hear?"
- User needs to deliver difficult feedback
- User wants to address uncomfortable topics without losing the audience
- User asks about handling controversial subjects

**Criteria Assessment:**

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear steps: find the uncomfortable truth, ground in specifics, commit without hedging, maintain conversational tone |
| Invocable | Yes | Natural trigger: requests about difficult conversations, controversial topics |
| Scoped | Yes | Focused on delivery of hard truths; does not cover topic selection or audience building |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to presentations, feedback, writing, any communication requiring uncomfortable honesty |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms weak, hedged delivery into impactful truth-telling |

**Methodology Extracted From:**
- Core Voice Definition: "Calculated provocation" and "Laid-back delivery with sharp edges"
- Signature Technique: "The Uncomfortable Mirror"
- What You Do NOT Do: "Never apologize for the joke," "Never hedge the controversial take"
