#### 1. character-truth-scene-builder
**Rationale:** Core Reiner methodology with clear framework and universal applicability to character-based storytelling.

**Evidence from expertise.md:**
- "The Character-Truth Framework" section provides 5-step process
- Applies to sitcom writing, scene construction, dialogue creation, character development
- Clear pattern: Establish character → place in situation → ask "What would they do?" → find comedy in authentic response → test against consistency

**Actionability:** ✓ Five explicit steps with clear decision points
**Invocable:** ✓ Triggers: "sitcom scene," "character-driven comedy," "scene structure," "what would character do," "authentic behavior"
**Scoped:** ✓ Single responsibility: construct scenes from character truth rather than imposed jokes
**Reusable:** ✓ Works across sitcom, screenplay, short story, any character-based narrative
**Valuable:** ✓ Transforms approach from "what's funny?" to "what's true?"—foundational shift in methodology

**Skill Scope:**
- Input: Character definition + situation OR generic scene concept
- Output: Scene constructed from authentic character choices with comedy emerging from truth
- Boundaries: Requires established character traits; not for abstract scenarios or joke-first comedy; character must drive action

#### 2. straight-man-interview-framework
**Rationale:** Signature Reiner technique from "2000 Year Old Man" with clear structure and broad application.

- Perfected through 40+ years of "2000 Year Old Man" performances
- Clear application: interview format, collaborative improvisation, dialogue development
- Pattern: Establish premise → ask simple questions → listen/follow → set up don't compete → maintain reality → ask deepening follow-ups

**Actionability:** ✓ Six explicit steps with examples
**Invocable:** ✓ Triggers: "interview format," "setup questions," "collaborative improvisation," "make partner look good," "dialogue development"
**Scoped:** ✓ Single responsibility: create interviewer questions/reactions that maximize partner's brilliance
**Reusable:** ✓ Works for interviews, improvised dialogue, podcast formats, collaborative comedy, two-person scenes
**Valuable:** ✓ Provides systematic approach to generous straight-man setup; unique Reiner technique not easily replicated

**Skill Scope:**
- Input: Partner character/persona + topic/premise
- Output: Interviewer questions and reactions that set up partner for maximum comedy/insight
- Boundaries: Two-person format only; requires one person in setup role; not for monologues or multi-party conversations; interviewer must stay grounded while partner explores

#### 3. three-act-sitcom-engine
**Rationale:** Foundational structure Reiner perfected in *The Dick Van Dyke Show* with clear mechanics and universal applicability.

**Evidence from expertise.md:**
- "The Three-Act Sitcom Engine" section provides detailed structure with timing
- Act One (7-8 min): Setup + problem introduction
- Act Two (12-14 min): Complication through character choices
- Act Three (6-8 min): Resolution through character growth + button joke
- Applies to all half-hour comedy, short-form narrative, character-driven story structure

**Actionability:** ✓ Three acts with specific timing, content requirements, and emotional beats
**Invocable:** ✓ Triggers: "episode structure," "sitcom outline," "story beats," "narrative arc," "plot development," "half-hour comedy"
**Scoped:** ✓ Single responsibility: structure complete story with setup-complication-resolution and character growth
**Reusable:** ✓ Works for sitcom episodes, short films, short stories, any narrative requiring complete arc in limited time
**Valuable:** ✓ Provides master template from creator of character-driven sitcom; ensures emotional and comedic payoff

**Skill Scope:**
- Input: Story concept or premise + characters
- Output: Three-act structure with specific beats, timing, escalation pattern, and resolution
- Boundaries: Half-hour format with character growth; not for serialized drama without resolution, sketch comedy without arc, or stories without emotional truth; requires character consistency throughout
