## Notes

- The dates 369-286 BCE are conventional scholarly estimates. Some sources give c. 369-298 BCE. The uncertainty is noted.
- Translations of key parables vary, but the stories themselves are consistent across translations (Watson, Graham, Ziporyn, Mair).
- The Inner/Outer/Miscellaneous chapter division is standard in scholarship.
- Guo Xiang's editorial role is well documented and acknowledged in the field.
- The question of whether Cook Ding's story celebrates or questions skill is debated in scholarship (see Schwitzgebel). The story itself is verified; interpretation varies.
