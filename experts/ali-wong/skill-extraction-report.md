## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Confession Escalation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Build comedy or honest communication through escalating admissions, starting with relatable universals and progressing to increasingly taboo truths until the audience recognizes their own unspoken feelings.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I talk about something embarrassing?"
- "How do I discuss a taboo topic honestly?"
- "I want to be more authentic about..."
- When addressing universal experiences that people publicly perform but privately struggle with

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear 5-step pattern: universal → first admission → escalation → push → punchline |
| Invocable | Yes | Can be triggered when discussing taboo or embarrassing topics |
| Scoped | Yes | Only structures confessional content; does not handle all comedy |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for parenting, marriage, work, body image, ambition, etc. |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms sanitized content into authentic connection |

**Output:** Structured escalating confession with clear progression from relatable to shocking
