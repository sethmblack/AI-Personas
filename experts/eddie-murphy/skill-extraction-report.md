## Extraction Criteria

Skills must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

---

## HIGH Priority Skills

### 1. character-explosion

**Description:** Transform flat descriptions or narratives into explosive multi-character scenes where Eddie Murphy embodies all participants with distinct voices, physicality, and attitudes.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Clear steps - identify participants, assign voice/physicality to each, perform rapid character switches
- ✓ Invocable: "Make this a character scene," "Bring these people to life," "Do the Eddie Murphy version"
- ✓ Scoped: Focused on character embodiment transformation
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to any narrative, dialogue, or scenario description
- ✓ Valuable: Transforms boring content into dynamic, memorable performances

**Framework Source:** "The Character Arsenal Method" from expertise.md - multiple characters in rapid succession, each with distinct voice/physicality/attitude

**Priority:** HIGH - This is Murphy's signature technique and most distinctive contribution

---

### 2. energy-amplifier

**Description:** Take low-energy or flat content and inject Eddie Murphy's explosive, kinetic energy through physical descriptions, rhythm, and infectious enthusiasm.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Add movement descriptions, amplify with laughter, increase pace, inject physical comedy
- ✓ Invocable: "Amp this up," "Add Eddie's energy," "Make this more explosive"
- ✓ Scoped: Focused on energy transformation only
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to any text that needs more life
- ✓ Valuable: Turns boring presentations/content into engaging performances

**Framework Source:** "The Energy Amplification Method" from expertise.md - kinetic comedy, physical commitment, sustained high energy

**Priority:** HIGH - Energy is what makes Murphy distinctive from other character comedians

---

### 3. fearless-truth-bomb

**Description:** Identify uncomfortable truths in a situation and express them with Eddie Murphy's raw, unfiltered confidence and no-apology style.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Identify taboo/uncomfortable truth, state it directly, ride the shock wave, find humanity in it
- ✓ Invocable: "What's the real truth here?" "Give it to me raw," "No filter version"
- ✓ Scoped: Focused on authentic, unfiltered truth-telling
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to any situation needing honest assessment
- ✓ Valuable: Cuts through politeness and corporate-speak to real insights

**Framework Source:** "The Fearless Truth Framework" from expertise.md - say what everyone thinks but won't say

**Priority:** HIGH - Core to Murphy's comedy philosophy and cultural impact

---

## MEDIUM Priority Skills

### 4. murphy-dialogue-naturalizer

**Description:** Transform written, formal, or stilted dialogue into authentic, flowing conversation that sounds like real people talking.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Identify formal language, replace with vernacular, add rhythm and flow
- ✓ Invocable: "Make this sound real," "Naturalize this dialogue"
- ✓ Scoped: Focused on dialogue authenticity only
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to any dialogue or conversation
- ✓ Valuable: Makes scripts and content feel authentic rather than written

**Framework Source:** Murphy's stated technique: "From the very beginning, I always tried to make dialogue flow comfortably; I always did that to make it seem more authentic."

**Priority:** MEDIUM - Important technique but not as distinctive as character work or energy

---

## LOW Priority Skills

### 5. physical-comedy-amplifier

**Description:** Add physical comedy elements (walks, stances, facial expressions, movements) to enhance verbal comedy.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ Actionable: Identify comedy moments, add physical descriptions
- ✓ Invocable: "Add physical comedy," "What's the physicality?"
- ⚠ Scoped: Overlaps significantly with energy-amplifier skill
- ✓ Reusable: Applies to performance content
- ⚠ Valuable: Limited to performance contexts, less useful for general writing

**Priority:** LOW - Functionality largely covered by energy-amplifier; physical comedy is a component of that broader skill rather than standalone
