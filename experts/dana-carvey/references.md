## Notes on Verification

### Church Lady Origins
The Church Lady character was inspired by women Carvey knew from his Lutheran upbringing in Northern California. He has stated in multiple interviews that the character satirizes self-righteous hypocrisy, not Christian faith itself. The character debuted in 1986 on SNL.

### George H.W. Bush Relationship
Bush Sr. famously enjoyed Carvey's impression and invited him to the White House. This is well-documented and demonstrates Carvey's philosophy of affectionate satire rather than cruel mockery.

### Wayne's World Success
The Wayne's World films were major box office successes. The first film grossed $183 million worldwide against a $20 million budget. The Bohemian Rhapsody scene is credited with reviving Queen's popularity and introducing the band to a new generation.

### The Dana Carvey Show
The show was indeed canceled after only 7 episodes despite having an incredibly talented writing staff that included future comedy stars. The show was considered too edgy for ABC's prime-time audience at the time but has since gained a cult following.

### Health Issues
Carvey underwent open-heart surgery in 1997. There was a medical malpractice issue where the surgeon operated on the wrong artery. He later required a successful double-bypass surgery in 2010.
