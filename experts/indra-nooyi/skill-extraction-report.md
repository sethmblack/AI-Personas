**Trigger:** "How do I make my business more purposeful?" or "Assess our strategy for purpose" or "Is our purpose authentic?"

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Four pillars provide explicit evaluation structure
- Invocable: YES - Clear trigger phrases ("make my business purposeful")
- Scoped: YES - Evaluates strategy-purpose alignment specifically
- Reusable: YES - Applies to any organization regardless of industry
- Valuable: YES - Prevents expensive purpose initiatives that fail

### 2. portfolio-transformation-framework

**Source Pattern:** Portfolio Transformation / Portfolio Reshaping (2006-2018)
**Purpose:** Systematically evaluate and transform a product/service portfolio toward healthier, more sustainable offerings without abandoning heritage
**Trigger:** "How do I transform our product portfolio?" or "How healthy is our portfolio?" or "Shift our offerings toward sustainability"
**Inputs:** Current product/service catalog, revenue by product, customer segments, sustainability goals, competitive landscape
**Outputs:** Portfolio categorization (Fun/Better/Good-for-You), revenue mix analysis, reformulation opportunities, acquisition targets, public target recommendations, transformation roadmap
**Reasoning:** Explicit 5-step diagnostic with proven PepsiCo results (70%→50% "Fun-for-You" shift). Applicable to any consumer goods, food/beverage, or service business. Saves weeks of ad-hoc portfolio analysis.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Five explicit diagnostic steps with categorization framework
- Invocable: YES - Clear trigger ("transform our portfolio")
- Scoped: YES - Portfolio analysis and transformation only
- Reusable: YES - Generalizes beyond PepsiCo to any portfolio business
- Valuable: YES - $4B+ transformation proves framework effectiveness

**Purpose:** Build and communicate a case for long-term investments when facing short-term pressure from investors, analysts, or boards
**Trigger:** "How do I defend long-term investments?" or "Analysts are pressuring for short-term results" or "Board wants to cut R&D"
**Inputs:** Proposed investment, short-term costs, long-term benefits, stakeholder objections, competitive landscape, investor composition
**Outputs:** Investment defense brief with: quantified long-term opportunity, cost-of-not-investing analysis, bridge strategy (short-term accommodation), investor segmentation strategy, communication plan
**Reasoning:** Proven framework from Nooyi's successful defense against Trian (67% return to activist who dropped campaign). Clear steps for quantifying vulnerability, building coalitions, and offering bridges. High-stakes decisions where framework prevents capitulation.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Five explicit steps for building defense case
- Invocable: YES - Clear triggers around investor pressure
- Scoped: YES - Investment defense specifically, not general strategy
- Reusable: YES - Applies to any long-term investment under pressure
- Valuable: YES - Prevents value-destroying short-term decisions

**Inputs:** Current role demands, family situation, existing support systems, non-negotiable commitments, guilt patterns

**Reasoning:** Nooyi's honest framework explicitly rejects "balance" myth. Five steps with practical focus on support systems and guilt management. Emotionally valuable for leaders struggling with trade-offs.

**Criterion Evaluation:**
- Actionable: YES - Five explicit steps from myth-busting to guilt management
- Invocable: YES - Clear triggers around work-life concerns
- Scoped: YES - Integration audit specifically, not career coaching
- Reusable: YES - Applies to any leader with demanding career and family
- Valuable: YES - Provides honest framework absent in most work-life advice
