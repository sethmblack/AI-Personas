## HIGH Priority

### 1. pompous-character-framework

**Source Pattern:** The Pompous Character Archetype and Character Building sections
**Purpose:** Create authority figures whose carefully constructed dignity can be systematically undermined for comedic effect
**Trigger:** When creating characters for comedy, satire, or social commentary; when needing to embody authority that can be challenged
**Inputs:** Character's source of authority, self-perception, specific dignity markers, pressure points where dignity can be challenged
**Outputs:** Fully realized pompous character with clear physical/vocal markers, escalation path, and authentic breaking points
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 5-step process (establish authority, build markers, identify pressure points, escalate challenges, allow authentic break)
- Invocable: Triggered by requests to "create authority figure," "build pompous character," or "design comedic villain"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of pompous character creation; boundaries clear (character development only, not full narrative)
- Reusable: Applies to acting, sketch writing, character design, satire, social commentary, teaching
- Valuable: Saves significant effort in character development; creates memorable, multi-dimensional characters rather than flat caricatures

### 2. reactive-escalation

**Source Pattern:** Reactive Comedy section and The Korman Methodology table
**Purpose:** Build comedic tension through increasingly desperate attempts to maintain control in the face of chaos
**Trigger:** When performing comedy, improvising, or creating scenes with building tension; when playing straight against chaos
**Inputs:** Baseline control state, source of disruption, character's investment in maintaining control, escalation timeline
**Outputs:** Structured escalation pattern with specific reactions at each stage, culminating in authentic breaking point
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 5-step process (establish control, introduce disruption, respond specifically, escalate both sides, reach breaking point)
- Invocable: Triggered by requests to "build comedic tension," "react to improvisation," or "create escalating frustration"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of reactive escalation; distinct from general improvisation or character creation
- Reusable: Applies to comedy performance, sketch writing, improvisation, teaching, creating dramatic tension
- Valuable: Provides structure for reactive performance; enables building to payoff rather than random chaos

- Scoped: Single responsibility of ensemble support; boundaries clear (enhancing others, not self-promotion)
- Reusable: Applies to ensemble performance, team creativity, collaborative comedy, improvisation, teaching, leadership
- Valuable: Enables generous collaboration that makes everyone stronger; prevents ego-driven competition in creative work

## Notes

The three identified frameworks (Pompous Character, Reactive Escalation, Ensemble Support) represent Korman's most transferable and actionable methodologies. They are distinct, well-bounded, and applicable beyond sketch comedy.

**Why certain rich material was rejected:**

- **The Breaking Character Phenomenon**: While fascinating and central to Korman's work, it lacks the clear process structure needed for a skill. It's more of an outcome of other factors (commitment, reactive escalation, genuine surprise) than a methodology itself.

- **Physical/Vocal Precision**: These are important techniques but function as components of the larger "pompous-character-framework" rather than standalone skills.

- **Tim Conway Dynamic**: This describes a successful partnership and complementary strengths but doesn't provide a repeatable process for creating such dynamics.

- **Brooks Collaboration Insights**: These are valuable principles but lack the step-by-step structure that makes a skill directly invocable.

**Integration of techniques into frameworks:**

The pompous-character-framework incorporates:
- Physical precision (posture, gesture, movement)
- Vocal control (diction, pacing, tone)
- Character building methods
- Finding humanity in pomposity

The reactive-escalation framework incorporates:
- Specific frustration building
- The visible struggle
- Precise corrections
- The controlled explosion

The ensemble-support-technique incorporates:
- Making partners look good
- Reactive generosity
- Straight structure provision
- Shared success philosophy

**Potential future skills:**

If expertise.md is expanded with more detailed processes, consider:

- **comedy-through-commitment**: Playing ridiculous material with dramatic sincerity

These would require more explicit step-by-step frameworks than currently exist in the expertise file.
