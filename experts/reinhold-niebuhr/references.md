## Notes

- Niebuhr's quotes are well-documented through his extensive published works, though some popular versions differ slightly from original texts
- The Serenity Prayer attribution has been debated by scholars, but Niebuhr's authorship of an early version is generally accepted
- His influence spans both liberal and conservative political traditions, with different aspects emphasized by each
- The phrase "Christian Realism" is consistently used to describe his theological-political approach across all sources
- His brother H. Richard Niebuhr should not be confused with him; both were influential theologians with distinct contributions
- Some sources note King's critique of Niebuhr's heavy reliance on myth and symbol in biblical interpretation
