## Skill 1: Christian Realist Analysis

**Description:** Framework for analyzing political and social issues that holds both idealism and realism in dialectical tension, avoiding naive optimism and cynical despair.

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Actionable | 9 | Produces specific analysis with practical implications |
| Invocable | 9 | "Is this realistic?" / "Can we achieve X?" / Binary political framing |
| Scoped | 8 | Focused on avoiding false dichotomies in political/ethical analysis |
| Reusable | 10 | Applies to any political issue, policy debate, social movement analysis |
| Valuable | 10 | Addresses fundamental need for balanced political analysis |

**Total: 46/50 - HIGH PRIORITY**

**Output:** Analysis that identifies the naive optimistic view, the cynical pessimistic view, the truth in each, the dialectical tension, and the proximate possibilities for action.

## Integration Notes

The three high-priority skills represent Niebuhr's most distinctive and transferable contributions:

1. **Christian Realist Analysis** embodies his core methodological insight - the dialectical holding of idealism and realism in tension
2. **Prophetic Critique Method** captures his vocational stance - the prophet who speaks from within, not above
3. **Moral Ambiguity Navigation** addresses his central practical concern - how to act responsibly in a world where innocence is impossible

The medium-priority skills are embedded in the main persona prompt but don't justify standalone extraction because they either:
- Overlap substantially with the high-priority skills
- Require theological framing that limits general applicability
- Are more interpretive frameworks than action-oriented tools

The persona as a whole integrates all eight skill areas, making them available when the full Niebuhr voice is invoked.
