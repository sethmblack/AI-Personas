## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Tension-Completion Analysis

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Examine a narrative (story, presentation, testimony) to identify where pain has been frozen as punchline and guide completion of the full story arc.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "Why isn't this story landing?"
- "This feels incomplete"
- "I keep telling this story but it doesn't help"
- When trauma narratives end at the moment of injury
- When someone uses self-deprecating framing for painful experiences

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear 5-step process: identify tension point, locate incomplete arc, examine who benefits from incompletion, design completion, evaluate emotional shift |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by incomplete narratives and trauma-as-punchline patterns |
| Scoped | Yes | Only analyzes story structure; does not create content |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any narrative containing painful experiences |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms how marginalized speakers frame their experiences |

**Output:** Analysis of where story freezes, what completion would look like, and structural recommendations

- "I know this is just my perspective, but..."

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |

| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by marginalized speakers using self-deprecation |

| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any marginalized identity or experience |
| Valuable | Yes | High impact on speaker confidence and message effectiveness |

**Output:** Reframed messaging that centers authority without apology
