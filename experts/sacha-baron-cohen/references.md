## Legal Cases

### Roy Moore v. Sacha Baron Cohen (Who Is America?, 2018)

**Background:** Roy Moore sued Baron Cohen for $95 million after appearing in a "Who Is America?" segment where fake character "Erran Morad" used a mock "pedophile detector" device.

**Timeline:**
- **July 2021:** U.S. District Judge John P. Cronan dismissed the lawsuit
- **July 2022:** Second Circuit Court of Appeals unanimously rejected Moore's appeal

**Ruling:** The court found the segment was "clearly comedy" and that Moore had signed a release waiver barring future claims.

### Fraternity Brothers Lawsuit (Borat, 2006)

**Plaintiffs:** Two University of South Carolina fraternity members who appeared in the RV scene making racist and sexist statements.

**Claims:** Producers used alcohol to coerce participation; told them film would not be shown in the United States.

**Outcome:** Dismissed under California's anti-SLAPP statute; plaintiffs had signed releases.

**Sources:**
- [GW Hatchet - Frat brothers lash back at 'Borat'](https://gwhatchet.com/2006/11/20/frat-brothers-lash-back-at-borat/)
- [NPR - 'Borat' Faces Legal Challenges on Public Releases](https://www.npr.org/2006/11/14/6487313/borat-faces-legal-challenges-on-public-releases)

### Village of Glod, Romania Lawsuit (Borat, 2006)

**Plaintiffs:** Residents of Glod, Romania, whose village was depicted as Borat's hometown in Kazakhstan.

**Claims:** $30 million lawsuit alleging exploitation; claimed they were told the film was a documentary about poverty.

**Outcome:**
- December 2006: Initially dismissed by U.S. District Judge Loretta Preska
- April 2008: Case dismissed again citing insufficient evidence

**Defense:** 20th Century Fox revealed Baron Cohen had donated $5,000 to the town plus paid location fees and provided school/business supplies.

**Sources:**
- [Fox News - Romanians Say 'Borat' Misled Them](https://www.foxnews.com/story/romanians-say-borat-misled-them)
- [Fox News - Romanians File $30M Suit](https://www.foxnews.com/story/romanians-file-30m-suit-over-depiction-in-borat)
- [Jonathan Turley - Very Nice: Borat Wins Defamation Case](https://jonathanturley.org/2008/04/03/very-nice-borat-wins-defamation-case/)

### Other Borat Lawsuits (2006)
- Driving instructor lawsuit - Dismissed
- Etiquette instructor lawsuit - Dismissed
- All cases dismissed in early litigation stages due to signed releases and First Amendment protections

**Source:** [Hollywood Reporter - Do Sacha Baron Cohen's Targets Have a Shot at Winning a Lawsuit?](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/do-sacha-baron-cohens-targets-have-a-shot-at-winning-a-lawsuit-1128129/)

- **2005:** Following Borat's MTV Movie Awards appearance, Kazakhstan's Foreign Ministry threatened to sue; borat.kz website taken down
- **Later:** Kazakhstan embraced the film for tourism, adopting "Very nice!" slogan

## Who Is America? (2018) - Notable Incidents

### Jason Spencer Resignation

**Subject:** Jason Spencer, Georgia State Representative (District 180)

**What Happened:** Spencer appeared with Baron Cohen's character "Erran Morad," during which he:
- Dropped his pants
- Used racial slurs repeatedly
- Made racist impersonations

**Political Fallout:**
- Georgia House Speaker David Ralston called actions "reprehensible"
- Governor Nathan Deal condemned the behavior
- Spencer resigned effective July 31, 2018

## Philippe Gaulier References

### About the School
- **Founded:** 1980 (after leaving Jacques Lecoq)
- **Locations:** Paris (1980-1991), London/Cricklewood (1991-2002), Sceaux (2005-2011), Etampes (2011-present)
- **Teaching Philosophy:** "Le jeu" (play), embracing failure ("flop"), pleasure ("plaisir")

### Notable Alumni (besides Baron Cohen)
Emma Thompson, Helena Bonham Carter, Roberto Benigni, Rachel Weisz, Simon McBurney, Geoffrey Rush, Kathryn Hunter

### Baron Cohen on Gaulier
> "Gaulier is the greatest living teacher of clown and modern theatre, and the funniest man I've ever met."
