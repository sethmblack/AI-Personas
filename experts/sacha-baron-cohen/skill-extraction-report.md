## HIGH Priority

### 1. character-exposure-design

**Source Pattern:** The Naive Interviewer + Status Reversal (combined)
**Purpose:** Design a character persona that creates optimal conditions for subjects to reveal their authentic beliefs and hidden assumptions
**Trigger:** "Design a character to expose this subject's real views" or "Create an interview persona that will disarm this type of subject"
**Inputs:** Subject profile (role, known positions, likely defenses), context (setting, format), desired revelations (what you want to uncover)
**Outputs:** Character definition (background, speech patterns, status position), interview strategy, naive questions list, anticipated revelation points
**Reasoning:** Core methodology from Baron Cohen's work. Combines multiple techniques (naive questioning, status reversal, absurdist innocence) into single character design workflow. HIGH priority because: estimated 3+ uses/week for journalists, documentarians, user researchers; saves 30+ minutes of character design work; has 5+ explicit steps (character background, status position, speech pattern, question design, revelation anticipation).

### 2. belief-stress-test

**Source Pattern:** The Absurd Proposal + The Logical Extension
**Purpose:** Test whether stated positions are genuinely held beliefs or performative stances by extending them to logical conclusions
**Trigger:** "Test whether this position is genuinely held" or "Stress-test this belief through logical extension"
**Inputs:** Subject's stated position, context for testing, boundaries of absurdity acceptable in context
**Outputs:** Series of extension questions/proposals, predicted response patterns, analysis of what responses reveal
**Reasoning:** Powerful diagnostic tool that reveals the difference between genuine conviction and performative positioning. HIGH priority because: applicable across multiple domains (interviews, negotiations, debates, research); saves significant time in identifying authentic vs. performative positions; has clear methodology (identify position, extend logically, present sincerely, observe response).
