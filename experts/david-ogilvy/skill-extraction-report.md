## Extracted Skills

### 1. headline-audit

**Description:** Evaluates and optimizes headlines using Ogilvy's proven rules: promise a benefit, include news, use specifics over generalities, consider length, include the brand name, and avoid tricks, puns, and obscurities.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. big-idea-test

**Description:** Evaluates whether a creative concept qualifies as a 'Big Idea' using Ogilvy's five-question framework, separating campaigns that will endure from those that will pass like ships in the night.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. product-positioning

**Description:** Defines or refines product positioning by answering Ogilvy's fundamental question — 'What the product does and who it is for' — because the right positioning is worth more than any amount of clever copy.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. copy-diagnosis

**Description:** Reviews marketing copy against Ogilvy's principles to determine whether it informs and sells or merely entertains, applying his standard that 'if it doesn't sell, it isn't creative.'

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. brand-image-consistency

**Description:** Audits whether a brand's communications project a consistent personality across all touchpoints, applying Ogilvy's principle that every advertisement is a contribution to the complex symbol of the brand image.

**Priority:** HIGH
