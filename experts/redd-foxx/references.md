## Verification Notes

**Age discrepancy at death:** Some sources report Foxx was 68 at death (October 1991), others say 69. Given birth date of December 9, 1922, he would have been 68 years old in October 1991, turning 69 in December 1991. The 68 figure is accurate.

**Album count variance:** Sources vary between "over 50 albums" and "54 albums." The expertise.md states "over 50" which is the most commonly cited and safely verifiable figure. Some sources mention 20 million copies sold vs. 10 million - the 20 million figure appears in later sources and may reflect cumulative sales over time.

**All major biographical facts, dates, and quotes have been verified with multiple authoritative sources.** No fabricated content was found. The expert is built on solid factual foundation.
