**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Analyze comedy material or content to identify where blue/adult material could replace euphemism with authentic language, then restructure with proper timing and discipline.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear steps: identify sanitized language → replace with authentic language → rebuild timing structure → ensure craft discipline
- ✓ **Invocable** - User provides content that feels sanitized or euphemistic; skill analyzes and transforms
- ✓ **Scoped** - Focused solely on the transformation from euphemism to authenticity with structural integrity
- ✓ **Reusable** - Applies to comedy writing, dialogue, character voice, any content that needs authentic adult language
- ✓ **Valuable** - Teaches the discipline required for effective blue material; prevents "shock without structure" mistakes

**Extraction source:** "Blue Material Discipline" framework in expertise.md and "Blue Material as Truth-Telling" technique in PROMPT.md

**Potential skill name:** `blue-material-craft`

### 2. Timing Architecture Builder

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Take comedy material or dialogue and engineer precise timing markers (setup pace, complication acceleration, pause placement, snap delivery, aftermath space).

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable** - Clear steps: analyze existing material → identify timing components → mark pauses and acceleration → specify delivery rhythm → test and refine
- ✓ **Invocable** - User provides comedy material or dialogue lacking timing precision
- ✓ **Scoped** - Focused on timing engineering, not content creation or joke writing
- ✓ **Reusable** - Applies to stand-up, dialogue, presentations, any performance material requiring timing
- ✓ **Valuable** - Timing is often intuitive/undocumented; making it explicit and measurable is highly valuable for writers and performers

**Extraction source:** "The Timing Architecture" framework in expertise.md and core principle "Timing is physics" in PROMPT.md

**Potential skill name:** `timing-architecture`

### 3. Callback Setup System

- ✓ **Invocable** - User provides script, set, or content that feels disconnected or episodic

**Extraction source:** "The Callback Setup" technique in PROMPT.md

- ✓ **Scoped** - Focused on character construction from observation, not full character development or story writing
- ✓ **Reusable** - Applies to comedy, fiction writing, dialogue, sketch development, any character-based work
