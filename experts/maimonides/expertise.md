# Maimonides - Domain Knowledge

## Biographical Summary

**Moses ben Maimon (Maimonides/Rambam)** (1138-1204) was a Sephardic Jewish philosopher, physician, and Torah scholar.

- **Birth:** 1138 (or 1135), Córdoba, Spain
- **Death:** December 12, 1204, Fustat (Cairo), Egypt
- **Titles:** Rambam (Rabbi Moses ben Maimon)
- **Profession:** Rabbi, physician, philosopher
- **Known For:** Guide for the Perplexed, Mishneh Torah, 13 Principles of Faith

---

## Major Works

| Work | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Mishneh Torah (1180) | 14-volume code of Jewish law; comprehensive and systematic |
| Guide for the Perplexed (1190) | Philosophical masterpiece; reconciling faith and Aristotelian philosophy |
| Commentary on the Mishnah | Including the 13 Principles of Faith |
| Medical works | Including treatises on health and pharmaceuticals |

---

## Key Concepts

### Negative Theology
- Cannot describe what God is, only what God is not
- Every positive attribute is inadequate
- Apophatic approach to the divine

### 13 Principles of Faith
1. God's existence
2. God's unity
3. God's incorporeality
4. God's eternity
5. God alone should be worshipped
6. Prophecy exists
7. Moses's prophecy is supreme
8. Torah is from heaven
9. Torah is unchangeable
10. God knows human deeds
11. Reward and punishment
12. The Messiah will come
13. Resurrection of the dead

### Reason and Revelation
- Both from God; cannot contradict
- Philosophy clarifies Scripture
- Scripture contains hidden wisdom

---

## Physician and Sage

- Personal physician to Sultan Saladin
- Wrote medical treatises
- Integrated health of body and soul
- Famous saying: "Give a man a fish..."

---

## Legacy

- Most influential medieval Jewish philosopher
- 13 Principles became liturgical creed
- Bridge between Jewish and Islamic philosophy
- Model for faith-reason harmony
- "From Moses to Moses, there arose none like Moses"
