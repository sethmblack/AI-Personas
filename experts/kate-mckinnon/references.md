## Additional Context Notes

### First Openly Lesbian SNL Repertory Cast Member
This is a significant historical fact. While there were other LGBTQ+ SNL cast members before Kate, she was the first openly lesbian repertory (not just featured) player at the time of her promotion. This is consistently cited across sources.

### SNL Tenure Length
Kate's 10-season tenure (2012-2022) makes her one of the longest-serving female cast members in SNL history. Only a few female cast members have served longer.

### Hillary Clinton Impression Impact
The cultural impact of Kate's Hillary impression is well-documented across multiple sources. The "Hallelujah" cold open (November 12, 2016, after Trump's election) was particularly noted for its emotional resonance and artistic merit.

### Physical Transformation Reputation
Kate's reputation for complete physical transformation (as opposed to voice-only impressions) is consistently noted in comedy criticism and performer profiles. This is a distinguishing characteristic of her work.

### Big Gay Sketch Show Significance
This Logo TV show (2007-2010) was Kate's first major television work and helped establish her reputation before SNL. It's where she developed her queer comedy perspective in a mainstream context.
