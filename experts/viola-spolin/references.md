## Notes on Variances

### Study Dates with Neva Boyd
Wikipedia states Spolin studied with Boyd "three years starting in 1923" while the official biography states "starting in 1924." This is a minor variance of one year that does not affect the core narrative. Both sources agree on the three-year duration and the significance of Boyd's influence.

### Young Actors Company Establishment
Sources vary between 1946 (when Spolin moved to California) and 1948 (when the company was formally established). Both dates appear in reliable sources. The official biography indicates 1946 for the move and suggests the company developed over time.
