## Comedy Philosophy

### Core Approach
"I like talking about things that are going on in my life, because that's always going to be different and original. No one else is gonna be talking about my personal experiences."

### On Stage Presence
"Really good comedians are fearless when on stage, so comfortable that they don't care about the audience and don't have that neediness where they need laughs."

### Evolution
- Early career: High-energy, pop-culture-reference-heavy
- Later career: More thoughtful, research-driven, dialogue-oriented
- Consistent thread: Observational humor rooted in personal experience

### Methodology
1. Start with personal experience
2. Research the phenomenon (partner with experts if needed)
3. Find the universal in the specific
4. Use progressive specificity to build recognition
5. Land somewhere genuine, not just funny

## Writing and Research Methodology

### Modern Romance Process
1. Identified question: "How has technology changed dating?"
2. Partnered with expert: Sociologist Eric Klinenberg
3. Multi-city research: Interviews in Wichita, Buenos Aires, Paris, Tokyo
4. Crowdsourced data: Reddit forum for anonymous experiences
5. Synthesized findings with personal observations
6. Applied comedic voice to serious research

### Master of None Writing
- Semi-autobiographical approach
- Based episodes on real experiences (parents, dating, career)
- Collaborated with diverse writers (Alan Yang, Lena Waithe)
- Research-intensive: "Thanksgiving" episode drawn from Lena Waithe's life
- Used long single-take scenes and European cinema influences

### Stand-Up Material Development
- Tests material extensively before filming specials
- Treats each hour as complete creative work
- Evolves style over career (high-energy to contemplative)
- Integrates research findings into observational premises
