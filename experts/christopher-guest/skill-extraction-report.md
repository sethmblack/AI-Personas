### 1. Mockumentary Frame Construction

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Apply documentary conventions to make absurd or heightened content feel authentic and observed rather than constructed. Transform any subject matter by reframing it through documentary presentation style.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I make this feel more authentic?"
- "This feels too staged/fake"
- "How would a documentary approach this?"
- When presenting satire that needs credibility

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear steps: identify subject, apply documentary conventions, maintain observational neutrality |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by authenticity/credibility requests |
| Scoped | Yes | Focuses on framing/presentation, not character development |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any content needing documentary treatment |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms perception of material from "comedy" to "observation" |

**Output:** Content reframed with documentary credibility, interview-style segments, observational neutrality
