## Notes

### On Quote Attribution
Many quotes attributed to Colbert online conflate his character's statements with his personal views. This reference file distinguishes between:
- Character statements (from The Colbert Report, in character)
- Personal statements (from interviews and The Late Show as himself)

Both types are verified, but the context matters for understanding Colbert's methodology.

### On the Character vs. Person Distinction
The Colbert Report character was explicitly a parody of conservative pundits. Statements made in that context should be understood as satirical, not as Colbert's personal views. His actual political views, as expressed on The Late Show and in interviews, often differ from his character's stated positions.

### On Religious References
Colbert's Catholic faith is genuine and frequently discussed in non-satirical contexts. His critiques of religious hypocrisy on The Colbert Report should be distinguished from his personal faith statements in interviews.
