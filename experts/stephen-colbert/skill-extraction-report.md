## Extraction Criteria

Each skill candidate must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Truthiness Detection

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Analyze statements, claims, or arguments to identify when emotional appeal is substituting for factual support—the "truthiness" pattern that Colbert identified as central to modern discourse.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "Why do people believe this despite evidence?"
- "Analyze this claim for emotional reasoning"
- "Is this truthiness?"
- When examining viral misinformation
- When analyzing political rhetoric

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear 5-step detection framework in expertise.md |
| Invocable | Yes | Can be triggered by requests to analyze claims |
| Scoped | Yes | Only identifies truthiness; does not create counter-arguments |
| Reusable | Yes | Applies to any claim in any domain |
| Valuable | Yes | Saves time analyzing why false beliefs persist |

**Output:** Report identifying truthiness markers: emotional appeal, identity-based reasoning, evidence rejection patterns, certainty-to-evidence ratio

### 2. Satirical Character Construction

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Build a committed satirical persona that embodies the position you wish to critique, enabling character-based satire rather than direct attack.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How would I parody this position?"
- "Create a satirical character for..."
- "I want to critique X through satire"
- When direct critique feels preachy or ineffective
- When developing comedy bits or pieces

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear process: identify target, find their logic, inhabit it fully |
| Invocable | Yes | Can be triggered when satire is needed |
| Scoped | Yes | Creates character; executing the bit is separate |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any satirical target |
| Valuable | Yes | Character-based satire is harder to dismiss than direct critique |

**Output:** Character profile with beliefs, rhetorical style, blind spots, and suggested escalation points

### 3. Interview Escalation Technique

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Take a stated position and escalate it to its logical absurd conclusion through "Yes, And" improv technique, forcing the position-holder to either agree with something ridiculous or contradict themselves.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "Expose the flaw in this argument"
- "How do I challenge this position comedically?"
- Preparing for debates or interviews
- When someone states something that sounds reasonable but has absurd implications
- When direct contradiction would be ineffective

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear improv-based process: accept premise, heighten, reveal absurdity |
| Invocable | Yes | Can be triggered for specific arguments or positions |
| Scoped | Yes | Only generates the escalation; delivery is separate |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any position with hidden implications |
| Valuable | Yes | More effective than direct argumentation in many contexts |

**Output:** Escalation sequence showing the logical steps from stated position to absurd conclusion

### 4. Sincere Pivot Deployment

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Transition from sustained irony or satire to genuine emotional expression, using the contrast to amplify the sincere message.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "When should I drop the satirical tone?"
- After sustained ironic content needs grounding
- When genuine connection matters more than comedy
- When sincerity would be more impactful than continued irony
- Moments requiring authentic vulnerability

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear technique: establish ironic baseline, identify pivot moment, shift to authentic voice |
| Invocable | Yes | Can be triggered when sincerity is needed |
| Scoped | Yes | Only handles the transition; content before/after is separate |
| Reusable | Yes | Works in any context mixing irony and sincerity |
| Valuable | Partial | Valuable but requires judgment about when to use |

**Output:** Transition point identification and suggested sincere content framing
