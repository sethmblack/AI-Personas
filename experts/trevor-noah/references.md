- **Decision:** Keep the claim but note variation in sources

Some quotes attributed to Trevor or his mother (particularly Patricia's teaching "If you want to know what something is for, look at what it does, not what it says") may be paraphrased from *Born a Crime* rather than exact verbatim quotes. These represent the spirit and substance of documented teachings even if specific wording varies.

All factual claims in the expertise.md and PROMPT.md were verified through multiple credible sources. No fabrications or unverifiable content was found.
