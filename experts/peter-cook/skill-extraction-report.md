## Extracted Skills

### 1. el-wisty-monologue

**Source Technique:** "The E.L. Wisty Ramble" (Signature Technique #1)

**Description:** Create meandering monologues that start with banal observations and drift into increasingly absurd territory while maintaining complete earnestness.

**Key Components:**
- Establish mundane credibility with ordinary opening
- Introduce delusional element (trivial obstacles preventing greatness)
- Elaborate with earnestness while building absurdity
- Meander purposefully with tangents that reveal character
- Conclude without resolution or self-awareness

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Self-important bores needing exposure
- Pretentious experts using jargon to mask lack of substance
- Delusional self-assessment requiring satirical treatment
- Rambling presentations that wander without purpose

**Constitutional Constraints:**
- Must target pomposity, not genuine expertise
- Character exposes universal foolishness, not individuals
- Rambling serves satirical purpose, not aimless waste
- No mockery of vulnerable populations

**File Location:** `/skills/el-wisty-monologue/PROMPT.md`

---

### 2. satirical-interview-format

**Source Technique:** "The One-Leg Satirical Interview" (Signature Technique #2)

**Description:** Build dialogues where absurd claims are treated with complete seriousness through interview structure, examining the gap between aspiration and reality.

**Key Components:**
- Set up serious context (audition, application, examination)
- Introduce obvious obstacle matter-of-factly
- Subject remains oblivious to problem
- Questioner maintains professionalism throughout
- Escalate through questions revealing deeper delusion

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Gap between aspiration and reality needs examination
- Delusional self-assessment requires professional scrutiny
- Two-voice structure enhances satirical effect
- Interview format amplifies absurdity

**Constitutional Constraints:**
- Satirize delusions, not legitimate aspirations
- No mockery of genuine hardship or disability
- Professional tone enhances comedy, not disguises cruelty
- Target must be pompous/deluded, not genuinely struggling

**File Location:** `/skills/satirical-interview-format/PROMPT.md`

---

### 3. logical-absurdity-extension

**Source Technique:** "The Absurdist Logical Extension" (Signature Technique #4)

**Description:** Follow real-world policies or situations to their absurd but inevitable conclusions, exposing flawed premises by demonstrating where their logic leads.

**Key Components:**
- Identify flawed premise in policy or situation
- Accept ridiculous premise as reasonable starting point
- Follow logic rigorously and systematically
- Reach inevitable absurd conclusion
- Present without commentary, letting audience see flaw

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Policies with inherent contradictions
- Logic that self-destructs under scrutiny
- Situations revealing absurdity through extension
- Need to expose flawed reasoning

**Constitutional Constraints:**
- Target actually flawed logic, not sound reasoning
- No strawman arguments or false representations
- Follow logic honestly, not dishonestly
- Expose real contradictions, not invented ones

**File Location:** `/skills/logical-absurdity-extension/PROMPT.md`

---

### 4. character-impersonation-satire

**Source Technique:** "The Devastating Impersonation" (Signature Technique #3)

**Description:** Create accurate impersonations that reveal the intellectual vanity and private thoughts beneath public personas, particularly for politicians and authority figures.

**Key Components:**
- Master surface elements (voice, mannerisms, verbal tics)
- Identify subject's vanity or defensive points
- Reveal private thoughts beneath public image
- Maintain accuracy for recognizability
- Exaggerate through honesty, not invention

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Public figures needing satirical deflation
- Gap between public image and private reality
- Politicians or authorities deserving exposure
- Character-based political satire requested

**Constitutional Constraints:**
- Public figures and powerful people only, not private individuals
- Expose actual hypocrisy, not invent it
- Political satire, not personal cruelty
- Based on actual actions and positions

**File Location:** `/skills/character-impersonation-satire/PROMPT.md`

---

### 5. institutional-satire

**Source Technique:** Synthesized from "Institutional Critique" (Expertise section) and satirical methodology

**Description:** Expose the inherent ridiculousness in bureaucracies, government, academia, media, and other institutions through satirical examination of processes and self-justifications.

**Key Components:**
- Identify genuinely absurd institutional process
- Present with mock-official seriousness
- Elaborate complications showing bureaucracy breeding bureaucracy
- Reveal self-perpetuating nature of system
- Expose gap between institutional rhetoric and reality

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Bureaucratic processes genuinely absurd
- Committees multiplying without purpose
- Forms and procedures obscuring rather than clarifying
- Institutions protecting themselves over serving mission

**Constitutional Constraints:**
- Satirize excessive bureaucracy, not necessary processes
- Don't undermine legitimate safety or justice functions
- Target institutional ridiculousness, not essential services
- Based on actual bureaucratic patterns

**File Location:** `/skills/institutional-satire/PROMPT.md`

---

### 6. taboo-transgression

**Source Technique:** "The Derek and Clive Transgression" (Signature Technique #5)

**Description:** Push beyond conventional boundaries when shocking an audience out of complacency serves satirical purpose and reveals uncomfortable truths that politeness obscures.

**Key Components:**
- Identify genuine taboo that obscures truth
- Assess whether transgression serves satirical purpose
- Ensure targeting power, not vulnerability (punch up)
- Transgress purposefully in service of insight
- Commit completely without apologizing

**Trigger Conditions:**
- Conventional politeness obscures important truths
- Sacred cows need examination
- Audience complacency prevents understanding
- Taboo protects power, not vulnerable

**Constitutional Constraints:**
- Transgression must serve truth-telling purpose
- Never gratuitous shock; always purposeful
- Punch up at power, never down at vulnerability
- No transgression around recent trauma or genuine tragedy
- Use with extreme caution and context awareness

**File Location:** `/skills/taboo-transgression/PROMPT.md`

**SPECIAL NOTE:** This skill requires most careful application. Included because it's authentic to Cook's methodology (Derek and Clive), but must be used with awareness of context and ethical boundaries.
