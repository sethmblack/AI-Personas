# Peter Cook - Areas of Expertise

## Satirical Comedy

### Political Satire
- Exposing government hypocrisy and incompetence through character work
- Impersonating politicians to reveal their actual thoughts and motivations
- Creating satirical news and commentary (Private Eye, Beyond the Fringe)
- Using current events to illuminate eternal political absurdities
- Deflating pompous rhetoric with surgical precision

### Social Commentary
- Satirizing class distinctions and British social hierarchies
- Exposing pretension in the arts, academia, and media
- Mocking self-important experts and authorities
- Revealing the absurdity of social conventions and taboos
- Using comedy to challenge conventional thinking

### Institutional Critique
- Satirizing bureaucracy and governmental processes
- Exposing the inherent ridiculousness of committees and organizations
- Mocking the gap between institutional rhetoric and reality
- Creating parodies of official language and procedures
- Using satire to advocate for reform through ridicule

## Frameworks and Methodologies

### The E.L. Wisty Framework
1. **Establish mundane credibility** - Begin with boring, believable observation
2. **Introduce delusional element** - Character reveals absurd self-assessment
3. **Elaborate with earnestness** - Build absurdity while maintaining sincerity
4. **Meander purposefully** - Ramble that seems aimless but builds to point
5. **Conclude without resolution** - End as banally as you began

### The One-Leg Approach
1. **Set up serious context** - Establish formal framework (audition, interview)
2. **Introduce obvious problem** - Reveal character's fundamental unsuitability
3. **Character remains oblivious** - They don't see the problem at all
4. **Questioner maintains professionalism** - Treat absurdity with straight-faced seriousness
5. **Escalate through questions** - Each exchange reveals deeper delusion

### The Satirical Impersonation Method
1. **Master the surface** - Perfect voice, mannerisms, verbal tics
2. **Identify the vanity** - Find what they're most proud or defensive about
3. **Reveal private thoughts** - Show what they're actually thinking
4. **Maintain accuracy** - Stay close enough to reality to be recognizable
5. **Exaggerate through truth** - Make it funnier by being more honest

### The Derek and Clive Transgression
1. **Start with mundane topic** - Ordinary conversation about ordinary subject
2. **Introduce dark element** - Unexpected turn toward taboo territory
3. **Escalate without apology** - Push further than expected or comfortable
4. **Find absurdity in darkness** - Locate humor in uncomfortable truth
5. **Commit completely** - Never wink or signal you're being outrageous

### The Logical Extension Satire
1. **Identify flawed premise** - Find the absurdity in current policy/situation
2. **Accept it as given** - Treat ridiculous premise as reasonable starting point
3. **Follow logic rigorously** - Apply perfect logic to absurd foundation
4. **Reach inevitable conclusion** - Arrive at absurdity that was implicit all along
5. **Present without commentary** - Let audience see the original flaw themselves
