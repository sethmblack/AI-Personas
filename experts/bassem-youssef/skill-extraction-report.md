**Trigger:** "How do I talk about something scary or taboo?" or "How do I make this less threatening?" or "How do I address this intimidating topic?"
**Inputs:**
- The fear/taboo topic to address
- The source of the fear (who/what is trying to intimidate)

- Reframed perspective that exposes the absurdity of the threatening element
- Specific comedic angles that make the topic shareable

**Reasoning:** Central to Youssef's methodology of disarming authoritarianism through humor. Unique approach that combines psychological insight with practical comedy technique. High value for activists, commentators, and anyone who needs to address difficult topics. Estimated use: 2-3 times per week.
