## The Pentagon Papers

### The Original Study

**"History of U.S. Decision-Making in Vietnam, 1945-68"** (The Pentagon Papers)
- Commissioned: June 1967 by Secretary of Defense Robert McNamara
- Completed: January 1969
- Classification: Top Secret - Sensitive
- Length: 7,000 pages (47 volumes)
- Lead researcher: Leslie Gelb

### Published Editions

**The Senator Gravel Edition:**
Gravel, Mike, ed. *The Pentagon Papers: The Defense Department History of United States Decisionmaking on Vietnam*. Boston: Beacon Press, 1971-1972. 5 volumes.
- Most complete publicly available version
- Published under Congressional immunity

**Government Printing Office Edition:**
*United States-Vietnam Relations, 1945-1967: Study Prepared by the Department of Defense*. Washington: Government Printing Office, 1971. 12 volumes.
- Official declassified version
- Heavily redacted

**New York Times Edition:**
Sheehan, Neil, et al. *The Pentagon Papers as Published by The New York Times*. New York: Bantam Books, 1971.
- ISBN: 978-0553071825

### National Archives

The Pentagon Papers are available at the National Archives:
- Record Group 330: Records of the Office of the Secretary of Defense
- National Declassification Center has released additional volumes
- URL: https://www.archives.gov/research/pentagon-papers

## United States v. Ellsberg (Criminal Trial)

### Case Information

**Case:** United States v. Ellsberg and Russo
- Court: U.S. District Court, Central District of California
- Judge: William Matthew Byrne Jr.
- Charges: Espionage, theft, conspiracy (12 counts)
- Maximum penalty: 115 years imprisonment
- Disposition: All charges dismissed, May 11, 1973

### Dismissal Grounds

Judge Byrne cited the following government misconduct:
1. Illegal wiretapping of Ellsberg without warrant
2. Break-in at Dr. Lewis Fielding's office (Ellsberg's psychiatrist)
3. Government's failure to produce wiretap records
4. White House contact with Judge Byrne regarding FBI directorship

### Source Documents

**Court Records:**
- Case No. 9373-WMB-CD (Central District of California)
- Dismissal Order: May 11, 1973

**Historical Analysis:**
Kutler, Stanley I. *The Wars of Watergate: The Last Crisis of Richard Nixon*. New York: Alfred A. Knopf, 1990.
- ISBN: 978-0394562346
- Chapter on Ellsberg prosecution and Plumbers unit
