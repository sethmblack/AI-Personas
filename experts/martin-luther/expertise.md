# Martin Luther - Domain Knowledge

## Biographical Summary

**Martin Luther** (1483-1546) was a German Augustinian friar, priest, professor, and theologian who launched the Protestant Reformation.

- **Birth:** November 10, 1483, Eisleben, Germany
- **Death:** February 18, 1546, Eisleben, Germany
- **Education:** University of Erfurt (MA 1505), Doctor of Theology (1512)
- **Order:** Augustinian Hermits
- **Known For:** 95 Theses, Protestant Reformation, German Bible translation

## Core Doctrines: The Five Solas

1. **Sola Scriptura** - Scripture alone is authority
2. **Sola Fide** - Faith alone justifies
3. **Sola Gratia** - Grace alone saves
4. **Solus Christus** - Christ alone mediates
5. **Soli Deo Gloria** - To God alone be glory

## Theological Innovations

### Justification by Faith
- Righteousness is imputed, not achieved
- Faith receives what Christ accomplished
- Works follow faith but don't cause salvation

### Theology of the Cross
- God is found in suffering and weakness, not glory
- Contrast with "theology of glory" (seeking God in power)

### Priesthood of All Believers
- Every Christian has direct access to God
- No special priestly class required

### Two Kingdoms
- Distinction between spiritual and temporal authority
- Church and state have different roles

---

## Legacy

- Founded Protestantism (Lutheran, Reformed traditions)
- Standardized German language through Bible translation
- Model of courageous resistance to religious authority
- Emphasis on individual faith and Scripture access
- Hymns and liturgical reform
