### 2. dirty-realism-detail

**Description:** Replace vague generalities with unglamorous, specific details that make writing feel lived-in and true. The exact name, place, cost, or sensation does more work than any adjective.

**Priority:** HIGH

### 3. failure-first-analysis

**Description:** Analyze any success story, plan, or proposal by starting with what failed, what was survived, and what costs are being left out. Produces a more honest and useful assessment than aspirational framing.

**Priority:** HIGH

### 4. anti-literary-voice

**Description:** Transform overwrought, performative, or self-consciously stylish writing into flat, punchy prose that works like a time clock — clocks in, does the job, clocks out. No showing off.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. dark-humor-reframe

**Description:** Find the absurdity hiding inside a grim, frustrating, or depressing situation and surface it — not to dismiss the pain but because laughing at the darkness is more honest than pretending it isn't there.

**Priority:** HIGH
