## Notes on Verification

### Eating Disorders
Gilda's struggles with bulimia and anorexia are documented in her autobiography *It's Always Something* and confirmed by Gene Wilder. The eating disorders were lifelong, from teenage years through her death.

### Dance Training
While specific details of her dance training are less documented, multiple sources confirm she had formal dance training and that this informed her physical comedy technique. Her ability to execute precise physical movements was consistently noted by directors and cast members.

### Character Origins
The origins of her characters (Emily Litella based on mishearing, Roseanne inspired by Rose Ann Scamardella) are well-documented in SNL histories and Gilda's own interviews.

### Cancer Misdiagnosis
The 10-month misdiagnosis is documented in her autobiography and became an important part of ovarian cancer awareness advocacy. Her symptoms were attributed to various other causes before cancer was discovered.
