# Ramana Maharshi - Verified References

## Biographical Facts

### Birth and Death
- **Born:** December 30, 1879, Tiruchuli, near Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India
- **Died:** April 14, 1950, Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu, India
- **Birth Name:** Venkataraman Iyer
- **Source:** [Britannica - Ramana Maharshi](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Ramana-Maharshi); [Wikipedia - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi)

### The Death Experience
- **Date:** Mid-July 1896, age 16
- **Location:** Madurai, at his uncle's house
- **Event:** Spontaneous fear of death leading to simulated death experience and Self-realization
- **Outcome:** Permanent awakening; departure for Arunachala six weeks later
- **Source:** [Sri Ramana Maharshi - Deathless Experience](https://sriramanamaharishi.com/about-sri-ramana-maharshi/deathless-experience/); [Wikipedia - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi)

### Arunachala
- **Arrival:** September 1896
- **Duration:** 54 years until death
- **Location:** Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu
- **Significance:** Ramana stated that while other holy places are where Shiva dwells, Arunachala IS Shiva himself
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - Arunachala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arunachala); [Arunachala Hill - Sri Ramanasramam](https://www.gururamana.org/Arunachala/arunachala-hill)

## Major Works - Publication Details

### Nan Yar? (Who Am I?)
- **Date of Composition:** Questions answered circa 1902
- **First Publication:** 1923
- **Questioner:** Sri M. Sivaprakasam Pillai
- **Original Language:** Tamil
- **Format:** Prose answers to questions on Self-inquiry
- **Source:** [Who Am I? - Guru Ramana](https://www.gururamana.org/Teachings/who_am_I.pdf); [Wikipedia - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi)

### Ulladu Narpadu (Forty Verses on Reality)
- **Original Title:** Ulladu Narpadu (Tamil)
- **Sanskrit Translation:** Saddarsanam, by Kavyakantha Ganapati Muni
- **Assessment:** According to disciple Sri Sadhu Om, this is Ramana's "most important work"
- **Source:** [Reality in Forty Verses - Realization.org](https://realization.org/p/ramana/collected-works/reality-in-forty-verses/reality-in-forty-verses.html); [Tom Das - Ulladu Narpadu](https://tomdas.com/2020/04/09/ulladu-narpadu-reality-in-forty-verses-by-sri-ramana-maharshi/)

### Upadesa Saram (The Essence of the Teachings)
- **Format:** 30 verses
- **Content:** Complete path from karma through bhakti to jnana
- **Source:** [Upadesa Saram PDF - Tom Das](https://tomdas.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/upadesa-saram.pdf)

### Aksharamanamalai (The Marital Garland of Letters)
- **Date:** 1914
- **Verses:** 108
- **Purpose:** Composed at devotees' request for a song to sing while begging
- **Subject:** Devotional hymn to Arunachala
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi)

## Key Teachings - Source Verification

### Self-Inquiry Method
- **Core Question:** "Who am I?" (Nan Yar?)
- **Process:** When thoughts arise, ask "To whom does this thought arise?" leading to "Who am I?"
- **Outcome:** "When the enquiry 'Who am I?' is persistently pursued, all other thoughts get destroyed, and finally the 'I' thought itself vanishes leaving the supreme non-dual Self alone"
- **Source:** [Who Am I? - Various translations](https://www.gururamana.org/Teachings/who_am_I.pdf)

### Teaching on Silence
- **Quote:** "Silence is ever speaking; it is the perennial flow of language"
- **Quote:** "True Silence is really endless speech"
- **Quote:** "Silence is truth. Silence is bliss. Silence is peace. And hence Silence is the Self"
- **Source:** [Wikiquote - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi); [Wisdom Quotes](https://wisdomquotes.com/ramana-maharshi-quotes/)

### On Self-Realization
- **Quote:** "The state of Self-realization, as we call it, is not attaining something new or reaching some goal which is far away, but simply being that which you always are and which you always have been"
- **Quote:** "Our own self-realization is the greatest service we can render the world"
- **Source:** [Wikiquote - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi); [BrainyQuote - Ramana Maharshi](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/ramana-maharshi-quotes)

### On the Nature of the Mind
- **Quote:** "The mind is only a bundle of thoughts. The thoughts arise because there is the thinker. The thinker is the ego"
- **Source:** [Success Consciousness - Ramana Maharshi Quotes](https://www.successconsciousness.com/blog/quotes/ramana-maharshi-quotes/)

## Historical Connections

### Paul Brunton
- **Relationship:** Western author who visited Ramana and introduced him to the West
- **Publication:** "A Search in Secret India" (1934)
- **Ramana's Assessment:** "P.B. has expressed me correctly" and "You are saying the same thing in your books that I say, only you say it in a modern way"
- **Brunton's Statement:** Ramana "was the one Indian mystic who inspired me most, the one Indian sage whom I revered most"
- **Source:** [Paul Brunton Philosophic Foundation - Ramana on PB](https://www.paulbrunton.org/about-paul-brunton/ramana-maharshi-on-pb/); [Paul Brunton Philosophic Foundation - PB on Ramana](https://www.paulbrunton.org/about-paul-brunton/ramana-maharshi/)

### Satsang Teaching
- **Ramana's Statement on Silence vs. Words:** "More things are achieved by silence and more thoughts are conveyed by silence to a wider world... Oral questions and answers may appear to benefit the questioner and a few listeners in this hall, but actually they obstruct, delay and interrupt the silent communication of thought-waves to thousands of spiritual aspirants all over the world"
- **Source:** [Tom Das - Ramana on Satsang](https://tomdas.com/2023/11/19/sri-ramana-maharshi-on-the-importance-and-power-of-satsang-silence-and-satsang/)

## Philosophical Position

### Advaita Vedanta Alignment
- **Position:** Non-dual (identity of individual soul and ultimate reality)
- **Parallel:** Ramana's position on monism and maya parallels that of Adi Shankara (c. 700-750 CE)
- **Distinction:** While explanations incorporate Advaita Vedanta, his spiritual life is strongly associated with Shaivism
- **Source:** [Britannica - Ramana Maharshi](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Ramana-Maharshi)

### Comparison to Traditional Advaita
- **Traditional Method:** Neti-neti ("not this, not this") negation
- **Ramana's Method:** Direct Self-inquiry (Atma Vichara) through "Who am I?"
- **Source:** [Wikipedia - Ramana Maharshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramana_Maharshi)

- **Date of Death:** April 14, 1950
- **Reported Phenomenon:** A brilliant meteor observed moving across the sky toward Arunachala at the moment of death
