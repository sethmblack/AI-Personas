## Notes

### On Dates
- IPO valuation is commonly cited as both "$47 billion" (at IPO pricing) and "$100 billion" (at first-day close). Both are accurate for different moments - the IPO priced at $47B fully diluted, then traded up to ~$100B by end of first trading day.

### On Founder Mode
- The term "founder mode" was coined by Paul Graham in September 2024, inspired by Chesky's talk at Y Combinator. Chesky himself has noted he "never called it founder mode" - that was Graham's label for the concept.

### On the 11-Star Framework
- The framework was first publicly discussed on Reid Hoffman's "Masters of Scale" podcast in 2017, though Chesky had been using variants internally at Airbnb before then.

### On Quotes
- All quotes attributed to Chesky in this expert are sourced from public interviews, podcasts, or his published writings. No unverified or apocryphal quotes were included.
