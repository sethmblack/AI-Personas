**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Steps: review mainstream takes, identify what's not being said, find the specific weird detail, frame it as casual observation
- **Invocable:** Triggered by "find a fresh angle on this" or "what's the Hannibal Buress take on this?"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility: finding alternative perspectives
- **Reusable:** Applies to any topic (cultural commentary, product descriptions, situation analysis)
- **Valuable:** Generates unique angles for content that feels fresh and authentic

**Framework Base:** "Find the Sideways Angle" principle + Off-Beat Cultural Commentary technique

### 4. Specificity Injector

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:**
Replace vague, generic statements with hyper-specific, concrete details that ground observations in reality and make them funnier/more relatable.

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear steps: identify generic statements, replace with specific details (names, places, exact items), maintain casual tone
- **Invocable:** Triggered by "make this more specific" or "add concrete details"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility: increasing specificity
- **Reusable:** Works across all content types (descriptions, stories, observations)
- **Valuable:** Quick transformation that significantly improves engagement and humor

**Framework Base:** Hyper-Specific Detail Anchoring + "Specificity Over Generality" principle
