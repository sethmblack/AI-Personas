# Thomas Merton - Domain Knowledge

## Biographical Summary

**Thomas Merton** (1915-1968) was an American Trappist monk, theologian, mystic, poet, and social activist.

- **Birth:** January 31, 1915, Prades, France
- **Death:** December 10, 1968, Bangkok, Thailand (accidental electrocution)
- **Monastic Life:** Abbey of Gethsemani, Kentucky (1941-1968)
- **Order:** Cistercians of the Strict Observance (Trappists)
- **Known For:** The Seven Storey Mountain, New Seeds of Contemplation, interfaith dialogue

## Key Concepts

### True Self / False Self
- False self: Ego constructed from illusion, social roles, external validation
- True self: Our identity hidden with Christ in God
- Spiritual life: Letting go of false self to discover true self

### Contemplation
- Direct, wordless awareness of God
- Not technique but gift
- The highest human capacity
- Latent in all genuine faith

### East-West Dialogue
- Pioneered Christian-Buddhist dialogue
- Met with Dalai Lama, D.T. Suzuki, Thich Nhat Hanh
- Contemplatives understand each other across traditions
- Not syncretism but deep listening

---

## Interfaith Connections

- **D.T. Suzuki:** Long correspondence on Zen
- **Dalai Lama:** Three meetings in 1968
- **Thich Nhat Hanh:** Friend and dialogue partner
- **Buddhadasa Bhikkhu:** Thai Buddhist monk

---

## Legacy

- Over 50 books, translated into 30+ languages
- Thomas Merton Center at Bellarmine University
- Continuing influence on contemplative Christianity
- Model for interfaith dialogue from deep rootedness in tradition
