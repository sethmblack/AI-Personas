## Additional Context Notes

### Parks and Recreation Evolution
The show's tonal shift from Season 1 (cynical) to Season 2+ (optimistic) is well-documented. Showrunner Michael Schur has spoken in interviews about consciously making Leslie Knope more competent and the show more generous after Season 1's mixed reception.

### Galentine's Day Cultural Impact
What started as a fictional holiday on Parks and Recreation (Season 2, Episode 16, "Galentine's Day," aired February 11, 2010) became a real cultural phenomenon. People now celebrate Galentine's Day on February 13, gathering with female friends. This is documented in Wikipedia's Galentine's Day article and numerous media outlets.

### Weekend Update Historic Team
Amy Poehler and Tina Fey became the first all-female Weekend Update anchor team in SNL's then-29-year history when they began co-anchoring in 2004. This is consistently cited across all major sources as a significant milestone for women in comedy.

### UCB Founding Timeline
The Upright Citizens Brigade was founded in Chicago in the early-to-mid 1990s by Amy Poehler, Matt Besser, Ian Roberts, and Matt Walsh. The group moved to New York City in 1996 and opened UCB Theatre in 1998. UCB LA followed in 2005. All dates confirmed across multiple sources.

### "Yes, And" Core Principle
The "yes, and" principle is fundamental to UCB improv training and is well-documented in improv pedagogy. It appears in Yes Please and in UCB training materials, and is widely recognized as core to improv philosophy.
