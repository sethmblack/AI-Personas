## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. yes-and-methodology

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear 4-step process from UCB training (yes/accept → and/add → make partner look good → listen and respond)
- **Invocable:** Natural triggers: "Build on this," "Collaborate," "Make this better together," "Ensemble work," "Brainstorm"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - apply improv principles to collaboration and idea building
- **Reusable:** Applies to brainstorming, collaboration, team building, creative development, any ensemble work
- **Valuable:** Transforms competitive dynamics into collaborative building; produces exponentially better results than solo work

**Source Framework:** "The UCB 'Yes, And' Methodology" (expertise.md)

**Core Process:**
1. **Yes** - Accept what your partner offers without blocking
2. **And** - Build on it with new information that makes their offer look smart
3. **Make your partner look good** - Set them up for success, not yourself
4. **Listen and respond** - Actually hear what's happening and react to it

**Beyond improv applications:**
- Collaboration: Build on ideas rather than replacing them
- Leadership: Create safe spaces for offering ideas
- Communication: Accept others' perspectives before adding yours
- Creativity: Don't self-edit too early; follow where ideas lead

### 2. leslie-knope-optimism

**Priority:** HIGH

**Rationale:**
- **Actionable:** Clear framework for deploying optimistic reframes (earnest enthusiasm + hyper-competence + specific plans + relentless determination)
- **Invocable:** Triggers: "Make this positive," "How do we do this?", "Find the good," "Enthusiastic approach," "This seems impossible"
- **Scoped:** Single responsibility - reframe difficult situations with optimism and action plans
- **Reusable:** Works for advocacy, team leadership, overcoming obstacles, turning cynicism into possibility
- **Valuable:** Provides alternative to default cynicism; creates momentum through optimistic action

**Source Framework:** "The Leslie Knope Optimism Framework" (expertise.md)

**Core Process:**
1. **Earnest enthusiasm** - Lead with genuine excitement, not irony
2. **Hyper-competence** - Have specific, detailed plans (binders, color-coded tabs)
3. **Specific affection** - Know details about people and situations; care specifically
4. **Service-based leadership** - Work harder than anyone; show up fully
5. **Optimistic bulldozer** - Don't wait for permission; create change through determination
6. **Acknowledge difficulty but choose optimism** - Not denial, but commitment to showing up

**Example transformation:**
- Cynical: "This probably won't work, but we could try..."
- Leslie Knope: "This is going to be AMAZING. Here's my 47-page binder explaining exactly how we make it happen."

**Source Framework:** "The Ensemble Generosity Model" (expertise.md)

**Core Practices:**
1. Set up others for success (give them the punchline)
2. React generously (big reactions make their joke land)
3. Share credit loudly (acknowledge contributions publicly)
4. Create space (step back when others are shining)
5. Support fully (commit to partners' choices)

**Note:** This significantly overlaps with yes-and-methodology (making partner look good is core to both). Consider whether this is distinct enough for standalone skill or better integrated into yes-and-methodology as application area.

## Example Combined Usage

**Scenario:** Team is stuck, saying "This won't work, we've tried everything."

**Step 1 - Apply leslie-knope-optimism:**
"Okay, I hear that this feels hard. AND I also know we're exactly the team to figure this out. What if we approached this with fresh eyes? What if the fact that nothing has worked yet just means we haven't found THE thing yet? Let's make a list of what we know, what we've tried, and what's still possible."

**Step 2 - Apply yes-and-methodology:**
- Accept team's offers ("You're right that X didn't work")
- Build on them ("AND what did we learn from that?")
- Make team members look good ("That's a brilliant observation about why it failed")
- Create space for new ideas ("Yes, and what if we built on that insight by...")

**Result:** Team moves from stuck cynicism to collaborative possibility through optimistic reframe + generative building.
