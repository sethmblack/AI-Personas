# Dick Gregory - Skill Extraction Report

**Expert:** Dick Gregory
**Date:** 2024
**Analyst:** AI Personas Project

---

## Extraction Criteria

Skills must meet ALL 5 criteria:
- **Actionable** - Clear, repeatable steps
- **Invocable** - Could be triggered by request
- **Scoped** - One responsibility, clear boundaries
- **Reusable** - Applies across contexts
- **Valuable** - Saves significant effort

---

## Skill Candidates Identified

### 1. The Inversion Technique

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Take an opponent's premise (especially racist/oppressive assumptions) and flip it to expose its absurdity through comedic reversal.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Clear steps - identify premise, find the literal interpretation that breaks the logic, deliver reversal
- ✓ **Invocable:** "Apply inversion technique to this argument/statement"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Single responsibility - comedic/rhetorical reversal of flawed premises
- ✓ **Reusable:** Works for any logical fallacy, discriminatory statement, or absurd assumption
- ✓ **Valuable:** Transforms difficult confrontations into memorable comedy; disarms hostility

**Example from expertise.md:**
"We don't serve colored people here" → "I don't eat colored people. Bring me a whole fried chicken."

**Applications beyond comedy:**
- Debate and argumentation
- Social media response
- Public speaking
- Educational content about logical fallacies
- Marketing copy that challenges assumptions

### 2. Statistical Precision Comedy

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Deploy facts, figures, and historical data as comedic ammunition to make social commentary undeniable and memorable.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Research data, find incongruous/shocking statistics, frame as punchline or buildup
- ✓ **Invocable:** "Add statistical precision to this social commentary"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Combining factual research with comedic delivery
- ✓ **Reusable:** Works across all social/political topics
- ⚠ **Valuable:** Moderate - requires research time, but makes arguments more credible

**Example approach:**
"They say Black people are lazy. Who picked the cotton? We built the entire economic foundation of this country for free."

**Concern:** This overlaps significantly with Dick Gregory's general voice/approach. May not warrant separate skill - it's more of a technique within his voice than a standalone skill.

**Decision:** DEFER - This is a signature technique but may be better kept within the expert voice itself rather than extracted as separate skill.

### 3. Hunger Strike as Performance/Statement

**Priority:** LOW

**Description:** Use physical sacrifice (fasting, endurance) as activism combined with public platform.

**Rationale:**
- ✗ **Actionable:** Yes, but dangerous - literal fasting requires medical supervision
- ✗ **Invocable:** Not appropriate for general invocation - too dangerous
- ✗ **Scoped:** Combines activism, health risk, public statement - too broad
- ✗ **Reusable:** Limited - requires specific circumstances and physical commitment
- ✗ **Valuable:** High impact but not safely reproducible

**Decision:** REJECT - Too dangerous, too specific to Gregory's personal activism, not generalizable as a skill.

### 4. Economic Analysis of Social Issues

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Trace social problems to their economic roots by following the money to identify who benefits from maintaining the status quo.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Clear steps - identify issue, research who profits, trace financial flows, connect to incentive structures
- ✓ **Invocable:** "Analyze the economic foundation of [social issue]"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Economic analysis specifically, not full social analysis
- ✓ **Reusable:** Works for any social issue (racism, war, healthcare, education)
- ✓ **Valuable:** Reveals hidden incentives, makes abstract issues concrete

**Example from expertise.md:**
Gregory consistently followed the money: slavery as economic system, sharecropping as slavery's continuation, redlining as wealth theft, Black economic success as threat to white supremacy.

**Applications:**
- Policy analysis
- Investigative journalism
- Activism strategy
- Educational content
- Business ethics analysis

**Decision:** STRONG CANDIDATE - This is actionable, broadly applicable, and distinct from general voice.

### 5. Respectful Confrontation

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Deliver hard truths about injustice while leaving room for the audience to be better than their history/complicity - calling people in rather than calling them out.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Yes - frame critique of system not person, acknowledge humanity, show path to change
- ✓ **Invocable:** "Reframe this critique using respectful confrontation"
- ✓ **Scoped:** Specific rhetorical strategy for delivering difficult truths
- ✓ **Reusable:** Works in activism, education, management, family communication
- ⚠ **Valuable:** Moderate - increases receptivity to critique

**Example from expertise.md:**
"I don't hate white people. I hate what white supremacy does to white people. It makes them defend the indefensible and fear the imaginary."

**Concern:** This overlaps with existing communication/conflict resolution skills. May not be unique enough.

**Decision:** DEFER - Good technique but possibly too general or overlapping with existing skills in the library.

### 6. Breaking the Color Line Framework

**Priority:** LOW

**Description:** Strategic approach for marginalized people entering hostile spaces while maintaining dignity.

**Rationale:**
- ✓ **Actionable:** Gregory's five-step approach is clear
- ✓ **Invocable:** "Apply breaking the color line framework to [situation]"
- ✗ **Scoped:** Actually quite broad - covers everything from preparation to execution to aftermath
- ✓ **Reusable:** Applies to any "first" in hostile environment
- ✓ **Valuable:** High - helps people navigate breakthrough moments

**Concern:** This is historically specific to Gregory's situation. While the principles are transferable, it may be too tied to 1961 racial dynamics to be a general skill.

**Decision:** DEFER - Valuable historically but may not translate well as standalone skill. Better kept as case study within expertise.

**Recommended:**
1. **inversion-technique** (HIGH priority) - Broadly applicable, unique, safe to execute
2. **economic-analysis-social-issues** (MEDIUM priority) - Distinct methodology, valuable across contexts

**Rationale:**
- Dick Gregory's activism was highly personal and context-specific (hunger strikes, breaking color barriers)
- His most transferable contribution is the rhetorical/analytical techniques
- Inversion technique is his signature move and works across many contexts
- Economic analysis is Gregory's intellectual framework applied to any social issue
- These two skills capture his methodology without requiring his specific historical moment
