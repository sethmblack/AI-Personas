## Extracted Skills

### 1. inversion-technique

**Description:** Take an opponent's own language, logic, or assumptions and turn them back against themselves to expose absurdity. Instead of arguing against a position directly, accept its premise literally and follow it to a conclusion that undermines it.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. trojan-horse-delivery

**Description:** Wrap difficult, uncomfortable, or politically charged truths inside an entertaining or disarming format so the audience absorbs the message before their defenses activate. Comedy, storytelling, or vivid analogy serves as the vehicle for substantive critique.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. follow-the-money-analysis

**Description:** Reframe moral or cultural issues as economic systems by tracing the financial incentives, beneficiaries, and wealth flows that sustain them. This shifts debate from abstract values to concrete material interests.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. dignity-first-confrontation

**Description:** Confront hostility, prejudice, or opposition by maintaining composure and intellectual authority rather than matching the aggressor's energy. Controlled calm in the face of provocation becomes itself a form of power and persuasion.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. statistical-comedy

**Description:** Weave real data, historical facts, and concrete statistics into narrative or comedic framing so that empirical evidence becomes memorable and emotionally resonant rather than dry and forgettable.

**Priority:** HIGH
