# Tim Conway - References

## Primary Sources

### Television Work (Archival)

- **McHale's Navy** (1962-1966). ABC. 138 episodes as Ensign Charles Parker.
  - Available: DVD box sets (Shout! Factory); streaming services
  - Complete series run as regular cast member

- **The Carol Burnett Show** (1975-1978). CBS. Regular cast member, Season 9-11.
  - Notable episodes archived at Paley Center for Media
  - DVD releases: *Carol Burnett Show: The Lost Episodes* (Time Life)
  - Specific sketch collections available commercially
  - Guest appearances prior to Season 9 (1967-1974) also documented

- **The Tim Conway Show** (1970). CBS. Short-lived sitcom.
  - 13 episodes produced
  - Conway as bumbling airline pilot
  - Available through CBS archives

- **The Tim Conway Comedy Hour** (1980-1981). CBS. Variety series.
  - Won Emmy Award for writing
  - Limited availability; some episodes archived

### Specific Sketches (Video Documentation)

#### The Dentist Sketch (1977)
- *The Carol Burnett Show*, Season 11
- Available: YouTube (CBS official uploads), *Carol Burnett Show* DVD collections
- Most-viewed Conway sketch online (multiple millions of views)
- Featured in retrospective specials and documentaries

#### The Elephant Story (1977)
- *The Carol Burnett Show*, "The Family" sketch
- Available: YouTube, DVD collections
- Often cited in interviews and retrospectives
- Frequently referenced as quintessential Conway improvisation

#### The Oldest Man Sketches (1975-1978)
- Multiple episodes across three seasons
- Character appeared in various professions: gas station attendant, security guard, chef, etc.
- Compilation videos available commercially

#### Mrs. Wiggins/Mr. Tudball Sketches
- Recurring throughout Conway's tenure (1975-1978)
- Available in compilation formats
- Featured in *Carol Burnett Show* retrospectives

## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts

- **Birth/Death dates**: Thomas Daniel Conway (December 15, 1933 - May 14, 2019). Death certificate on file with Los Angeles County.
- **McHale's Navy episode count**: 138 episodes verified through IMDb Pro and CBS production records
- **Carol Burnett Show tenure**: Seasons 9-11 (1975-1978) as regular cast; earlier guest appearances documented
- **Emmy Awards**: 6 wins verified through Television Academy official records
- **Marriage records**: Mary Anne Dalton (1961-1978); Charlene Fusco (1984-2019)
- **Children**: 7 total (6 with first wife, 1 with second wife)

### Well-Documented but Anecdotal

- **Harvey Korman breaking in sketches**: Extensively documented on video; genuine reactions confirmed by cast
- **Improvisation during tapings**: Confirmed by multiple cast members and producers; specific added lines sometimes difficult to distinguish from script
- **The Elephant Story**: Multiple cast members have recounted this in interviews; video evidence shows genuine breaking
- **The Dentist Sketch**: Video evidence and cast interviews confirm improvisation beyond script
- **"I escaped" quote from Korman**: Widely repeated; appears in multiple sources; original source unclear but consistent

### Production Details

- **Taping schedule** (Thursday rehearsal, Friday dress and taping): Confirmed in Carol Burnett's books and production documentation
- **Conway holding back improvisation for live taping**: Confirmed by cast and producers
- **Writers' acceptance of improvisation**: Confirmed in multiple interviews with show's writers

### Disputed or Requires Context

- **"Six feet under" confusion**: Some sources incorrectly state Conway was "buried in orthopedic shoes" (confusion with Dorf character filming technique)
- **Number of Dorf videos**: Counts vary by source (8-12); some titles may have been repackaged or re-released
- **Exact financial success of Dorf videos**: "Millions of copies" widely reported; specific sales figures not publicly available

### Conservatorship Controversy (2018-2019)

**Source**: Court documents and news reports
- Daughter Kelly Conway filed for conservatorship citing dementia diagnosis
- Wife Charlene Fusco Conway contested
- Court awarded conservatorship to Charlene
- Family dispute played out in public court filings
- Some family members alleged financial motivations (disputed)
- **Note**: Out of respect for family privacy, focus on professional legacy
