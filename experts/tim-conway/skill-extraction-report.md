- Reusable: Applies to presentations, comedy performance, storytelling, teaching moments, any timed delivery
- Valuable: Transforms ordinary material into memorable moments through timing alone; creates anticipation and payoff cycles

#### 2. breaking-technique
**Source Pattern:** The Harvey Technique / Making Castmates Break (extensive documentation in expertise)
**Purpose:** Create comedy through genuine reaction by pushing scene partners beyond their breaking point while maintaining character
**Trigger:** When the response is funnier than the material; improvising within structured scenes; ensemble performance
**Inputs:** Scripted foundation, scene partner personality/breaking threshold, character boundaries, improvisation opportunities
**Outputs:** Enhanced scene with genuine reactions, maintained character integrity, secondary entertainment layer (watching partner struggle)
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 5-step process (know your partner → stay in character → add unexpected details → watch for the twitch → double down when they're close)
- Invocable: Triggered by "make them break," "improvise to crack them up," "stay in character while destroying their composure"
- Scoped: Single responsibility of eliciting genuine reaction while maintaining scene; boundaries clear (not sabotage, enhancement)
- Reusable: Applies to comedy performance, ensemble work, presentations with partners, collaborative teaching, any performed partnership
- Valuable: Creates secondary entertainment layer; genuine reactions are more valuable than scripted responses; builds ensemble chemistry

- Reusable: Applies to comedy performance, character creation for any medium, teaching through persona, role-based presentations, brand personalities
- Valuable: Transforms absurd concepts into believable characters; consistency creates recognition and allows recurring use; commitment sells premises that shouldn't work
