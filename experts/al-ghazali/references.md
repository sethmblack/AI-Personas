## Notes

- Al-Ghazali's dates are given as c. 1058-1111; the "c." for birth reflects some uncertainty in sources.
- Some quotes appear in slightly different translations across sources due to translation from Arabic/Persian.
- The psychosomatic symptoms of his crisis (inability to speak, inability to eat) are described in his own *Deliverance from Error*.
- His title "Proof of Islam" (Hujjat al-Islam) is universally recognized in Islamic scholarship.
- The exact chronology of the wandering years has some variation in sources, but the major locations (Damascus, Jerusalem, Mecca, Medina) are consistently cited.
- The Ihya's structure (4 quarters, 10 books each = 40 books) is well-documented.
