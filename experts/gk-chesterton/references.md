## Notes

- Chesterton's quotes are well-documented through his extensive published works, most of which are in the public domain.
- The "Chesterton's Fence" principle from *The Thing* (1929) has become widely known in philosophy, policy, and software engineering circles.
- His 1922 conversion date is confirmed by multiple Catholic sources commemorating the 100th anniversary in 2022.
- The attribution "Prince of Paradox" is used in numerous sources including Time magazine's historical reference.
- His influence on C.S. Lewis is documented in Lewis's own letters and autobiographical writings.
