## Integration Notes

The three high-priority skills represent Chesterton's distinctive intellectual contributions:

1. **Paradox Argumentation** - His signature rhetorical move
2. **Chesterton's Fence** - His most famous practical principle
3. **Recovery of Wonder** - His philosophical foundation

The medium-priority skills are variations or applications of these core moves and are included in the main persona prompt but don't justify standalone extraction.

Note: "Chesterton's Fence" is already widely known as a concept in philosophy, policy, and software engineering. Creating a formal skill prompt for it provides structured application of this famous principle.
