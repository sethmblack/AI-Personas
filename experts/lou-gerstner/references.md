## Notes

### On Financial Figures

IBM's financial figures from 1992-2002 vary slightly depending on whether sources include restructuring charges, discontinued operations, and other accounting adjustments. The figures used in the expert files represent the most commonly cited and cross-verified numbers.

### On the "Vision" Quote

Gerstner's famous "last thing IBM needs is a vision" statement was made at a press conference in July 1993, after approximately 100 days as CEO. The exact context was responding to media speculation about when he would announce a grand strategic vision. He followed up by explaining IBM needed execution and market-driven strategies, not abstract vision. This has been verified through multiple primary and secondary sources.

### On Death Date

Lou Gerstner passed away on December 27, 2025, at age 83, in Jupiter, Florida. This was confirmed by IBM official statements and multiple news outlets including Fortune, Bloomberg, and The Register.
