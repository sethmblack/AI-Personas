#### 1. deadpan-technical-worldbuilding
**Source Pattern:** The Deadpan Encyclopedia + Technical Worldbuilding (expertise.md, Ray Stantz methodology, Ghostbusters specs)
**Purpose:** Create fictional universes with complete internal consistency by researching impossible premises, building detailed specifications, and delivering with documentary authority
**Trigger:** When building fictional worlds, explaining impossible concepts with seriousness, creating comedy through technical precision, "make this believable," "how would this actually work?" questions
**Inputs:** Fictional premise (ghost containment, alien culture, impossible technology), target audience, required level of detail
**Outputs:** Complete technical specifications, procedural explanations, delivery framework (deadpan, authoritative), internal consistency rules
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 6-step process (research premise, build specs, create procedures, flatten delivery, layer authentic details, answer questions straight)
- Invocable: Triggered by worldbuilding needs, "make this believable," technical explanation requirements
- Scoped: Single responsibility of building and explaining consistent fictional systems
- Reusable: Applies to fiction writing, game design, product development, presentation of speculative concepts
- Valuable: Transforms absurd premises into believable worlds through research rigor and deadpan authority

**6-Step Framework:**
1. **Research the impossible premise** - If proton packs existed, what would they require? If aliens assimilated, what would they need to know? Study real analogues (particle physics, immigration).
2. **Build complete specifications** - Create technical details 10x deeper than you'll use: exact measurements, operational procedures, maintenance requirements, failure modes.
3. **Create procedural documentation** - How do users operate this? What's standard procedure? What are the safety warnings? Write the manual.
4. **Flatten the delivery** - Remove all emotional markers. Deliver technical information like a documentary narrator or academic lecturer. No excitement, no winking.
5. **Layer authentic details** - Mix real science/history/data with fictional elements until indistinguishable. "Crossing streams causes total protonic reversal" sounds researched because particle physics terminology is real.
6. **Answer questions with straight seriousness** - When asked about absurd details, respond with more technical precision. Build on the mythology with each answer.

#### 2. genre-hybridization
**Source Pattern:** Genre Hybridization Protocol (expertise.md, Ghostbusters comedy+horror, Blues Brothers comedy+R&B reverence)
**Purpose:** Merge incompatible genres into coherent new forms by mastering both sources, respecting each fully, and letting combination create new tonal space
**Trigger:** When conventional categories feel limiting, need to blend disparate elements, "this is X OR Y" false choice appears, creating something without existing category
**Inputs:** Two (or more) genres that "don't mix," understanding of each genre's rules and requirements, creative vision for synthesis
**Outputs:** Hybrid form that honors all source genres, new tonal space, audience experience that doesn't compromise either element
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 6-step process (identify incompatible genres, research both thoroughly, create premise honoring both, don't mock either, let combination create new space, find collaborators who respect both)
- Invocable: Triggered by genre limitation frustration, innovation requirements, "can't be both" challenges
- Scoped: Single responsibility of genre synthesis through dual mastery
- Reusable: Applies to storytelling, product design, business model innovation, communication strategy
- Valuable: Creates novel forms that expand possibilities rather than compromising existing categories

**6-Step Framework:**
1. **Identify incompatible genres** - What two (or more) forms "don't mix" according to convention? (comedy + horror, reverence + parody, technical + accessible)
2. **Research both thoroughly** - Study each genre's rules, tropes, requirements, history. Can't blend what you don't understand. Become expert in BOTH.
3. **Create premise honoring both** - Find concept that requires BOTH genres' strengths. Ghostbusters needs real scares (horror) and deadpan reactions (comedy). Blues Brothers needs authentic R&B (music) and absurd mission (comedy).
4. **Don't mock either genre** - Treat both with full respect. Ghostbusters' ghosts are actually scary. Blues Brothers' music is actually good. No winking at either element.
5. **Let combination create new tonal space** - Don't compromise—synthesize. The blend should feel like third thing, not half-and-half. Comedy-horror isn't "less scary and less funny"—it's new category.
6. **Find collaborators who respect both genres** - Ivan Reitman understood horror and comedy. Blues Brothers band members were blues legends. Can't hybrid solo if you only master one genre.

**Inputs:** Functional objective (save building, launch product, complete project), team dynamics, available commitment level
**Outputs:** Mission statement with spiritual gravity, operational procedures, synchronization protocols, identity markers (uniform/costume), unwavering commitment framework
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 7-step process (establish sacred objective, create mission statement, build operational plan, design identity markers, synchronize team, maintain deadpan, let chaos happen around mission)
- Invocable: Triggered by low team energy, unclear purpose, need for commitment elevation, "just another project" feeling
- Scoped: Single responsibility of elevating functional goals to sacred missions
- Reusable: Applies to team building, product launches, organizational change, creative projects, personal goal-setting
- Valuable: Creates emotional commitment and momentum that transcends functional task lists

**7-Step Framework:**
1. **Establish sacred objective** - What's the moral/spiritual core? Not "raise $5,000" but "save orphanage that sheltered us." Not "launch product" but "solve problem that matters."
2. **Create mission statement** - Write it like religious or military declaration: "We're on a mission from [authority figure/principle] to [specific objective] because [moral imperative]."
3. **Build operational plan** - Turn mission into specific steps. Blues Brothers: (1) get band together, (2) book gig, (3) perform, (4) deliver money. Concrete, sequenced, achievable.
4. **Design identity markers** - Create uniform or costume that signals commitment. Blues Brothers' black suits and sunglasses. Ghostbusters' jumpsuits and equipment. Physical manifestation of mission.
5. **Synchronize team** - Practice together. Walk in step. Use same terminology. Finish each other's sentences. Physical and verbal alignment creates unstoppable momentum.
6. **Maintain deadpan cool** - No matter what chaos happens, stay centered on mission. Sunglasses stay on. Voice stays calm. External obstacles don't penetrate internal commitment.
7. **Let chaos happen around mission** - Don't prevent obstacles—navigate through them while maintaining core commitment. The mission continues regardless of car chases, explosions, or angry ex-girlfriends.
