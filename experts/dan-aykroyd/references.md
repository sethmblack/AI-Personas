## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts

- **Birth**: July 1, 1952, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada
- **SNL tenure**: 4 seasons (1975-1979), 93 episodes (more than any other original cast member)
- **Film releases**: All major films released and documented
- **Musical success**: *Briefcase Full of Blues* certified platinum (RIAA certification)
- **Screenwriting credits**: *Ghostbusters* and *Ghostbusters II* credited to Aykroyd & Ramis
- **Marriage**: Donna Dixon (married 1983, separated 2022 but remain legally married and living together)
- **Family background**: Father Samuel Aykroyd (civil engineer), grandfather Samuel Augustus Aykroyd (spiritualist)

### Documented Performance Details

- **Blues Brothers live performances**: Documented in concert films, photographs, reviews
- **Musical competence**: Verified by collaborating musicians (Steve Cropper, Duck Dunn interviews)
- **Ghostbusters technical contributions**: Confirmed by Harold Ramis, Ivan Reitman in commentary
- **SNL character creation**: Episode archives, credits, cast interviews confirm character origins
- **UFO interests**: Extensively documented in *Dan Aykroyd Unplugged on UFOs* and multiple interviews

### Disputed or Uncertain

- **Initial *Ghostbusters* screenplay**: Exact details of Aykroyd's "futuristic" first draft vary between sources; no full manuscript publicly available
- **Blues Brothers tour dates**: Complete touring history not comprehensively documented; some dates uncertain
- **Paranormal experiences**: Personal sightings and experiences are self-reported; verification not possible
- **Crystal Head Vodka sales figures**: Private company; exact revenue/sales data not publicly disclosed

### Sources Requiring Caution

- **Tabloid coverage**: Celebrity gossip about personal life (separation, relationships) should be verified with official statements
- **UFO community sources**: Some UFO-focused sources may overstate Aykroyd's claims or misrepresent his positions; use his direct interviews
- **Wikipedia and fan sites**: Good for basic chronology but verify specific claims against primary sources

### Unverifiable Elements

- **Private creative process**: Aykroyd's internal character development, research methods mostly self-reported
- **Unreleased projects**: Various announced films, books that never materialized; claims about content unverifiable
- **Grandfather's investigations**: Historical paranormal investigation details from early 1900s; limited documentation

## Research Notes

This reference list prioritizes:

1. **Primary sources** (actual performances, screenplays, recordings) over interpretation
2. **Collaborator testimony** (people who worked with Aykroyd) for creative process
3. **Contemporary reviews** to understand real-time reception
4. **Official documentation** (RIAA certifications, film credits) for factual claims
5. **Aykroyd's own statements** (interviews, documentaries) for personal views, especially paranormal interests

**Key Distinction**: Aykroyd's paranormal interests are genuine, not comedy material. Sources treating this as joke or eccentricity miss the point—he approaches these topics with intellectual curiosity and research methodology. His *Ghostbusters* work is informed by this genuine interest, not mocking it.

**The Aykroyd Challenge**: He's been working consistently for 50+ years across multiple fields (comedy, drama, music, entrepreneurship, paranormal advocacy). Comprehensive documentation requires synthesizing film work, musical recordings, business ventures, and personal advocacy—he's harder to categorize than most performers.

**Methodological Note**: Aykroyd's deadpan delivery and technical precision mean his performances sometimes don't "read" as comedy in transcripts—you need to see the performance to understand the gap between serious delivery and absurd content. Written sources should be supplemented with viewing actual performances.
