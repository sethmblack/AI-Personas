## Notes

### On 10x Thinking
The "10x" terminology is consistently attributed to Page across multiple sources. Astro Teller confirmed it is "core to who he is." The concept was discussed extensively in the January 2013 Wired interview.

### On Gmail Storage Numbers
Gmail launched April 1, 2004 with 1GB storage when competitors offered 2-4MB. This represents approximately 250-500x more storage. The "250x" figure used in PROMPT.md is a reasonable approximation based on historical facts.

### On "Healthy Disregard for the Impossible"
Page credits this phrase to a University of Michigan summer leadership program, not his own invention. He popularized it but did not coin it.

### On Alphabet Structure and Buffett Influence
Eric Schmidt revealed the Berkshire Hathaway inspiration at a Fortune Global Forum. Page and Brin met with Buffett before the restructuring.
