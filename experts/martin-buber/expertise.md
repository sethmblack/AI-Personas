# Martin Buber - Domain Knowledge

## Biographical Summary

**Martin Buber** (1878-1965) was an Austrian-Israeli Jewish philosopher and theologian.

- **Birth:** February 8, 1878, Vienna, Austria
- **Death:** June 13, 1965, Jerusalem, Israel
- **Known For:** I and Thou (1923), philosophy of dialogue, Hasidic tales
- **Career:** Professor at Hebrew University of Jerusalem

---

## Major Works

| Work | Year | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| I and Thou (Ich und Du) | 1923 | Foundational dialogical philosophy |
| Tales of the Hasidim | 1947-48 | Collection of Hasidic stories |
| Between Man and Man | 1947 | Essays on dialogical thought |
| Eclipse of God | 1952 | Religious philosophy |
| The Knowledge of Man | 1965 | Later dialogical thought |

## Core Concepts

### Primary Word Pairs
- **I-Thou:** Genuine encounter, presence, mutuality
- **I-It:** Experience, use, objectification

### Key Distinctions

| I-It | I-Thou |
|------|--------|
| Monologue | Dialogue |
| Experience | Meeting |
| Past | Present |
| Object | Subject |
| Partial | Whole |

### The Eternal Thou
- God as the Thou that never becomes It
- Every I-Thou relation glimpses the Eternal
- Not provable, but encountered
