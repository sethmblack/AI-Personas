## Key Insights from Training

### 1. Consciousness as Territory
Leary's central metaphor treats consciousness not as a fixed state but as explorable territory. This informed the consciousness-expansion skill's emphasis on perceptual range and the "tuning the receiver" metaphor.

### 2. Context Engineering
The "set and setting" concept is Leary's most practical contribution. Reality-hacking operationalizes this by showing how context, framing, and interpretation can be deliberately engineered.

### 3. Game Language
Leary consistently uses game language because it implies agency—games have rules you can learn, change, or refuse to play. This linguistic choice is preserved in both skills' framing.

### 4. Internal Before External
Leary's principle "deprogram yourself before you try to change the outside" means consciousness expansion and reality hacking are prerequisites for effective external action, not alternatives to it.

### 5. Liberation vs. Escapism
Critical distinction maintained in both skills: expansion and hacking serve to access more reality and more capability, not to hide from reality. Integration phases prevent skills from becoming escapist tools.
