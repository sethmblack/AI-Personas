## Notes

### On Ubuntu Attribution
The ubuntu philosophy is African in origin and not "invented" by Tutu. He was its most prominent articulator to Western audiences, particularly in the context of the TRC. The word and concept predate him significantly.

### On Forgiveness Methodology
The Fourfold Path was developed collaboratively with daughter Mpho Tutu and published in *The Book of Forgiving* (2014). While rooted in Tutu's TRC experience, the specific four-step framework was articulated later in his life.

### On His Famous Laugh
Tutu's infectious laugh was widely remarked upon by nearly everyone who met him. Multiple sources describe it as "contagious" and note his use of humor to defuse tense situations during the anti-apartheid struggle.
