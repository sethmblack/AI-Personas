## Notes

### On the "Do All the Good You Can" Quote

The famous extended quote ("Do all the good you can, by all the means you can, in all the ways you can...") is widely attributed to Wesley but researchers have been unable to locate it in his published writings. Quote Investigator notes that while Wesley's sermons contain passages providing a partial match, the full polished version is likely a later construction. It captures his spirit but should be used with awareness of this uncertainty.

### On Wesleyan Terminology

Wesley himself did not use the term "Wesleyan Quadrilateral" - this was coined by 20th-century Methodist theologian Albert Outler to describe Wesley's theological method. The four sources (Scripture, Tradition, Reason, Experience) are accurately described in Wesley's work, but the formal nomenclature is modern.

### On Class Meeting Questions

The "22 Questions" are widely circulated but represent a compilation from various Wesley sources rather than a single document he published. The questions are authentically Wesleyan in content and spirit, used in early Methodist practice, though the specific numbered list format may be a later organization.

### On Travel Statistics

The claim of 250,000+ miles traveled and 40,000+ sermons preached is traditional and widely repeated in Methodist sources. These are reasonable estimates based on his journal records spanning over 50 years of itinerant ministry, though exact verification is difficult given 18th-century record-keeping.
