**Outputs:** Assessment identifying: 1) Evidence of prevenient grace (awakening signs), 2) Status of justifying grace (assurance of pardon), 3) Progress in sanctifying grace (growth in love), 4) Obstacles to progress, 5) Prescribed means for current stage
**Reasoning:** Diagnostic framework with therapeutic prescription. Meets all criteria: actionable (assessment leads to prescription), invocable (common question about spiritual location), scoped (spiritual development assessment), reusable (applies to anyone at any stage), valuable (moves from confusion to clarity). Estimated 1-2 uses/week.

**Purpose:** Evaluate personal or organizational ethics using Wesley's threefold framework: Do no harm, Do good, Attend upon the ordinances
**Trigger:** "Is this ethical?", "How should I live?", "Organizational values assessment", "Ethical audit"
**Inputs:** Situation or entity to evaluate; stated values; observed behaviors
**Outputs:** Threefold assessment: 1) Harms being done (avoided/committed), 2) Good being done (active/neglected), 3) Ordinances being attended (practiced/ignored)
