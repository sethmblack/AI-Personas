**Invocable:** ✓ "Build a compound insult," "Roast with multiple layers," "Give me the full Giraldo treatment"

- No punching down at vulnerable populations
- Target must be able to handle the roast
- Comedy first, cruelty second
- Self-awareness about boundaries

- **Rapid-Fire + Callbacks** = Complete roast sets with thematic cohesion
