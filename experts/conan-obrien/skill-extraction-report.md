## Extraction Criteria

## Skill Candidates

### 1. Self-Deprecation as Armor

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Transform personal insecurities, failures, and weaknesses into comedic material that disarms criticism before it can be delivered, creating permission for vulnerability and absurdity.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I handle criticism?"
- "I'm worried about looking foolish"
- "How do I talk about my failures?"
- When entering territory where the speaker could be attacked

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear steps: identify vulnerabilities, attack them first, own them completely |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by situations requiring defense or vulnerability |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically addresses converting weakness to strength |
| Reusable | Yes | Works in any context where criticism is possible |
| Valuable | Yes | Core technique for public figures, speakers, leaders |

**Output:** Material that pre-strikes potential criticisms through self-mockery

**Core Steps:**
1. Identify what you could be criticized for
2. Attack it before anyone else can
3. Exaggerate the flaw to absurd proportions
4. Treat it as simply who you are, not a defense
5. Let the self-mockery create permission for sincerity later

### 2. Absurdist Escalation

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Take a reasonable premise and push it incrementally past logic, each step seeming to follow naturally until arriving somewhere impossible, with humor coming from the escalation process itself.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I make this funnier?"
- "This premise is too normal"
- "How do I escalate a bit?"
- When an observation has unrealized comedic potential

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear escalation structure with identifiable stages |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when content needs to be pushed further |
| Scoped | Yes | Only addresses comedic escalation |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for any premise with escalation potential |
| Valuable | Yes | Core technique for comedy writing and improvisation |

**Output:** Escalated version of input premise with stage-by-stage development

**Core Steps:**
1. Start with recognizable observation or premise
2. Identify what would be slightly more extreme
3. Push that version one step further
4. Find the logical-but-absurd next step
5. Commit to the absurdity completely
6. Accept the impossible as mundane reality

**Escalation Structure:**
- Stage 1: Normal premise
- Stage 2: Slight exaggeration
- Stage 3: Logical extension to absurdity
- Stage 4: Complete surreal break
- Stage 5: Matter-of-fact acceptance

### 3. Interview as Collaboration

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Approach conversations as jazz sessions rather than interrogations, following the other person's energy, building on what they give you, and creating something together rather than extracting information.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I have better conversations?"
- "How do I interview someone?"
- "My conversations feel like interrogations"
- When preparing for important dialogues

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear principles: follow energy, build on responses, find unexpected threads |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered by conversation or interview preparation |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically addresses collaborative dialogue |
| Reusable | Yes | Works for interviews, meetings, difficult conversations |
| Valuable | Yes | Transforms mechanical Q&A into genuine connection |

**Output:** Guidance for collaborative conversation with specific techniques

**Core Steps:**
1. Enter with curiosity, not an agenda
2. Listen for the unexpected thread
3. Follow energy rather than planned questions
4. Build on what they give you
5. Let tangents develop if they're interesting
6. Match their energy level (raise or lower as needed)
7. Find the shared absurdity

### 4. Pivot to Genuine

**Priority:** HIGH

**Description:** Structure comedy so that moments of genuine emotion or sincerity land with maximum impact by creating contrast with surrounding absurdity.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "When should I be sincere?"
- "How do I make the serious part land?"
- "How do I balance comedy and meaning?"
- When sincerity needs to be embedded in lighter material

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear structure: earn through comedy, then drop the bit |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when meaning needs to emerge from comedy |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically addresses comedy-to-sincerity transitions |
| Reusable | Yes | Works in speeches, writing, performance, conversation |
| Valuable | Yes | Creates memorable emotional moments |

**Output:** Structure that positions sincere content for maximum impact

**Core Steps:**
1. Establish comedic voice and rhythm
2. Build trust through genuine laughs
3. Identify the sincere point you want to make
4. Signal the shift (tone change, direct address)
5. Deliver the genuine moment without comedy
6. Don't immediately undercut with another joke
7. Let the moment breathe before returning to comedy

### 5. Callback Architecture

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Plant details early in content that can be returned to later, usually at unexpected moments, creating payoffs that reward audience attention.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I tie this together?"
- "I want the ending to feel connected"
- "How do callbacks work?"
- When building content with potential echo points

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Clear method: plant detail, let it fade, return unexpectedly |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when building longer-form content |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically addresses structural callbacks |
| Reusable | Yes | Works in comedy, speeches, writing |
| Valuable | Partial | High value in long-form; less essential for short content |

**Output:** Content with callback map showing plant points and return points

**Core Steps:**
1. Identify a detail that has return potential
2. Plant it early without emphasis (let it seem throwaway)
3. Let it fade from active attention
4. Return to it at an unexpected moment
5. Act surprised that the callback is happening
6. Optionally return multiple times with increasing absurdity

### 6. Physical Commitment Comedy

**Priority:** MEDIUM

**Description:** Commit fully to physical comedy without reservation or dignity, using the contrast between serious person and absurd action for comedic effect.

**Trigger Conditions:**
- "How do I make physical comedy work?"
- "Should I do this bit?"
- "I feel silly doing this"
- When physical element could enhance comedy

**Criteria Assessment:**
| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
|-----------|------|----------|
| Actionable | Yes | Core principle: commit fully, hold nothing back |
| Invocable | Yes | Triggered when physical comedy is being considered |
| Scoped | Yes | Specifically addresses physical performance |
| Reusable | Partial | Applies primarily to performed comedy |
| Valuable | Yes | The difference between physical comedy working or failing |

**Output:** Guidance for physical comedy commitment

**Core Steps:**
1. Decide if physical element serves the bit
2. If yes, commit with zero reservation
3. Do not telegraph that you're embarrassed
4. Treat the absurd action as completely normal
5. Let the contrast (serious person, silly action) create the comedy
6. Never break to show you know it's silly
