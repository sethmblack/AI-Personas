# Banksy - References

## Primary Sources

### Books by Banksy
- *Banging Your Head Against a Brick Wall* (2001) - Self-published, first collection of work and commentary
- *Existencilism* (2002) - Self-published, continued documentation
- *Cut It Out* (2004) - Self-published, third collection
- *Wall and Piece* (2005, Century/Random House) - Major compilation containing material from previous books, most widely distributed
- *Cut And Run* (2023) - Published to accompany Glasgow exhibition at Gallery of Modern Art

## Pest Control Authentication

### Official Information
- Website: https://pestcontroloffice.com/
- Established: 2008-2009
- Authentication fees: 50 GBP (screen-prints), 100 GBP (originals)
- Policy: Will not authenticate street pieces removed from walls (prevents incentivizing theft)

### Documentation
- Pest Control Certificates of Authenticity (COA) format and requirements

## Notes on Verification

### Confirmed Facts
- Bristol origins and DryBreadZ Crew membership documented through multiple sources
- Stencil technique shift (c. 2000) and "rubbish lorry" origin story from Banksy's own accounts
- All major artwork locations, dates, and auction prices verified through multiple sources
- Pest Control authentication process documented through official channels

### Uncertain or Disputed Elements
- **Identity**: Robin Gunningham identification is journalistic claim (Mail on Sunday 2008), never confirmed or denied by Banksy
- **Birth date**: July 28, 1974 from Mail on Sunday claim - unverified
- **Early career timeline**: Some dates approximate based on available documentation
- **Mr. Brainwash authenticity**: Whether Thierry Guetta/Mr. Brainwash is genuine or performance art remains deliberately ambiguous

### Removed or Relocated Works
- Many street pieces have been removed, relocated, or destroyed
- Some removed works have resurfaced in controversial circumstances (e.g., West Bank slingshot rat appearing in Tel Aviv, 2022)
- Palestinian Tourism Ministry statement on theft of works from barrier wall

### Attribution Caution
- Numerous works attributed to Banksy have not been authenticated by Pest Control
- Only works with Pest Control COA should be considered definitively authentic
- Street pieces are particularly problematic as Pest Control will not authenticate removed works
