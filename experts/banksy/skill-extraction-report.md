### HIGH Priority

#### 1. context-hijack
**Source Pattern:** The Wall Selection Framework
**Purpose:** Choose optimal placement for guerrilla messages by evaluating context, audience, permanence, risk, and spreadability.
**Trigger:** When deciding where to place content, messaging, or interventions for maximum impact without traditional platforms.
**Inputs:** Message/content to place, available placement options, target audience, risk tolerance.
**Outputs:** Ranked placement recommendations with reasoning for each criterion.
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 5-step evaluation process (context resonance, audience access, permanence calculation, legal/risk assessment, photographic quality)
- Invocable: Triggered by "where should I place this?" or "help me choose a location/platform"
- Scoped: Single responsibility (placement selection), clear boundaries
- Reusable: Applies to physical placements, digital content, marketing, activism, any context where location matters
- Valuable: Saves significant effort by providing systematic evaluation framework vs. guessing

#### 2. message-distillation
**Source Pattern:** The Simplification Protocol
**Purpose:** Transform complex messages into immediately comprehensible, maximally impactful single images or phrases.
**Trigger:** When a complex idea needs to be communicated instantly to broad audiences.
**Inputs:** Complex message or argument, target audience, constraints (medium, space, time).
**Outputs:** Single sentence message, identified image/symbol, subverted element, stripped-down essential form.
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 5-step process (state in one sentence, find embodying image, identify symbol to subvert, remove non-essential, test comprehension at both child and scholar levels)
- Invocable: Triggered by "simplify this message" or "make this idea immediate"
- Scoped: Focused solely on distillation, clear start and end points
- Reusable: Applies to advertising, activism, teaching, presentations, any communication needing instant impact
- Valuable: Transforms bloated messaging into memorable, shareable content

### MEDIUM Priority

#### 3. system-infiltration
**Source Pattern:** The Infiltration Framework
**Purpose:** Work within systems to critique or transform them from inside, using their own rules and blind spots.
**Trigger:** When direct confrontation would fail but internal subversion could succeed.
**Inputs:** Target system/institution, desired critique or change, available access points, acceptable risk level.
**Outputs:** Infiltration plan including rules to leverage, blind spots identified, entry method, critique placement, reveal timing.
**Reasoning:**
- Actionable: Clear 6-step process (understand rules, find loophole/blind spot, enter through legitimate channel, plant critique, reveal after fact, let system response prove point)
- Invocable: Triggered by "how do I critique this from inside?" or "infiltrate this system"
- Scoped: One responsibility (infiltration planning), clear boundaries
- Reusable: Applies to institutional critique, activism, whistleblowing, organizational change, art interventions
- Valuable: Provides methodology for asymmetric action against powerful systems

**Note:** Rated MEDIUM because it requires careful judgment about ethics and legality. The framework itself is sound, but users must supply their own risk assessment.
