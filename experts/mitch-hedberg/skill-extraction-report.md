## Skill Candidates Identified

### HIGH PRIORITY

#### 1. literalist-reframe
**Description:** Transform common expressions, idioms, or instructions by taking them at absolute face value to expose hidden absurdities.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ Actionable: Clear process (identify idiom → interpret literally → follow logic → deliver deadpan)
- ✅ Invocable: User can request "reframe this literally" or "give me the Hedberg take"
- ✅ Scoped: Single responsibility (literal reinterpretation), clear boundaries
- ✅ Reusable: Works on any idiomatic expression, sign, instruction, or cliche
- ✅ Valuable: Provides instant comedy transformation that would be hard to generate without the framework

**Input:** Text containing idioms, expressions, instructions, or common phrases
**Output:** Deadpan literal reinterpretation that reveals absurdity

**Example:**
- Input: "A picture is worth a thousand words"
- Output: "That's a bad deal for the picture. Words are free. If I have a thousand words, I can just say them. But if someone wants a thousand words for my picture, I'm keeping the picture."

---

#### 2. absurd-escalation
**Description:** Take a mundane observation and follow its internal logic to increasingly absurd but technically plausible conclusions.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ Actionable: Clear steps (start with mundane fact → apply strict logic → escalate → maintain deadpan)
- ✅ Invocable: "Escalate this absurdly" or "follow the weird logic"
- ✅ Scoped: Single focus (logical escalation), doesn't drift into other comedy forms
- ✅ Reusable: Applies to any ordinary situation, rule, or observation
- ✅ Valuable: Transforms boring content into memorable absurdist comedy

**Input:** Ordinary observation, situation, or fact
**Output:** Series of escalating observations that follow weird but valid logic

**Example:**
- Input: "Vending machines dispense snacks"
- Output: "I like vending machines because snacks are better when they fall. If I buy a candy bar at a store, I often drop it so it achieves its maximum flavor potential. The store owner doesn't like that. He said 'You're ruining the merchandise.' I said 'I'm enhancing it through gravitational flavor activation.' He disagreed."

---

#### 3. one-liner-compression
**Description:** Compress an observation, joke, or commentary into the shortest possible form while maintaining maximum comedic impact.

**Rationale:**
- ✅ Actionable: Clear steps (identify core insight → remove all non-essential words → test for punchline → verify deadpan delivery works)
- ✅ Invocable: "Compress this to a one-liner" or "Give me the Hedberg version"
- ✅ Scoped: Focused on compression and joke structure, not content generation
- ✅ Reusable: Works on any joke, observation, or commentary that's too verbose
- ✅ Valuable: Transforms rambling humor into sharp, quotable one-liners

**Input:** Verbose joke, observation, or commentary
**Output:** Compressed one-liner with setup and punchline in minimal words

**Example:**
- Input: "I've been thinking about how strange it is that we have these plants in our homes that aren't real, they're artificial, and yet we're supposed to take care of them as if they were real, but I forgot to do that and they died anyway"
- Output: "My fake plants died because I did not pretend to water them."

**Rationale:**
- ✅ Actionable: Process exists (identify mundane element → find technical description → apply weird lens → deliver casually)
- ✅ Invocable: "Make this surreal" or "Hedberg-ize this topic"
- ✅ Scoped: Focused on recontextualization, clear boundaries
- ✅ Reusable: Works on any mundane topic
- ⚠️ Valuable: Somewhat overlaps with literalist-reframe and absurd-escalation; adds value but less distinct

**Input:** Boring topic or description
**Output:** Surreal observation that makes the familiar strange

**Note:** Medium priority because it significantly overlaps with literalist-reframe and absurd-escalation. May not be worth creating as separate skill.
