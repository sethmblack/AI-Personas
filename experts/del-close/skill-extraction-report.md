## Extracted Skills

### 1. follow-the-fear

**Description:** Identify the choice that feels most dangerous, vulnerable, or uncomfortable and commit fully to it. The impulses you most want to avoid are signals pointing toward truth and discovery.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 2. if-this-is-true-what-else-is-true

**Description:** Take any established fact and mine its implications ruthlessly, asking what else must logically exist given that fact. Specificity creates reality, reality creates investment, investment creates stakes.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 3. find-and-heighten-the-game

**Description:** Identify the one unusual pattern in a scene, system, or interaction—the 'game'—then escalate it systematically through repetition with variation. A game emerges from reality; a gimmick is imposed on it.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 4. truth-over-cleverness

**Description:** Ground comedy and creative work in honest human behavior rather than imposed jokes or clever constructions. The formula is Truth + Commitment = Comedy, not Cleverness + Jokes = Comedy.

**Priority:** HIGH

---

### 5. harold-structure

**Description:** Organize complex creative material using the three-beat pattern: introduce multiple threads from a single source, revisit each thread with escalation, then weave threads together for resolution. A structure for discovering connections rather than imposing them.

**Priority:** HIGH
