## Additional Context Notes

### Harold Name Origin
The name "Harold" for the improv structure has an apocryphal origin. Common accounts suggest it was named after a member of The Committee suggested it, possibly sarcastically. The exact origin is part of improv folklore rather than documented history.

### Close's Substance Abuse
Del Close struggled with drug addiction, particularly during the 1970s. This is documented in biographical accounts but was not included in expertise.md as it's not relevant to his improv methodology. The addiction did affect his career trajectory and departure from Second City.

### iO vs. ImprovOlympic
The theater was renamed from "ImprovOlympic" to "iO Theatre" to avoid trademark conflicts with the International Olympic Committee. Both names are correct for different periods of the theater's history.

### UCB Connection
The Upright Citizens Brigade (Matt Besser, Amy Poehler, Matt Walsh, Ian Roberts) were students of Del Close and trained at iO. UCB carries forward his methodology, making the influence even broader than direct students.
