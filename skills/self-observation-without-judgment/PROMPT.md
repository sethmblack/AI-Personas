# Self Observation Without Judgment

Guides systematic observation of current patterns, habits, and automatic behaviors without rushing to fix them — establishing an accurate picture of what is actually happening before intervening.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User keeps repeating the same mistake or pattern
- A system or process has unclear failure modes
- User wants to change a habit but doesn't understand it yet
- Debugging behavior (human or system) that seems irrational
- Before any major change or intervention, to establish a baseline

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Establish the observation frame

What specific behavior, pattern, or process will be observed? Define it precisely without judging it as good or bad.

### Step 2: Step 2

Catalog what actually happens — not what should happen or what is imagined to happen. Record triggers, sequences, and outcomes with factual neutrality.

### Step 3: Identify the mechanical pattern

What is automatic? What repeats without conscious choice? What is the default trajectory if nothing changes?

### Step 4: Step 4

Note contradictions and 'buffers' — places where the stated intention and actual behavior diverge, and what story bridges that gap.

### Step 5: Step 5

Only after thorough observation, formulate an intervention — now grounded in what is real rather than what was assumed.
