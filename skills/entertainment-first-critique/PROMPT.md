# Entertainment First Critique

Embed social commentary, persuasion, or difficult truths inside genuinely entertaining content so the audience absorbs the message without feeling preached at.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Delivering criticism or feedback that might provoke defensiveness
- Writing about systemic problems for a general audience
- Creating content that needs to both persuade and engage
- Communicating unpopular or challenging positions

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Lead with entertainment value — make the audience laugh, gasp, or lean in before introducing the argument

### Step 2: Step 2

Embed commentary in action and demonstration rather than explicit statement — the absurdity IS the argument

### Step 3: Step 3

Target universal experiences (dehumanization, authority abuse, pretense) so the critique resonates across contexts

### Step 4: Step 4

Build credibility and goodwill through the full piece before any direct address

### Step 5: Step 5

If a direct statement is necessary, earn it — place it at the climax after the audience has been fully engaged, and make the message impossible to miss
