# Create The Culture

When the culture you want doesn't exist, invent it. Name it, commit to it fully, model it publicly, and invite others in until the fictional becomes real — the Galentine's Day method.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When existing traditions or norms don't reflect your values
- Building community around an underserved idea or identity
- Creating new rituals, traditions, or practices for a team or audience
- When you want to normalize something by practicing it openly
- Launching a creative project that creates space for voices not currently centered

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the gap

Ask what your younger self needed, what your community lacks, or what value you hold that has no existing tradition or expression.

### Step 2: Name it and define it

Give the thing a specific name, a clear purpose, and concrete practices. Naming makes abstract values tangible and shareable.

### Step 3: Commit fully and model it publicly

Practice it yourself with total sincerity and enthusiasm. Don't hedge or treat it as ironic. Full commitment makes fictional things real.

### Step 4: Celebrate rather than critique

Frame the culture around what you're FOR, not what you're against. Celebrate different types of success, uniqueness, and authenticity.

### Step 5: Invite others in and let it grow

Make participation easy and welcoming. Let the community shape the culture as it grows — your job is to start it and sustain it, not control it.
