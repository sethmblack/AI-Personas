# AI Safety Governance

A skill for evaluating AI systems for safety implications using principled framework

## When to Use
- Assessing safety of AI deployment
- Policy/governance questions
- Risk assessment for advanced AI
- Evaluating organizational AI practices

## Inputs
1. The AI system to evaluate
2. Capabilities and limitations
3. Deployment context
4. Stakeholders affected

## Methodology

### Phase 1: Capability Assessment
What can this system do:
- Current capabilities
- Failure modes
- Potential misuse
- Autonomous action potential

### Phase 2: Risk Identification
Map potential harms:
- Malicious use (cyberattacks, misinformation, weapons)
- Malfunction (unintended harm, reliability issues)
- Misalignment (pursuing wrong objectives)
- Concentration of power

### Phase 3: Governance Evaluation
Who controls this:
- Democratic oversight?
- International coordination?
- Transparency and auditability?
- Accountability mechanisms?

### Phase 4: Safety Measures
What safeguards exist:
- Testing and evaluation protocols
- Monitoring deployment
- Kill switches or limits
- Alignment research

### Phase 5: Recommend Frameworks
Based on risk level:
- Self-regulation sufficient? (NO for high-risk)
- Mandatory registration/testing?
- International coordination needed?
- Deployment restrictions?

## Output Format
1. Capability Profile
2. Risk Assessment (malicious use, malfunction, misalignment)
3. Current Governance State
4. Safety Measures Present
5. Recommendations for Governance
6. Urgency and Priority

## Constraints
- Neither dismiss nor exaggerate risks
- Consider both capability and safety camps
- International coordination essential for powerful systems
- Precautionary principle for existential risks
- Balance innovation and safety

## Example
**Input**: Evaluate new reasoning language model
**Output**: Capabilities: Advanced reasoning, long-horizon planning, code generation. Risks: Could find exploits, aid bioweapons research, exhibit deceptive behavior. Governance: Currently self-regulated by lab. Safety Measures: Internal testing, but not transparent. Recommendation: Mandatory safety testing before deployment, international reporting requirement, enhanced monitoring for dual-use risks. Urgency: HIGH - capabilities advancing faster than safety measures.
