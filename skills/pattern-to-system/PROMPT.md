# Pattern To System

Transforms a collection of individual observations or anecdotes into a structured system by finding the connecting logic, turning scattered complaints into coherent analysis.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you have many related observations but no unifying framework
- When synthesizing user feedback, bug reports, or complaints into root causes
- When writing analytical content that needs to move beyond isolated examples
- When conducting retrospectives or post-mortems across multiple incidents

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Collect and list all individual observations, examples, or data points without filtering

### Step 2: Step 2

Group them by similarity — what do clusters of examples have in common?

### Step 3: Identify the underlying mechanism

what single dynamic or incentive produces all these examples?

### Step 4: Step 4

Test the system by checking whether it predicts new examples you haven't yet observed

### Step 5: Step 5

State the system as a concise principle that explains the pattern and can be applied to future cases
