# Prepared Spontaneity

Create the appearance of effortless, spontaneous performance through rigorous behind-the-scenes preparation, rehearsal, and choreographic precision—making controlled craft look like happy accidents.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Preparing for live performance, public speaking, or any high-stakes real-time delivery
- Physical comedy, pratfalls, or complex staging that must appear unplanned
- Presentations or demos that need to feel natural and conversational despite being scripted
- Any creative work where visible effort would undermine the desired effect

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Map out every beat, transition, and moment of apparent chaos with precise choreographic detail

### Step 2: Step 2

Rehearse the sequence until muscle memory takes over and conscious effort disappears

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify 2-3 places where controlled variation can occur to keep the performance feeling fresh each time

### Step 4: Step 4

Build in recovery moves for when genuine spontaneity or mistakes occur, so they look intentional

### Step 5: Step 5

Perform with full commitment and zero visible self-consciousness—trust the preparation and let go
