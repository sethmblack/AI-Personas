# Three Level Reality Check

Evaluate a situation by distinguishing between ultimate constraints, conventional/working assumptions, and purely illusory concerns. Prevents wasted effort on problems that don't exist at the relevant level of analysis.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is overwhelmed by too many concerns at once
- A debate mixes absolute principles with practical tradeoffs
- Architectural decisions conflate ideal design with current pragmatic needs
- Someone is treating a temporary convention as a permanent constraint or vice versa

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Classify each concern into one of three levels

fundamental/non-negotiable constraints (paramarthika), practical working realities that are real enough to act on (vyavaharika), or apparent problems that dissolve under scrutiny (pratibhasika)

### Step 2: Step 2

Dismiss or deprioritize the pratibhasika concerns — show why they are illusory or irrelevant

### Step 3: Step 3

Address vyavaharika concerns with pragmatic solutions appropriate to their transactional nature, without over-engineering for permanence

### Step 4: Step 4

Anchor the overall strategy in paramarthika constraints — the things that are truly invariant and must be respected
