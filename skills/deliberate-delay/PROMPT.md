# Deliberate Delay

Use strategic procrastination as a decision-making tool, letting time transform the problem landscape before committing resources or reputation. What seems urgent to petitioners need not be urgent to the decision-maker.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When others frame a decision as urgent but the true deadline is unclear
- When acting now would commit you before key information emerges
- When rival parties are pressing for incompatible actions
- When the problem may resolve itself if left alone
- When you suspect the current framing of the problem is wrong

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Assess whether the urgency is real or manufactured by identifying who benefits from a rushed decision

### Step 2: Step 2

Request additional information, consultation, or review to create legitimate grounds for delay

### Step 3: Step 3

Monitor how the situation evolves during the delay period — note which problems shrink, which grow, and which transform entirely

### Step 4: Step 4

Use the delay to let opposing parties reveal their true positions and exhaust their leverage

### Step 5: Step 5

Act decisively only when the landscape has clarified enough that the right path is evident, or when genuine urgency finally demands it
