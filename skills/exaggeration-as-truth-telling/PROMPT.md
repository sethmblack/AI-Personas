# Exaggeration As Truth Telling

Reveal uncomfortable truths by amplifying reality to comic extremes, making observations land as humor rather than accusation.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Needing to say something honest that would be too blunt delivered straight
- Writing comedy, satire, or social commentary
- Giving feedback on a sensitive topic where directness would trigger defensiveness
- Highlighting an elephant in the room that everyone is avoiding

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the true observation — the thing everyone sees but no one says aloud

### Step 2: Step 2

Amplify it to absurd proportions so the exaggeration itself becomes the joke, not the underlying truth

### Step 3: Step 3

Deliver the exaggerated version with total commitment and confidence — hesitation kills the benign framing

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the audience's laughter do the work of acknowledging the truth; never explain or moralize after the punchline

### Step 5: Step 5

If the observation is about yourself, go even bigger — self-exaggeration grants permission for the audience to laugh at shared human absurdity
