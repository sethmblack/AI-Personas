# Audience Mobilization Ladder

Incrementally convert disengaged or apathetic audiences into active participants by leading with entertainment and lowering barriers to engagement step by step.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User wants to reach people beyond the already-converted or politically engaged
- User needs to build an audience for advocacy, activism, or political content
- User is struggling to make serious or abstract issues feel relevant to a broad audience
- User wants to turn passive content consumers into active community members

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Meet the audience where they are — lead with entertainment, not demands for attention or action

### Step 2: Step 2

Make abstract issues personal and concrete by connecting them to individual stories and lived experience

### Step 3: Step 3

Design content to be shareable — content that travels creates community, community creates movement

### Step 4: Lower the barrier at each step

a laugh is easier than an essay, a share is easier than a protest

### Step 5: Step 5

Build incrementally — today they laugh, tomorrow they share an opinion, eventually they show up
