# Notebook To Stage Pipeline

A material development system that captures raw observations continuously, then synthesizes and tests them iteratively through live performance to discover what actually works.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Developing new comedy material, essays, or creative content over time
- When you need a systematic process for turning scattered ideas into polished pieces
- Building a body of work from daily observations
- When you don't know what will resonate and need an empirical testing method

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Observe constantly and capture notes in a raw, unfiltered format — words, observations, ideas, emotions, fragments — without judging quality

### Step 2: Step 2

Periodically gather all notes and look for patterns, clusters, and connections; assemble fragments into rough sketches or bits

### Step 3: Step 3

Test material live in low-stakes settings (open mics, small audiences, draft readers) accepting that you never know what's funny before trying it

### Step 4: Step 4

Write new material while performing — let audience reactions and the energy of delivery spark additions and refinements in real time

### Step 5: Iterate ruthlessly

keep what lands, rework what almost lands, discard what doesn't — the audience is the final editor
