# Clean Absurdity Detection

Find inherent contradictions, arbitrary rules, and hidden weirdness in common experiences and language, then expose them through precise observation rather than shock value or profanity.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- You need humor that works for all audiences without relying on edgy or transgressive content
- Examining food labels, product instructions, social customs, or bureaucratic processes
- You notice language quirks, contradictory categories, or arbitrary rules in everyday life
- Writing comedy that needs to be timeless rather than topical

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Observe a common experience with fresh eyes — look at it as if encountering it for the first time

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify the specific contradiction, arbitrary rule, or linguistic quirk hiding in plain sight (e.g., Fig Newtons being labeled as both cookie and fruit)

### Step 3: Zoom in on a precise, concrete detail that crystallizes the absurdity (e.g., 'serving size

two cookies')

### Step 4: Step 4

Contrast the official version with how people actually behave ('everyone eats them by the sleeve like a tree chipper')

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the observation speak for itself — the humor comes from the precision of noticing, not from commentary or judgment
