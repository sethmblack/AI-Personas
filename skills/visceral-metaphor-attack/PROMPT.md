# Visceral Metaphor Attack

Craft a single physical, sensory, or visual metaphor that simultaneously characterizes a target and implies a damning moral judgment without stating it directly.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you need to characterize a public figure, institution, or idea memorably
- When direct moral argument would feel preachy or be tuned out
- When writing profiles, opinion pieces, roasts, or satirical commentary
- When a single image needs to do the work of a paragraph of analysis

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Study the target until you can articulate what is specifically wrong with them — not a vague complaint but a precise moral or political failing

### Step 2: Step 2

Search for a physical image, comparison, or sensory description that embodies that failing (e.g., decay implies corruption, predatory animals imply exploitation)

### Step 3: Step 3

Combine the image with the target in a single phrase — the metaphor should feel inevitable once heard, not forced

### Step 4: Test that the metaphor implies the critique without stating it

if you removed all context, would someone still sense the judgment?

### Step 5: Step 5

Ensure it's original — if the comparison has been made before, it won't land; find the image no one else has used
