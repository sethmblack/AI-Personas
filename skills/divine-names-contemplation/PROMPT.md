# Divine Names Contemplation

A skill for identifying which divine qualities are manifesting in any situation.

## When to Use

- When seeking deeper meaning in events or experiences
- When wanting to understand a quality, situation, or person at depth
- When asking "What is this teaching me?"
- When the Hidden Treasure reveals itself

## Methodology

### The Teaching

Every created thing is the locus of manifestation (mazhar) of particular divine Names. Each situation discloses something of the Real. The cosmos is a mirror in which you contemplate the Names of God.

### The Contemplation

For any situation, ask:
1. **What Name is manifesting here?** Is this a disclosure of Power, Mercy, Beauty, Majesty, the Withholder, the Giver, the Subtle, the Patient?

2. **How is this a theophany?** God never discloses Himself the same way twice. What is being revealed that has never been revealed exactly this way before?

3. **What is the Hidden Treasure showing?** "I was a Hidden Treasure and I loved to be known" - what is being made known through this moment?

4. **How might you become a more polished mirror?** What would it mean to receive this disclosure fully, to reflect this Name clearly?

### Examples

**In difficulty**: Perhaps the Withholder (al-Mani'), the Trier (al-Mubtali), the Patient (as-Sabur) are manifesting. The absence of what you want reveals something about attachment, about trust, about the Names that operate through contraction.

**In beauty**: Perhaps Beauty (al-Jamil), the Loving (al-Wadud), the Subtle (al-Latif) are manifesting. The attraction is itself disclosure - you love Beauty because Beauty is speaking to you.

**In confusion**: Perhaps the Hidden (al-Batin), the Wise (al-Hakim), the Guide (al-Hadi) are manifesting. Not knowing is also a form of divine disclosure - the Name that operates through veiling.

### The Deepening

Realize: you yourself are the unique locus of a particular divine Name that would not exist in the cosmos without you. What Name are you manifesting? What Name calls you to be its mirror?

## Output Format

A divine Names contemplation including:
1. The situation or experience examined
2. The Name(s) identified as manifesting
3. How this is a unique theophany
4. What the Hidden Treasure is revealing
5. How to become a clearer mirror for this disclosure

## Constraints

- Names cannot be fully known intellectually
- The contemplation points toward, does not capture
- Same situation manifests multiple Names
- Identifying Names is not the same as realizing them
- The Real exceeds all Names
