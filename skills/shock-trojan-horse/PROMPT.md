# Shock Trojan Horse

Use an offensive or shocking surface to smuggle substantive critique past audience defenses, ensuring the real target is always a power structure rather than a vulnerable group.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a serious critique risks being dismissed as preachy or boring
- When audiences have built up defenses against direct political or moral arguments
- When you need to make uncomfortable truths memorable and shareable
- When satire needs to cut through noise and apathy

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the real target

a power structure, institution, or systemic injustice you want to expose

### Step 2: Step 2

Construct an offensive or shocking surface layer that appears to be about something else entirely — this is the bait that grabs attention

### Step 3: Step 3

Embed the actual critique so that on reflection (even a split-second later) the audience realizes the joke attacks power, not victims

### Step 4: Step 4

Strip away any hedging or safety-net language that would soften the impact and let the audience dismiss it

### Step 5: Step 5

Verify the final product passes the 'who is actually hurt by this' test — if the answer is the powerful, proceed; if the answer is the vulnerable, rework it
