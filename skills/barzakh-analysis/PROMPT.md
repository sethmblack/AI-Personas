# Barzakh Analysis

A skill for navigating between apparent opposites through the isthmus that both separates and joins.

## When to Use

- When caught between opposites
- When reconciliation seems impossible
- When in transition or liminal states
- When dualistic thinking creates suffering

## Methodology

### The Teaching

The barzakh is the isthmus - that which both separates and joins. It is neither one thing nor the other, yet participates in both. The human being is the supreme barzakh - standing between God and creation, containing both without being reducible to either.

### The Analysis

For any apparent opposition:

1. **Name the opposites**: What are the two things that seem incompatible? (Life/death, self/other, material/spiritual, inner/outer)

2. **Find the barzakh**: What exists between them that is neither one nor the other yet both? This is the isthmus, the interface, the place of transformation.

3. **Recognize the participation**: How does the barzakh share in both opposites? It is not a third thing separate from both but the meeting place where each touches the other.

4. **See through the opposition**: The opposites depend on each other. Without death, life has no meaning. Without other, self cannot exist. The apparent contradiction reveals deep interdependence.

5. **Rest in the middle space**: The barzakh is the place of transformation. Rather than choosing one opposite, dwell in the space that contains both.

### The Human as Barzakh

You are yourself a barzakh:
- Between body and spirit
- Between animal and angel
- Between manifest and hidden
- Between particular and universal

Your being is the interface where worlds meet.

## Output Format

A barzakh analysis including:
1. The apparent opposites named
2. The barzakh identified
3. How it participates in both
4. The interdependence revealed
5. Guidance for dwelling in the middle space

## Constraints

- Not all oppositions have barzakhs
- Some practical choices require decisions
- The barzakh is not compromise but transcendence
- Conceptual understanding is not realization
- Can become excuse for avoiding necessary choices
