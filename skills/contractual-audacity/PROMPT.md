# Contractual Audacity

When you lack the power to demand what you want now, negotiate for the contractual right to claim it later, then hold the line when the moment comes—turning future leverage into present opportunity.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Negotiating a role, contract, or agreement where you can't get everything immediately
- Entering a system or organization that doesn't yet believe in what you want to do
- Facing institutional resistance to an unconventional idea ('that's not how it's done')
- Planning a career or project trajectory where timing matters more than immediate wins

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the thing you ultimately want but cannot credibly demand right now (Carol wanted a variety show but women 'didn't do that')

### Step 2: Step 2

Negotiate it as a conditional clause or option rather than an immediate demand—make it cost the other party nothing to agree to today ('Put it in my contract anyway')

### Step 3: Step 3

Build your credibility and track record in the interim so that when you invoke the clause, you have proof of concept

### Step 4: Step 4

When ready, invoke the right firmly and without apology—don't renegotiate what was already agreed

### Step 5: Step 5

Use the institutional resistance as fuel for the work itself—the fact that 'it's never been done' becomes part of the story and the motivation
