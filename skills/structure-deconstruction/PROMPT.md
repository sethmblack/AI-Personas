# Structure Deconstruction

Reveals the hidden mechanics of a format or structure (comedy set, essay, meeting, pitch deck) by explicitly narrating what will happen before it happens, forcing the audience to engage with craft and content rather than being manipulated by surprise.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a presentation, argument, or piece of writing relies on structural manipulation (surprise reveals, withholding information, emotional ambush)
- When you want an audience to focus on substance rather than spectacle
- When designing a talk, document, or experience where transparency would build more trust than suspense
- When critiquing a format that uses its structure to control rather than inform the audience

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Map the existing structure

outline the format's conventional moves (e.g., hook, build, reveal, resolution) and identify where it relies on surprise or withholding

### Step 2: Pre-empt the structure

tell the audience what you're going to do before you do it — spoil your own format

### Step 3: Execute the content with precision

now that the audience knows the structure, the quality of the ideas and craft must carry the work

### Step 4: Identify what the transparency reveals

note what becomes visible when the structural tricks are removed — does the content hold up on its own?

### Step 5: Reflect on the power dynamic

did the original structure serve the audience or control them? Does the deconstructed version redistribute that power?
