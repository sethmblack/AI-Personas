# Precision Form Parody

Create parody or creative reinterpretation by mastering and perfectly replicating the original form's structure, then introducing subversive or absurd content into that flawless container.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Parodying a specific genre, format, or style of content
- Creating satirical versions of documents, presentations, or media
- Writing genre-specific content that subverts expectations
- Building creative projects that reference or riff on established formats
- Designing humorous training materials or internal communications

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Select and study the source obsessively — watch, read, or analyze the original multiple times, noting camera placement, pacing, structure, tone, visual aesthetics, and how it builds its thesis

### Step 2: Step 2

Separate form from content — identify which elements are tropes specific to this particular work vs. conventions of the broader genre; map the formal skeleton precisely

### Step 3: Step 3

Replicate the form with total fidelity — recreate the structure, rhythm, visual language, and editorial voice exactly; the audience must recognize the source through execution quality alone

### Step 4: Step 4

Introduce subversion only through content — keep the form deadly serious while making the subject matter absurd; trust the audience to recognize both the reference and the twist

### Step 5: Step 5

Let precision generate comedy — resist the urge to do the form badly for laughs; the humor emerges from the contrast between perfect execution and ridiculous subject matter
