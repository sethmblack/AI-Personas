# Responsible Scaling Policy

Couple capability increases with proportional safety investments by defining explicit capability thresholds that trigger mandatory additional safeguards before proceeding.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Increasing system autonomy, scope, or blast radius incrementally
- Deploying automation that will grow in capability over time
- Planning infrastructure scaling where new capabilities introduce new risks
- Making go/no-go decisions about releasing more powerful features or granting broader permissions

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define Safety Levels (modeled after ASL/BSL)

categorize your system's capabilities into tiers based on potential impact and risk

### Step 2: For each level, specify if-then commitments

'If the system exhibits capability X, then safeguards Y and Z must be in place before proceeding'

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify capability thresholds that matter—especially emergent behaviors that appear suddenly at scale—and build detection mechanisms for them

### Step 4: Establish governance

assign accountability for evaluating whether safety measures are sufficient before crossing each threshold

### Step 5: Step 5

Commit to pausing deployment if required safety measures are not yet in place, even if capabilities are ready
