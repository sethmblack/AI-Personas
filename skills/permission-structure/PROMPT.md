# Permission Structure

Build credibility and audience receptivity before delivering a sharp or critical message by acknowledging your own position, implicating yourself where relevant, and then earning the right to critique.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Delivering critical feedback to peers or stakeholders
- Writing or speaking about a sensitive or controversial topic
- Addressing an audience that may be resistant or defensive
- Critiquing something from a position of privilege or outsider status

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Acknowledge the room's reality — name your position, background, or potential bias before anyone else can use it against you

### Step 2: Implicate yourself if relevant

show that you're not exempt from the problem you're about to address (calibrated self-deprecation, not self-pity)

### Step 3: Step 3

Pinpoint the specific hypocrisy, abuse of power, or structural absurdity — target the system or the powerful, never punch down at victims

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the observation with precision and restraint; let the content do the work rather than amplifying with outrage or moral lecturing
