# Iron Hand Velvet Glove

Exploit others' underestimation by presenting apparent vulnerability or softness while maintaining absolute resolve underneath, then deploying decisive force at the moment of maximum strategic advantage.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When opponents or counterparts are underestimating you based on surface characteristics
- When a negotiation requires you to appear flexible while holding firm on essentials
- When you need to deliver a hard decision but want to preserve the relationship
- When projecting full strength prematurely would provoke unnecessary resistance
- When you need to discipline or correct while maintaining loyalty and morale

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the assumption others are making about your weakness or softness, and let it stand uncorrected

### Step 2: Step 2

Use warmth, emotional generosity, and personal connection to build genuine bonds that make your eventual firmness more devastating and harder to resist

### Step 3: Step 3

When the decisive moment arrives, act with unmistakable clarity and force — make the contrast between your usual softness and your current resolve impossible to miss

### Step 4: Step 4

After the decisive action, immediately return to warmth — frame the hard act as painful necessity, not preference, to preserve future relationships

### Step 5: If challenged on the contradiction, own both sides openly

the softness is real, the steel is also real, and wisdom lies in knowing which moment demands which
