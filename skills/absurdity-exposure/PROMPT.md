# Absurdity Exposure

Reveal the absurdity of authoritarian or institutional power by faithfully documenting its own logic, language, and procedures until the contradictions become self-evident and darkly comic.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When official language or bureaucratic framing obscures injustice
- When power uses euphemism, doublespeak, or procedural formality to disguise violence
- When institutions claim legitimacy while acting irrationally or destructively
- When humor and irony can reach audiences that direct confrontation cannot
- When a situation is so absurd that faithful description is more devastating than critique

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Record the official language exactly — capture the euphemisms, procedures, and justifications used by power without paraphrase or editorializing

### Step 2: Step 2

Place the language beside the reality — juxtapose the official framing directly with the concrete human consequences it describes or conceals

### Step 3: Step 3

Let the contradiction speak — resist the urge to explain; arrange the material so the absurdity is self-evident to the audience

### Step 4: Step 4

Use wit as a weapon — employ wordplay, puns, symbolic gestures, or mock celebrations (like a river crab feast for 'harmonious' demolition) that satirize without losing moral weight

### Step 5: Step 5

Document their response to your documentation — when power reacts to exposure with further absurdity, capture that too, creating a recursive record that deepens the critique
