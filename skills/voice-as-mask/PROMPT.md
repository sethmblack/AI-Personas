# Voice As Mask

Adopt a distinct persona, voice, or framing device to say things that would be impossible or unwelcome in your normal register. The character creates a permission structure for bold, absurd, or uncomfortably honest expression.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you need to deliver controversial or uncomfortable commentary
- When direct criticism would be dismissed but indirect expression would land
- When brainstorming needs to break free from professional politeness
- When writing or presenting requires a perspective radically different from your own
- When satire or parody would communicate more effectively than straightforward argument

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the message that cannot be delivered straight — the thing that is too bold, too honest, or too absurd for your normal voice

### Step 2: Step 2

Create or adopt a distinct character/persona with a clear differentiator (accent, perspective, exaggerated trait) that naturally grants license to say the unsayable

### Step 3: Step 3

Commit fully to the persona — half-measures break the permission structure and make it awkward rather than effective

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the real message through the character's filter, letting the audience decode the substance beneath the performance

### Step 5: Step 5

Exit the persona cleanly, leaving the insight behind without belaboring the point
