# Format Subversion Critique

Use the conventions, tropes, and audience expectations of a medium or format against itself to expose its assumptions, revealing how the form shapes and limits content.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Critiquing a medium, genre, or format from within it
- Creating meta-commentary that is also functional content
- Exposing how format conventions smuggle in unexamined assumptions
- Making audiences aware of the invisible rules they've been accepting

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the unspoken conventions of the format you're working in — what does the audience expect to happen and why?

### Step 2: Step 2

Follow those conventions faithfully at first to establish trust and familiarity — the audience needs to feel comfortable before you pull the rug

### Step 3: Step 3

Introduce a single deviation from convention that forces the audience to notice the convention itself — the violation makes the rule visible

### Step 4: Step 4

Escalate the deviation to reveal what the convention was hiding or assuming — show what the format normally prevents you from saying

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the audience sit in the discomfort of seeing the format's machinery exposed — resist the urge to resolve back into comfort
