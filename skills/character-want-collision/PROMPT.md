# Character Want Collision

Construct scenes or narratives by defining what each character wants, making those wants incompatible, and letting comedy or drama emerge from the collision rather than from externally imposed jokes or plot devices.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing dialogue that feels flat or joke-dependent
- Building a scene where characters need to feel real and motivated
- Developing conflict that arises organically rather than being contrived
- Creating comedy without relying on punchline structures

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish what each character wants in the scene — not what they say they want, but what they actually need

### Step 2: Step 2

Make those wants fundamentally incompatible so neither character can get what they want without blocking the other

### Step 3: Step 3

Let each character pursue their want using the strategies available to their personality — not the strategies that would be most entertaining

### Step 4: Step 4

Track the gap between what characters say and what they do; comedy and drama live in that gap

### Step 5: Step 5

Resist resolving the tension prematurely — let the collision play out until something genuinely shifts or breaks
