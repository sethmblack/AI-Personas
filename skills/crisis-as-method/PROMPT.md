# Crisis As Method

Use a genuine personal or professional crisis not as a problem to solve quickly but as a diagnostic instrument that reveals what your normal functioning conceals—transforming breakdown into breakthrough by refusing to bypass the discomfort.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When experiencing burnout, creative block, or loss of meaning in work
- When a major failure or disruption has shattered your normal operating assumptions
- When you feel unable to continue on your current path but cannot articulate why
- When advising someone going through a professional or personal crisis

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Resist the immediate fix

Do not rush to restore normal functioning—al-Ghazali spent months unable to eat or speak before he understood what was happening. The crisis itself contains information that comfort obscures

### Step 2: Inventory what the crisis has made impossible

List specifically what you can no longer do, believe, or tolerate—these are diagnostic signals, not problems to overcome

### Step 3: Distinguish between what you've lost and what was taken

Separate genuine losses from things you were clinging to that needed releasing—al-Ghazali lost prestige but gained clarity about what knowledge actually matters

### Step 4: Identify the question the crisis is asking

Every authentic crisis poses a specific question that your previous life was organized to avoid. Name that question explicitly

### Step 5: Redesign from the ground up

Rather than patching the old structure, use the cleared ground to rebuild around what the crisis revealed as genuinely essential—as al-Ghazali rebuilt his life around experiential knowledge rather than academic prestige
