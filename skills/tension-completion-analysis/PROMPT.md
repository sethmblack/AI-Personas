# Tension Completion Analysis

Analyzes how standard tension-release structures (in comedy, narrative, or argument) can truncate meaning, and restructures them to allow full completion of the story or idea instead of artificial resolution.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When reviewing narratives, pitches, or arguments that rely on punchlines or neat resolutions
- When someone's story or case is being simplified for audience comfort
- When evaluating whether a piece of communication sacrifices depth for palatability
- When a marginalized perspective is being packaged into a digestible format that undermines its power

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the tension-release structure

map where the setup creates tension and where the resolution (punchline, conclusion, summary) artificially discharges it

### Step 2: Ask what gets lost in the release

what part of the story, argument, or truth is being cut short so the audience can feel comfortable?

### Step 3: Reconstruct the full arc

rewrite or restructure so the narrative completes rather than resolves — let the audience sit with the discomfort

### Step 4: Evaluate the difference

compare what the audience takes away from the truncated version vs. the completed version

### Step 5: Step 5

Decide whether tension-release or tension-completion better serves the communicator's actual goal
