# Argument From Desire

Identify a persistent, unsatisfied longing or need and reason backward to what must exist to explain it, using the pattern that innate desires correspond to real fulfillments.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When exploring why users feel dissatisfied despite having many options
- When designing products or experiences that address deep unmet needs
- When a person or audience expresses a vague yearning they can't articulate
- When building a case that something missing from the current landscape should exist

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify and name the specific longing or dissatisfaction — describe it concretely even if the person experiencing it cannot

### Step 2: Step 2

Catalog all existing solutions or satisfiers that have been tried, and document why each falls short

### Step 3: Establish the pattern

for every well-known innate desire (hunger, thirst, curiosity), a corresponding real fulfillment exists

### Step 4: Step 4

Reason by analogy that the persistent, unsatisfied desire likely points to a real but currently missing fulfillment

### Step 5: Step 5

Describe or propose what that fulfillment would look like, using the shape of the desire as a specification
