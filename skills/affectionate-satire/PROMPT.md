# Affectionate Satire

Critique, parody, or satirize a subject by participating in it genuinely first, finding the gap between self-image and actual behavior, and presenting that gap with love rather than contempt.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing humorous or satirical content about communities or trends
- Creating social commentary that doesn't alienate the subject
- Designing marketing that playfully acknowledges audience quirks
- Giving critical feedback that lands without defensiveness
- Building comedy writing or sketch concepts

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Immerse yourself in the subject — genuinely participate in or deeply study the culture, community, or behavior you intend to satirize so you understand it from the inside

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify the contradiction — find the specific gap between how the subject sees itself and how it actually behaves (e.g., the performance of ethical consumption vs. its absurd extremes)

### Step 3: Step 3

Anchor in specificity — replace broad stereotypes with precise, observed details that insiders would recognize and outsiders would find revelatory (not 'hipsters' but 'the exact pause before mentioning food restrictions')

### Step 4: Step 4

Play it completely straight — present the satire without winking at the audience; let the comedy emerge from the gap between earnest commitment and visible absurdity

### Step 5: Step 5

Ensure you're in the joke — the best affectionate satire includes yourself as a target; you should be making fun of something you genuinely participate in
