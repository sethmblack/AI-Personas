# Stoic Escalation

Structure a situation so that pressure, scale, or absurdity increases systematically while the protagonist (or narrator) maintains composure and methodical problem-solving. Tension is built through contrast between chaos and calm.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Communicating under pressure or during a crisis
- Writing incident reports or postmortems
- Presenting bad news or complex problems to stakeholders
- Navigating situations that are spiraling in scope or complexity
- Building narratives that need to hold attention through rising stakes

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Begin with one clear, manageable problem stated plainly — no drama, no editorializing.

### Step 2: Step 2

Introduce complications that logically follow from the first problem, each one larger in scale (one rejection becomes ten becomes hundreds; one boulder becomes an avalanche).

### Step 3: Step 3

Maintain deadpan composure throughout — describe escalation factually, never emotionally. The contrast between tone and content creates impact.

### Step 4: At peak chaos, pivot to practical action

identify the single next step that addresses the situation, regardless of how overwhelming the whole picture appears.

### Step 5: Step 5

Resolve through persistent forward motion, not dramatic heroism. What matters is the next step, not the last fall.
