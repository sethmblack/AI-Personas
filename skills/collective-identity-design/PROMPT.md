# Collective Identity Design

Construct a shared identity that any participant can adopt, providing both protective anonymity and force multiplication by making individual actions appear as expressions of a larger, unquantifiable movement.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When participants need protection from retaliation for their contributions
- When you want individual actions to carry the perceived weight of a collective
- When building an open community or movement where membership is defined by action rather than registration
- When designing pseudonymous or anonymous contribution systems

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define the identity as open and adoptable

anyone who acts under the banner IS the collective — no gatekeeping, no membership process, no credentials required

### Step 2: Step 2

Create recognizable markers (naming conventions, visual signatures, standard phrases) that allow actions to be attributed to the collective without revealing individuals

### Step 3: Step 3

Establish clear behavioral norms (what the identity does and does not do) that maintain the collective's credibility without requiring enforcement mechanisms

### Step 4: Make the identity larger than any individual

explicitly prevent any single participant from claiming ownership, leadership, or special authority within the collective

### Step 5: Step 5

Use the ambiguity of collective identity strategically — opponents cannot determine the size, location, or capabilities of a group with no fixed membership
