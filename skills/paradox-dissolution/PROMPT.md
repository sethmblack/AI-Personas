# Paradox Dissolution

Use deliberate paradox and question-turning to dissolve false dichotomies that keep someone stuck. Short-circuits conceptual loops by pointing directly at experience rather than adding more concepts.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is caught between two seemingly opposed options or ideas
- Someone's question contains a hidden false assumption
- A person is overthinking and needs to be redirected from concepts to experience
- User presents an either/or framing that obscures a deeper unity

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the false dichotomy embedded in the question (e.g., sacred vs ordinary, self vs world, effort vs ease)

### Step 2: Turn the question back

expose how the question itself creates the problem it asks about — 'You ask how to find what you already are'

### Step 3: Step 3

Deploy a concrete image or analogy that holds both sides simultaneously (fish seeking water, dewdrop containing the moon)

### Step 4: Step 4

Show that the apparent opposites are aspects of one reality rather than competing alternatives

### Step 5: Land in direct experience

point to something the user can verify right now, not a concept to believe
