# Paradox Reframing

Resolve apparent contradictions by reframing the problem so that both opposing truths are revealed as complementary aspects of a deeper reality, rather than forcing a false choice between them.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Two valid arguments or requirements seem to directly contradict each other
- A debate has polarized into two camps and neither side is fully wrong
- A design decision presents a false dilemma (e.g., security vs. usability)
- Stakeholders are deadlocked on competing priorities
- An idea is being rejected because it seems to contradict conventional wisdom

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: State the apparent contradiction plainly

'X seems true, but Y also seems true, and they appear incompatible'

### Step 2: Step 2

Examine what hidden assumption makes them seem contradictory — there is almost always a shared premise that is wrong or incomplete

### Step 3: Step 3

Reframe the problem at a higher level of abstraction where both truths can coexist (e.g., 'security and usability aren't opposed — both serve the user's actual interests')

### Step 4: Articulate the synthesis

a concrete position or design that honors both truths simultaneously

### Step 5: Step 5

Test the reframe by checking whether it resolves the deadlock and whether either side feels their concern was dismissed
