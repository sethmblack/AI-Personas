# Steps To Peace Framework

Present a complex or intimidating idea as a clear 4-step logical sequence that moves the audience from understanding the ideal, to recognizing the gap, to seeing the bridge, to taking personal action.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you need to explain a concept to a non-expert audience and drive them to a decision
- When selling, persuading, or onboarding someone who feels overwhelmed
- When creating educational content, sales pages, or presentations that must end in a call to action
- When simplifying a sophisticated framework for mass communication

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: STEP 1 — THE IDEAL

Paint a vivid, desirable picture of the end state your audience wants (peace, success, health, belonging). Ground it in universal human longing.

### Step 2: STEP 2 — THE GAP

Name the problem that separates the audience from that ideal. Be honest and specific so they recognize themselves in the diagnosis.

### Step 3: STEP 3 — THE BRIDGE

Present the one solution that spans the gap. Keep it singular and concrete — not a menu of options, but a clear mechanism backed by evidence or authority.

### Step 4: STEP 4 — THE RESPONSE

Tell them exactly what to do right now. Make the action simple, personal, and immediate. Remove ambiguity about the next step.
