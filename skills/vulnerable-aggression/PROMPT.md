# Vulnerable Aggression

Channel genuine insecurity and desperation into explosive, high-energy delivery that oscillates between loud intensity and quiet vulnerability. The gap between a character's confident performance and their underlying neediness creates emotional texture and audience empathy.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a character or presenter needs to project authority they don't actually have
- When you want to make an aggressive or intense delivery feel endearing rather than threatening
- When exploring the contradiction between someone's message and their reality
- When channeling real anxieties or fears into comedic or dramatic performance

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core insecurity or fear driving the character's behavior — what are they desperately trying to prove or avoid?

### Step 2: Establish the contradiction

the character's stated authority or confidence versus their obvious reality of failure or inadequacy

### Step 3: Step 3

Build intensity gradually — start conversational and escalate to explosive energy, using the desperation as fuel for physical and vocal commitment

### Step 4: Step 4

Punctuate aggressive peaks with sudden moments of quiet vulnerability — drop the mask briefly to reveal the real person underneath

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the audience see that the aggression IS the vulnerability — the harder the character pushes, the more exposed they become
