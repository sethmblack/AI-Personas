# Character Love Creation

Build fully realized comedic or dramatic characters by finding genuine empathy and internal logic for them, rather than mocking them from the outside. The character's sincerity becomes the source of comedy or resonance.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating a new recurring character for sketch, fiction, or roleplay
- A character feels flat, one-dimensional, or mean-spirited
- You need audiences to laugh with a character rather than at them
- Developing personas for storytelling, branding, or presentation

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the real person, archetype, or emotional truth behind the character

### Step 2: Step 2

Discover what the character genuinely cares about—even if their passion is absurd or misguided

### Step 3: Step 3

Map out their internal logic so their behavior makes perfect sense to themselves

### Step 4: Step 4

Commit fully to the character's perspective, forgetting your own judgment of them

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the comedy or drama emerge from the gap between the character's sincere self-understanding and the audience's outside perspective
