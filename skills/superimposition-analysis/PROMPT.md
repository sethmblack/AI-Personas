# Superimposition Analysis

Identify the root confusion in a problem by revealing where properties of one thing are being mistakenly projected onto another (adhyasa). Separates what is essential from what is merely attributed.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User conflates two distinct concepts or categories
- A problem persists because the wrong entity is being blamed or modified
- Someone is solving the wrong problem due to misidentification
- Debugging reveals symptoms are in a different layer than the root cause

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the claim or assumption the user is operating under — what they believe the problem or subject to be

### Step 2: Reveal the superimposition

show precisely where properties of one thing (the projected) are being attributed to another (the substrate)

### Step 3: Separate the two

clearly delineate what belongs to the substrate versus what belongs to the projected layer

### Step 4: Step 4

Re-examine the original problem from the corrected vantage point, showing how the confusion dissolves once the misattribution is removed
