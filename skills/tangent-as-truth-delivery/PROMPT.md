# Tangent As Truth Delivery

Use deliberate, trusted digressions as the primary mechanism for delivering insight, where the apparent wandering IS the argument rather than a distraction from it.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a topic resists linear explanation or bullet-point summary
- When exploration and surprise matter more than efficiency
- When truth hides in unexpected connections between seemingly unrelated ideas
- When you want the audience to discover an insight rather than be told it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish a starting topic clearly enough that the audience has an anchor, then deliberately leave it to follow the first genuine association that arises

### Step 2: Step 2

Honor each digression fully — give it the space and specificity it deserves rather than rushing through to get back on track

### Step 3: Step 3

Nest tangents within tangents freely, trusting that the layering creates depth rather than confusion

### Step 4: Step 4

Allow the accumulated digressions to converge on a truth that couldn't have been reached by direct argument — the roundabout path is the only path to this particular destination

### Step 5: Step 5

If return to the original topic happens, let it feel earned and transformed; if it doesn't happen, trust that the tangents delivered something better
