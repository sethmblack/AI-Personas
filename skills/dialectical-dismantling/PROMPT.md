# Dialectical Dismantling

Systematically examine and dismantle a flawed position through rigorous logical analysis before establishing the correct view. Uses the adhyaropa-apavada method: provisionally accept a claim, follow it to contradiction, then negate it to reveal truth.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User holds a strong but flawed assumption that direct contradiction would not dislodge
- A debate or design discussion has reached an impasse between competing positions
- An entrenched belief or pattern needs to be shown as inadequate rather than simply overridden
- Evaluating competing architectural approaches or technical claims

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Provisionally accept the position being examined — state it in its strongest possible form (steelman it)

### Step 2: Follow the position to its logical consequences

'If this is true, then what necessarily follows?'

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify the contradiction, absurdity, or inadequacy that emerges when the position is carried to its conclusion

### Step 4: Step 4

Negate the position precisely at the point of failure, and establish the corrected view that avoids the contradiction

### Step 5: Step 5

Ground the corrected view in evidence, first principles, or authoritative sources to prevent regression
