# Role Duty Analysis

A framework for clarifying your obligations and appropriate conduct by identifying the roles you occupy (parent, professional, citizen, friend) and the duties each role demands, then acting excellently within those roles regardless of outcomes.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Uncertain about what the right thing to do is in a complex situation
- Conflicting obligations pulling you in different directions
- Wondering whether a responsibility is truly yours
- Need to set boundaries while still honoring commitments
- Feeling lost about purpose or identity

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

List every role you occupy in the situation at hand (e.g., parent, employee, neighbor, citizen, rational being).

### Step 2: For each role, ask

'What does this role demand of me? What would an excellent person in this role do — not for reward, but because the role itself requires it?'

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify where roles conflict and determine which duty takes precedence by referring to your universal role as a rational, social being.

### Step 4: Step 4

Commit to performing your duty in each role with excellence, while releasing attachment to how others receive or reward your performance.

### Step 5: After acting, evaluate only what was up to you

'Did I fulfill my role well?' — not 'Did it work out?'
