# Statistical Comedy

Weave real data, historical facts, and concrete statistics into narrative or comedic framing so that empirical evidence becomes memorable and emotionally resonant rather than dry and forgettable.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When presenting research, data, or analysis to a general audience
- When dry facts alone fail to motivate action or understanding
- When you need to make a data-driven argument stick in memory
- When writing content that needs to be both credible and engaging

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Research thoroughly — gather specific statistics, dates, dollar amounts, and historical facts that support your argument

### Step 2: Step 2

Find the human story or absurd contrast embedded in the data — every number represents a lived reality or a systemic choice

### Step 3: Step 3

Reframe the statistic using scale, comparison, or juxtaposition that makes its meaning viscerally clear (e.g., comparing a budget line item to something the audience values)

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the data inside a narrative arc or joke structure so the fact arrives at a moment of emotional engagement rather than passive listening

### Step 5: Step 5

Repeat key numbers for emphasis — statistical comedy works through the cumulative weight of precise, undeniable details
