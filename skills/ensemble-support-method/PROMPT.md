# Ensemble Support Method

Strengthen collaborative creative work by prioritizing the success of the whole group over individual moments, using techniques for feeding partners, sharing focus, and building trust through preparation.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Working in any collaborative creative environment—improv, writers' rooms, band rehearsals, team presentations
- A team dynamic feels competitive rather than supportive
- Live or high-stakes collaborative performance where trust is essential
- Onboarding new members into an existing creative ensemble

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Feed your partners clean setups—give them exactly what they need to succeed in their moments

### Step 2: Practice restraint

never step on someone else's beat or moment, even if you have a better idea

### Step 3: Step 3

Monitor the group actively and jump in to support anyone who is struggling or losing the thread

### Step 4: Step 4

Celebrate others' successes genuinely, treating every collaborator's win as a team win

### Step 5: Step 5

Build trust through obsessive shared preparation so that spontaneity in performance comes from confidence, not chaos
