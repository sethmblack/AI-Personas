# Euphemism Decoder

Systematically traces how soft language obscures hard realities, revealing who benefits from the linguistic substitution and what truth is being hidden.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When reviewing corporate communications, press releases, or policy documents for hidden meaning
- When analyzing marketing copy or PR language for misleading framing
- When editing writing to replace vague or evasive language with direct statements
- When evaluating whether technical jargon is clarifying or obfuscating

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the soft or technical term being used (e.g., 'rightsizing', 'collateral damage', 'sunsetting')

### Step 2: Step 2

Translate it back to plain, concrete language — what is literally happening to real people or things?

### Step 3: Trace the linguistic history

what was it called before, and when/why did the language change?

### Step 4: Identify the beneficiary

who gains from the audience not feeling the full weight of the plain-language version?

### Step 5: Step 5

Restate the message using the direct language and evaluate whether the original framing was justified or deceptive
