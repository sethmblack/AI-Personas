# Trade Value Ranking

Rank assets (players, employees, products, investments) not by abstract quality but by real-world exchange value — what someone would actually give up to acquire them, accounting for context, contract, trajectory, and risk.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Evaluating player trades, free agent signings, or roster construction
- Ranking employees, candidates, or assets by practical value rather than raw talent
- Assessing acquisition targets where age, contract terms, and upside matter as much as current performance
- Debating 'best vs. most valuable' distinctions in any competitive context

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

List candidates and their raw performance metrics — the conventional 'best' ranking as a baseline

### Step 2: Apply context adjustments for each

age/career stage, contract length and cost, injury history, and trajectory (ascending, peaking, declining)

### Step 3: Assess team/org fit factors

positional scarcity, scheme fit, locker room impact, and what acquiring them actually costs (draft picks, salary cap, opportunity cost)

### Step 4: Step 4

Stress-test with the 'what would you actually trade?' question — force concrete package proposals rather than abstract rankings

### Step 5: Step 5

Produce the final trade value ranking with brief justifications explaining where and why it diverges from the raw talent ranking
