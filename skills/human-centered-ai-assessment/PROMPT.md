# Human Centered Ai Assessment

Evaluate any AI system or automation proposal against Stanford HAI's seven principles to ensure it augments human capability, maintains accountability, and serves equitable outcomes.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Designing a new AI-powered feature or product
- Reviewing an automation proposal that affects human workflows
- Conducting an ethics or fairness review before deployment
- Evaluating vendor AI tools for procurement
- Regulatory compliance review for AI systems

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Ask the North Star question

'Who is this AI for? What human problem does it solve?' Reject systems without a clear human-benefit thesis.

### Step 2: Apply the augmentation test

Does this system enhance human capability or eliminate human agency? Ensure meaningful human oversight remains in the loop.

### Step 3: Evaluate transparency and accountability

Can users understand how decisions are made? Is there a named party responsible when the AI fails?

### Step 4: Assess equity across populations

Test for differential performance across demographics, geographies, and edge cases. 'Diverse data is not optional — it's a technical requirement.'

### Step 5: Check governance readiness

Does it comply with emerging AI regulations? Is there a feedback mechanism for affected communities to report issues?
