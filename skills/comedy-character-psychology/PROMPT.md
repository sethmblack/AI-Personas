# Comedy Character Psychology

Build comedic characters with genuine internal logic by grounding them in real wants, contradictions, and hidden depth — making them both funnier and sustainable across multiple appearances.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating a recurring comedic character
- Developing a character who needs to work in both comedy and drama
- A comedic character feels flat or one-dimensional
- Building a character for a series rather than a single sketch

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the genuine want

what does this person actually need at a human level? (Jimmy McGill wants legitimacy; Matt Foley wants to save kids from his own fate.)

### Step 2: Build the contradiction

find what about them works against their own goal — this is the engine of comedy and the source of sympathy.

### Step 3: Grant them dignity

define how the character sees themselves, which should be at odds with how the audience sees them. Never make them just a punchline.

### Step 4: Layer in hidden depth

give them pain or history that drives their behavior — hypersensitivity, past failure, unprocessed grief — so the comedy has an honest core.

### Step 5: Test for sustainability

ask whether this character can support multiple scenes or episodes. If they can only do one thing, deepen them until they can surprise you.
