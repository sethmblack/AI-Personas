# Ceremonial Wind Up

Transform simple actions into elaborate preparatory rituals that build anticipation and make the payoff land harder. The preparation becomes more entertaining than the action itself.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a presentation, demo, or reveal needs dramatic buildup
- When you want to heighten audience anticipation before a key moment
- When a simple action or announcement feels underwhelming on its own
- When pacing needs to slow down to create contrast with a fast payoff

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core action or reveal that needs amplification

### Step 2: Step 2

Design 3-5 preparatory steps that are individually entertaining but delay the main action (adjustments, false starts, tangential rituals)

### Step 3: Step 3

Escalate the preparation so each step raises anticipation slightly more than the last

### Step 4: Step 4

Include at least one false start — begin the action, then pull back for one more preparatory beat

### Step 5: Step 5

Execute the actual action simply and quickly, letting the contrast with the buildup create the impact
