# Volume To Quality Ratio

Generate a high volume of ideas with emotional detachment, accepting a brutal selection ratio (e.g., 50:1) as standard operating procedure rather than failure, so that quality emerges from quantity.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Brainstorming solutions to a problem and getting attached to the first idea
- Feeling blocked or perfectionistic about creative output
- Working on time-sensitive deliverables where iteration speed matters
- Building a practice of regular creative or strategic output

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Set a quantity target that feels excessive (e.g., 10x what you need) and timebox the generation phase — speed over polish

### Step 2: Detach emotionally from each individual idea

label them by number, not by how clever they feel, to prevent attachment

### Step 3: Step 3

Apply ruthless selection criteria after generation is complete — evaluate against the specific context, audience, and moment rather than abstract quality

### Step 4: Step 4

Track your hit rate over time and notice how it improves as your instincts develop; early career is shotgun (many attempts, low hit rate), mature practice is targeted (fewer attempts, higher hit rate)

### Step 5: Step 5

Treat every rejection as data, not defeat — the idea may be excellent but wrong for this moment
