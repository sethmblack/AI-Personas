# Say Do Gap Analysis

Exposes the contradiction between stated values or rhetoric and actual behavior or outcomes, making the gap itself the central finding.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When auditing an organization's actions against its mission statement or stated values
- When reviewing political or corporate promises against track records
- When analyzing whether a codebase, architecture, or process matches its documentation
- When evaluating whether cultural narratives match measurable reality

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Document the stated claim, value, or promise in the subject's own words

### Step 2: Step 2

Gather concrete evidence of actual behavior, outcomes, or metrics

### Step 3: Step 3

Place the claim and the evidence side by side and articulate the specific gap

### Step 4: Step 4

Ask 'who benefits from the gap remaining unnoticed?' to identify structural incentives

### Step 5: Formulate the honest version

rewrite the stated claim to match reality
