# Lead From The Front

Build trust and unlock extraordinary effort by visibly sharing the hardest risks and burdens alongside your team. Personal example is the most powerful form of leadership communication — do yourself what you ask of others.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Team morale is low or people are hesitant to take on a difficult task
- A leader needs to build credibility with a new team
- The stakes are high and people need to see commitment, not just hear it
- There is a gap between what leadership asks and what leadership does
- A crisis demands presence rather than delegation

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the hardest, riskiest, or least desirable part of the current challenge — the thing people are avoiding or dreading

### Step 2: Take it on yourself first and visibly

don't delegate the worst parts while keeping the comfortable ones; be first into the difficulty

### Step 3: Share the conditions

if the team is working weekends, work weekends; if they lack resources, operate with the same constraints before asking them to

### Step 4: Demonstrate vulnerability

let people see you struggle, get wounded, and persist — perfection is less inspiring than visible resilience

### Step 5: Transition from example to empowerment

once trust is built, elevate others to lead their own fronts while maintaining your visible commitment
