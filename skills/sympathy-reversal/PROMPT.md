# Sympathy Reversal

Systematically complicate audience allegiance by revealing the legitimate motivations of apparent villains and the flaws and complicity of apparent victims, forcing moral complexity over easy categorization.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A story has a clear hero-villain dynamic that feels too simple
- A character is functioning as a pure antagonist without genuine motivation
- The audience is too comfortable in their moral judgments about characters
- Writing satire or dark comedy that needs to implicate the audience

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Introduce the apparent victim and apparent villain with enough clarity that the audience forms initial sympathies

### Step 2: Step 2

Reveal the antagonist's legitimate motivations — not excuses, but real human needs driving their behavior

### Step 3: Step 3

Reveal the protagonist's flaws and complicity in the conflict — show how they benefit from or enable the dynamic they claim to oppose

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the audience sit in moral confusion without providing a narrator voice or authorial judgment to resolve it

### Step 5: Step 5

Refuse to collapse back into easy categories at the conclusion — let complexity stand as the final word
