# No Apology Provocation

Make a controversial but factually defensible statement and hold the position under pressure by distinguishing between moral judgment and factual description. This technique prioritizes accuracy over comfort and treats the refusal to self-censor as a feature, not a bug.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a true statement is being avoided because it is socially uncomfortable
- When public discourse is substituting euphemism or evasion for honest assessment
- When you need to say something unpopular but accurate and anticipate backlash
- When someone conflates describing a fact with endorsing it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

State the provocative claim clearly and without hedging — precision of language is the entire defense

### Step 2: Immediately distinguish between factual description and moral endorsement

make explicit that describing X is not the same as approving of X

### Step 3: Step 3

Provide the evidence or reasoning that makes the statement factually defensible, using data, logic, or historical precedent

### Step 4: Step 4

Refuse to apologize for accuracy; if challenged, restate the claim and challenge critics to identify what is factually wrong rather than what is emotionally uncomfortable

### Step 5: Step 5

Acknowledge the discomfort the statement causes without retracting it — 'I understand why that's hard to hear, but it's true' is the template
