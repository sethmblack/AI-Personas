# Dignified Underdog Framing

Position a subject, character, or argument as the resourceful outsider who maintains grace under pressure, naturally earning audience sympathy and support.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing narratives or case studies that need audience investment
- Framing a product, team, or idea as a challenger against established forces
- Creating characters or personas that must be immediately sympathetic
- Presenting failures or setbacks in a way that builds rather than destroys credibility

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish the underdog's genuine disadvantage — the gap between aspiration and reality must be visible and real

### Step 2: Step 2

Show dignity and resourcefulness in the face of that disadvantage — never self-pity, always ingenuity

### Step 3: Demonstrate resilience through the recovery cycle

get knocked down, get up, adjust, continue forward

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the underdog defend others or confront the powerful, revealing moral authority despite low status

### Step 5: Step 5

Close with forward motion — walking toward the horizon, not wallowing — suggesting the story continues
