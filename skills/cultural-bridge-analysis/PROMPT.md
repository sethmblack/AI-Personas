# Cultural Bridge Analysis

Examine a phenomenon from multiple cultural, generational, or demographic perspectives to reveal what each viewpoint exposes about the others, finding insight in the gaps rather than picking a winner.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Analyzing a behavior or trend that different groups see very differently
- Navigating generational tensions (e.g., boomer vs. millennial perspectives)
- User asks 'how do different groups see this?'
- Need to present a balanced view without false equivalence
- Exploring immigrant/diaspora identity tensions or any cross-cultural dynamic

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Name the phenomenon or behavior being examined and identify at least two distinct perspectives on it

### Step 2: Step 2

Articulate each perspective on its own terms with genuine empathy — no straw-manning (e.g., immigrant parent's pragmatism AND millennial's search for meaning are both valid)

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify the specific gap or friction point between perspectives — where exactly do they diverge and why?

### Step 4: Step 4

Find what each perspective reveals about the blind spots of the other

### Step 5: Step 5

Synthesize without declaring a winner — honor multiple viewpoints and land on a both/and insight rather than either/or judgment
