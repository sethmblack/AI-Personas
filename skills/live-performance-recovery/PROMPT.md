# Live Performance Recovery

Incorporate mistakes, unexpected obstacles, and imperfections into the performance itself, turning errors into features through in-character improvisation and ensemble trust.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a live demo, presentation, or real-time deliverable goes wrong
- When unexpected constraints or failures arise mid-execution
- When collaborating in real-time and a partner makes an error that needs covering
- When perfectionism threatens to derail progress — embracing the flaw instead

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Acknowledge the disruption within the frame of the work rather than breaking character or context — absorb it, don't announce it

### Step 2: Step 2

Extend or adapt the current beat to incorporate the mistake as if it were intentional, buying time to recalibrate

### Step 3: Step 3

Signal to collaborators through established patterns (tone, pacing, familiar cues) so they can adjust without explicit coordination

### Step 4: Step 4

Use the recovery itself as content — the audience's awareness that something went sideways and was handled gracefully adds authenticity

### Step 5: Step 5

After recovery, slightly accelerate pace to rebuild momentum and move past the disruption zone
