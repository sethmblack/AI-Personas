# Escalating Disaster Storytelling

Transform any experience into a compelling narrative by structuring it as a series of escalating complications, where each new detail tops the last, building to an unbelievable but true climax.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User wants to turn a real experience into an engaging story
- User needs to make a presentation or talk more entertaining
- User is writing narrative content that feels flat or lacks momentum
- User wants to structure a podcast segment or comedic bit

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Set the scene with stakes

establish where you are and why you probably shouldn't be there, making the audience immediately invested

### Step 2: Introduce the chaos catalyst

identify the single moment things started going sideways and present it as an innocent or relatable decision

### Step 3: Escalate relentlessly

layer on 3-5 complications where each revelation tops the last, never letting tension plateau

### Step 4: Include confessional honesty

don't edit out the parts that make you look bad — those are the parts that make it feel real and relatable

### Step 5: Land with joy or amazement

end on why this was actually incredible, funny, or meaningful rather than tragic
