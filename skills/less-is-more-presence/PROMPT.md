# Less Is More Presence

Achieve maximum impact through minimal means — using stillness, silence, reduction, and selective gesture so that every element that remains carries outsized significance.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a scene, design, or piece of writing feels cluttered or over-explained
- When a supporting role or secondary element needs to be memorable without dominating
- When conveying complex internal states like isolation, ennui, or quiet joy
- When the audience is sophisticated enough to read subtext and fill in gaps

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with the full version — write, design, or perform everything you think the moment needs

### Step 2: Remove fifty percent

cut dialogue, gestures, adjectives, features — whatever is most expendable

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify the single most essential element that survived and make it the anchor

### Step 4: Step 4

Replace removed content with intentional negative space — pauses, whitespace, stillness, silence

### Step 5: Step 5

Test whether the audience can reconstruct what was removed from what remains; if not, add back only the minimum needed
