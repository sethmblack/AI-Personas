# Body Truth Deployment

Ground abstract emotional or social experiences in graphic, specific physical reality to make them undeniable, using anatomical detail and visceral sensation to bypass intellectual defenses and normalize taboo conversations.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When discussing experiences that are usually euphemized or sanitized (postpartum, aging, illness, sexuality)
- Writing about bodily experiences that audiences pretend don't exist
- When abstract emotional claims need concrete anchoring to land
- Creating content about health, parenthood, or physical transformation
- When you need to normalize a conversation by refusing to look away from physical specifics

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the abstract or euphemized version of the experience (e.g., 'recovery after childbirth is hard')

### Step 2: Step 2

Replace every abstraction with a specific anatomical or physical detail (e.g., mesh underwear, nipple shields, stitches, fluids)

### Step 3: Step 3

Describe the physical sensation viscerally — what it feels like, not just what it looks like — making the audience's body react

### Step 4: Step 4

Refuse to provide an escape hatch or softening qualifier — no 'but it's all worth it' redemption arc

### Step 5: Let the specificity do the normalizing work

by naming the unspeakable in clinical-yet-comedic detail, you make it speakable for everyone
