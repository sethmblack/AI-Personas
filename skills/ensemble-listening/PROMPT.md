# Ensemble Listening

Elevate collaborative creative work by prioritizing genuine listening and authentic reaction over pre-planned contributions. Trust that supporting others' ideas and responding to what was actually offered — rather than redirecting to your own agenda — produces better collective work.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Collaborating on creative work with partners — writing rooms, brainstorms, improv, co-creation
- A group dynamic feels competitive rather than generative
- You catch yourself planning your next move instead of being present with collaborators
- Building long-term creative partnerships where trust and safety matter

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Listen to collaborators as if hearing them for the first time — resist the urge to anticipate, categorize, or prepare a response while they're still contributing

### Step 2: Step 2

Respond to what was actually said or offered, not what you expected or hoped they would say — let your reaction be genuine rather than strategic

### Step 3: Build on their offers before introducing your own

find what's alive in their contribution and extend it before redirecting

### Step 4: Step 4

Trust that supporting others elevates everyone — when partners feel safe, they take bigger risks, which gives you better material to work with

### Step 5: Stay present rather than planning your next moment

the best contributions come from authentic reaction, not pre-loaded material
