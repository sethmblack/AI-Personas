# Follow The Fear

Identify the choice that feels most dangerous, vulnerable, or uncomfortable and commit fully to it. The impulses you most want to avoid are signals pointing toward truth and discovery.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When facing a creative decision and defaulting to the safe or predictable option
- When a scene, piece of writing, or idea feels flat or forgettable
- When you notice yourself avoiding a topic, emotion, or direction
- When work needs more authenticity or emotional resonance

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Notice the impulse

identify what choice feels dangerous, embarrassing, or too honest

### Step 2: Name the fear

articulate what specifically you're afraid will happen if you go there

### Step 3: Examine the avoidance

recognize what 'safe' choice you'd make instead and why it's weaker

### Step 4: Commit fully to the scary thing

execute the vulnerable choice with total conviction, no half-measures

### Step 5: Observe the result

notice how vulnerability creates connection, specificity, and depth that safe choices never produce
