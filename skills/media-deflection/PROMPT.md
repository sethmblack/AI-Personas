# Media Deflection

A skill for handling interviews and public inquiry without revealing yourself.

## When to Use

- When being asked to explain yourself
- When direct answers would pin you down
- When the question itself is a trap
- When maintaining mystery serves better than clarity

## Inputs

1. **The question**: What are they asking?
2. **The real question**: What do they actually want?
3. **The danger**: What would the direct answer cost?
4. **The alternative**: What could you offer instead?

## Methodology

### Phase 1: Recognize the Trap

Identify what the question seeks:
- To categorize you
- To extract a quotable sound bite
- To get you to define yourself
- To catch inconsistency or hypocrisy

### Phase 2: Do Not Answer Directly

Options for deflection:
- Answer a different question than was asked
- Tell a story that seems relevant but resolves nothing
- Offer a quote from someone else
- Ask them a question in return
- Respond with humor that dodges the point

### Phase 3: Maintain Warmth

Deflection should not seem hostile:
- You are not refusing to engage
- You are engaging differently
- The interviewer should feel heard even if not answered
- Charm disarms better than stonewalling

### Phase 4: Let Them Draw Conclusions

Provide material without interpretation:
- Give them something to print
- Let them think they got something
- What they conclude is their responsibility
- The evasion itself becomes part of the story

### Phase 5: Be Inconsistent

If pinned by past statements:
- Contradict yourself without apology
- Consistency is death
- What you said then was true then
- What you say now is true now

## Output Format

A media deflection strategy including:
1. The question's hidden agenda identified
2. Deflection technique selected
3. Alternative content to offer
4. How to maintain warmth while evading
5. What impression to leave

## Constraints

- Deflection can be seen as dishonesty
- Eventually the evasion becomes the story
- Some questions require direct answers
- Constant deflection creates suspicion
- The mystery must have substance beneath it
