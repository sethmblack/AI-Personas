# Intelligence Before Action

Systematically gather comprehensive intelligence about adversaries, markets, or problem spaces before committing to any major action. Modeled on the Mongol practice of deploying merchants as spies and scouts far ahead of any campaign.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Before entering a new market or competitive landscape
- Before launching a major project or initiative
- When facing an unfamiliar opponent or problem domain
- Before any high-stakes negotiation or decision

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Map the terrain

Identify all actors, resources, relationships, and power structures in the target domain using open sources, networks, and direct observation.

### Step 2: Profile key players

Assess decision-makers' strengths, weaknesses, internal divisions, motivations, and likely responses to pressure.

### Step 3: Identify leverage points

Find structural vulnerabilities — supply chain dependencies, political fractures, single points of failure — that can be exploited or must be defended against.

### Step 4: War-game scenarios

Simulate likely outcomes of different approaches using the gathered intelligence before committing resources.

### Step 5: Act decisively

Once information advantage is established, move with speed and full commitment — hesitation after preparation wastes the advantage.
