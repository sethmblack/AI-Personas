# Improvisational Crowd Reading

Read an audience or group in real time and generate material spontaneously from observable details rather than relying on prepared scripts.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Giving a speech or presentation to an unfamiliar audience
- Facilitating a meeting or workshop that feels flat or disengaged
- Performing stand-up or emceeing an event
- Needing to pivot away from prepared material that isn't landing

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Scan the room for observable details

what people look like, what they do, how they're seated, their energy level

### Step 2: Step 2

Pick a specific, concrete detail about a person or the situation and build an exaggerated riff from it — never generalize, always particularize

### Step 3: Step 3

Gauge the response instantly; if the audience leans in, escalate the riff — if they pull back, redirect to a new target or self-deprecate

### Step 4: Step 4

Make each interaction unique to the moment so the audience feels they're witnessing something unrepeatable

### Step 5: Step 5

Treat the audience's reactions as your primary material — their laughter, groans, and silence are all fuel for the next line
