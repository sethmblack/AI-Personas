# Reference Cascade Construction

Stack 3-5 references from completely different domains in rapid succession to build an argument through accumulation, where the sheer density and range of allusions becomes the persuasive force.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A single metaphor or comparison isn't enough to convey the magnitude of the point
- Building toward a comedic or rhetorical climax
- Demonstrating the universality of an observation across domains
- Creating a sense of overwhelming evidence through analogy
- Energizing a passage that has become too linear or logical

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core quality or claim you want to establish through accumulated evidence-by-analogy

### Step 2: Step 2

Select references from at least 3 different domains (e.g., one classical/historical, one scientific/philosophical, one pop culture/sports, one everyday/absurd) that each illuminate the same quality from a different angle

### Step 3: Step 3

Arrange them in order of escalating surprise or absurdity, with the most unexpected comparison last

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver them at high velocity with minimal connective tissue—use commas, dashes, or short conjunctions rather than full explanatory sentences between references

### Step 5: Step 5

Follow the cascade with either a brief meta-acknowledgment of the excess or an immediate pivot to the final point, never letting the energy dissipate into explanation
