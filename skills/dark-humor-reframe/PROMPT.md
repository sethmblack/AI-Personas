# Dark Humor Reframe

Find the absurdity hiding inside a grim, frustrating, or depressing situation and surface it — not to dismiss the pain but because laughing at the darkness is more honest than pretending it isn't there.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When discussing a painful, frustrating, or bleak situation that needs levity without dismissiveness
- When writing about failure, bureaucracy, or systemic absurdity
- When a user is stuck in despair-mode thinking and needs a shifted perspective
- When content about hardship needs to feel human rather than grim or preachy

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core misery or frustration — state it plainly without softening it

### Step 2: Find the absurd element

the rule that makes no sense, the irony no one mentions, the detail that would make a stranger laugh

### Step 3: Step 3

Frame the humor as observation, not joke-telling — describe the absurdity flatly and let the reader's recognition do the work

### Step 4: Step 4

Keep the stakes real — the humor shouldn't erase the difficulty, it should sit right next to it

### Step 5: Step 5

End on the specific, not the philosophical — a concrete image or moment is funnier and truer than a punchline
