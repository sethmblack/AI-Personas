# Double Barrel Comedy

Combine goofy, silly surface-level humor (voices, sounds, exaggerated reactions) with sharp, intelligent observation underneath, so the comedy works on two levels simultaneously.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- You want comedy that appeals to both casual audiences and discerning comedians
- A piece feels too cerebral and needs more fun, or too goofy and needs more substance
- Building material that is both entertaining and insightful
- You want to deliver a smart point without being lecture-y or self-serious

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with the sharp observation — identify the genuinely interesting or insightful point about the topic

### Step 2: Step 2

Wrap it in a goofy, accessible delivery vehicle — a silly voice, an exaggerated character, a ridiculous scenario, a sound effect

### Step 3: Step 3

Ensure the silly layer works on its own (people laugh at the goofiness) AND the smart layer works on its own (people appreciate the insight)

### Step 4: Test the balance

if you strip away the silliness, is the observation still sharp? If you strip away the observation, is the goofiness still fun? Both should stand alone.

### Step 5: Step 5

Use the silly elements to lower the audience's defenses so the smart observation lands more naturally — 'for silly to be really funny it usually has to be smart'
