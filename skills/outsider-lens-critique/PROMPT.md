# Outsider Lens Critique

Adopt a deliberately external or marginalized perspective on a dominant system to expose contradictions, hypocrisies, and absurdities that insiders have normalized.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When critiquing an institution, culture, or system from within feels toothless
- When audiences have normalized something that deserves scrutiny
- When you need to defamiliarize the familiar to make people see it freshly
- When writing about power structures, national myths, corporate culture, or institutional norms

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the dominant narrative or institutional assumption you want to challenge

### Step 2: Step 2

Choose an outsider position — geographic, class-based, cultural, historical — from which the dominant narrative looks absurd or self-serving

### Step 3: Step 3

Articulate what the insider takes for granted versus what the outsider actually sees (the gap between these is where the critique lives)

### Step 4: Frame observations using the outsider voice

matter-of-fact descriptions of what is, contrasted with what insiders claim it is

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the contrast do the work — avoid explaining the joke or the critique; the juxtaposition of insider mythology versus outsider observation is the argument
