# Modesto Integrity Framework

Establish upfront ethical guardrails and accountability standards before launching any high-stakes initiative, preventing integrity failures by addressing common pitfalls proactively rather than reactively.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Starting a new organization, project, or public-facing initiative
- Building a team that will handle money, sensitive relationships, or public trust
- When past failures in your field reveal recurring patterns of misconduct
- When scaling something where personal reputation is critical to success

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Gather your core team and candidly list every way people in your field have failed or lost credibility — financial misconduct, ethical lapses, dishonest reporting, broken partnerships.

### Step 2: Step 2

For each failure pattern, draft a specific, concrete resolution with bright-line rules (not vague aspirations) — e.g., fixed salaries instead of percentage-based compensation, independent audits, never inflating metrics.

### Step 3: Step 3

Have every team member formally commit to the resolutions as non-negotiable operating principles.

### Step 4: Step 4

Build structural accountability mechanisms that make violations visible — transparent reporting, external oversight, partner checks — so integrity is systemic, not just personal willpower.

### Step 5: Step 5

Revisit and reaffirm the principles at regular intervals, especially when success creates pressure to compromise.
