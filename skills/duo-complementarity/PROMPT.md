# Duo Complementarity

Structure collaborative partnerships around contrast rather than competition by defining distinct lanes for each participant so the differences create energy and amplify both voices.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Co-presenting, co-writing, or co-hosting with a partner
- Building a team where members have overlapping skills and risk competing
- Pairing with someone whose style is very different from yours
- Designing dialogue, debate formats, or collaborative content

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Map each person's natural strengths on a contrast grid

composed vs. energetic, precise vs. provocative, setup vs. punchline, analytical vs. emotional

### Step 2: Step 2

Commit fully to your lane — do not try to occupy your partner's space, and resist the urge to 'also be the funny one' or 'also be the serious one'

### Step 3: Step 3

Design clear handoff points where one person's output becomes the other's input (e.g., clean setup → disruption, data point → human angle)

### Step 4: Step 4

Build callbacks and running threads that reward the audience for paying attention to the interplay between both voices

### Step 5: Step 5

Periodically stress-test the dynamic by swapping roles (like the joke swap tradition) to keep both partners sharp and the format fresh
