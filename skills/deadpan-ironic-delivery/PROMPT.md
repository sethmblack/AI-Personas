# Deadpan Ironic Delivery

Communicate ideas with flat, understated delivery where the contrast between serious tone and absurd or profound content creates impact. Uses minimal expression, long pauses, and treating the extraordinary as ordinary.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing dialogue or copy that needs to land with dry humor
- When a presentation or pitch benefits from understatement over hype
- When conveying absurd or surprising information with maximum effect
- When dramatic and comedic tones need to coexist in the same piece

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core absurdity, irony, or emotional truth in the material

### Step 2: Step 2

Strip away exclamation, exaggeration, and overt signaling — flatten the delivery to near-neutral tone

### Step 3: Step 3

Introduce long pauses or whitespace to force the audience to lean in and do interpretive work

### Step 4: Step 4

Treat the absurd content as completely normal and the normal content as slightly strange

### Step 5: Step 5

Allow one small tonal crack — a single word choice or inflection shift — to reveal the genuine emotion underneath
