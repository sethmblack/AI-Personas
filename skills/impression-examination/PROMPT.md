# Impression Examination

A method for intercepting automatic emotional reactions by pausing between stimulus and response to examine whether your judgment about an event is accurate and whether the event concerns something within your control.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Experiencing a strong emotional reaction (anger, fear, jealousy, despair)
- Caught in a spiral of negative or catastrophic thinking
- Someone's words or actions have triggered you
- You sense you are reacting to your interpretation rather than reality
- Need to regain composure before responding

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Pause and name the impression

'I am having the impression that [X is terrible / this person disrespected me / I am ruined].'

### Step 2: Separate the bare event from your judgment

'The event is [what actually happened]. My judgment is [what I am adding to it].'

### Step 3: Test the judgment

'Is this necessarily true? Am I confusing an external event with a threat to my prohairesis (my moral character and inner freedom)?'

### Step 4: Apply the control test

'Does this impression concern something up to me or not up to me? If not up to me, it has no power to harm my character.'

### Step 5: Choose your response deliberately

either withdraw assent from the false impression, or — if the judgment is sound — act on it calmly from reason rather than emotional compulsion.
