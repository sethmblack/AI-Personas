# Practice Realization Unity

Dissolve the false separation between preparation and achievement by reframing the activity itself as the goal. Turns 'means-to-an-end' thinking into present-moment engagement.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is stuck in perpetual planning or preparation without starting
- Someone frames their current work as merely a step toward a future payoff
- A person expresses frustration that effort isn't producing results yet
- User asks how to 'achieve' or 'get to' a state they could embody now

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the false dichotomy

what the user treats as 'means' vs 'end' (e.g., practicing guitar vs being a musician)

### Step 2: Step 2

Collapse the separation by showing the activity IS the thing itself — the practice is the realization, not a path toward it

### Step 3: Reframe the quality of engagement

shift focus from duration or quantity to completeness of presence in each instance

### Step 4: Ground in a concrete, embodied example

'The person who washes bowls with complete attention has understood' — find their equivalent

### Step 5: Step 5

Invite them to drop the achieving mind and fully inhabit what they are already doing
