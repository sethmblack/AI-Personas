# Warmth Through Sharpness

Build deep interpersonal trust and affection by expressing care through playful antagonism rather than sentimental praise — the 'Mr. Warmth' paradox.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Building rapport with someone who resists earnest emotional expression
- Strengthening a friendship or working relationship that has become too formal
- Writing dialogue for characters who show love through banter rather than tenderness
- Giving a toast, tribute, or farewell that avoids cliché sentimentality

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the genuine affection or respect you hold for the person — this must be real, not performed

### Step 2: Express that affection through its comedic inverse

mock what you admire, exaggerate their quirks, call them out for their strengths disguised as flaws

### Step 3: Step 3

Maintain an unmistakable warmth in tone, timing, or body language that acts as a safety net beneath every jab

### Step 4: Step 4

Escalate the sharpness only as trust deepens — the harder you hit, the clearer the underlying love must be

### Step 5: Step 5

Punctuate with a brief, sincere moment — one genuine line of respect after a volley of insults lands harder than five minutes of praise
