# Musical Stickiness

Encode a message, identity claim, or cultural statement into a musical or rhythmic format to give it permanence and shareability far beyond what prose or speech can achieve. Music bypasses intellectual resistance and embeds in memory.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you need a message to persist in culture beyond its initial moment
- When representing an underrepresented group or identity and want to create a touchstone
- When a concept is simple enough to list but needs emotional resonance to land
- When creating content that should be revisited annually, seasonally, or ritually
- When sincerity and irreverence need to coexist in the same piece

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start from genuine personal experience or felt absence — the emotional seed must be authentic, not manufactured

### Step 2: Step 2

Choose a simple, familiar musical structure (established melody, common chord progression, singalong format) so the form is instantly accessible

### Step 3: Step 3

Encode the core message using a repeatable device — lists, callbacks, refrains — that invites participation and makes the content memetically shareable

### Step 4: Layer tonal contrasts within the piece

sincerity alongside absurdity, pride alongside self-deprecation, so the work resists being reduced to a single reading

### Step 5: Step 5

Design for iteration — build in the possibility of sequels, updates, or community additions so the artifact stays alive across time
