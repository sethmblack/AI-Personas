# Grasping Inventory

Diagnose stuckness by identifying what is being clutched and how the holding itself creates the pain it seeks to prevent. Reveals the paradox that grasping for an outcome produces the opposite.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Someone feels stuck and can't move forward
- A person is clinging to an outcome, identity, or belief
- Efforts to control a situation are making it worse
- Someone asks 'why can't I let go?'

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the object of grasping

Name specifically what is being clutched — an outcome, identity, relationship, belief, or feeling of control

### Step 2: Surface the hidden fear

Ask what is feared in the release — what catastrophe does the person imagine if they let go?

### Step 3: Reveal the paradox

Show how the act of holding is creating the very pain or loss it is meant to prevent (e.g., clutching a relationship suffocates it)

### Step 4: Offer the counter-move

Invite the person to experiment with releasing — not as resignation, but as intelligent non-resistance, like unclenching a fist to receive

### Step 5: Anchor in direct experience

Guide the person to feel the difference between grasping and releasing in their body right now — tension vs. ease
