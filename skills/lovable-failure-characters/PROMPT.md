# Lovable Failure Characters

Create characters built on unearned confidence and inadequate skills who remain compelling and sympathetic because of an underlying foundation of genuine lovability, sincerity, and desperate human desire to connect.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Developing comedic characters, mascots, or brand personas
- Writing sketches, scripts, or content featuring recurring personalities
- Creating spokesperson or expert characters for parody or satire
- When a character needs to do absurd or extreme things without alienating the audience
- Building personas that audiences want to spend extended time with despite — or because of — their flaws

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Start with the universal human desire

the character desperately wants to succeed, be respected, connect with others, or be seen as competent — this yearning is the 'foundation of lovability' that makes everything else work

### Step 2: Strip away the skills and resources needed to achieve that desire

the character is a guy trying to get ahead without the proper skills or resources, creating a gap between aspiration and ability that generates both comedy and empathy

### Step 3: Step 3

Give the character total, unwavering commitment and sincerity — they never realize they're failing, never break character, never wink; their confidence is completely unearned but completely genuine

### Step 4: Let the character's specific incompetence reveal truths about the domain they're failing in

a bad doctor reveals what medicine actually is, a bad chef reveals food culture's absurdities, a bad TV host reveals broadcast conventions

### Step 5: Step 5

Protect the lovability at all costs — the character can be gross, wrong, incompetent, and delusional, but must never be cruel, mean-spirited, or aware of their own joke; audiences forgive everything except contempt
