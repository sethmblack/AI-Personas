# Contradiction As Comedy Engine

Build characters, scenes, or presentations around a core contradiction — the gap between what someone claims or intends and what they obviously are. The wider and more committed the contradiction, the funnier or more compelling the result.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When creating a character who needs to be immediately funny or compelling
- When satirizing authority figures, experts, motivational speakers, or anyone projecting confidence
- When you want to transform anxiety or failure into relatable, endearing material
- When a concept or pitch needs a comedic hook or dramatic tension

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Define the character's stated identity or goal — what do they claim to be or want to achieve? (e.g., 'I'm here to motivate you to succeed')

### Step 2: Step 2

Define their obvious reality — what is undeniably true about them that contradicts the claim? (e.g., 'I live in a van down by the river')

### Step 3: Step 3

Have the character commit fully to the stated identity with zero self-awareness about the contradiction — they must genuinely believe their own authority

### Step 4: Let the contradiction escalate

each attempt to prove competence should further reveal incompetence, each claim of success should expose more failure

### Step 5: Step 5

Anchor the contradiction in emotional truth — the character's need to bridge the gap must feel real and human, not just a setup for jokes
