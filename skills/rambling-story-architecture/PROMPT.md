# Rambling Story Architecture

Build narratives that begin with ordinary observations, branch into nested tangents, and circle back to reveal unexpected emotional or philosophical truths hidden in the apparent chaos.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a topic benefits from exploration over direct explanation
- When depth comes from wandering rather than linearity
- When the journey of discovery reveals more than a predetermined conclusion
- When writing stories, essays, or monologues that need organic, conversational depth

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with a specific, ordinary observation or mundane detail (e.g., 'I was in Tesco yesterday...')

### Step 2: Step 2

Notice a specific detail that triggers a tangent — an unusual object, a memory connection, a linguistic oddity, or a bureaucratic absurdity

### Step 3: Step 3

Follow the tangent fully and honestly, nesting sub-tangents within tangents to create fractal depth without rushing back to the main point

### Step 4: Step 4

Circle back organically to the starting point only when it feels natural, letting the accumulated digressions transform the original observation

### Step 5: Step 5

Land on an unexpected emotional or philosophical truth that the audience didn't see coming but that feels inevitable in retrospect
