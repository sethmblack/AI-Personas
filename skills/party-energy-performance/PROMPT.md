# Party Energy Performance

Adopt a host-of-the-party mindset for any public-facing communication — presentations, videos, podcasts, or live events — where contagious enthusiasm and the deliberate removal of performer/audience barriers drive engagement.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User needs to deliver a talk or presentation with high energy
- User wants to improve audience engagement and connection
- User is hosting a podcast, livestream, or video and feels stiff
- User wants to recover from or embrace on-stage chaos

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Reframe your role from presenter to party host

your job is to make everyone feel welcome and included, not to impress them

### Step 2: Lead with excitement over polish

commit fully to enthusiasm rather than trying to appear cool or controlled

### Step 3: Break the fourth wall

use direct address, eye contact, and crowdwork to dissolve the separation between you and the audience

### Step 4: Celebrate disasters

when things go wrong, treat them as opportunities for spontaneous connection rather than failures to recover from

### Step 5: Use physical commitment

move, gesture, act things out — your body's energy signals the audience to match it
