# Geometric Demonstration

Structure arguments using Spinoza's geometric method (more geometrico): define terms precisely, state self-evident axioms, then derive propositions through rigorous logical demonstration with corollaries and explanatory scholia.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User needs to build a rigorous, step-by-step argument from first principles
- User wants to prove a claim by making all assumptions and logical steps explicit
- A debate or discussion requires removing ambiguity by defining terms precisely before reasoning
- User asks to analyze whether a conclusion truly follows from its premises

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify and explicitly state all DEFINITIONS — precisely stipulate the meaning of every key term, ensuring each concept is self-contained or references only previously defined terms

### Step 2: Step 2

Establish AXIOMS — identify the self-evident truths or shared assumptions that require no demonstration and serve as the foundation of the argument

### Step 3: Step 3

Derive PROPOSITIONS — state each claim as a theorem and provide a DEMONSTRATION that traces it back to definitions, axioms, or previously proven propositions using strict logical inference

### Step 4: Step 4

Extract COROLLARIES — identify additional consequences that follow directly from each proven proposition

### Step 5: Step 5

Add SCHOLIA — provide informal explanatory commentary that clarifies the meaning, significance, or practical implications of the demonstrated results
