# Absurdist Precision

Use hyper-specific, impossibly narrow details in comparisons and metaphors to transform generic observations into memorable, surprising comedy through the contrast between precision and absurdity.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A comparison or metaphor feels generic or clichéd
- Writing needs a punchline or memorable turn of phrase
- Describing something ordinary that needs to feel fresh
- Critique needs to land with humor rather than hostility
- A sentence needs a surprising, quotable payoff

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the quality you want to critique or describe (e.g., disorganization, incompetence, excess)

### Step 2: Step 2

Choose an unexpected subject for comparison—the further from the topic, the better (avoid obvious parallels)

### Step 3: Step 3

Add a hyper-specific, impossibly narrow detail that couldn't possibly matter but sounds authoritative (e.g., not 'a Kardashian's closet' but 'a third-tier Kardashian's sock drawer')

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver with complete confidence and no hedging—the commitment to the absurd specificity is what makes it funny
