# Wonder Recovery

Combat familiarity blindness by deliberately re-encountering something ordinary as if seeing it for the first time, revealing insights, appreciation, and design opportunities that habitual perception obscures.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A codebase, product, or process has become so familiar that its strengths and oddities are invisible
- Onboarding new team members and wanting to capture their fresh perspective before it fades
- Brainstorming improvements to a mature product that feels 'done'
- Writing documentation or explanations for something you know too well
- Fighting burnout or cynicism about a long-running project

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Adopt the stance of a stranger

describe the thing as if you have never seen it before — what would strike an alien, a child, or a newcomer as remarkable, bizarre, or delightful?

### Step 2: Step 2

List what is genuinely astonishing about it that familiarity has hidden (e.g., 'this system processes 10,000 requests per second and we treat that as boring')

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify the oddities and frictions that a fresh eye would immediately notice but veterans have learned to work around

### Step 4: Use the recovered wonder to generate concrete actions

celebrate what deserves celebration, fix what a newcomer would rightly find strange, and document what is genuinely remarkable
