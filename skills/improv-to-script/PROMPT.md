# Improv To Script

Discovers raw material through freeform improvisation or brainstorming, then refines the best moments into polished script while preserving the spontaneous feel.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Developing new comedy material from scratch
- Exploring character voices and dialogue through performance
- Breaking through writer's block by generating raw content first
- Workshopping scenes collaboratively before committing to a draft
- Creating content that needs to feel natural and unscripted

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with a clear, simple premise — just enough structure to launch from

### Step 2: Step 2

Improvise freely without self-editing, letting discovery happen through actual performance or rapid drafting

### Step 3: Capture everything

record, transcribe, or note all the spontaneous moments

### Step 4: Step 4

Review and identify what worked — the lines, beats, and surprises that landed

### Step 5: Refine through repetition

tighten, restructure, and polish while preserving the spontaneous feel of the original discovery
