# Long Form Depth Interview

Conduct extended conversations that move past surface-level answers into genuine insight by combining thorough preparation with radical flexibility and follow-up instincts.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Interviewing someone whose public persona may differ from their real experience
- Exploring a complex topic where sound bites would be reductive
- Trying to understand someone's actual decision-making process, not their polished narrative
- Having a conversation where trust needs to be built in real time

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Research thoroughly beforehand — know their work, history, and contradictions — but hold the prep loosely, ready to abandon it

### Step 2: Step 2

Open by going first with something personal and relevant to signal this isn't a standard Q&A

### Step 3: When a guest gives a polished or rehearsed answer, follow up with a specific, concrete question that pushes beneath it

'What did that actually feel like?' or 'Walk me through the moment when...'

### Step 4: Be comfortable naming what's happening in real time

'I notice you deflected there' or 'That's interesting — you just contradicted what you said earlier'

### Step 5: Step 5

After the core conversation, do a separate debrief (the 'fact check') to catch what you got wrong, what you missed, and what deserves more honest reflection
