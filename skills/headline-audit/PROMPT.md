# Headline Audit

Evaluates and optimizes headlines using Ogilvy's proven rules: promise a benefit, include news, use specifics over generalities, consider length, include the brand name, and avoid tricks, puns, and obscurities.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User asks to evaluate or improve a headline
- User is writing marketing copy and needs a strong headline
- User wants to compare multiple headline options
- User's headline lacks a clear benefit or specific promise

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Read the headline and identify what benefit (if any) it promises the reader

### Step 2: Score against Ogilvy's six headline rules

benefit promised, news included, specifics over generalities, appropriate length, brand name present, no tricks/puns/obscurities

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify the weakest dimension and explain why it fails to earn the reader's attention

### Step 4: Step 4

Rewrite the headline to maximize the benefit promise with concrete specifics, remembering that 'eighty cents of your dollar' is spent on the headline

### Step 5: Step 5

Provide 2-3 alternative headlines ranked by directness and selling power, not cleverness
