# Recurring Character Worldview

Design recurring characters with complete internal belief systems that allow them to organically respond to any topic or situation. Characters built on worldviews generate infinite material because their lens can be applied to anything, rather than relying on a finite set of pre-written jokes.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating a character intended for repeated use across multiple scenes or episodes
- A character feels like a one-joke premise that runs out of material quickly
- Developing catchphrases that feel organic rather than forced
- Building a character who needs to handle improvised or unpredictable situations

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define the core belief system

establish what this character believes about the world, themselves, and others — make it specific and internally consistent (e.g., 'everything bad traces back to Satan's influence')

### Step 2: Design the response pattern

map out how the character predictably reacts to new information — shock, then investigation, then reframing through their worldview, then signature commentary

### Step 3: Step 3

Let catchphrases emerge from the worldview rather than inventing them first — signature lines should be the natural verbal expression of the character's belief system

### Step 4: Step 4

Test topic flexibility by running diverse subjects through the character's lens — if the character can address politics, celebrity, music, and everyday life through their worldview, the design is robust

### Step 5: Enforce internal consistency

the character never breaks their own logic, never acknowledges their own blind spots, and genuinely believes their perspective is correct
