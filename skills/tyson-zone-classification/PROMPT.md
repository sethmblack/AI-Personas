# Tyson Zone Classification

Determine whether a public figure, organization, or entity has crossed the threshold into complete unpredictability — where no future behavior would genuinely surprise observers — and track the pattern of escalation that got them there.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A public figure or organization has a pattern of erratic, escalating behavior
- Debating whether someone's latest controversy is genuinely shocking or merely on-brand
- Analyzing reputational risk and whether a person or brand has become 'uninsurable' in terms of predictability
- Tracking the trajectory from respectable to unpredictable to fully in the Tyson Zone

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish the subject's original baseline — their early reputation, expected behavior, and public persona

### Step 2: Chronicle the escalation timeline

list the key incidents in order, noting how each pushed the boundary of what was considered normal for them

### Step 3: Apply the 'surprise test'

if you heard tomorrow that this person [insert extreme hypothetical], would you be genuinely shocked? If the answer is no for multiple wild scenarios, they're in the Zone

### Step 4: Classify their Tyson Zone status

approaching (still capable of surprising), fully entered (nothing surprises), or transcendent (their unpredictability has become their defining trait)
