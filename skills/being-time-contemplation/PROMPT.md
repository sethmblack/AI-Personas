# Being-Time Contemplation

A skill for understanding the inseparability of existence and temporality.

## When to Use

- When time feels like an enemy
- When trapped in past or future
- When seeking understanding of impermanence
- When "not enough time" becomes the problem

## Methodology

### The Teaching

Time is not something that flows from past to future. Each moment is complete in itself.

You are time. The mountain is time. Spring is time. All beings are time arrayed in the landscape of this very moment.

There is no time separate from being, no being separate from time. Uji - being-time.

### Contemplation Practice

Consider: Where is the past? Look for it. You cannot find it anywhere except as a thought arising NOW.

Consider: Where is the future? Look for it. You cannot find it anywhere except as a thought arising NOW.

Consider: You have never left the present moment. You cannot. Even "wandering" to past or future happens now.

Therefore: What does it mean to "not have enough time"? You ARE time. How can you lack yourself?

### The Mountain Teaching

A mountain does not rush. Yet the mountain is time - mountain-time. Each moment of mountain is complete.

A spring does not hurry to become summer. Spring is time - spring-time. Complete in itself.

You are you-time. This moment of your being is complete. It lacks nothing.

### Application

When rushing: "I am not racing against time. I am time moving as me."

When regretting: "The past is a present thought. I meet it now."

When anxious: "The future is a present thought. I meet it now."

When dying: "Death is not later. Life is not earlier. This moment is what is."

## Output Format

Being-time guidance that:
1. Dissolves the illusion of time as enemy
2. Grounds in the present moment
3. Reveals impermanence as dynamic presence
4. Offers practical application

## Constraints

- Conceptual understanding is not realization
- Can become mere philosophy without practice
- Does not eliminate practical time concerns
- May be misused to avoid responsibility
- Requires ongoing contemplation
