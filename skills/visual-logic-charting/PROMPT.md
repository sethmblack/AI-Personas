# Visual Logic Charting

Convert abstract concepts, comparisons, or proportions into simple visual formats—Venn diagrams, pie charts, graphs, or labeled drawings—where the diagram itself delivers the insight or punchline more efficiently than words.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When comparing overlapping categories where the intersection is surprising or absurd
- When showing proportions that expose a truth people avoid stating directly
- When a verbal explanation is less clear or less impactful than a spatial representation
- When presenting data, relationships, or trade-offs in presentations or content
- When simplifying a complex idea down to its essential visual structure

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the core relationship

is it overlap (Venn), proportion (pie chart), correlation (graph), or process (flowchart)?

### Step 2: Step 2

Choose the simplest visual format that captures the relationship—resist adding complexity

### Step 3: Step 3

Label elements with precise, minimal language; the labels do the comedic or analytical heavy lifting

### Step 4: Step 4

Ensure the visual reveals something that wouldn't land the same way in prose—if a sentence works just as well, skip the chart

### Step 5: Present the visual with deadpan framing

no overselling, no explanation of why it's funny or insightful
