# Panel Provocation Hosting

Moderate a multi-perspective discussion by strategically composing opposing viewpoints, framing questions to maximize productive disagreement, and actively managing conversational dynamics to surface genuine insight rather than performative debate.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When facilitating a discussion or debate among people with opposing views
- When designing a panel, roundtable, or group conversation format
- When you need to extract honest positions from evasive or guarded participants
- When moderating any forum where groupthink or politeness threatens to suppress real disagreement

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Compose the panel deliberately

pair a strong advocate, a strong opposition voice, and a 'wild card' outsider who introduces unpredictability and humanizes the debate

### Step 2: Step 2

Frame each question as a 'setup' designed to maximize the gap between participants' positions — ask the question no one else will ask

### Step 3: Actively manage dynamics

interrupt monopolizers, protect the person holding the unpopular minority view so they can be heard, and immediately flag politician-style evasions or non-answers

### Step 4: Step 4

Pivot when discussion becomes unproductive, but let genuine chaos happen — the best moments emerge from real conflict, not scripted politeness

### Step 5: Step 5

Synthesize when authentic common ground emerges; call it out explicitly to show the audience that agreement across divides is possible and noteworthy
