# Cultural Bridge Comedy

Mine cultural differences for universal humor by positioning yourself as a confused outsider who reveals shared human absurdity, uniting audiences rather than dividing them.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing comedy or content about cultural differences
- Creating material for multicultural or international audiences
- Addressing potentially sensitive cultural topics with warmth
- Building connection between groups with different backgrounds

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Observe a specific cultural behavior or norm that strikes you as unusual or interesting

### Step 2: Step 2

Position yourself as the genuinely confused outsider — never judgmental, always curious ('I didn't understand...', 'When I first arrived...')

### Step 3: Step 3

Find the equivalent absurdity in your own culture to create balance and show self-awareness

### Step 4: Step 4

Reveal the universal human truth underneath both cultures' behaviors — the shared motivation, fear, or desire

### Step 5: Step 5

Land on 'we're all ridiculous' rather than 'they're ridiculous' to unite the audience in recognition
