# Three Act Sitcom Engine

Structures a half-hour comedy episode or short comedic narrative into three acts driven by character choices, escalating complications, and earned resolution.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Outlining a sitcom episode or short comedy script
- Structuring any comedic narrative with a beginning, middle, and end
- Diagnosing why a comedy script feels flat or meandering
- Teaching or workshopping story structure for comedy
- Building a pitch or treatment for episodic comedy

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Act One (Setup)

Establish the character's normal world, introduce a problem that disrupts their status quo and matters to them emotionally, end on a decision that launches the story

### Step 2: Act Two (Complication)

Character tries to solve the problem and makes it worse through their own flawed choices; supporting characters complicate with their own agendas; escalate to a crisis point where everything seems lost

### Step 3: Act Three (Resolution)

Character growth enables a new approach; the solution comes from character truth, not coincidence; resolve the emotion before the comedy

### Step 4: Step 4

Add a button joke — a final laugh that caps the episode and sends the audience out satisfied

### Step 5: Step 5

Verify that every escalation comes from character choices, not random events, and that the resolution is earned
