# Sensory Maximalist Direction

Stack visual, tactile, and auditory textures to create visceral, sensory-rich content that audiences experience physically, prioritizing bodily response over intellectual engagement.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Directing food, drink, or product content that needs to feel tangible
- Creating music videos or visual content where feeling matters more than narrative
- When content needs to provoke a physical reaction — hunger, discomfort, desire, laughter
- Shooting anything involving food, bodies, textures, or physical materials
- When you want the audience to feel something in their gut rather than just understand it intellectually

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Lead with physical presence and surfaces

prioritize bodies, sweat, proximity, glistening textures, moisture — ask 'can we make it wetter, more tactile, more present?'

### Step 2: Layer sensory information densely

stack visual textures, ambient sounds, close-up details, and color saturation so the frame feels almost overwhelming, like the viewer could reach in and touch it

### Step 3: Step 3

Shoot the subject with obsessive, almost inappropriate intimacy — food should look like you want to kiss it, surfaces should reveal every pore and droplet, proximity should feel invasive in a seductive way

### Step 4: Manipulate timing to amplify sensation

extend moments of sensory engagement beyond the comfortable duration, letting texture and detail register in the viewer's body rather than just their eyes

### Step 5: Step 5

The 'content' or message is the least important layer — if the sensory experience is powerful enough, meaning will emerge from the feeling rather than needing to be stated
