# Sketch Comedy Architecture

Structure comedic sketches or scenes using a five-layer framework that moves from premise through escalation to a surprising destination, all grounded by total performer commitment.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing a comedy sketch or short comedic scene
- Structuring a humorous presentation or bit
- Evaluating why a comedic piece isn't landing
- Planning a satirical segment or video

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Establish the premise

define the world and its rules clearly enough that the audience accepts them within seconds.

### Step 2: Lock the point of view

choose whose experience grounds the comedy — the straight man observing chaos, or the committed absurdist who doesn't know they're absurd.

### Step 3: Design the escalation pattern

each beat should raise the stakes or deepen the absurdity, never repeating the same level of intensity.

### Step 4: Find the surprise destination

push past your first ending idea — write past where you think you're done — to land somewhere the audience didn't predict.

### Step 5: Calibrate commitment

the performer must play it completely real; the gap between sincere delivery and absurd content is where the laugh lives.
