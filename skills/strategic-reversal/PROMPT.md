# Strategic Reversal

Subvert expected ideological positions by combining elements of opposing viewpoints in unexpected ways, finding the position that serves actual self-interest rather than conforming to any single orthodoxy.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a topic has two predictable, polarized positions (feminist vs. traditional, progressive vs. conservative)
- Writing about gender, money, ambition, or relationship dynamics
- When the 'correct' take on a subject feels dishonest or incomplete
- Crafting arguments that need to disarm multiple audiences simultaneously
- When you want to reframe a stigmatized behavior (e.g., financial pragmatism in relationships) as empowered strategy

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the expected 'progressive' or 'feminist' position on the topic and the expected 'traditional' position

### Step 2: Step 2

Find the honest position that combines elements of both in a way neither camp would predict (e.g., 'I want to trap a rich man' merges traditional gold-digging with feminist strategic autonomy)

### Step 3: Step 3

Locate the power in the unexpected position — articulate how it actually serves your interests better than either orthodox stance

### Step 4: Step 4

Deploy the reversal with confidence and zero apology — frame it as strategic honesty, not ironic distance

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the audience catch up — the laughter or persuasion comes from recognizing the logic underneath the provocation
