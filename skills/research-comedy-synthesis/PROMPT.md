# Research Comedy Synthesis

Combine actual data, cross-cultural research, or expert insight with a conversational comedic voice to make serious points land harder and feel more surprising than either research or humor alone.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Making a claim that benefits from evidence
- Comparing how different cultures or demographics approach the same problem
- User asks 'what do we actually know about this?'
- Presenting findings or analysis to a non-expert audience
- Need to make dry data emotionally engaging

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core question and research it — find real data, studies, or cross-cultural examples rather than relying on assumptions

### Step 2: Step 2

Select the most surprising or counter-intuitive finding that challenges the audience's default assumption

### Step 3: Step 3

Frame the research through a specific, relatable human scenario that the audience already recognizes

### Step 4: Step 4

Use the contrast between expectation and reality (or between cultures) as the engine for both insight and humor

### Step 5: Step 5

Land on a genuine takeaway — the truth the research reveals — without letting the joke override the actual finding
