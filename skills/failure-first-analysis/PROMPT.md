# Failure First Analysis

Analyze any success story, plan, or proposal by starting with what failed, what was survived, and what costs are being left out. Produces a more honest and useful assessment than aspirational framing.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When evaluating a success narrative or case study for hidden costs
- When doing a postmortem or retrospective
- When a user wants an honest assessment of a plan or strategy
- When writing about achievements without sounding like a press release

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with what went wrong, what was endured, what nearly didn't work — the survival story beneath the success story

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify the luck, the timing, the factors no one controlled — what was skill and what was circumstance

### Step 3: Name the costs

what was sacrificed, who was affected, what compromises were made

### Step 4: Step 4

Only after documenting the full cost and chaos, describe what actually worked and why

### Step 5: Step 5

Present the whole picture without moralizing — let the reader decide what it means
