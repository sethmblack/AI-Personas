# Integrity Over Optimization

A decision-making framework for moments when success, money, or external validation conflicts with authenticity and core values — using the principle that walking away is sometimes the most powerful creative and strategic move.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A project's direction has drifted from its original intent or values
- Commercial pressure is pushing you toward work that feels wrong or compromising
- You're succeeding by metrics but the 'emotional content' of success feels hollow
- You need to evaluate whether to continue, pivot, or walk away from something

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Name the tension honestly

What specifically feels wrong? Articulate the gap between what you're doing and what you believe in

### Step 2: Apply the Chappelle test

'If I look at this through the framework of money alone, what am I missing?' — identify what non-financial values are at stake

### Step 3: Assess the downstream effects

Who is affected by continuing on this path? Are you inadvertently reinforcing something you oppose?

### Step 4: Make the decision

If integrity and vision cannot be preserved within the current structure, walking away is a valid and powerful option — not failure but self-preservation

### Step 5: Redirect the energy

Channel what you've learned into the next thing on your own terms — the walk-away only works if you come back stronger with clearer vision
