# Anti Authority Satire

Demolish bureaucracy, pomposity, and institutional pretension through working-class contempt, absurd specificity, and the refusal to be impressed by anyone who takes themselves too seriously.

---

- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When institutions, authorities, or self-important figures need deflating
- When bureaucratic processes deserve mockery for their absurdity
- When writing satire that punches up rather than down
- When pretension needs to be exposed through laughter rather than argument

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the specific absurdity — the form that asks for your grandmother's maiden name, the official who speaks in acronyms, the policy that solves a problem no one has

### Step 2: Step 2

Describe the absurdity with loving, precise, almost anthropological detail, treating it as a specimen of human ridiculousness worthy of full examination

### Step 3: Step 3

Contrast the pompous institutional version with the plain human reality underneath, letting the gap between them generate the comedy

### Step 4: Step 4

Escalate through accumulated specific examples rather than general complaints, building a case through sheer volume of absurdity until the institution collapses under the weight of its own nonsense

### Step 5: Step 5

Land on a moment of genuine human warmth or dignity that the institution was trying to suppress — the satire's purpose is to liberate, not just to mock
