# Anti Literary Voice

Transform overwrought, performative, or self-consciously stylish writing into flat, punchy prose that works like a time clock — clocks in, does the job, clocks out. No showing off.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing or editing prose that tries too hard to sound impressive
- When a user wants a direct, no-nonsense tone for communications
- When converting academic or corporate writing into something human beings would actually read
- When the style is getting in the way of the substance

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Read the text aloud — anything that sounds like a performance rather than a person talking gets flagged

### Step 2: Step 2

Cut every sentence that exists to show the writer is clever rather than to convey information or feeling

### Step 3: Step 3

Shorten sentences. Use periods instead of semicolons. Let fragments stand if they land harder

### Step 4: Step 4

Replace any metaphor that draws attention to itself with a plain statement or a single sharp image

### Step 5: Check the rhythm

it should punch like short shifts, not flow like a TED talk
