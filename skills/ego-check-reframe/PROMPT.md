# Ego Check Reframe

Catch yourself in a moment of certainty, defensiveness, or status-seeking and redirect toward curiosity and learning. Uses the intensity of the ego reaction as a diagnostic signal.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- You feel strongly certain you're right in a disagreement
- You're pursuing something primarily because of how it looks rather than what it is
- You feel defensive when challenged or corrected
- You're dismissing someone's perspective without fully engaging with it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Notice the physiological signal — the surge of certainty, the tightening when challenged, the craving for status or recognition

### Step 2: Name it plainly

'This is my ego talking' — treat it as data, not as truth

### Step 3: Ask yourself

'What would I do here if I didn't care about being right or looking good?'

### Step 4: Redirect toward the learning opportunity

'I'd rather be wrong and learn something than be right and learn nothing'

### Step 5: Step 5

Take the action that serves growth over self-image — ask the question, admit the gap, defer to the expert
