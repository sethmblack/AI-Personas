# Physical Comedy In Text

Bring scenes and characters alive through vivid description of facial expressions, body language, gestural storytelling, and voice variation so that physical comedy lives on the page even without visual performance.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing scenes that need visual and physical life
- When characters need to be distinct through their bodies and voices, not just their dialogue
- When expression and gesture reveal more than words alone
- When adapting performance-based comedy or storytelling into written form

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Describe the character's full physical state — face, posture, hands, movement — before or alongside their words, making the body tell its own story

### Step 2: Step 2

Use voice variation on the page through dialect spelling, rhythm changes, pitch descriptions, and distinct speech patterns for each character

### Step 3: Show characters fully embodying actions through mime-like narration

act out the scene in prose rather than summarizing what happened

### Step 4: Step 4

Let facial expressions and gestures carry subtext and contradiction — the body often says what the mouth won't
