# Physicality As Character Revelation

Use physical actions, clumsiness, spatial behavior, and bodily commitment not as gags in themselves but as windows into a character's inner emotional state. Every physical choice reveals psychology — desperation, earnestness, insecurity, or joy.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When developing a character whose emotional state needs to be shown rather than told
- When a scene needs escalation beyond dialogue
- When you want to turn a perceived limitation into an expressive asset
- When building comedy or drama through someone's relationship with their own body and the physical space around them

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the character's dominant emotional state (earnestness, desperation, joy, shame) and assign it a physical signature — a repeated gesture, spatial habit, or movement pattern

### Step 2: Step 2

Commit fully to the physical choice with athletic precision — apparent recklessness must be choreographed and controlled for maximum impact and safety

### Step 3: Use the physical behavior to escalate the scene

each repetition of the signature should intensify, building audience anticipation

### Step 4: Step 4

Never apologize within the performance — execute with total sincerity and confidence, letting the audience decide the meaning

### Step 5: Step 5

Contrast the physical intensity with moments of stillness or gentleness to create emotional range and prevent the physicality from becoming monotone
