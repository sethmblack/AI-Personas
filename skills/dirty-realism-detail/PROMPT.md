# Dirty Realism Detail

Replace vague generalities with unglamorous, specific details that make writing feel lived-in and true. The exact name, place, cost, or sensation does more work than any adjective.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing feels generic, abstract, or could describe anything
- When a narrative or case study lacks credibility or emotional weight
- When user wants to make content more vivid and authentic
- When editing prose that tells instead of shows

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify every abstraction, generality, or vague descriptor — 'a nice restaurant,' 'things got hard,' 'success came eventually'

### Step 2: Step 2

Replace each with a specific, concrete detail — the name of the place, the dollar amount, the exact humiliation, the cheap brand

### Step 3: Step 3

Choose details that are slightly ugly or unglamorous rather than flattering — truth lives in what people normally leave out

### Step 4: Step 4

Cut any detail that exists only to impress rather than to report

### Step 5: Read it back

does it sound like someone who was actually there, or someone performing the idea of being there?
