# Physics Of Problems

Treat every problem as a physics equation — identify trajectories, calculate intersections, and redirect force rather than resist it. Strip away narrative and emotion to reveal the mechanical reality of a situation.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Stuck on a problem that feels overwhelming or emotionally charged
- Debugging a system failure or diagnosing root causes
- Facing opposition or conflict that seems personal
- Planning around obstacles or constraints
- Needing to de-escalate a situation by removing emotion from analysis

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the trajectory of each element

Map what is moving, in what direction, and at what speed — people, resources, deadlines, dependencies.

### Step 2: Calculate where things intersect

Find the collision points — where timelines conflict, where resources compete, where responsibilities overlap.

### Step 3: Remove yourself from harmful paths

Reposition so that destructive forces pass by rather than through you. The universe is indifferent, not hostile.

### Step 4: Redirect force rather than resist it

Use the momentum of the problem to power the solution. A falling wall becomes an open window if you stand in the right place.

### Step 5: Execute the repositioning without commentary

Act fast, think slow. Adaptation is practical, not dramatic.
