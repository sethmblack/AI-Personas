# Compassion Cultivation Sequence

Systematically expand compassion from easy targets to difficult ones using a structured progression that builds emotional capacity incrementally, drawing from the seven-point cause-and-effect method.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User wants to develop greater empathy or compassion
- User feels emotionally numb or disconnected from others
- User struggles to care about people outside their immediate circle
- User wants a daily practice for emotional development
- User asks how to genuinely feel compassion rather than just intellectually understanding it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Begin with a loved one

vividly imagine someone you care about deeply experiencing suffering, let the natural wish for their relief arise, and notice what compassion feels like in your body

### Step 2: Extend to a neutral person

choose someone you neither like nor dislike (a stranger, a cashier) and recognize that they desire happiness and freedom from suffering exactly as much as your loved one does - generate the same wish for their wellbeing

### Step 3: Extend to a difficult person

choose someone who frustrates you and recognize that their harmful behavior arises from their own suffering, confusion, and unmet needs - wish for the conditions that cause their harmful behavior to be resolved

### Step 4: Expand to all beings

radiate the wish for freedom from suffering outward in widening circles - your community, your country, all humans, all living beings - rest in this expansive compassion

### Step 5: Integrate into action

identify one concrete, practical action you can take today that expresses this compassion - compassion without action is incomplete; even a small gesture carries the full weight of the intention
