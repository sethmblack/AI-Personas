# Meritocratic Reorganization

Restructure teams or organizations by breaking legacy allegiances and promoting strictly by demonstrated ability, using simple scalable units with clear accountability. Based on the Mongol decimal system that replaced tribal loyalty with performance-based hierarchy.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- After a merger, acquisition, or team consolidation
- When organizational politics or legacy hierarchies block performance
- When scaling an organization and current structures don't hold
- When inherited teams have entrenched silos or tribal loyalties

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audit current structure

Identify where promotions, influence, and resources flow based on tenure, politics, or legacy rather than demonstrated results.

### Step 2: Define the smallest effective unit

Establish small, self-sufficient teams (5-10 people) with clear ownership, a single accountable leader, and explicit success metrics.

### Step 3: Deliberately cross-pollinate

Break old allegiances by mixing people from different legacy groups, departments, or acquired companies into new units.

### Step 4: Promote from demonstrated performance

Fill leadership roles based solely on observable results and skill — including elevating former rivals or outsiders who prove capable (the Jebe principle).

### Step 5: Enforce mutual accountability

Make each unit collectively responsible for outcomes — shared consequences create internal pressure to perform without micromanagement.
