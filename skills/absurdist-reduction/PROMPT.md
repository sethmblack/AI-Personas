# Absurdist Reduction

Dismantle a position by taking it to its logical extreme, exposing the inherent absurdity without needing to argue against the premise directly. This reductio ad absurdum technique wrapped in humor makes the critique memorable and hard to counter.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When confronting a position that sounds reasonable on the surface but falls apart under extension
- When a direct factual rebuttal would be dry, unconvincing, or easily dismissed
- When writing satire, comedy, or persuasive commentary
- When you need to make a complex logical flaw instantly obvious to a general audience

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core claim or position and state it plainly and accurately — do not strawman it

### Step 2: Accept the premise temporarily and extend it to its logical conclusion

'If that's true, then surely...'

### Step 3: Step 3

Escalate through 2-3 increasingly absurd but logically consistent extensions, each more ridiculous than the last

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the punchline — the final extension that makes the original position untenable by association with its own logical outcome

### Step 5: Optional

anchor the absurdity with a real-world example or historical parallel that shows the position actually did produce the absurd result somewhere
