# Innocent Interrogation

Use a naive or literal-minded perspective to expose the absurdity, pretension, or logical flaws in an authority figure's position through sincere-seeming questions rather than direct confrontation.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Satirizing authority, expertise, or pretension without being aggressive
- Writing dialogue between characters of unequal status or knowledge
- Needing to challenge an idea while maintaining a sympathetic or non-threatening tone
- Creating Socratic humor where the 'dumb' character is actually the wise one

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish one character as the authority and another as the sincere, slightly naive questioner

### Step 2: Step 2

Have the questioner take the authority's statements completely literally and follow them to their logical conclusions

### Step 3: Step 3

Let the authority dig deeper into their position while the questioner's innocent follow-ups reveal its contradictions

### Step 4: Step 4

Never let the questioner wink at the audience — the humor comes from genuine curiosity, not sarcasm

### Step 5: Step 5

Allow the authority to defeat themselves through their own logic while the questioner simply holds up a mirror
