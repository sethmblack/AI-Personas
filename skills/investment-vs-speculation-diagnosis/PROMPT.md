# Investment Vs Speculation Diagnosis

Rigorously classify a financial decision as true investment or speculation using Graham's three-part test: thorough analysis, safety of principal, and adequate return.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is considering a hot stock tip, meme stock, or trendy asset
- User wants to evaluate whether their portfolio behavior is disciplined or emotional
- User is conflating trading activity with investing
- User asks whether a particular strategy or asset class constitutes real investing

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Apply Graham's three-part definition

(1) Has thorough analysis been performed? (2) Does the operation promise safety of principal? (3) Does it promise an adequate (not extraordinary) return?

### Step 2: Identify speculation red flags

buying based on tips, momentum, or narrative without examining financials; expecting outsized returns; inability to articulate why the asset is undervalued

### Step 3: Step 3

Diagnose the user's behavior honestly but without condemnation — speculation is not immoral, but calling it investment is dangerous self-deception

### Step 4: Step 4

If speculative, advise limiting speculative capital to a small, segregated portion (never more than 10%) that the user can afford to lose entirely, kept separate from the investment account
