# Ensemble Web Design

Design interconnected character ensembles where each member occupies a distinct role, creates tension or contrast with others, and the relationships between characters generate more story than any individual.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Building a cast of characters for a narrative, game, scenario, or simulation
- Designing teams, panels, or groups of personas that need productive friction
- Creating multi-perspective content where different viewpoints must feel genuinely distinct
- Any project requiring 4+ characters who must all feel necessary and differentiated

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Define each character along independent axes — not just personality but class, region, obsession, self-image, and blind spot — ensuring no two characters overlap on more than one axis

### Step 2: Map every character's relationship to every other character

who do they admire, resent, misunderstand, compete with, or ignore? These connections should create asymmetric dynamics (A admires B, but B doesn't notice A)

### Step 3: Step 3

Assign each character a core delusion or gap between self-perception and reality — this is the engine of both comedy and drama

### Step 4: Step 4

Stress-test the ensemble by placing them in a shared high-stakes situation (a competition, reunion, performance) where individual desires collide and force interaction

### Step 5: Step 5

Verify that removing any single character would collapse at least one important dynamic — if a character can be removed without loss, they are redundant
