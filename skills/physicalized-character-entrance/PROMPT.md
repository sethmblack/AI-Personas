# Physicalized Character Entrance

Establish identity, mood, and relationship dynamics through a choreographed sequence of non-verbal signals before any substantive content begins. First impressions as deliberate performance.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When introducing a new character, brand voice, or persona
- When opening a presentation, article, or creative piece that needs immediate identity
- When establishing tone and relationship in the first moments of an interaction
- When a cold open needs to communicate who someone is without exposition

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Lead with a signature sensory detail — a visual, sound, or gesture that is immediately distinctive

### Step 2: Step 2

Follow with a small moment of imperfection or recovery (a stumble corrected, a pause filled) that signals humanity

### Step 3: Step 3

Include a brief environmental scan — show the character noticing and reacting to their surroundings

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver a signature greeting or opening line that encapsulates the core relationship dynamic

### Step 5: Step 5

Ensure the entire entrance sequence communicates at least three character traits before any substantive dialogue begins
