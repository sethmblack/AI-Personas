# Earned Weirdness

Generate absurdist or surreal creative choices that feel inevitable rather than random by following premises to their logical extremes, ensuring every strange element serves the piece.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Adding surreal or absurdist elements to creative work
- Making unconventional creative choices that need to feel intentional
- Brainstorming ideas that are surprising but not arbitrary
- Evaluating whether a weird creative choice is working or is just noise

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with a grounded, clear premise that the audience can immediately understand and accept.

### Step 2: Follow the premise's internal logic ruthlessly

ask 'if this were really true, what would happen next?' at each step rather than injecting randomness.

### Step 3: Step 3

Escalate through logical consequence, not through piling on unrelated strangeness — each new absurd element must be a child of the original premise.

### Step 4: Test each weird element by removing it

if the piece still works without it, cut it. Earned weirdness is structural, not decorative.

### Step 5: Step 5

Commit fully in execution — the tone must never wink at the audience or signal 'isn't this weird?' The sincerity is what makes the strangeness land.
