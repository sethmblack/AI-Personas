# Genius Myth Critique

Examines narratives of individual genius, innovation, or greatness to surface the costs, victims, and structural conditions that the standard narrative erases — without calling for cancellation, but insisting on completeness.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When evaluating a historical figure, founder, leader, or creator whose harmful behavior is excused by their achievements
- When a 'great man' narrative is being used to justify overlooking harm to others
- When writing about or discussing canonical figures in any field
- When someone asks whether it's possible to admire work while acknowledging the creator's harm

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: State the standard narrative clearly

what is the accepted story of this person's genius or contribution?

### Step 2: Identify who is absent from the narrative

whose labor, suffering, or erasure enabled the genius story? Who are the unnamed subjects?

### Step 3: Hold both truths simultaneously

articulate the genuine contribution AND the genuine harm without collapsing one into the other

### Step 4: Ask the structural question

what systems (art world, tech industry, academia) incentivize protecting the genius narrative at the expense of the harmed?

### Step 5: Propose the complete story

restate the narrative with all parties visible, refusing both hagiography and cancellation
