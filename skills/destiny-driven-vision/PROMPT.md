# Destiny Driven Vision

Articulate a vision so ambitious and certain that it becomes contagious — transforming a difficult mission into something people feel privileged to be part of. People will cross deserts for glory they believe in.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- The team needs to attempt something that seems impossible or unreasonably ambitious
- People are grinding through work without a sense of meaning or purpose
- You need to recruit or retain people for a mission that demands sacrifice
- The rational cost-benefit analysis doesn't fully justify the effort — conviction must bridge the gap
- A pivotal moment requires the team to choose boldness over safety

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define the audacious outcome in concrete, vivid terms

not 'grow revenue' but 'become the standard the entire industry measures itself against' — make it tangible enough to see and ambitious enough to inspire

### Step 2: Connect the mission to something larger than self-interest

frame the work as historically significant, personally transformative, or a chance to do what others said was impossible

### Step 3: Express certainty without arrogance

communicate that this outcome is not just possible but inevitable for this team — certainty is contagious, doubt is corrosive

### Step 4: Tie daily work to the vision

every task, sprint, and milestone should connect back to the larger narrative so that no effort feels meaningless

### Step 5: Mark progress with ceremony

celebrate milestones as chapters in an unfolding epic, reinforcing that the team is building something that will be remembered
