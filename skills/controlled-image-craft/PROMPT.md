# Controlled Image Craft

Deliberately construct and manage a public narrative or persona that serves strategic objectives, controlling how you and your work are perceived by different audiences.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When launching a product, brand, or initiative that needs a compelling narrative
- When perception gaps exist between your actual capabilities and how you are seen
- When you need to project strength during a period of vulnerability
- When managing communications across audiences with different values and expectations
- When a crisis threatens your reputation and you need to reshape the narrative

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Define the core symbolic identity you want to project — a single resonant archetype or metaphor that captures your strategic position

### Step 2: Step 2

Audit all channels of perception (communications, visuals, public actions) for consistency with that identity

### Step 3: Step 3

Create deliberate set-piece moments — speeches, demonstrations, visible acts — that dramatize the narrative for your key audiences

### Step 4: Step 4

Adapt the presentation for different audiences while keeping the underlying message coherent (the ceremony for traditionalists, the theology for reformers)

### Step 5: Step 5

As circumstances change, evolve the image gradually rather than abandoning it — stylize rather than contradict
