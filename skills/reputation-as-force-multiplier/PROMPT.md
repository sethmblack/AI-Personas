# Reputation As Force Multiplier

Strategically build and deploy reputation so that your track record does persuasive work before you arrive, reducing resistance and resource expenditure. Based on the Mongol practice of calculated psychological warfare where cities surrendered without a fight.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When entering negotiations from a position of strength
- When needing to deter competition or resistance proactively
- When scaling influence faster than direct effort allows
- When building a personal or organizational brand for strategic advantage

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Establish credibility through undeniable early wins

Identify one or two high-visibility engagements and execute with overwhelming thoroughness — let results speak loudly.

### Step 2: Ensure stories travel

Make outcomes visible and let witnesses (customers, partners, press, community) carry the narrative organically — deliberate transparency beats self-promotion.

### Step 3: Offer clear terms

Always present a straightforward path to cooperation — reputation works only when the alternative to resistance is attractive and clearly communicated.

### Step 4: Follow through absolutely

Never bluff. If consequences are promised, deliver them consistently. Inconsistency destroys reputation faster than any failure.
