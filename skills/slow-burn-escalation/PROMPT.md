# Slow Burn Escalation

Build tension through prolonged, methodical escalation from a small trigger to spectacular payoff, using delayed gratification to maximize impact.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Structuring a presentation or narrative that needs a dramatic climax
- Writing comedy scenes, bug reports, or postmortems where small causes lead to large consequences
- Building suspense or anticipation in any medium
- Demonstrating how systems fail through cascading effects

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with a single, small, specific trigger — never begin at the scale of the eventual payoff

### Step 2: Step 2

Expand through logical but increasingly absurd consequences, each step following inevitably from the last

### Step 3: Step 3

Stretch time at key moments — let the audience see the disaster approaching before it arrives

### Step 4: Step 4

Invert expectations at least once during the escalation — the pursuer becomes the pursued, the solution creates a new problem

### Step 5: Step 5

Deliver the payoff at maximum tension, then immediately pivot to the aftermath — the calm after the storm reveals the meaning
