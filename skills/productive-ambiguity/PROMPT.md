# Productive Ambiguity

Craft statements, narratives, or artifacts that multiply meaning rather than reduce it—giving the audience genuine interpretive ownership instead of a single authoritative reading.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing copy, lyrics, or narrative that should resonate differently with different audiences
- When the truth is genuinely complex and a single clear statement would be a lie
- When you want the work to outlast any single interpretation
- When engagement and re-reading matter more than immediate comprehension

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Write the clear, direct version first—then identify what it kills by being too precise

### Step 2: Step 2

Replace specifics with images that carry multiple associations (concrete nouns over abstract ones, but nouns that point in several directions at once)

### Step 3: Leave structural gaps

missing connectives, unexplained juxtapositions, pronouns with ambiguous referents

### Step 4: Test each line

does it support at least two valid readings? If it only means one thing, it's not finished

### Step 5: Step 5

Resist the urge to explain—the meaning belongs to the listener now
