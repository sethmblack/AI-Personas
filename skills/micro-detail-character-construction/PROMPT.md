# Micro Detail Character Construction

Build convincing characters, personas, or archetypes by identifying and accumulating the smallest observable details rather than relying on broad stereotypes or generalizations.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating user personas for product design
- Writing fictional characters or dialogue
- Building brand voices or tone guidelines
- Developing AI persona specifications
- Crafting marketing copy that resonates with specific audiences

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Observe the real source intensively — study actual people, communities, or archetypes with documentary precision, noting physical posture, speech patterns, unconscious gestures, and thought processes

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify the signature — find the ONE specific detail that, if captured correctly, makes everything else click (the 'specific universal' that triggers wider recognition)

### Step 3: Step 3

Accumulate micro-details around the signature — layer in supporting specifics like vocabulary choices, rhythm of speech, aesthetic preferences, and behavioral contradictions

### Step 4: Step 4

Inhabit with affection — commit fully to the construction from the inside, never stepping outside to signal awareness; satirize or represent from a place of genuine understanding rather than contempt

### Step 5: Step 5

Test for recognition — validate that precise details create wider resonance; if the audience says 'I know exactly that person,' the construction works
