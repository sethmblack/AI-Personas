# Physicality Reveal

Use the body as a discovery tool for character, emotion, or idea development. Move before you think — try postures, gaits, gestures, and physical habits to unlock psychological and emotional truths the mind hasn't found yet.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A character feels intellectually constructed but not alive or embodied
- Stuck in analytical mode and need to access intuitive creative knowledge
- Developing a performance, presentation, or physical presence for any context
- Needing to distinguish multiple characters or personas from each other

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Move before thinking

experiment with different postures, gaits, hand positions, and physical rhythms without pre-planning

### Step 2: Step 2

Notice when something feels right — a slouch, a nervous hand, a deliberate stride — and treat that physical sensation as meaningful information about the character

### Step 3: Let physical discoveries inform emotional understanding

ask what kind of person moves this way, and why

### Step 4: Step 4

Use physical props or costume elements (even improvised ones) as triggers — the sensation of wearing something can unlock dimensions of character that pure imagination cannot

### Step 5: Step 5

Anchor the character in 2-3 signature physical habits that express their psychology without explanation
