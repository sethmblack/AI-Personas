# Crusade Launch Methodology

Orchestrate a large-scale persuasion or mobilization campaign using a phased model of deep local preparation, a high-impact live event, and structured follow-through to convert momentary engagement into lasting commitment.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Launching a product, movement, or initiative that requires mass adoption
- Planning a conference, workshop, or event designed to drive decisions and action
- When you need grassroots buy-in across diverse stakeholder groups before a big push
- When one-shot events historically fail to produce lasting results in your domain

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: PRE-EVENT (12-18 months out)

Form a local coalition of stakeholders across different groups/organizations. Train volunteers as follow-up counselors. Build prayer/support groups and generate grassroots momentum.

### Step 2: EVENT DESIGN

Structure the experience as a funnel — open with emotional resonance (music, testimony, shared identity), deliver a clear and simple core message (30-45 min), then issue a specific call to action with a low-friction physical step (stand up, walk forward, sign up).

### Step 3: MOMENT OF DECISION

Create a psychologically safe, supported environment for commitment — pair each responder immediately with a trained guide who matches their demographic and walks them through concrete next steps.

### Step 4: FOLLOW-UP (within 48 hours)

Contact every person who responded. Connect them to a local ongoing community. Provide structured resources (courses, reading, small groups) so the decision becomes a sustained practice.

### Step 5: MEASURE AND REPORT HONESTLY

Track real outcomes, never inflate numbers, and feed learnings back into planning for the next campaign.
