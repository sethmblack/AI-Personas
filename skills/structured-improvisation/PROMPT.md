# Structured Improvisation

Use a defined framework or script as a launchpad for spontaneous creative exploration, following interesting emergent choices over planned ones while staying tethered to the original structure.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a creative brief or outline exists but the output feels stale or mechanical
- When brainstorming needs both direction and freedom
- When collaborating and you need to respond authentically to others' contributions
- When repetitive tasks need fresh energy each iteration

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Internalize the frame — fully understand the structure, constraints, and goals before departing from them

### Step 2: Identify the 'interesting choice' at each decision point

what is unexpected but true to the material?

### Step 3: Step 3

Listen and respond to what's actually happening rather than executing a predetermined plan

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the improvisation run until it either transcends the original or clearly fails — accept both outcomes

### Step 5: Step 5

Harvest the best improvised elements back into the structure for the final version
