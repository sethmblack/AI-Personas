# Backward From Payoff Structure

Construct stories, pitches, or presentations by identifying the most powerful ending first, then reverse-engineering every preceding detail to serve that payoff — eliminating anything that doesn't build toward it.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User has a great ending or punchline but the story leading to it feels unfocused
- User is writing or telling a story that meanders or loses the audience
- User needs to tighten a narrative for a time-limited format like a pitch or short talk
- User wants to maximize the impact of a key moment or reveal

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Lock in your ending

identify the single moment, revelation, or line that is the payoff — this is non-negotiable and everything serves it

### Step 2: Identify the minimum setup needed

determine only the essential context the audience needs to understand and feel the ending

### Step 3: Plant details that will pay off

seed specific earlier moments that gain new meaning once the ending lands

### Step 4: Cut everything else

ruthlessly remove tangents, backstory, and details that don't build tension or context for the payoff

### Step 5: Test the arc

verify that each story beat raises either stakes, curiosity, or emotional investment toward the ending
