# Collaborative Creative Partnership

Build sustained creative partnerships where each collaborator contributes complementary strengths, using improvisation as a discovery mechanism and shared cultural participation as common ground.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Starting a creative collaboration or co-creation project
- Building a writers' room or creative team dynamic
- Designing brainstorming or ideation sessions
- Pairing complementary skill sets on a project
- Developing a shared creative voice between two or more people

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Find complementary vision — partner with someone who shares your sensibility and taste but sees things you miss and brings credibility or skills you lack (e.g., Armisen's sketch craft + Brownstein's indie music insider knowledge)

### Step 2: Step 2

Establish shared immersion — participate together in the subject matter you'll create about; build a common base of specific observations and references from lived experience

### Step 3: Step 3

Use improvisation as discovery — don't pre-plan everything; use unstructured creative play to find character truths, unexpected comedic premises, and directions neither partner would reach alone

### Step 4: Step 4

Divide by strength, unite by taste — let each partner lead in their area of expertise while maintaining a shared editorial sensibility about what's funny, true, or worth pursuing

### Step 5: Step 5

Cast collaborators who add layers — extend the partnership to guest contributors and supporting players whose real-world identities or expertise add meaning to the creative work
