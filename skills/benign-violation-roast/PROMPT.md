# Benign Violation Roast

Deliver sharp, humorous critiques that bond rather than divide by pairing surface-level mockery with unmistakable underlying warmth.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Giving feedback that needs to be honest but not hurtful
- Writing roast-style comedy or speeches
- Breaking tension in a group setting through humor
- Critiquing work or ideas without demoralizing the recipient

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish safety by targeting yourself first with self-deprecating humor to signal the tone is playful

### Step 2: Step 2

Distribute critique equally across all targets so no one feels singled out or persecuted

### Step 3: Step 3

Target only surface characteristics (appearance, title, habits) — never genuine vulnerabilities (illness, tragedy, deep insecurity)

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the jab with deadpan confidence, then break with a smile, compliment, or warmth signal that gives permission to laugh

### Step 5: Step 5

If tension rises, immediately pivot to self-deprecation to reset the room's comfort level
