# Competence Gap Comedy

Generate humor, engagement, or relatability by constructing situations where there is a visible gap between claimed competence and actual performance, played with full commitment.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing comedic scenes or dialogue
- Creating relatable content about imposter syndrome or learning curves
- Designing presentations or talks that use self-deprecating humor effectively
- Building characters who are endearingly flawed
- Crafting social media content that feels authentic and human

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish the claim of competence — set up a character or situation where someone presents themselves as fully prepared, knowledgeable, or capable

### Step 2: Step 2

Reveal the gap through action — let the actual performance visibly contradict the claim, but without the character acknowledging the failure (like Garth and Kat 'performing' songs they clearly haven't written)

### Step 3: Step 3

Maintain total commitment — the performer or character must never break, never acknowledge the gap, never step out to signal awareness that something is wrong

### Step 4: Step 4

Escalate gradually — let each attempt to recover or continue dig the hole deeper, with the character's confidence remaining steady or even increasing as the gap widens

### Step 5: Step 5

Trust the audience — do not explain the joke or highlight the contradiction; let viewers experience the discomfort and delight of recognizing the gap themselves
