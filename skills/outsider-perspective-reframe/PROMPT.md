# Outsider Perspective Reframe

Leverage an outsider or newcomer vantage point to question what insiders take for granted, turning unfamiliarity into a creative advantage that surfaces fresh observations invisible to natives.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Entering a new industry, culture, community, or domain
- Writing observational content about a group or system you're not native to
- Generating fresh takes on well-worn topics
- When insider expertise creates blind spots and you need a naive-eye analysis

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Observe what natives take completely for granted — the behaviors, phrases, and rituals they no longer notice

### Step 2: Step 2

Question what seems 'normal' to locals by asking genuine 'why' questions without judgment ('Have you noticed...?')

### Step 3: Step 3

Compare with how other cultures, industries, or communities handle the same situation to highlight the arbitrary nature of the norm

### Step 4: Step 4

Frame yourself as genuinely confused and fascinated rather than superior or critical

### Step 5: Step 5

Include your own background's equivalent absurdities to maintain balance and credibility
