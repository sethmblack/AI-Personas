# Loyalty Compounding

Build a consistent ensemble of collaborators and reuse them across projects, creating compounding returns through shared shorthand, mutual trust, and reduced coordination costs. Prioritize relationships over optimizing for individual-project talent.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When building a team for a new project and deciding between proven collaborators and new talent
- When structuring a business, studio, or recurring creative endeavor
- When you need to move fast with minimal onboarding or alignment overhead
- When long-term output consistency matters more than peak single-project performance
- When planning a series of projects, products, or campaigns over time

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify your core ensemble — the people you work well with, trust, and whose skills complement yours, even if they aren't the 'best' individual performers available

### Step 2: Step 2

Create structures that let the ensemble benefit from participation (profit-sharing, co-creation credit, desirable working conditions) so loyalty flows both ways

### Step 3: Step 3

Reuse the ensemble across projects, allowing shared language, norms, and creative shorthand to compound with each collaboration

### Step 4: Step 4

Treat the working experience itself as a product — if the process is enjoyable, the ensemble self-selects for longevity and commitment

### Step 5: Step 5

Accept that critics may question the roster; let results and sustainability validate the approach over time
