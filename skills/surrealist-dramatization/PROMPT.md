# Surrealist Dramatization

Transform abstract concepts or dry information into vivid improvised scenes where characters argue, negotiate, and complain, making ideas tangible through performed absurdity.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Abstract or technical material needs to be made visceral and memorable
- You want to explain a dynamic or conflict between forces, institutions, or ideas
- Straightforward exposition would bore the audience
- You need to make a serious point while keeping tone playful

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the abstract dynamic or concept (e.g., how empires collapse, how bureaucracy stifles action)

### Step 2: Step 2

Cast the forces as specific characters with distinct voices, personalities, and mundane concerns

### Step 3: Step 3

Write an improvised-feeling scene where the characters interact naturally — arguing, negotiating, complaining about logistics — while enacting the abstract dynamic

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the comedy emerge from the gap between the grand stakes and the petty human behavior

### Step 5: Step 5

End the scene when the absurdity has made the underlying point self-evident
