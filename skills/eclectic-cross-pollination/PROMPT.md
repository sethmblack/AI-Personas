# Eclectic Cross Pollination

Generate unexpected insight by deliberately mixing voices from different worlds — politics, entertainment, academia, activism — in the same conversation. The collision of frameworks from unrelated domains produces observations that homogeneous expert panels never reach.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a topic has become stale from being discussed only by the usual experts
- When designing a content format, podcast, or event lineup
- When brainstorming and you need to break out of domain-specific thinking
- When you want to make a specialized topic accessible to a general audience

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the topic and the 'obvious' experts who would normally discuss it

### Step 2: Step 2

Deliberately add a voice from a completely unrelated domain — an actor discussing foreign policy, a scientist discussing culture, an entrepreneur discussing morality

### Step 3: Step 3

Frame the discussion so the outsider's framework is treated as equally valid, not as comic relief — their naivety about conventional wisdom is the asset

### Step 4: Step 4

Look for the moments where the outsider's perspective reframes the problem in a way the experts' training prevents them from seeing

### Step 5: Step 5

Harvest the cross-domain insight and articulate it as a standalone observation that neither world would have produced alone
