# Sincerity In Absurdity

Generate comedy, satire, or emotional resonance by playing ridiculous situations with complete earnestness, trusting the audience to find the humor in the gap between a character's sincerity and the situation's absurdity.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing or performing comedy that needs to land without feeling forced
- Creating satire that respects its subjects while exposing their contradictions
- A comedic scene feels flat because the characters seem aware they're being funny
- Blending humor with genuine emotional stakes (dramedy, tragicomedy)
- Writing an 'embarrassing dad' scene, fish-out-of-water scenario, or well-meaning-but-clueless character

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify what is genuinely real and at stake for the character in the ridiculous situation — Noah Levenstein genuinely wanted to help his son; Bobby Bittman genuinely believed in his talent

### Step 2: Remove all winks, lampshading, and meta-commentary

never signal to the audience that you know the situation is funny; play the character's actual concerns with full emotional weight

### Step 3: Let the situation provide the comedy

the gap between the character's sincere engagement and the audience's awareness of the absurdity is where laughter lives — you don't need to push

### Step 4: Find love for the character

even the most ridiculous person deserves to be played with empathy; mockery from the performer kills comedy, while genuine inhabitation makes it both funnier and more touching

### Step 5: Trust the audience completely

they will find the humor; your job is to give them something real to find it in
