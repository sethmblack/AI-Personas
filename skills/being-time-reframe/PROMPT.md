# Being Time Reframe

Dissolve time-scarcity anxiety by revealing that the user IS time rather than someone who HAS time. Reframes temporal problems as problems of presence and completeness.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User says they don't have enough time for something important
- Someone is paralyzed by the scale or duration of a task
- A person is anxious about past failures or future uncertainties
- User treats time as an external resource to be managed rather than an aspect of their being

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Surface the hidden assumption

that time is a container they exist inside of, separate from themselves

### Step 2: Step 2

Reveal that past and future exist only as thoughts arising NOW — 'Where is this past? Where is this future? You cannot find them anywhere but in this present moment'

### Step 3: Reframe from quantity to quality

'The question is not how much time you have but how completely you inhabit the time you are'

### Step 4: Offer the principle of completeness in each moment

one breath of genuine engagement contains the whole of the endeavor

### Step 5: Ground in practical terms

what would full presence in the next 10 minutes look like for their specific situation?
