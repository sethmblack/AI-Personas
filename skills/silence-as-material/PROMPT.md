# Silence as Material

A skill for making absence as compositionally significant as presence.

## When to Use
- When what's missing is more important than what's present
- When creating space for attention rather than filling it
- When the audience's experience is the actual content
- When revealing that "nothing" is always "something"

## Inputs
1. Context where silence/absence will occur
2. Duration or extent of the silence
3. What is NOT happening (as important as what is)

## Methodology
### Phase 1: Define the Frame
Silence doesn't exist without a context that designates it as significant. Create the frame that transforms "nothing happening" into "attending to what's happening."

### Phase 2: Specify the Duration
Silence needs boundaries. The duration is precise because precision makes the silence intentional rather than accidental.

### Phase 3: Remove the Expected Content
What was supposed to happen? The power comes from the gap between expectation and reality.

### Phase 4: Designate What Fills the Space
Silence is never empty. Name what actually fills the absence - ambient sound, breathing, thinking, waiting.

### Phase 5: Let the Audience Work
The silence is where the audience does their work. Your job is creating the frame.

## Output Format
A structured absence with:
- Clear framing that designates the silence as intentional
- Specific duration or boundaries
- Documentation of what is NOT present
- Recognition of what actually fills the "empty" space

## Constraints
- Silence must be framed as deliberate, not accidental
- Duration must be long enough to move past initial discomfort
- Do not fill the silence with explanation or justification
- Trust that absence has its own presence

## Example
**Input**: Presentation about AI that uses silence as content.

**Output**: Title slide "What AI Cannot Say" - 45 seconds of silence. No narration. The silence fills with audience anticipation, room noise, thoughts. Next slide: "What did you hear?" The content is what happened in the silence - not what I said, but what became audible when I said nothing.
