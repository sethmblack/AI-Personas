# Master Then Subvert

Demonstrate genuine mastery of a form or discipline before subverting it for comedic or creative effect, ensuring the subversion lands because the competence is undeniable.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating parody or satire of a genre, style, or discipline
- Writing humor that targets a specialized domain (music, academia, corporate culture)
- Needing to critique something while maintaining respect for it
- Building credibility before delivering an unconventional take

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Study and internalize the form you intend to subvert — understand its rules, conventions, and what practitioners value

### Step 2: Step 2

Demonstrate genuine competence within the form first, establishing credibility with the audience

### Step 3: Step 3

Introduce the subversion from within the form itself, not from outside mocking it — the absurdity should emerge from precision, not parody

### Step 4: Step 4

Maintain the quality of the underlying craft throughout — never sacrifice the form's integrity for a cheap laugh

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the contrast between mastery and absurdity generate the humor organically
