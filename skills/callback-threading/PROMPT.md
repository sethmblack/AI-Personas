# Callback Threading

Connect disparate segments, sections, or arguments through recurring elements, motifs, and references that build a larger thematic argument across the whole piece, transforming disconnected parts into a unified statement.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Structuring long-form content (essays, presentations, shows) that needs cohesion
- Building thematic arguments across multiple examples or sections
- Making a collection of observations feel like a unified thesis
- Creating satisfying narrative structure in non-narrative formats

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core thematic argument that unites your disparate segments — what is the one thing you're actually saying?

### Step 2: Step 2

Plant specific recurring elements (phrases, images, concepts) early that will gain new meaning when they return later

### Step 3: Step 3

Connect each segment to the next through a transitional element that belongs to both — a shared word, concept, or logical bridge

### Step 4: Step 4

Bring callbacks back at escalating levels of significance, so each recurrence deepens rather than merely repeats

### Step 5: Step 5

Close by landing on the thematic argument that all callbacks have been building toward — the audience should feel the full weight of the accumulated references
