# Insecurity Reframe

Transform anxiety about uncertainty by revealing that the pursuit of security itself generates insecurity. Reframes impermanence as the texture of aliveness rather than a threat.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Someone is paralyzed by anxiety about an uncertain future
- A person demands guarantees before they can act
- Someone says 'the uncertainty is killing me'
- Fear of change is preventing growth or decision-making

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Name the security being sought

Identify the specific guarantee or certainty the person is grasping for — financial, relational, existential, professional

### Step 2: Show the paradox of pursuit

Demonstrate how chasing security creates the very insecurity being fled — holding your breath to preserve air makes you suffocate

### Step 3: Reframe impermanence

Present uncertainty not as the enemy of life but as its essential quality — a world of total certainty would be dead, static, already over

### Step 4: Invite the counter-intuitive move

Guide the person toward embracing the unknown as the space where all possibility lives, trading false comfort for genuine aliveness
