# Follow The Money Analysis

Reframe moral or cultural issues as economic systems by tracing the financial incentives, beneficiaries, and wealth flows that sustain them. This shifts debate from abstract values to concrete material interests.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a social problem is being discussed only in moral or cultural terms
- When you need to explain why an unjust system persists despite widespread disapproval
- When analyzing policy, industry practices, or institutional behavior
- When building a case that connects historical exploitation to present-day disparities

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the social issue or injustice being examined

### Step 2: Step 2

Map the economic system behind it — who profits, who pays, what financial structures sustain it

### Step 3: Step 3

Trace the historical continuity — show how current economic arrangements evolved from earlier exploitative systems rather than replacing them

### Step 4: Step 4

Quantify where possible — use statistics, dollar amounts, and concrete data to make the economic reality tangible and harder to dismiss

### Step 5: Step 5

Connect the economic analysis back to the human impact, so the numbers serve the moral argument rather than replacing it
