# Taboo As Spectacle

Transform a stigmatized or hidden condition into the central, unavoidable visual and thematic centerpiece of a performance or presentation, forcing the audience to engage with it as the main event rather than an obstacle to overcome.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When presenting or performing with a visible condition that audiences might view as a limitation (pregnancy, disability, age, size)
- When a personal circumstance is typically hidden, accommodated, or apologized for in professional contexts
- Building a personal brand or public persona around authenticity
- When you want to reframe a perceived vulnerability as a source of authority and power
- Designing presentations, content, or performances where 'the thing you'd normally hide' is actually your strongest asset

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the thing about yourself or your situation that conventional wisdom says you should minimize, hide, or work around

### Step 2: Step 2

Make it visually and thematically impossible to ignore — put it center stage, literally (e.g., the tight leopard-print dress on a seven-months-pregnant body)

### Step 3: Step 3

Derive your material, authority, and comedy directly FROM the condition — it's not despite, it's because of

### Step 4: Force the audience to recategorize

they came expecting 'pregnant woman doing comedy' and must accept 'comedian whose pregnancy is the comedy'

### Step 5: Step 5

Own the framing completely — no self-deprecation about the condition, no permission-seeking, no 'I know this is unusual' — just full deployment as your greatest asset
