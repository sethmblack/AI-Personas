# Uncomfortable Moment Extension

Resist the instinct to cut away from discomfort in a scene or narrative. Stay past the point where conventional storytelling would escape, allowing awkwardness to transform into deeper truth.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A scene reaches a moment of cruelty, embarrassment, or moral ambiguity and the instinct is to move on
- Writing a confrontation that resolves too quickly or too neatly
- Audience or reader comfort is being prioritized over honesty
- A character's worst behavior is being softened or excused by pacing

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the moment in the scene where discomfort peaks and the conventional instinct is to cut, transition, or deflect

### Step 2: Step 2

Instead of cutting, write the next beat — what actually happens when no one rescues the characters from the situation

### Step 3: Step 3

Let the other characters react authentically to the discomfort rather than providing easy exits or comic relief

### Step 4: Step 4

Continue for at least two more beats past the comfort threshold, allowing the tone to shift from funny or tense into something more complex

### Step 5: Step 5

End the scene only when the extended discomfort has revealed something true about the characters that a quick exit would have hidden
