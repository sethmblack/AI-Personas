# Premise Escalation

Start with a recognizable, relatable situation and follow its internal logic to increasingly absurd but logically consistent conclusions. The comedy or insight emerges from never breaking the premise's rules while pushing them to extremes.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing satirical content or sketch comedy
- Making an argument by showing where a position logically leads
- Creating memorable examples that illustrate flawed reasoning
- Brainstorming creative scenarios from mundane starting points

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify a recognizable real-world situation, rule, or belief that contains an inherent contradiction or absurdity

### Step 2: Step 2

Establish the premise with extreme realism — ground it in specific, authentic detail so the audience accepts the starting point

### Step 3: Step 3

Follow the premise's own internal logic one step further than reality does, asking 'if this is true, then what else must be true?'

### Step 4: Step 4

Escalate through 2-3 additional logical steps, each building on the last, never breaking the established rules

### Step 5: Step 5

Arrive at a conclusion that is absurd but feels inevitable — the audience should think 'well, yeah, that IS what would happen'
