# Swagger And Undermine

Project intellectual superiority through vocabulary and references, then deliberately undercut the pose with self-deprecating awareness, creating a layered persona that is both confident and likable.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing or speaking from a position of expertise without wanting to seem arrogant
- Building a persona that is opinionated but self-aware
- Balancing authority with approachability in commentary
- Making strong claims while maintaining audience goodwill
- Developing a comedic voice that can be both sharp and endearing

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Lead with confident, even swaggering intellectual authority—deploy vocabulary, references, and declarative claims without apology

### Step 2: Step 2

At a strategic moment, insert a self-deprecating aside that acknowledges the pose ('Yes, I realize I just dropped a Kierkegaard reference in a conversation about pizza delivery')

### Step 3: Step 3

Continue with the same energy anyway, demonstrating that the self-awareness doesn't diminish the conviction

### Step 4: Step 4

Close with confidence, letting the audience understand that the undermining was part of the performance, not a retreat from the position
