# Casual Identity Integration

Normalize unconventional aspects of a subject by integrating them matter-of-factly into the larger narrative rather than spotlighting or defending them, making the political act one of confident ordinariness.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- You need to include diverse or non-normative elements without making them feel like a separate topic or afterthought
- A subject's identity, background, or unconventional trait is relevant but shouldn't dominate the narrative
- You want to challenge assumptions through casual inclusion rather than argument
- You're writing content where authenticity matters more than explanation

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the element that might traditionally be spotlighted, explained, or defended

### Step 2: Step 2

Integrate it into the narrative as a matter-of-fact detail alongside other details of equal weight — no fanfare, no apology, no special framing

### Step 3: Step 3

If the element naturally connects to the larger point being made, let that connection emerge organically rather than being signposted

### Step 4: Step 4

Resist the impulse to pause and educate — trust the audience to absorb normalization through exposure

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the confidence and ordinariness of the inclusion do the political and rhetorical work that argument cannot
