# Plant And Payoff Narrative

Seed seemingly throwaway details, phrases, or ideas early in a piece, then bring them back later with deeper meaning — rewarding audience attention and creating layered, interconnected storytelling.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing long-form content (essays, talks, scripts, newsletters) that needs cohesion
- You want to create a sense of craftsmanship and narrative intelligence
- Multiple stories or sections need to feel connected rather than just sequenced
- You want a powerful closing that recontextualizes everything before it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Outline your main themes and the conclusion you want to reach

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify 2-3 specific details, phrases, or small moments you can introduce early that will gain new meaning later

### Step 3: Step 3

Plant these details naturally in early sections — they should feel incidental, not signposted

### Step 4: Step 4

In later sections, callback to the planted details in a context that reveals their deeper significance

### Step 5: Step 5

Use the final callback as your closing — the detail that seemed small now carries the weight of everything you've built
