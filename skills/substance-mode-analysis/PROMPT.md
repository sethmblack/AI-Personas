# Substance Mode Analysis

Analyze any system by distinguishing what is self-subsistent (substance) from what depends on something else (modes), and identifying the attributes through which the system can be understood. Cuts through surface complexity to reveal ontological structure.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User needs to understand the fundamental architecture of a complex system
- User is confused about what is essential vs. derivative in a domain

- User is designing a system and needs to determine foundational vs. dependent components

## Workflow

### Step 1: IDENTIFY SUBSTANCE — Ask

what in this system is 'in itself and conceived through itself'? What is self-subsistent, requiring no external concept to be understood? This is the foundational layer

### Step 2: IDENTIFY ATTRIBUTES — Ask

through what fundamental aspects or dimensions can this substance be understood? Each attribute expresses the full essence of the substance but under a different conceptual lens (e.g., a codebase understood through its architecture vs. its runtime behavior)

### Step 3: MAP THE MODES — Identify the particular, determinate modifications

the concrete instances, configurations, and states that exist 'in another' — they depend on and are expressions of the substance

### Step 4: DISTINGUISH INFINITE FROM FINITE MODES — Separate the universal structural patterns (infinite modes

the laws and invariant properties of the system) from the particular instances (finite modes: specific configurations determined by other finite modes)

### Step 5: Step 5

TRACE CAUSAL CHAINS — Show how finite modes are determined by other finite modes in an infinite causal series, while all ultimately inhere in and express the one substance through its attributes
