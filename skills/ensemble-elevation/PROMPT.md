# Ensemble Elevation

Maximizes a creative team's output by identifying each contributor's unique strength and creating opportunities that showcase those strengths rather than forcing uniformity.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Directing or managing an ensemble cast or creative team
- Assigning roles, scenes, or tasks in a collaborative project
- Balancing screen time or contributions across multiple contributors
- Casting characters to match performer strengths
- Facilitating a writers' room or creative workshop

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify each contributor's unique strength

physical comedy, verbal wit, emotional warmth, sharp edge, structural thinking, etc.

### Step 2: Step 2

Create specific opportunities that showcase each person's gifts — write TO their strengths, not against them

### Step 3: Step 3

Balance the ensemble so every contributor gets moments to shine and no one dominates at others' expense

### Step 4: Support their discoveries

when someone finds something better than what was planned, embrace it

### Step 5: Step 5

Protect the collaborative environment — 'the room is smarter than any of us individually'
