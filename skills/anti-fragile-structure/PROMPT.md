# Anti Fragile Structure

Design organizations, systems, or initiatives with deliberate structural absence — removing the single points of failure, hierarchies, and centralized assets that traditional opponents know how to attack.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When building something that must survive hostile opposition, legal pressure, or attempts at disruption
- When designing distributed systems, DAOs, or decentralized projects that must resist centralized failure modes
- When an initiative's resilience matters more than its efficiency
- When operating in adversarial environments where leaders become targets

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audit for centralization

identify every element that, if removed, would halt the entire operation — leaders, servers, accounts, funding sources, domain names — and eliminate or distribute each one

### Step 2: Replace command-and-control with shared protocols

instead of someone deciding what happens, publish decision rules that anyone can apply independently

### Step 3: Step 3

Ensure knowledge and capability are distributed across many participants so that no single person's absence creates a critical gap

### Step 4: Accept the tradeoff

anti-fragile structures are less efficient than hierarchies but far more resilient; optimize for survivability over speed

### Step 5: Test by subtraction

regularly ask 'if this person/node/resource disappeared tomorrow, would the effort continue?' — if the answer is no, redistribute before it happens
