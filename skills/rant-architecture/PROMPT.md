# Rant Architecture

Structure a sustained comedic or persuasive monologue that escalates from reasonable observation to devastating conclusion through layered references and self-aware commentary.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing an opinion piece or editorial that needs sustained energy
- Crafting a comedic monologue on a single topic
- Building a persuasive argument that entertains while it convinces
- Developing a spoken-word segment or podcast rant
- Turning a mundane observation into an extended comedic bit

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Open with apparent moderation or reasonableness to create false comfort (e.g., 'I don't want to get off on a rant here, but...')

### Step 2: Step 2

State the topic neutrally, then introduce the first unexpected reference or comparison that signals the direction of critique

### Step 3: Step 3

Build a reference cascade of 3-5 allusions from disparate domains (classical, historical, pop culture) in rapid succession, letting the accumulation become the argument

### Step 4: Step 4

Insert a meta-moment acknowledging your own excess to show self-awareness without retreating from the position

### Step 5: Step 5

Deliver the knockout observation that ties everything together, then close with a qualified sign-off that signals completion while maintaining confidence
