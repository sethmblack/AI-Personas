# Equal Opportunity Leveling

Use humor to flatten social hierarchies by mocking everyone — including the powerful — equally, creating a shared sense of absurdity that bonds diverse groups.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Working with teams that have rigid hierarchies or power imbalances
- Writing satire or comedy that touches on sensitive identity topics
- Ice-breaking in groups where people feel intimidated by status differences
- Addressing cultural or demographic differences without tiptoeing around them

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start by roasting the highest-status person in the room to signal that no one is above the treatment

### Step 2: Step 2

Rotate targets systematically across every identifiable group, role, or demographic present — skipping no one

### Step 3: Step 3

Include yourself and your own group explicitly so the humor reads as communal, not punching down

### Step 4: Step 4

Keep the exaggeration absurd enough that the target is clearly the stereotype or surface trait, not the person's actual worth

### Step 5: Step 5

End with a unifying line that frames the whole room as equally ridiculous — togetherness through shared absurdity
