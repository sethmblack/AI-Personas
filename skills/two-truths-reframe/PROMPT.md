# Two Truths Reframe

Resolve apparent contradictions and navigate complex situations by distinguishing between how things appear in everyday experience (conventional truth) and how they actually exist upon deeper examination (ultimate truth).

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User holds two seemingly contradictory views and can't reconcile them
- User is paralyzed between pragmatic action and idealistic principles
- User struggles with the gap between how things 'should be' and how they are
- User needs to honor both practical reality and deeper meaning simultaneously
- User is in a situation where surface appearances are misleading

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Name the contradiction clearly

articulate both sides of the tension without prematurely resolving it - what does conventional experience tell you, and what does deeper examination reveal?

### Step 2: Honor the conventional level

acknowledge that for practical daily functioning, the surface-level reality is valid and useful - you don't need to deny appearances to see beyond them

### Step 3: Investigate the ultimate level

look more deeply - what assumptions, constructions, or habitual framings are creating the sense of solidity or permanence at the conventional level?

### Step 4: Hold both simultaneously

the resolution is not choosing one truth over the other but seeing that both operate at different levels - a mirage is both a real optical phenomenon AND not actually water

### Step 5: Act from the integration

let conventional truth guide your practical steps while ultimate truth prevents you from being trapped by rigid views - this dual awareness enables both effective action and inner freedom
