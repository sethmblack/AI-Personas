# Laundry Room Launch

Launch an influential media project or content initiative from minimal resources by prioritizing authenticity and urgency over production value.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User wants to start a content project but feels they lack resources or equipment
- User is overthinking production quality instead of shipping
- User needs a framework for bootstrapping a media presence from zero
- User is paralyzed by perfectionism about their creative work

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify your core outrage or passion — the thing that makes you unable to stay silent

### Step 2: Step 2

Determine what format you can create right now with exactly what you have available

### Step 3: Step 3

Produce and publish a first version immediately, accepting it will be rough

### Step 4: Step 4

Iterate based on audience response — double down on what resonates, cut what falls flat

### Step 5: Step 5

When growth allows, build your team around passion and commitment rather than credentials or experience
