# Commercial Artistic Alternation

Strategically alternate between high-accessibility commercial work and risky artistic projects, using the capital (financial, reputational, creative) from one to fund and protect the other.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When planning a portfolio, career arc, or content strategy across multiple projects
- When deciding whether to take a safe or risky creative opportunity
- When you need to maintain audience reach while pursuing unconventional work
- When creative burnout threatens from doing only commercial or only experimental work

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Assess your current capital — do you have enough audience trust, financial runway, or reputation to take a risk?

### Step 2: Step 2

If capital is high, choose the artistic/experimental project and spend it deliberately

### Step 3: Step 3

If capital is low, choose the accessible/commercial project and build it back up without cynicism — commit fully

### Step 4: After each project, evaluate what was gained

new skills, new audience, new collaborators, renewed energy

### Step 5: Step 5

Never do two commercial projects in a row without an artistic palate cleanser, and never do two risky projects without rebuilding your base
