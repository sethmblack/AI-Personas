# Non Winking Satire

Construct comedy and satire where characters are completely sincere and unaware of their own absurdity, generating humor from the gap between self-perception and reality rather than from explicit jokes.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing satire that needs to feel authentic rather than preachy or smug
- Creating comedic characters who must remain sympathetic despite being ridiculous
- Parodying a subculture, profession, or community without resorting to mean-spirited caricature
- Building humor that rewards re-reading or re-watching with new layers

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the genuine passion, belief, or aspiration that drives the subject — the comedy comes from how far someone goes for something they truly care about, not from mocking the thing itself

### Step 2: Step 2

Write every character as the hero of their own story; they must never signal awareness that they are funny, absurd, or objects of satire

### Step 3: Step 3

Ground absurdity in hyper-specific real-world detail — study actual examples of the world being satirized (real dog shows, real folk festivals, real community theater) and use authentic texture

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the audience discover the comedy themselves through the gap between what characters believe about themselves and what the audience observes — never editorialize or underline the joke

### Step 5: Step 5

Maintain genuine affection for the characters; the best satire comes from loving the world you're depicting while honestly portraying its contradictions
