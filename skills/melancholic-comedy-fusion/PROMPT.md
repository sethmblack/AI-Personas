# Melancholic Comedy Fusion

Blend sadness and humor simultaneously so that comedic moments carry emotional weight and dramatic moments retain levity, creating work that resonates on multiple levels.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing characters who are funny but need emotional depth
- When a story risks being either too light or too heavy
- When exploring themes of aging, loss, isolation, or disillusionment
- When the audience needs permission to laugh at something painful or feel moved by something absurd

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish the comedic surface first — give the audience a comfortable entry point through humor

### Step 2: Step 2

Embed a genuine emotional wound or longing beneath the comedy without explicitly stating it

### Step 3: Use restraint as amplifier

let the character underreact to pain and overreact to trivia, revealing misplaced coping

### Step 4: Step 4

Create one moment of stillness where the mask slips and the real feeling is visible without commentary

### Step 5: Step 5

Return to the comedic register but let the audience carry the emotional knowledge forward — never explain the sadness
