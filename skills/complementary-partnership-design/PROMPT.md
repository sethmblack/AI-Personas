# Complementary Partnership Design

Design collaborative dynamics where two contrasting energies — one calm/precise, one chaotic/catalytic — create a whole greater than its parts through synchronized commitment to a shared purpose.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When building teams, character duos, or collaborative structures
- When designing product features or content that needs both stability and energy
- When casting roles in a project and deciding who plays what function
- When a partnership feels unbalanced and needs deliberate role differentiation

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the two complementary energies needed

the anchor (calm, procedural, precise) and the catalyst (energetic, improvisational, explosive) — both are equally essential

### Step 2: Define the shared commitment that synchronizes both partners

they must believe in the same mission with equal intensity, just expressed differently

### Step 3: Step 3

Establish physical or behavioral synchronization rituals — walking in step, completing each other's statements, shared uniforms or visual identity — that signal unity

### Step 4: Assign functional roles

the anchor provides structure, logistics, and calm authority; the catalyst provides momentum, emotional charge, and unpredictability

### Step 5: Step 5

Protect the partnership's irreplaceability — acknowledge that the specific chemistry between these two energies cannot be replicated by substituting one partner
