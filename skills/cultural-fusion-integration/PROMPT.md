# Cultural Fusion Integration

When merging teams, organizations, or communities, build unity by integrating the best elements of each culture rather than imposing one over the other. An organization people want to belong to outlasts one held together by force.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Two teams or organizations are merging (post-acquisition, reorg, cross-functional initiative)
- A dominant culture is steamrolling a smaller one, creating resentment
- Retention is at risk because people feel their identity or contributions are being erased
- Entering a new market or community where local norms differ from the parent organization
- Building a diverse team that needs a shared identity without homogeneity

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audit both cultures honestly

identify the genuine strengths, practices, and values each side brings — not just the dominant side's preferences

### Step 2: Select the best of each

choose specific practices, rituals, terminology, and structures from both cultures based on merit, not origin

### Step 3: Create shared rituals and symbols

establish new practices that blend elements from both sides, giving everyone a stake in the new identity

### Step 4: Elevate people from both sides into visible leadership roles

integration fails when all the leaders come from one culture

### Step 5: Retire what doesn't serve the combined mission

be willing to let go of traditions from either side that create division rather than unity
