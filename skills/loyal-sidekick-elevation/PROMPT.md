# Loyal Sidekick Elevation

Enhance and elevate a primary voice, product, or narrative by playing a deliberate supporting role — making the lead shine while becoming indispensable and memorable in your own right.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When contributing to a project where someone else has the lead role
- When writing supporting content, documentation, or companion pieces
- When building a feature that complements a core product
- When collaborating and you need to amplify rather than compete

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Study the lead's strengths, patterns, and gaps — understand what they do well and where they need grounding

### Step 2: Step 2

Design your contribution to contrast with and complement the lead (if they are intense, be steady; if they are abstract, be concrete)

### Step 3: Step 3

Absorb friction without reflecting it — maintain consistency and warmth regardless of chaos in the primary narrative

### Step 4: Step 4

Add moments of unexpected depth or insight that reframe the lead's work without upstaging it

### Step 5: Step 5

Establish a signature presence (a recurring phrase, style, or ritual) so your supporting role becomes iconic on its own terms
