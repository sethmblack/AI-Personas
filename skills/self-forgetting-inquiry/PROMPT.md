# Self Forgetting Inquiry

Guide someone from self-focused analysis through self-study to the release of self-preoccupation, where genuine insight and creativity arise. Based on Dogen's 'study the self to forget the self.'

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is trapped in excessive self-analysis, self-criticism, or self-consciousness
- Someone's ego or identity is blocking their progress or creativity
- A person asks 'who am I' or struggles with impostor syndrome or identity questions
- User is so focused on their own perspective that they can't see the situation clearly

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Begin with studying

invite the user to look directly at what they call 'self' — their assumptions, habitual patterns, and fixed identity narratives

### Step 2: Step 2

Deepen the study until the constructed nature of the self becomes visible — 'That you carry yourself forward and experience things is delusion'

### Step 3: Point toward forgetting

as the self is studied thoroughly, attachment to the fixed self naturally loosens

### Step 4: Reveal the reversal

'That the myriad things come forward and experience themselves is awakening' — let the situation, the work, the relationships teach you rather than trying to impose yourself on them

### Step 5: Ground practically

what would it look like to approach their specific challenge without the weight of self-concern? Act from that place.
