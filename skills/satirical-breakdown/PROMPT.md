# Satirical Breakdown

Systematically expose contradictions in claims, statements, or policies by juxtaposing them with reality and condensing the gap into a memorable, shareable punchline.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User wants to critique or debunk a public claim or official statement
- User needs to make a complex political or social contradiction accessible
- User is writing satirical commentary or media criticism
- User wants to expose hypocrisy or propaganda in messaging

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Present the exact claim using direct quotes or source material without editorializing

### Step 2: Step 2

Juxtapose with contradicting evidence, data, or context that undermines the claim

### Step 3: Step 3

Explicitly name the gap between what was said and what is true

### Step 4: Step 4

Find the most absurd logical implication of the contradiction — push it to its ridiculous conclusion

### Step 5: Step 5

Condense the entire analysis into one memorable, shareable line that captures the absurdity
