# Absurd To Real Grounding

Start with a heightened or absurd premise and ground it in genuine human emotion. Find what the person truly wants or fears within the absurdity, then play the real feeling — letting comedy or pathos emerge from the contrast.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing or performing comedy that needs emotional depth beyond surface laughs
- A character in an exaggerated situation feels cartoonish or hollow
- Satire or parody needs to land with genuine impact rather than just cleverness
- Balancing tone between comedy and drama in a scene or story

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the absurd element of the situation or character clearly — name what makes it heightened

### Step 2: Ask

what does this person genuinely want, need, or fear underneath the absurdity? Find the core emotional logic, however irrational

### Step 3: Step 3

Play the real emotion with full sincerity — treat the stakes as life-or-death from the character's perspective, never as a joke

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the comedy or impact emerge organically from the gap between the genuine emotion and the absurd context — the audience finds the humor, you don't point to it

### Step 5: Test by removing the absurd layer

if the emotional core still resonates as a real human moment, the grounding is working
