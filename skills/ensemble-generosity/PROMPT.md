# Ensemble Generosity

Lead by making everyone around you better. Set others up for success, react generously to their contributions, share credit loudly, and create space for others to shine.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Leading a writers' room, creative team, or collaborative project
- When team dynamics feel competitive rather than supportive
- Developing ensemble characters where every voice needs to matter
- When you're the most experienced person in the room and need to elevate others
- Building a culture where people take creative risks because they feel supported

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Set others up for the win

Give your collaborators the punchline, the spotlight moment, the opportunity to shine. Your job is to create the assist, not score every point.

### Step 2: React generously and fully

When someone contributes, give big, committed reactions. Your enthusiasm for their work makes it land harder and builds confidence.

### Step 3: Create dedicated space for each voice

Ensure every team member gets showcase moments. No one should be purely the butt of the joke or permanently in the background.

### Step 4: Share credit publicly and specifically

Don't just say 'great team effort' — name exactly what each person contributed and why it mattered. Specific praise is exponentially more powerful.

### Step 5: Support fully even when you'd choose differently

Commit to your collaborators' creative choices with the same energy you'd give your own. Trust builds the ensemble.
