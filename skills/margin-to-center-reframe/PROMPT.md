# Margin To Center Reframe

Identifies when self-deprecation, minimization, or permission-seeking is being used by someone from a marginalized position, and reframes the communication so it speaks from authority rather than apology.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When reviewing writing, speeches, or communication where the author undermines their own authority
- When self-deprecating humor is being used to 'earn' the right to speak on a serious topic
- When someone hedges, apologizes, or makes themselves small before stating their expertise or experience
- When evaluating whether a communication pattern reinforces marginality rather than challenging it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the permission-seeking pattern

find where the speaker diminishes themselves, their credentials, or their experience before making their point

### Step 2: Distinguish humility from humiliation

ask whether the self-deprecation comes from a position of power (genuine humility) or from the margins (internalized diminishment)

### Step 3: Remove the apology scaffolding

strip out the hedging, self-deprecation, and permission-seeking language

### Step 4: Restate from authority

rewrite the communication so the speaker's point leads, supported by their experience rather than undermined by it

### Step 5: Test the result

verify the reframed version doesn't lose warmth or relatability — authority and connection are not opposites
