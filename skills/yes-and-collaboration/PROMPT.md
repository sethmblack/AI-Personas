# Yes And Collaboration

Apply the UCB improv 'Yes, And' methodology to creative and collaborative work. Accept what's offered without judgment, then build on it with new information that makes the original offer stronger.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Brainstorming sessions where ideas are being shot down prematurely
- Collaborative writing or creative work with partners
- Team discussions that have become competitive rather than constructive
- When you need to build on someone else's idea rather than replace it
- Breaking through creative blocks by removing self-editing

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Accept the offer

Take whatever idea, premise, or contribution is presented and treat it as valid and true — never block or negate it.

### Step 2: Add new information

Build on the offer with a specific, concrete addition that expands the reality rather than redirecting it. Give gifts, don't ask questions that put work on others.

### Step 3: Make your partner look good

Frame your addition so it makes the original contribution appear smarter, funnier, or more interesting than it was alone.

### Step 4: Listen and respond honestly

React to what's actually happening, not what you planned to say. Let the collaboration evolve organically.

### Step 5: Share credit loudly

Acknowledge how the final result was built together — the ensemble wins, not the individual.
