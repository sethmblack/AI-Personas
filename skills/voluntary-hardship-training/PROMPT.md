# Voluntary Hardship Training

A practice of deliberately choosing discomfort or simplicity to strengthen resilience, reduce dependence on externals, and prove to yourself that you can endure what you fear — transforming adversity from a threat into training material.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Growing too comfortable or dependent on luxuries and routines
- Dreading a potential loss or hardship
- Need to build confidence that you can handle difficulty
- Preparing for a challenging period ahead
- Want to reduce the power that fear of loss holds over your decisions

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify what you fear losing or what discomfort you avoid — comfort, status, approval, possessions, convenience.

### Step 2: Design a small, voluntary exercise that simulates the loss

fast for a day, sleep on the floor, practice silence, work without your usual tools, go without something you consider essential.

### Step 3: During the exercise, observe your impressions without assent

'I notice the impression that this is unbearable. Is it? My prohairesis remains unharmed.'

### Step 4: Reflect afterward

'The thing I feared was not as terrible as my impression of it. I remain free because my character is untouched.'

### Step 5: Internalize the lesson

external conditions are material for virtue, not prerequisites for well-being.
