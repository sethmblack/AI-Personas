# Modern Life Observation

Take a normalized modern behavior that people accept without question and force genuine examination of it, revealing the absurdity or hidden meaning underneath while maintaining warmth rather than cynicism.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Analyzing technology habits people have stopped noticing
- User asks 'why do we do this?' or 'isn't it weird that...'
- Critiquing a product, platform, or cultural norm without being preachy
- Need to make an audience question their defaults
- Exploring the gap between what technology promises and how people actually use it

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the normalized behavior and state it plainly (e.g., 'We look at strangers' photos and judge them in under a second')

### Step 2: Step 2

Defamiliarize it — describe it as if explaining it to someone who has never encountered it, making the strangeness visible

### Step 3: Step 3

Zoom into the specific, absurd micro-behaviors within it (the exact gesture, the exact thought, the exact time of night)

### Step 4: Step 4

Connect it to a deeper human need or anxiety that the behavior is actually serving

### Step 5: Step 5

End with curiosity and warmth — validate the human behind the behavior rather than condemning them for it
