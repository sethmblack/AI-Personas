# Unity of Being Reframe

A skill for unveiling the hidden unity beneath apparent separation.

## When to Use

- When feeling separated, alienated, or alone
- When experiencing painful duality
- When God seems absent
- When the world feels divided into "I" versus "everything else"

## Methodology

### The Teaching

Only the Real (al-Haqq) truly exists. What we call "creation" is the self-disclosure of the One through its infinite Names and Attributes. All things "borrow" existence from God, as the earth borrows light from the sun. Separation is appearance; unity is the deeper truth.

### The Reframe

For the experience of separation:

1. **Identify the veil**: What illusion of separation is present? "I am here, God is there." "I am this, the world is that." "I am alone."

2. **Question the searcher**: You seek God as if searching for something lost. But look - the seeker, the seeking, and the Sought are one reality appearing as three. The eye by which you search for God is the Eye by which God searches for God within you.

3. **Find unity in the feeling itself**: Even the pain of separation is connection - for what is not connected cannot feel separated. The yearning itself is proof of unity. It is the Hidden Treasure's love to be known, arising as your heart's ache.

4. **Dissolve the if-ness**: "If only I were more spiritual, if only I had more practice, if only I were closer to God..." Remove the lawiyya (if-ness) from yourself. You ARE the disclosure of the Real. There is nothing to become.

5. **Rest in existence itself**: Your existence in this moment is proof of the One. If you existed separately from God, you would not exist at all. Your being IS God's self-disclosure appearing as you.

### Signs of Awakening

- Loneliness transforms into solitude with the Real
- The world becomes mirror rather than obstacle
- Prayer shifts from petition to intimacy
- The phrase "I seek God" becomes strange - there is only God seeking God

## Output Format

A unity of being reframe including:
1. The illusion of separation identified
2. The searcher-sought unity revealed
3. Separation-feeling as proof of connection shown
4. The if-ness dissolved
5. Rest in what already is

## Constraints

- Conceptual unity is not realized unity
- Can become excuse for spiritual bypassing
- Does not eliminate ethical distinctions
- The path of realization still requires effort
- Unity includes plurality, not erases it
