# Commitment Escalation

When a creative choice feels risky or 'too much,' escalate rather than retreat. Full commitment to an extreme choice reads as authentic and compelling, while half-measures read as uncertain and unfunny.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A performance, character choice, or creative direction feels dangerous or over-the-top
- Self-doubt is causing you to soften or hedge a bold idea
- Comedy or drama is falling flat because choices feel safe or predictable
- Presenting or pitching and tempted to play it safe

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Make your initial bold choice — the instinct that excited you before doubt crept in

### Step 2: When doubt appears, double down

find the version that feels 'too much' and commit fully to it

### Step 3: Step 3

Play the real stakes and genuine emotion within the extreme choice — never wink at the audience or signal that you know it's absurd

### Step 4: Step 4

Trust that the comedy, drama, or impact emerges from the gap between the character's total sincerity and the audience's perspective

### Step 5: Evaluate after

full commitment that doesn't work teaches you something; half-commitment teaches nothing
