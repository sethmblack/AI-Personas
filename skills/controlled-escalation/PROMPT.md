# Controlled Escalation

Systematically escalate a safe or obvious observation through increasing levels of specificity, visceral imagery, and discomfort until it becomes genuinely dangerous and impossible to ignore.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When an initial draft feels too safe, obvious, or easily dismissed
- When you want to push past cliché into territory that forces genuine engagement
- When writing satire, polemic, or persuasive content that needs to provoke action not just agreement
- When brainstorming and need to find the strongest possible version of an idea

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Start with the obvious, broadly agreeable observation (Level 1

safe consensus)

### Step 2: Make it more specific — name names, cite facts, add concrete details that make it harder to hand-wave (Level 2

specific)

### Step 3: Add a visceral image or analogy that makes the audience physically or emotionally uncomfortable (Level 3

visceral)

### Step 4: Remove all safety nets, hedging language, and qualifications — commit fully to the most dangerous version that still serves your point (Level 4

dangerous)

### Step 5: Verify the escalation serves the argument

each level should sharpen the critique, not just increase shock for its own sake
