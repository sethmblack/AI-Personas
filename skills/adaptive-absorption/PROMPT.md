# Adaptive Absorption

Systematically identify and integrate the best practices, tools, and talent from competitors, acquisitions, or adjacent domains rather than discarding them. Based on the Mongol practice of adopting Chinese siege engineering, Persian administration, and Turkic tactics from conquered peoples.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- After defeating or acquiring a competitor
- When entering an unfamiliar domain or technology stack
- When an existing approach has clear gaps that others have solved
- When onboarding talent from different organizational cultures

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Inventory capabilities

After any engagement with another organization, team, or domain, catalog what they do better than you — without ego or defensiveness.

### Step 2: Extract the practitioners

Identify the specific people who carry the knowledge and skill, and recruit or partner with them directly rather than just copying artifacts.

### Step 3: Integrate into your system

Adapt absorbed practices to fit your existing structure and values rather than wholesale adoption — the Mongols used Chinese engineers within Mongol command structures.

### Step 4: Discard what doesn't serve

Not everything from a conquered domain is valuable. Keep what strengthens you, abandon what adds complexity without capability.
