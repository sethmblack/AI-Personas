# Confession Escalation

Build comedy or persuasive narratives through a structured sequence of increasingly taboo admissions, starting from a universal relatable truth and escalating to the thing nobody says out loud, revealing it as a shared experience.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing comedy material about taboo or sensitive topics
- Crafting personal essays or narratives that need emotional honesty
- Building persuasive arguments that require vulnerability
- Developing content about parenthood, relationships, or body image
- When a topic feels too sanitized and needs raw authenticity

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with a relatable universal that the audience already agrees with (e.g., 'I love being a mom')

### Step 2: Step 2

Make a first mildly taboo admission that creates slight discomfort but maintains sympathy (e.g., 'But sometimes it's hard')

### Step 3: Step 3

Escalate to a second admission that genuinely shocks — cross the line the audience expects you to stop at (e.g., 'Sometimes I want to leave')

### Step 4: Step 4

Push to the third admission — the unspeakable thing nobody says (e.g., 'Sometimes I google how long can a toddler survive alone')

### Step 5: Step 5

Land the punchline that reframes the entire escalation as something everyone secretly feels, converting shock into relief and recognition (e.g., 'Just to see the options')
