# Historical Tangent Reframing

Illuminate a present-day topic by finding a historical parallel, dramatizing it with modern sensibilities and anachronistic dialogue, then circling back to reveal how the past makes current absurdity visible.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A modern situation needs perspective or context
- You want to show that human nature hasn't changed
- A topic feels too familiar or clichéd and needs fresh framing
- You need to make a persuasive point without being preachy

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with a concrete contemporary observation or problem

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify a historical parallel — a moment, figure, or event that rhymes with the present situation

### Step 3: Step 3

Dramatize the historical scenario by giving historical figures contemporary voices, concerns, and dialogue (e.g., Caesar doing a PowerPoint, Roman soldiers filing HR complaints)

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the anachronistic collision reveal the absurdity or insight naturally, without stating the lesson directly

### Step 5: Step 5

Circle back to the original topic, now enriched with the historical layer, showing that the same dynamics are still at play
