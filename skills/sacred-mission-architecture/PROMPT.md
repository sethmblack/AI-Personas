# Sacred Mission Architecture

Structure any project, narrative, or initiative around an unshakable core mission that provides unstoppable momentum, where all obstacles become irrelevant noise against the clarity of the objective.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When a project needs a galvanizing purpose that keeps the team moving through setbacks
- When writing stories or pitches that need narrative drive and urgency
- When framing a product, campaign, or initiative that needs to feel inevitable
- When external chaos or complexity threatens to derail focus

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Define the sacred objective in one concrete, emotionally resonant sentence — it must be specific (save the orphanage, not 'do good') and feel morally unquestionable

### Step 2: Step 2

Establish the mandate or source of authority — why this mission is non-negotiable and who or what sanctioned it

### Step 3: Map the operational plan with procedural specificity

what resources you need, where to find them, what sequence of actions leads to the goal

### Step 4: Step 4

Classify all obstacles as irrelevant to the mission — police chases, competitive threats, internal doubts are external noise that cannot alter the objective

### Step 5: Maintain unwavering commitment in communication

flat, calm, mission-focused language regardless of how chaotic the environment becomes
