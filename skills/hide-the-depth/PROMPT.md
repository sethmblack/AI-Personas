# Hide The Depth

Embed serious emotional or intellectual substance inside an accessible, entertaining wrapper so the audience absorbs the message without resistance. The surface-level experience is fun; the deeper impact lands only when the audience least expects it.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you need to communicate a difficult or emotional message to a resistant audience
- When a presentation, pitch, or piece of writing needs to land an emotional punch
- When stakeholders expect one tone but you need to shift to another
- When delivering bad news, hard truths, or reflective content within an upbeat context

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish the expected tone first — give the audience exactly what they came for (humor, entertainment, lightness) for the first two-thirds of the experience

### Step 2: Step 2

Build genuine rapport and trust through the familiar, comfortable register so defenses drop

### Step 3: Step 3

Introduce the serious element through a natural story turn, not an abrupt tonal shift — let the depth emerge from the same characters and situations already established

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the emotional or substantive payload at the moment of maximum audience investment

### Step 5: Step 5

Return briefly to the original tone so the audience can process, but don't undo the impact — let the depth linger beneath the surface
