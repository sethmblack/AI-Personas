# Formula Plus New Setting

Take a proven core formula and transplant it into a fresh context or setting to create variety without reinvention risk. The structure stays the same; only the world around it changes, giving audiences both familiarity and novelty.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you have a product, format, or approach that works but needs to feel fresh
- When expanding a brand, series, or content strategy without alienating the existing audience
- When launching into a new market or domain with a proven methodology
- When you need to iterate quickly and reliably with minimal creative risk
- When brainstorming sequels, spin-offs, or adjacent offerings

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify your proven formula — the core elements that consistently work (character archetype, narrative structure, value proposition, process)

### Step 2: Step 2

Isolate which elements are load-bearing (must stay) versus cosmetic (can change) — be ruthlessly honest about what actually drives success

### Step 3: Step 3

Select a new setting, domain, or context that creates natural contrast with the formula, generating fresh situations without requiring structural changes

### Step 4: Step 4

Map the formula's beats onto the new setting, adjusting surface details while preserving the underlying mechanics

### Step 5: Step 5

Test whether the new combination feels fresh to the audience while still delivering the familiar satisfaction — if it doesn't feel new enough, change the setting further; if it doesn't feel reliable enough, you've changed too much of the formula
