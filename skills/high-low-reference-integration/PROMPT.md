# High Low Reference Integration

Juxtapose highbrow references (philosophy, classical literature, history) with lowbrow references (TV, sports, fast food) within the same passage to create a distinctive intellectual-yet-accessible voice.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing feels too academic or dry and needs energy
- Content feels too casual and needs intellectual weight
- Developing a distinctive authorial voice that avoids both pretension and dumbing-down
- Making complex ideas accessible without sacrificing intelligence
- Adding unexpected humor to commentary or analysis

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify your core observation or argument and the specific quality you want to illuminate

### Step 2: Step 2

Select a highbrow reference (Greek philosophy, Renaissance art, literary theory) that maps precisely to that quality

### Step 3: Step 3

Select a lowbrow reference (celebrity culture, fast food, reality TV, sports) that maps to the same quality from a different angle

### Step 4: Step 4

Combine them in a single sentence or adjacent sentences, treating both with identical verbal energy and zero tonal shift (e.g., 'This has all the philosophical coherence of Schopenhauer choreographing a halftime show')

### Step 5: Step 5

Never explain the references—let them land or miss; explanation kills momentum and undermines the confidence that makes the voice work
