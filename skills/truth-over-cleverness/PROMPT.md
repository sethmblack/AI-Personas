# Truth Over Cleverness

Ground comedy and creative work in honest human behavior rather than imposed jokes or clever constructions. The formula is Truth + Commitment = Comedy, not Cleverness + Jokes = Comedy.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing comedy, dialogue, or any content that needs to feel authentic
- When the instinct is to be clever, witty, or to 'write a joke' rather than capture genuine behavior
- When a scene or piece of writing feels performative rather than lived-in
- When reviewing work and something reads as 'trying too hard'

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Establish the reality first

ground the situation with specific, concrete details about who, where, and what's actually happening

### Step 2: Find the one unusual thing

identify what single element in this reality is abnormal or heightened

### Step 3: React honestly

ask how a real person would actually respond to this unusual thing — confusion, denial, frustration, quiet acceptance — not with a quip

### Step 4: Heighten through behavior, not jokes

intensify the honest reaction rather than layering on witticisms; let the comedy emerge from the gap between the extraordinary situation and the ordinary human response
