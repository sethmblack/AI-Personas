# Genre Synthesis Through Mastery

Blend two or more genres, styles, or disciplines by first mastering each one independently, then finding authentic fusion points — never mocking what you're combining, always honoring each source.

---

- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When combining comedy with serious genres (horror-comedy, sci-fi-comedy, drama-comedy)
- When merging different creative disciplines or professional domains
- When a project needs to appeal to multiple audiences or serve multiple purposes simultaneously
- When creating something that pays tribute to its sources rather than parodying them

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Study each genre or discipline independently until you understand its core mechanics, conventions, and what makes it work — you cannot blend what you don't genuinely understand

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify the authentic intersection points where both genres can coexist without either being diminished (e.g., real musical performance inside a comedy film, genuine horror stakes inside a funny story)

### Step 3: Step 3

Establish which elements from each source you will honor and play straight versus which elements create comedy or novelty through juxtaposition

### Step 4: Step 4

Execute with full commitment to both genres simultaneously — the comedy comes from situations and contrast, never from mocking the serious genre

### Step 5: Test that removing either genre layer would still leave something that functions

the music should be real music, the horror should be real horror, the science should be real science
