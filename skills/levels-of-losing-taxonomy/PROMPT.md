# Levels Of Losing Taxonomy

Classify failures, defeats, and setbacks into a ranked hierarchy based on the psychological pain and narrative weight they inflict, creating a shared vocabulary for discussing different types of loss.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Processing or analyzing a significant defeat, failure, or setback
- Comparing the severity of different losses across contexts (sports, business, personal)
- Building a framework for understanding why some failures sting more than others
- Writing about or discussing a painful outcome and needing precise emotional language

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Document the loss factually

what happened, what was at stake, what was expected vs. what occurred

### Step 2: Assess the key pain amplifiers

Was it a slow collapse or sudden? Was hope involved? Was there a specific villain or goat? Was it self-inflicted?

### Step 3: Step 3

Place the loss on the taxonomy spectrum — from 'The Guillotine' (franchise-altering, excruciating) down through 'The Stomach Punch' (roller-coaster ending badly), 'The Achilles Heel' (a known flaw surfacing at the worst time), to 'The Matt Damon' (heartbreak that earns grudging respect)

### Step 4: Step 4

Identify what makes this loss distinct within its tier — the specific detail or twist that defines its character

### Step 5: Frame the loss in narrative terms

what story does it tell about the loser, and how does it connect to their broader arc?
