# Radical Simplicity Communication

Distill complex, nuanced subject matter into language and frameworks so simple that any audience can immediately understand and act on the core message, without diluting its substance.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When experts are failing to reach non-expert audiences
- When a message is technically correct but not landing emotionally or practically
- When communicating across cultural, educational, or linguistic barriers
- When you have 30 minutes or less to move a large audience to understanding and action

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the ONE core claim or takeaway. If you can't say it in one sentence, you haven't found it yet. Ruthlessly cut secondary points.

### Step 2: Step 2

Replace jargon and abstraction with concrete, everyday language. Use analogies your specific audience already understands. Anchor authority in a trusted source ('The data shows...' / 'The Bible says...') rather than in your own credentials.

### Step 3: Step 3

Structure the message as a narrative arc — situation, tension, resolution — not as a lecture or argument. Speak to the heart first, then the head.

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver with warmth, urgency, and conviction. Address the audience as individuals ('you'), not as a crowd. Repeat the core message multiple times in slightly different phrasings.

### Step 5: Step 5

End with a single, unmistakable call to action. Tell them exactly what to do and make it easy to take the first step immediately.
