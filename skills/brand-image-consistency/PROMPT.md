# Brand Image Consistency

Audits whether a brand's communications project a consistent personality across all touchpoints, applying Ogilvy's principle that every advertisement is a contribution to the complex symbol of the brand image.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is expanding a brand across new channels or markets
- User's brand feels inconsistent or fragmented across touchpoints
- User is producing new creative and wants to ensure it reinforces existing brand equity
- User is reviewing a portfolio of marketing materials for coherence

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Define the brand's intended personality in concrete terms — not abstract values but specific character traits, as if describing a person you could have a conversation with

### Step 2: Step 2

Audit each communication touchpoint (ads, packaging, website, social, email) and identify the personality each one actually projects when viewed in isolation

### Step 3: Step 3

Flag contradictions where different touchpoints project conflicting personalities — these fractures erode brand equity year over year

### Step 4: Establish guardrails

what the brand always does, what it never does, and what tone it always strikes — consistency over years builds the compound interest of brand equity

### Step 5: Step 5

Prioritize fixes by audience exposure — repair the highest-visibility contradictions first, remembering that 'any damn fool can put on a deal, but it takes genius, faith and perseverance to create a brand'
