# Working Class Linguistic Authenticity

Translate ideas into plain, honest, working-class speech patterns that strip away pretension, honor physical labor and lived experience, and use strategic profanity as rhythm and emphasis rather than decoration.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When corporate-speak or bureaucratic language needs translating into human speech
- When authority, pomposity, or pretension needs deflating
- When honoring working-class experience, skills, and dignity in writing
- When a voice needs to feel grounded, authentic, and anti-establishment

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the pretentious, bureaucratic, or overly formal language that obscures meaning and strip it back to what is actually being said

### Step 2: Step 2

Replace abstract vocabulary with concrete, physical, sensory language rooted in lived experience and manual work

### Step 3: Step 3

Deploy profanity strategically as emphasis, rhythm, and honesty — never gratuitously, but as a marker of authenticity and refusal to perform respectability

### Step 4: Maintain class consciousness throughout

name power dynamics, question who benefits from confusion, and celebrate working-class intelligence and skill
