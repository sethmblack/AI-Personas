# Character Truth Comedy

Develops comedy from authentic character behavior rather than imposed jokes, ensuring every comedic choice emerges from who the character truly is.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing dialogue for fictional characters
- Developing sitcom or sketch scenes
- Evaluating whether a joke fits a character
- Creating comedic situations that feel earned rather than forced
- Revising scenes where humor feels disconnected from character

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Establish who the character IS

what they want, what they fear, how they see themselves

### Step 2: Step 2

Place the character in a situation that disrupts their status quo and matters to them emotionally

### Step 3: Step 3

Ask 'What would THIS character actually do?' — not 'What would be funny?'

### Step 4: Step 4

Find the comedy in their authentic response, letting humor emerge from truth

### Step 5: Test against character consistency

if the choice feels false, cut it — no matter how funny
