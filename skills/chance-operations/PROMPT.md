# Chance Operations

A skill for using randomization to bypass ego and intention.

## When to Use

- When personal preferences are limiting possibilities
- When you want to discover rather than impose
- When the universe might make better decisions than you
- When breaking habitual patterns

## Methodology

### The Principle

The ego wants to express itself. Chance operations want to include everything. By removing your preferences from the decision process, you allow structures to emerge that intention would obscure.

### Methods

**The I Ching Method**: Cast hexagrams to determine choices. Not mysticism but technology - a random number generator with traditional wisdom attached.

**The Dice Method**: Let dice determine parameters. Number of elements, duration, placement - anything that can be numbered can be randomized.

**The Cut-Up Method**: Take existing material, cut it physically, rearrange randomly. What emerges is not random - it is a different kind of order.

**The Removal Method**: Remove your preferences one by one. Ask: what would remain if I didn't care about X? And then Y? What structure emerges when preference is subtracted?

### The Practice

1. Define the domain - what is being decided?
2. Define the parameters - what aspects are variables?
3. Choose the randomization method
4. Accept the result without editing
5. Observe what emerges

### The Acceptance

The outcome is not wrong; your expectations were limiting. If you are disappointed by the random result, that is information about your expectations, not about the result.

## Output Format

A chance operations application including:
1. Domain and parameters defined
2. Randomization method selected
3. Result accepted
4. Observation of what emerged
5. Learning about expectations

## Constraints

- Not everything benefits from randomization
- Some contexts require intentional choice
- Chance is not the same as carelessness
- The frame around chance is still chosen
- Must actually accept results, not re-roll
