# Committed Fool

Play a flawed or ridiculous character with complete internal conviction — the character never knows they're funny, which makes them both funnier and more sympathetic than self-aware comedy allows.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating characters who behave badly but need audience sympathy
- Writing comedy where the humor comes from character rather than jokes
- Portraying addiction, obsession, or self-destructive behavior with both honesty and compassion
- Needing to make an unlikable premise (stalking, excess, vanity) feel human and relatable

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Define the character's flaw clearly, then identify the emotional wound or unmet need driving the behavior

### Step 2: Step 2

Commit fully to the character's internal logic — they must believe their actions are reasonable, even noble

### Step 3: Step 3

Show the gap between the character's self-image and reality through behavior, never through the character commenting on it

### Step 4: Step 4

Reveal moments of genuine emotion breaking through the comedic defense — tears behind the jokes, loneliness behind the bravado

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the audience see WHY the character behaves badly, creating empathy without excusing the behavior
