# Topical Angle Finding

Systematically discover unexpected angles on any topic by rejecting the obvious take and searching for perspectives that feel surprising yet true once revealed.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing about a topic that everyone is already covering
- First draft feels predictable or generic
- Need to differentiate commentary from the crowd
- Preparing hot takes or opinion pieces on trending subjects

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the topic and write down the first 3 reactions anyone would have — then discard all of them

### Step 2: Ask

who is being hypocritical, what absurd implication is being ignored, and what would a translation into plain language reveal?

### Step 3: Generate 5-10 alternative angles using reframing techniques

reverse the subject/object, anthropomorphize an element, zoom way in or way out on scale

### Step 4: Apply the truth test

does the angle feel surprising AND true once stated? If it's only surprising, it's a gimmick; if it's only true, it's boring

### Step 5: Step 5

Refine the chosen angle by making the construction invisible — it should sound like a casual observation, not an engineered take
