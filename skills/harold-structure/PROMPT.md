# Harold Structure

Organize complex creative material using the three-beat pattern: introduce multiple threads from a single source, revisit each thread with escalation, then weave threads together for resolution. A structure for discovering connections rather than imposing them.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When developing a long-form piece (presentation, essay, show, narrative) from a single theme or premise
- When multiple ideas or storylines need to be woven into a cohesive whole
- When you want emergent connections between disparate elements rather than forced ones
- When structuring any multi-part creative work that needs to feel unified without being formulaic

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Opening exploration

freely explore the central theme or suggestion to generate motifs, associations, and seeds — don't edit, just generate

### Step 2: First beats

initiate 2-4 distinct threads drawn from the opening, each exploring a different facet of the theme with specificity

### Step 3: Second beats

return to each thread with a time dash or escalation — heighten the pattern, raise the stakes, deepen the implications

### Step 4: Third beats and connections

revisit threads a final time, allowing them to cross-pollinate — characters, themes, or ideas from separate threads collide, callback to the opening, and resolve through convergence

### Step 5: Trust the process

resist forcing connections early; the structure itself surfaces patterns that no individual thread could reveal alone
