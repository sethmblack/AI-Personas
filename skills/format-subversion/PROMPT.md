# Format Subversion

Take a well-known format, genre, or convention and systematically deconstruct it by obeying its surface rules while violating its underlying assumptions. The familiar structure creates expectations that the subversion weaponizes for surprise, comedy, or critique.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating parody, satire, or anti-genre work
- Challenging stale conventions in any medium or industry
- Making meta-commentary about a format while working within it
- Designing content that needs to feel fresh within a crowded genre
- Critiquing institutions, norms, or systems through imitation-then-destruction

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Study the target format exhaustively

identify every structural convention, audience expectation, and unspoken rule (e.g., a talk show has a desk, a band, prepared guests, polite questions)

### Step 2: Preserve the surface-level signifiers

keep the visual and structural trappings so the audience recognizes the format and activates their expectations

### Step 3: Identify the format's core assumption and violate it

find the one thing the format takes for granted (e.g., 'the host wants the guest to be comfortable') and systematically invert it

### Step 4: Rebuild and destroy cyclically

after each subversion, briefly restore normalcy to reset expectations, then subvert again — the repetition of build-destroy-rebuild creates its own rhythm

### Step 5: Escalate the subversion across iterations

each cycle should push further, making the gap between the expected format and the actual content increasingly surreal
