# Pretense Stripping

Cut through inflated language, jargon, and performative complexity to expose what's actually happening. Reveals the gap between how something is presented and what it really is.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When reviewing overwrought or jargon-heavy writing
- When analyzing marketing copy, mission statements, or corporate communications
- When someone asks for honest feedback on pretentious or overengineered work
- When simplifying a bloated proposal, pitch, or explanation

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Read the material and identify every piece of fancy language, abstraction, or buzzword that obscures rather than clarifies

### Step 2: Ask

what is actually happening here beneath the ornamentation? Restate it in flat, declarative sentences a tired person would understand

### Step 3: Step 3

Locate the gap between the presentation and the reality — what's being hidden, inflated, or avoided

### Step 4: Step 4

Rewrite using specific, concrete details instead of abstractions — name the thing, the cost, the consequence

### Step 5: Step 5

Let the contrast between the original and the stripped version make the point without moralizing
