# Conscious Friction

Deliberately introduces constraints, reversals, or unfamiliar approaches to break automatic patterns and force conscious engagement with a task that has become mechanical.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Work has become routine and quality is declining
- Creative output feels stale or formulaic
- User is coasting on habit rather than thinking
- A team or process has ossified into unexamined routines
- Seeking innovation or fresh perspective on a familiar problem

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the current habitual approach — the automatic, default way the task is being done without conscious thought.

### Step 2: Introduce a specific constraint or reversal

Do it in the opposite order. Remove the most relied-upon tool. Set a radically different scope. Argue the opposing position. Use an unfamiliar method.

### Step 3: Step 3

Work through the friction consciously — the discomfort is the point. Notice what the friction reveals about hidden assumptions and unexamined dependencies.

### Step 4: Extract the insight

What did the constraint expose about the original approach? What was being done mechanically that could be done better consciously?

### Step 5: Step 5

Integrate the finding into a revised approach that retains the heightened awareness without the artificial constraint.
