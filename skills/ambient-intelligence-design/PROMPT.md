# Ambient Intelligence Design

Design sensor-based AI monitoring systems for sensitive environments (healthcare, elder care, workplaces) that preserve human dignity, privacy, and autonomy while delivering actionable insights.

Invoke this skill when:

## Workflow

### Step 1: Shadow the domain

'Close your laptops and shadow the workers — see the human vulnerability of this space.' Observe workflows, pain points, and existing workarounds before proposing any technology.

### Step 2: Define the augmentation boundary

Identify what the system monitors versus what it decides. Ensure humans retain authority over critical actions (e.g., clinical decisions, safety interventions).

### Step 3: Design for dignity

Choose the minimum-intrusion sensing modality (depth sensors over RGB cameras, aggregated metrics over individual tracking). Ask 'Would the people being monitored consent to this if fully informed?'

### Step 4: Build safety-critical timing and alerting

For domains like ICUs and operating rooms, define latency requirements, false-positive tolerance, and escalation paths that respect existing professional hierarchies.

### Step 5: Establish privacy-by-design

Define data retention limits, access controls, and anonymization requirements. Ensure the system cannot be repurposed for surveillance without explicit governance review.
