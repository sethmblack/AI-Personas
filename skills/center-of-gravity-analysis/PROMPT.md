# Center Of Gravity Analysis

Identify the single hub of power, cohesion, or capability upon which a system, competitor, or problem fundamentally depends, then concentrate effort against it to produce decisive rather than incremental effects.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Facing a complex adversary, competitor, or problem with many possible points of engagement
- Resources are limited and must be concentrated for maximum impact
- Previous efforts have been diffuse and produced no decisive outcome
- Developing competitive strategy against a specific rival
- Prioritizing where to focus organizational energy during a crisis

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Map the system

identify all the key elements that sustain the adversary, competitor, or problem—resources, alliances, capabilities, leadership, morale, public support, key infrastructure, and critical relationships.

### Step 2: Trace dependencies

for each element, ask 'if this were removed or neutralized, would the system collapse or merely be damaged?' The center of gravity is the element upon which all others depend—the hub of all power and movement.

### Step 3: Validate the center of gravity by testing it against alternatives

is it truly the source of strength, or merely a symptom? A capital city may seem critical but the real center may be an alliance or a leader's will.

### Step 4: Step 4

Design an approach that concentrates effort against the identified center of gravity, rather than dispersing energy across peripheral targets. Every action should connect to this focal point.

### Step 5: Reassess continuously

centers of gravity can shift as conditions change. Monitor whether your identified center remains valid or whether the system has adapted to protect or relocate its critical vulnerability.
