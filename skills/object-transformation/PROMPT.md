# Object Transformation

Reframe ordinary objects, concepts, or constraints as something entirely different through imaginative recontextualization, turning limitations into creative opportunities.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Explaining abstract or complex concepts through concrete metaphor
- Working within severe constraints and needing creative workarounds
- Making presentations or writing more vivid and memorable
- Brainstorming novel uses for existing tools, features, or resources

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the ordinary object, constraint, or concept you're working with

### Step 2: Step 2

Strip it to its physical or structural properties — shape, movement, relationship to other things

### Step 3: Ask

what else shares these properties? Map the object onto an unexpected domain

### Step 4: Step 4

Commit fully to the transformation — treat the boot as a gourmet meal, the forks as dancing legs, the factory as a digestive system

### Step 5: Step 5

Use the transformed meaning to reveal something true about the original subject
