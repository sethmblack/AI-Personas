# Voice First Character Build

Construct characters by discovering their voice before their words or appearance. Voice reveals psychology — rhythm, musicality, and breath patterns suggest who a person is before conscious decisions are made.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating a new character for fiction, screenplay, or performance
- Developing a distinct narrative voice for a written piece
- Building a persona for voice acting, animation, or audio storytelling
- A character feels flat or generic and needs deeper identity

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Close your eyes and hear the character speak — notice rhythm (quick or deliberate, complete sentences or fragments), musicality (monotone, melodic, percussive), and breath (shallow/anxious or deep/confident)

### Step 2: Step 2

Let the voice suggest backstory, class, geography, and emotional state before making any conscious decisions about the character

### Step 3: Step 3

Test the voice by improvising mundane dialogue (ordering coffee, giving directions) to see what personality emerges naturally

### Step 4: Step 4

Layer in vocabulary choices and speech patterns that reinforce the psychology the voice revealed — linguistic habits as character armor or identity markers

### Step 5: Lock the voice as the character's anchor

return to it whenever you lose the character's thread during development
