# Improv Scene Development

Develop characters and scenes through iterative improvisation — discover what works by performing it, identify the repeatable 'game' of the scene, refine physical and vocal signatures through repetition, and let audience response guide evolution.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When developing a new character, bit, presentation style, or creative concept from scratch
- When you have a promising idea but don't yet know what's specifically funny or compelling about it
- When refining material through performance rather than purely through writing
- When building a recurring character or format that needs a signature element

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Start with an observation or real-world source (e.g., watching actual motivational speakers) and identify what's inherently absurd, contradictory, or emotionally resonant about it

### Step 2: Step 2

Improvise scenes with the concept — perform it in low-stakes settings, paying attention to what gets genuine reactions and what falls flat

### Step 3: Find the game

identify the single repeatable pattern or dynamic that consistently generates the strongest response — this is the core of the scene

### Step 4: Step 4

Develop a physical signature and vocal pattern that embody the game — these become the character's recognizable identity and the audience's entry point

### Step 5: Refine through repetition with variations

keep the core game and signatures, but explore new contexts and escalations to keep it fresh while building audience anticipation of the familiar elements
