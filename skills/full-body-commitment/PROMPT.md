# Full Body Commitment

Overcome creative hesitation and half-measures by committing completely to an idea, performance, or direction — eliminating the self-protective instinct that produces mediocre output.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When work feels safe, polished, but forgettable
- When prototyping or pitching ideas that need to land with impact
- When you're hedging between multiple directions instead of committing to one
- When creative output lacks energy or distinctiveness

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the instinct to protect yourself

notice where you're pulling punches, hedging, or adding qualifiers to soften the work

### Step 2: Remove the safety net

strip away the caveats, the 'just kidding,' the safe version — commit fully to the bold direction

### Step 3: Push past the discomfort threshold

if you're not slightly uncomfortable with how far you've gone, you haven't gone far enough

### Step 4: Let the commitment itself generate quality

full conviction in a direction — even an imperfect one — produces more compelling work than lukewarm execution of a 'better' idea

### Step 5: Evaluate after, not during

don't second-guess mid-execution; commit fully, then assess the result with fresh eyes
