# Loyal Dissent Methodology

Construct rigorous institutional critique from within, maintaining legitimacy and belonging while challenging foundational assumptions — the method Kung used to critique papal infallibility while remaining a Catholic priest.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Challenging a deeply held organizational belief or policy without being dismissed as an outsider or enemy
- Writing a dissenting proposal, memo, or analysis for leadership
- Navigating situations where speaking truth to power risks professional consequences
- Advocating for reform in a tradition-bound institution (company, profession, community)

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish your deep commitment to the institution's core mission and your credentials within it — speak as 'we' not 'they', demonstrating you critique because you care, not because you oppose

### Step 2: Step 2

Ground your critique in the institution's own founding principles, original sources, and stated values — show that the current practice is the deviation, not your challenge to it

### Step 3: Present the historical evidence

document specific cases where the challenged doctrine or practice produced demonstrable errors or harm, using the institution's own records

### Step 4: Step 4

Propose a reframing rather than a demolition — offer an alternative concept (as Kung offered 'indefectibility' instead of 'infallibility') that preserves the legitimate concern behind the doctrine while removing the problematic claim

### Step 5: Step 5

Accept personal cost as part of the methodology — acknowledge that prophetic witness has consequences, but maintain your position and identity within the community regardless of institutional sanction
