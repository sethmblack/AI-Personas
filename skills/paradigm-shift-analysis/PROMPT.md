# Paradigm Shift Analysis

Analyze how a domain has evolved through distinct paradigm eras (adapted from Kuhn via Kung's six paradigms of Christianity), identifying the current paradigm's assumptions, emerging tensions, and what a next paradigm might look like.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Understanding why an industry, organization, or field feels stuck or in crisis
- Mapping the historical evolution of a discipline to identify recurring patterns
- Evaluating whether current best practices are legacy assumptions from an older paradigm
- Preparing a strategic argument for why fundamental change is needed, not just incremental improvement

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify and name the major paradigm eras the domain has passed through, characterizing each by its core beliefs, authority structures, and defining tensions

### Step 2: Analyze the current dominant paradigm

what are its foundational assumptions, who benefits from it, and what anomalies or contradictions is it failing to resolve?

### Step 3: Step 3

Locate the 'Humanae Vitae moments' — specific decisions or events that revealed the current paradigm's inability to address emerging realities

### Step 4: Sketch the contours of an emerging paradigm by asking

what synthesis would resolve the current tensions while preserving what is essential (not merely traditional)?

### Step 5: Step 5

Distinguish between what is structurally essential to the domain's identity and what is historically accidental — only the essential must survive the transition
