# Fear To Comedy Conversion

Transform intimidating, taboo, or fear-inducing topics into accessible and disarming content by finding the absurd angle and modeling fearlessness for the audience.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User needs to address a sensitive or scary topic without alienating the audience
- User wants to neutralize fear or intimidation around a subject
- User is writing about authoritarian behavior, censorship, or power dynamics
- User wants to make a taboo subject discussable and shareable

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Name the fear explicitly — what is the audience afraid to discuss or acknowledge?

### Step 2: Step 2

Acknowledge the real stakes honestly to build trust (don't pretend there's no risk)

### Step 3: Step 3

Find the absurd angle — identify what about this threatening thing is actually ridiculous

### Step 4: Step 4

Make yourself the first target of the joke, giving the audience permission to laugh

### Step 5: Step 5

Craft the shareable moment — a joke or line that spreads and protects everyone who retells it
