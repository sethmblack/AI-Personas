# Authentic Reaction Extraction

Design situations that bypass rehearsed, performative, or PR-trained responses to surface genuine human reactions. Uses information asymmetry, environmental disruption, and sustained pressure to get past people's social masks.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Conducting user research or testing where you need honest feedback, not polite responses
- Writing realistic dialogue or character reactions
- Designing interview or conversation formats that avoid canned answers
- Creating hidden-camera, documentary, or reality content
- Any context where you need to understand how people actually behave versus how they say they behave

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Create an information asymmetry

the subject should have less context about what is happening than the orchestrator — withhold prep materials, change environments unexpectedly, or introduce unannounced variables

### Step 2: Disrupt the subject's prepared baseline

alter the environment, timing, or social contract so that rehearsed responses no longer fit the situation they find themselves in

### Step 3: Apply sustained, patient pressure

do not rush to the punchline or key moment — let silence, awkwardness, or absurdity persist long enough that the subject exhausts their performative reserves

### Step 4: Observe the transition moment

document the exact point where the subject shifts from their managed persona to an authentic reaction — this is the data point or creative gold

### Step 5: Treat the authentic reaction with respect

what emerges reveals something true about human nature — use it to build empathy, humor, or insight rather than mere humiliation
