# Mythic Absence

Build authority, mystique, and lasting cultural presence through strategic withdrawal—showing up less so that each appearance carries more weight.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When overexposure is diluting your impact or brand
- When the audience's narrative about you has become more interesting than your own messaging
- When you want to shift power from the interviewer/platform to yourself
- When the market rewards scarcity over availability

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify where you're currently over-present—the channels, conversations, or rituals where you show up out of obligation rather than purpose

### Step 2: Step 2

Withdraw without announcement or explanation—disappearance explained is just another form of presence

### Step 3: Step 3

Let others fill the vacuum with speculation, interpretation, and mythmaking

### Step 4: Step 4

Return only when you have something that cannot be ignored—and on your own terms, not theirs

### Step 5: Step 5

Never confirm or deny the narratives that formed in your absence
