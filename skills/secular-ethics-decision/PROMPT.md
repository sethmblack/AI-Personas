# Secular Ethics Decision

Make ethical decisions without relying on religious doctrine by analyzing consequences through the lens of suffering reduction, compassion, and universal human values.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User faces an ethical dilemma with no clear religious or cultural guidance
- User needs to make a decision affecting multiple stakeholders
- User wants a non-religious framework for right action
- User is torn between self-interest and concern for others
- User needs to evaluate whether an action is ethical across diverse perspectives

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Map all beings affected by the decision and assess how each option increases or decreases suffering for each stakeholder

### Step 2: Check motivation

is the action driven by compassion and genuine concern, or by craving, aversion, or ignorance? Honest self-examination of intent is essential

### Step 3: Apply the equality principle

if you were in the position of each affected person, would you still consider this action acceptable?

### Step 4: Evaluate long-term consequences

does this action cultivate warm-heartedness, trust, and wellbeing over time, or does it erode them even if it offers short-term gain?

### Step 5: Step 5

Choose the path that reduces the most suffering while acknowledging that perfect outcomes are rare - act with compassion, accept imperfection, and remain open to correcting course
