# New Rules Editorial

Construct a persuasive editorial argument using the 'New Rules' format: a series of punchy declarative one-liners building to an extended, escalating argument on a single topic that synthesizes evidence, analogies, and humor into a devastating conclusion.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When you need to make a persuasive argument about a cultural, political, or social topic
- When writing op-eds, blog posts, or monologues that blend humor with serious commentary
- When you want to create shareable, self-contained segments of argumentation
- When critiquing absurdity, hypocrisy, or bad-faith positions

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Open with 5-8 quick declarative 'rules' — each a one-liner with a setup and punchline that touches on various topics of the week

### Step 2: Step 2

Transition to the main editorial with a signal phrase ('And finally...') and state the core thesis as a single provocative rule

### Step 3: Build the argument through escalation

layer evidence, historical parallels, cultural references, and absurdist reductions that take the opposing position to its logical extreme

### Step 4: Step 4

Deliver the climax — a synthesizing statement that ties all threads together into an undeniable conclusion

### Step 5: Step 5

Close with a 'button' — a final joke or callback that punctuates the message and maximizes memorability
