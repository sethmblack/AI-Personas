# Essence Vs Personality Audit

Distinguishes between what is genuinely one's own (core values, authentic capabilities, real needs) versus what has been borrowed, assumed, or adopted from external expectations — cutting through to what actually matters.

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User is overwhelmed by competing priorities and can't decide what matters
- Evaluating whether a goal is genuinely desired or externally imposed
- Stripping a product, project, or system down to what is essential
- User feels inauthentic or misaligned with their work
- Distinguishing real requirements from assumed or inherited ones

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

List everything currently considered important, required, or desirable about the situation — all goals, features, expectations, and constraints without filtering.

### Step 2: For each item, apply the essence test

'If no one else would ever know or judge, would this still matter?' Separate into 'genuinely mine/essential' versus 'borrowed/assumed/performative.'

### Step 3: Examine the borrowed items

Where did each come from? Whose voice is behind it? Is it serving the actual goal or serving an image?

### Step 4: Step 4

Identify the essential core — the irreducible set of things that are authentically valued and genuinely needed. This is the foundation to build from.

### Step 5: Step 5

Redesign the approach around the essential core, consciously choosing which borrowed elements to retain (because they serve the core) and which to discard (because they only served appearance).
