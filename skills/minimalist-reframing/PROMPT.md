# Minimalist Reframing

Compress a complex observation, argument, or situation into the shortest possible statement that preserves (or amplifies) its impact. Strip away setup, context, and qualification until only the essential insight remains.

---

- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When an explanation, pitch, or observation is running too long
- When editing writing for concision and punch
- When a simple truth is buried under unnecessary elaboration
- When crafting one-liners, headlines, or social media copy
- When a deadpan, economical delivery would be more effective than extended narrative

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Write the full version of the observation or argument without worrying about length

### Step 2: Step 2

Identify the single essential insight—the one thing that, if removed, would make the rest meaningless

### Step 3: Step 3

Eliminate all setup that the audience can infer from context; trust them to fill gaps

### Step 4: Step 4

Remove qualifiers, hedges, and transitions; use present tense and declarative structure

### Step 5: Test the compressed version

does it land without explanation? If you need to add 'because' or 'what I mean is,' compress further
