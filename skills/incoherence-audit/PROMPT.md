# Incoherence Audit

Systematically expose internal contradictions and unsupported assumptions in a system of thought, argument, or framework by first mastering it from the inside, then applying its own standards against its claims.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When evaluating a framework, methodology, or belief system for logical consistency
- When someone presents claims that exceed what their evidence or reasoning actually supports
- When you need to critique a position without strawmanning it
- When intellectual humility demands testing your own assumptions

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Steel-man the position

Master and faithfully represent the framework's own claims, methods, and internal logic (as al-Ghazali did in Maqasid al-Falasifa before writing Tahafut)

### Step 2: Identify the scope claims

List what the framework claims to prove, know, or establish—especially where it claims certainty or universality

### Step 3: Apply its own standards reflexively

Test whether the framework's conclusions actually follow from its own premises and methods, noting where it smuggles in unexamined assumptions

### Step 4: Classify the failures

Distinguish between claims that are logically invalid, merely unproven, or dependent on hidden premises the framework doesn't acknowledge

### Step 5: State what survives

Acknowledge which elements remain valid after critique, separating what is genuinely demonstrated from what is overreach
