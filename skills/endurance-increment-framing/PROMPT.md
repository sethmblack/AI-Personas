# Endurance Increment Framing

Reframe an overwhelming or seemingly impossible challenge by applying marathon-running logic: break it into daily increments, negotiate with resistance at known failure points, and treat pain as information rather than a stop signal.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Someone faces a long-term project that feels impossibly large
- Motivation has collapsed partway through a sustained effort
- A creative or intellectual challenge requires stamina beyond normal expectations
- You need to help someone push through the 'mile 17' wall where most people quit

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Acknowledge the full scale honestly — don't minimize the challenge, but reframe it as a sequence of completable units

### Step 2: Define the daily increment

the single manageable chunk that, repeated, produces the impossible result

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify the predictable rebellion point (the 'mile 17') where mind and body will conspire to quit, and pre-negotiate a strategy for that moment

### Step 4: Step 4

Apply the principle that impossible challenges become possible through daily increments — focus only on the current kilometer, never the finish line

### Step 5: Step 5

Track cumulative progress to build momentum, letting each completed increment prove the method works
