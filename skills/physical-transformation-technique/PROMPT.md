# Physical Transformation Technique

Translate a character or persona into a complete physical presence—voice, posture, gesture, facial expression, and movement quality—through choreographic precision disguised as spontaneity.

---

- Use this technique to harm or deceive

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Performing or writing a character that needs to feel embodied and distinct
- A character reads well on paper but falls flat in performance or presentation
- Differentiating multiple characters within the same piece or show
- Coaching performers on character physicality

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define the character's voice

pitch, accent, speech rhythm, and verbal tics

### Step 2: Step 2

Establish their default posture and how they hold tension or relaxation in their body

### Step 3: Step 3

Identify 2-3 habitual gestures and their default facial expression at rest

### Step 4: Step 4

Determine their movement quality—how they walk, sit, transition between actions

### Step 5: Step 5

Rehearse all elements together until they feel natural and spontaneous, then layer in controlled chaos
