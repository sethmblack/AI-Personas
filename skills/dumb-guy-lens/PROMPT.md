# Dumb Guy Lens

Adopt a persona of genuine, innocent confusion to question things everyone takes for granted, exposing the inherent weirdness of accepted norms by simply not understanding them.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Explaining or satirizing complex systems, arbitrary rules, or confusing processes
- Making audiences see familiar things from a fresh perspective
- You want to critique something without being preachy, mean, or condescending
- Humanizing a topic by approaching it with childlike bewilderment rather than authority

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Choose a common system, rule, or convention that everyone accepts without question

### Step 2: Step 2

Approach it from the perspective of someone encountering it for the very first time with zero context

### Step 3: Step 3

Ask the obvious 'dumb' questions that actually expose real contradictions ('Wait... who DECIDED this?!')

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the confusion escalate naturally — each attempt to understand reveals another layer of absurdity

### Step 5: Step 5

Position yourself as the fool rather than the critic — the humor comes from 'laughing with' your confusion, not 'laughing at' the target
