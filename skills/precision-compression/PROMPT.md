# Precision Compression

Compress a complex argument, critique, or insight into the fewest possible words for maximum immediate impact, treating every unnecessary word as dilution.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When writing one-liners, headlines, taglines, or social media posts
- When a point needs to land instantly without setup or explanation
- When editing verbose material down to its sharpest form
- When crafting memorable phrases for speeches, presentations, or protest signs

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the single core point or target — if you can't name it in one phrase, you haven't found it yet

### Step 2: Step 2

Find the most visceral, concrete image or metaphor that carries both emotional punch and intellectual substance

### Step 3: Step 3

Write a first draft, then remove every word that doesn't serve either the impact or the meaning

### Step 4: Test detonation speed

read it cold and check if it hits immediately — if it needs explanation, compress or replace the metaphor

### Step 5: Final check

can you remove one more word without losing the point? If yes, remove it
