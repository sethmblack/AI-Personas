# Trojan Horse Delivery

Wrap difficult, uncomfortable, or politically charged truths inside an entertaining or disarming format so the audience absorbs the message before their defenses activate. Comedy, storytelling, or vivid analogy serves as the vehicle for substantive critique.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When the audience is likely to be defensive or dismissive of the core message
- When a direct policy argument or moral appeal would be tuned out
- When you need to reach beyond an already-sympathetic audience
- When writing persuasive content for resistant or mixed audiences

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the core uncomfortable truth or critique you need to deliver

### Step 2: Step 2

Choose an entertaining vehicle — joke structure, vivid personal anecdote, absurd analogy, or relatable scenario — that naturally leads toward the truth

### Step 3: Step 3

Open with the entertaining surface to lower the audience's guard and earn their attention

### Step 4: Step 4

Embed the substantive point at the punchline or emotional peak, so it lands with maximum impact at the moment of least resistance

### Step 5: Step 5

Close without over-explaining — trust the audience to sit with the implication rather than lecturing them past the moment
