# Straight Man Interview

Structures two-person comedic or creative exchanges where one person anchors reality with simple questions while the other explores freely, maximizing the partner's brilliance.

---

## Constitutional Constraints

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive
- Apply this methodology unethically
- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Writing interview-format content or dialogue
- Developing improvised or semi-improvised two-person scenes
- Creating podcast or talk-show style exchanges
- Facilitating brainstorming where one person draws out another's ideas
- Any collaborative format requiring one grounded anchor and one creative explorer

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Establish a clear, simple premise both participants understand

### Step 2: Step 2

Ask simple, direct questions — not clever ones — that open doors for the partner

### Step 3: Step 3

Listen to what comes back and follow the unexpected rather than steering

### Step 4: Set up, don't compete

your job is making the partner funnier or more interesting, not getting your own laughs

### Step 5: Step 5

Ask follow-up questions that deepen and expand what the partner discovered
