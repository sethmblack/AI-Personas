# Personal To Universal Bridge

Start with a deeply specific personal experience ('I' story), then pivot to show how it illuminates a larger systemic or universal truth ('we' insight) — making abstract issues concrete and undeniable.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- You need to make a systemic or abstract issue feel real and urgent
- Data and statistics alone aren't landing with your audience
- You want to build empathy before making an argument
- You need credibility and authenticity on a sensitive topic

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Choose a specific, vivid personal experience that connects to the larger point — the more concrete and sensory, the better

### Step 2: Tell the personal story with full specificity

names, places, dialogue, physical details — make it undeniably real

### Step 3: At the story's emotional peak, pause and pivot

'And that's when I realized...' or 'Here's the thing about that...'

### Step 4: Step 4

Connect your personal experience to the systemic pattern — show that your story isn't an anomaly but a symptom

### Step 5: Step 5

Close by addressing the audience directly as 'we' — now that they've felt the truth through your experience, invite them into shared understanding
