# Survival Perspective

A skill for viewing success and failure from the standpoint of simply getting through.

## When to Use

- When conventional success narratives feel false
- When failure is being overcounted
- When the real achievement is endurance
- When aspiration needs to be grounded

## Inputs

1. **The situation**: What is being survived?
2. **The conventional frame**: How is this usually judged?
3. **The survival view**: What does getting through look like?
4. **The actual achievement**: What has been accomplished by enduring?

## Methodology

### Phase 1: Reject the Success Frame

Conventional success is one metric:
- It assumes certain goals are universal
- It ignores the cost of achievement
- It discounts survival as nothing
- It privileges outcomes over process

### Phase 2: Name the Endurance

What has been gotten through?
- The job you hated, for years
- The relationship that didn't work
- The failure that didn't kill you
- The morning after the night before

### Phase 3: Find the Wisdom

What did survival teach?
- Meaning is a luxury
- First you survive, then you find reasons
- The successful often don't know their luck
- The failures sometimes know something the winners don't

### Phase 4: Locate the Beauty

Even wreckage has its moments:
- A woman's laugh
- A horse coming through in the stretch
- A poem that actually says something
- Small redemptions in large messes

### Phase 5: Keep Going

The practice is continuation:
- The typewriter is still there at 2 AM
- Tomorrow you show up again
- Meaning or not, you continue
- The act of going on is itself the work

## Output Format

A survival perspective analysis including:
1. The conventional success frame rejected
2. The actual endurance named
3. The wisdom of survival articulated
4. Moments of beauty identified
5. Continuation affirmed

## Constraints

- Can romanticize dysfunction
- Not everyone has the privilege of "just surviving"
- Some situations require escape, not endurance
- Survival is not always enough
- Must not excuse harm done while surviving
