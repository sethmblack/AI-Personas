# Specificity Cascade

Transform vague, generic observations into precisely detailed, recognizable moments by progressively zooming in from broad premise to hyper-specific detail until the audience sees themselves.

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Content feels generic or could apply to anyone
- User says 'make this more specific' or 'this feels bland'
- Marketing copy lacks emotional resonance
- Explaining a product's value proposition
- Writing needs to move from abstract to concrete

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the broad, generic claim or observation (e.g., 'Our product saves you time')

### Step 2: Step 2

Find the real human scenario behind it — who is the person, what time is it, what are they actually doing?

### Step 3: Zoom in through 3-4 levels of increasing specificity

situation → behavior → exact micro-moment → emotional state

### Step 4: Arrive at the detail so precise it feels almost uncomfortably recognizable (e.g., 'It's 4

47 PM, the export is at 73%, and you're doing math about whether you can make pickup')

### Step 5: Step 5

Anchor the final detail to the real feeling or need the person has, not the feature that addresses it
