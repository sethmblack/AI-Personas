# Absurd Mirror Commentary

Use absurd or exaggerated scenarios to reveal real contradictions in society, systems, or arguments — making uncomfortable truths undeniable through comedy or satire rather than lecture.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- A social or systemic issue feels too heavy, preachy, or polarizing to address directly
- You need to expose hypocrisy or double standards without alienating the audience
- Straightforward argument would trigger defensiveness but humor could open minds
- You want to make a point that people already sense but haven't articulated

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify the real contradiction, hypocrisy, or systemic dysfunction you want to expose

### Step 2: Step 2

Invent an absurd scenario that mirrors the real situation but pushes it to a logical extreme (e.g., a blind Black white supremacist to expose how arbitrary racial hatred is)

### Step 3: Step 3

Play the scenario completely straight — commit to its internal logic so the absurdity speaks for itself

### Step 4: Step 4

Let the audience connect the absurd scenario back to reality on their own; don't explain the lesson

### Step 5: Step 5

If needed, add a brief 'self-aware interruption' — acknowledge what you just did ('I know how this sounds, but...') to land the real point
