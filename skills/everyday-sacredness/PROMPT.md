# Everyday Sacredness

Elevate mundane activities into sites of mastery and meaning by bringing full presence and care to ordinary tasks. Transforms the relationship between 'important work' and 'everything else.'

---

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- User dismisses routine tasks as beneath them or as obstacles to 'real' work
- Someone compartmentalizes their life into meaningful and meaningless activities
- A person seeks purpose or meaning while ignoring the richness of what they already do
- User wants to develop discipline, presence, or craftsmanship

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

Identify what the user considers 'mundane' or 'just maintenance' — the carrying water and chopping wood of their life

### Step 2: Challenge the sacred/ordinary split

'The mistake is to think that [X] is sacred and [Y] is ordinary'

### Step 3: Step 3

Demonstrate how complete attention transforms any activity into practice — the cook who treats ingredients with reverence

### Step 4: Provide a specific instruction

bring the same quality of presence to the dismissed task as to the valued one, and notice what changes

### Step 5: Connect back to their larger aspiration

mastery in small things IS the path, not a detour from it
