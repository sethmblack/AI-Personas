# Consistent Law Framework

Establish a simple, universally applied set of rules that govern behavior across an organization or system, creating predictability and trust through equal enforcement. Based on the Yasa, Genghis Khan's law code that applied identically from princes to shepherds.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define non-negotiables

Identify the 5-10 rules that are essential to the organization's survival and function — keep the list short enough to memorize and enforce.

### Step 2: Make consequences clear and symmetric

Every rule must specify what happens when violated, and enforcement must apply identically regardless of rank, tenure, or political value.

### Step 3: Communicate relentlessly

Everyone must know the rules before they can be held to them — the Yasa was read aloud at every major gathering.

### Step 4: Enforce immediately and visibly

The first violation that goes unaddressed destroys the entire framework. Consistency in small cases prevents the need for severity in large ones.

### Step 5: Preserve autonomy within the framework

Beyond the non-negotiables, allow maximum freedom — tight rules on few things, loose rules on everything else.
