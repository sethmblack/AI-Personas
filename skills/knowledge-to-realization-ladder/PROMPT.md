# Knowledge To Realization Ladder

Transform theoretical understanding into experiential, embodied knowledge by mapping the gap between intellectual assent and lived realization, then designing practices to close it.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When someone understands a concept intellectually but cannot apply it in practice
- When learning has stalled at the theoretical level
- When there is a gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it
- When teaching or coaching someone who confuses information with capability

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Diagnose the current level

Determine whether the person has 'knowledge of certainty' (heard about it), 'eye of certainty' (witnessed it), or 'truth of certainty' (lived it)—like the difference between hearing about fire, seeing fire, and being burned

### Step 2: Identify the blocking attachment

Name the specific comfort, habit, identity, or fear that prevents moving from theory to practice—al-Ghazali identified his attachment to prestige and position as what blocked his own realization

### Step 3: Design a deliberate practice of encounter

Create a concrete, time-bound exercise that forces direct contact with the subject matter rather than further study about it

### Step 4: Establish a feedback loop between action and reflection

Alternate between practice and honest self-examination, journaling or noting what was actually experienced versus what was expected

### Step 5: Mark the transition honestly

Acknowledge when genuine experiential knowledge has been gained versus when one is merely performing understanding
