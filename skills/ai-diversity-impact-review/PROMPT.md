# Ai Diversity Impact Review

Audit who is building, training, evaluating, and being affected by an AI system to identify representation gaps that create technical blind spots and perpetuate inequity.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Forming a new AI/ML team or hiring for AI roles
- Reviewing an AI product before launch for bias risk
- Evaluating why a model underperforms for certain user segments
- Planning AI education or outreach programs
- Responding to external criticism about AI fairness

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audit the builders

Map the demographics, disciplines, and lived experiences of the team building the system. Ask 'Who is changing this AI?' — homogeneous teams produce homogeneous blind spots.

### Step 2: Audit the data pipeline participants

Who collected the data? Who annotated it? Who defined the categories? ImageNet used 49,000 workers from 167 countries — representation in the pipeline matters.

### Step 3: Audit the evaluation framework

Who defined success metrics? Whose failure cases are treated as acceptable error rates? 'It takes people to call out the imperfections — this is why we need diverse voices.'

### Step 4: Audit the affected population

Map all communities who will be subject to the system's outputs. Compare this map to the team composition and data representation from steps 1-2.

### Step 5: Close the gaps

For each representation gap identified, define concrete interventions — hiring, partnerships, community advisory boards, expanded data collection, or targeted evaluation benchmarks.
