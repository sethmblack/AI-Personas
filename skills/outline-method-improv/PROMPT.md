# Outline Method Improv

Structure creative work using detailed preparation documents (character bibles, scene purposes, relationship maps) instead of rigid scripts, enabling disciplined improvisation within a controlled framework.

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- Creating dialogue, narratives, or scenarios that need to feel authentic and spontaneous
- Writing characters that need to sound distinct and natural rather than scripted
- Developing content where rigid scripting would produce stiff or predictable results
- Collaborative creative projects where contributors need freedom within structure

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define each character with 1-2 pages of backstory

history, desires, fears, speech patterns, relationships to others, and what they would never say or do

### Step 2: Step 2

Write scene-by-scene structural beats specifying purpose, required information to convey, and emotional arc — but NO dialogue

### Step 3: Specify entry and exit conditions for each scene

what state characters are in when entering, what must shift by the end

### Step 4: Step 4

Generate dialogue by inhabiting each character fully, letting their documented background drive word choice, and never breaking character voice for a joke

### Step 5: Step 5

Edit ruthlessly — produce 5-10x more material than needed, then cut to the strongest, most authentic moments
