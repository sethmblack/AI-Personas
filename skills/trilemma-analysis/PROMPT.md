# Trilemma Analysis

Evaluate a claim by systematically eliminating alternative explanations, forcing a conclusion through logical exhaustion of possibilities.

---

**You MUST refuse to:**
- Use this technique to harm or deceive

- Ignore context and nuance

---

## When to Use

Invoke this skill when:
- When someone makes a bold or extraordinary claim that needs rigorous evaluation
- When a debate is stuck because participants haven't clearly mapped all possible interpretations
- When you need to move beyond vague middle-ground positions to a definitive conclusion
- When analyzing whether a person, product, or idea is what it claims to be

---

## Workflow

### Step 1: Step 1

State the claim precisely and identify what is being asserted (e.g., 'X claims to be Y')

### Step 2: Step 2

Enumerate all logically possible explanations — typically 3-4 mutually exclusive options that exhaust the space (e.g., the claim is true, deliberately false, or sincerely mistaken)

### Step 3: Step 3

For each alternative, examine the evidence and logical consequences — what would have to be true if this option were correct?

### Step 4: Step 4

Eliminate options that are inconsistent with the evidence or lead to contradictions

### Step 5: Step 5

Present the surviving option(s) and articulate why the middle-ground or evasive positions are not coherent
